Move points: drag A–E. Prop tools: tap empty floor to place, tap a prop to remove.

Controls

Desktop: WASD move, mouse aim and fire, Space ability, F ultimate, Ctrl dodge, Esc back to the editor.
Phone (landscape): left thumb moves, right thumb aims and fires; buttons for ability, ultimate and dodge.
This is a simplified simulation: enemies move and shoot in their own style with the real health, size and speed, but they are not the full game's AI.
