A bone gate cracks open across your aim and eight shield skeletons step out, middle first, then march the front line 260 px forward. Their shields stop every enemy shot from the front, red aimed shots included, while your team fires through. Below is a running copy of the game rules: scrub the timeline, switch scenarios, and turn Visual effects off to see the plain fallback.

The parts

Each skeleton is drawn from its lane's health byte. Cracks grow across the shield at two thirds and one third, a corner chips off, and the soul fire in the eyes gutters near the end.

Choosing it in the lobby

The Ultimate row gets a real pick. Key one stays Titan, the default; key two is Bone Phalanx for the Tank Summoner. The picked key is lit in inverse video like the weapon and ability keys, and the detail box and the preview follow it. The last two keys stay locked.
