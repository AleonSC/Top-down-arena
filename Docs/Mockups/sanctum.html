Sanctum

A holy dome drops around the healer. Enemy bullets die on its shell, and every ally inside is covered. Through the 7 seconds it sends a healing pulse out from the healer to the rim, again and again, and the last one is bigger.

Beat by beat

  1. 0.0 sCast. The dome opens from the healer to full size in 0.6 s and wipes every enemy bullet it grows over. (Same as today.)
  2. 0.3 sAllies can walk in through the shell. Bullets from outside stop at the rim and leave a ripple where they hit.
  3. 0.9 sFirst pulse. A ring of light leaves the healer and reaches the rim in 0.55 s. Each ally heals as the ring passes them, so those near the healer heal first.
  4. every 0.9 sSix normal pulses (20 each). The rim flares white each time a ring reaches it, so the pulse beat is readable from outside.
  5. 6.3 sFinale pulse: brighter, 30 healing. It lands just before the dome starts to flicker.
  6. 6.3 – 7.0 sThe shell flickers, then fades. Bullets can get in again.

The first pulse lands at 0.9 s, so the dome is not weaker than today's steady heal. Pick a look (A, B or C) and say if the pulse timing feels right.
