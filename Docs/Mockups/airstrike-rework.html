Soldier DPS: Airstrike

Gameplay mockup. Airstrike replaces the Cluster Grenade on the ability bar (the grenade moves to the Twin Rifle's Alt). You throw a smoke flare at your cursor and a jet runs a line across the whole arena through it. The view is the full 1600 × 900 arena, so you can see the line reach wall to wall.

      Build notes. One new AbilityId (at the end, before None) or a rename of AbilityId.Grenade, plus an FxKind for the flare, the lane and the jet; that is a snapshot change, so Session.ProtocolVersion goes up. The host lays the bombs (or rounds) as timed blasts down the line, the same way the Cavalry Call's lancers walk theirs (CavalryCall.cs). A and C run along your aim like the Cavalry Call does, so B is the one that feels least like the Tank's charge. Allies take no damage. Mastery scales damage and blast size, as for every ability. Numbers are starting points, not tuned.
