The Warden · intro

Three ~6 s entrances for the wave-5 boss, drawn with the fight's real shapes and colours: the pink octagon, the corner watchtower, push-bullet gates, riot doors and the stage marks on the boss bar. Approximate: browser glow and fonts won't match Ui.cs exactly.

A · Searchlight 6.0 s

Lights out, the tower's searchlight hunts across the yard, finds the middle, and the Warden drops into the pool.

0.0
Lights fade to red-black; a slow red wash on the walls (no flashing).
1.0
Watchtower lamp clicks on; the beam sweeps past the players, then locks on mid-arena.
2.6
Shadow grows in the pool; the Warden drops in. Slam at 3.0: shockwave, dust, screen shake.
3.2
Name stamped on like a prison stamp.
5.0
Lights back up, boss bar fills (ticks at 65 % and 30 %: Lockdown, Riot).

Reuses DrawWatchtower, DrawSearchlight, the last stand's darkness from DrawWardenAlarm.

B · Lockdown 5.6 s

Push-bullet gates close in from all four sides into a cell, a padlock snaps shut, and the Warden breaks the cell open.

0.0
Red warning strips; four gate walls slide in, speeding up.
1.3
They meet in a box mid-arena (clank, shake). Silhouette appears inside.
1.7
Padlock shackle drops shut; the Warden lights up pink.
2.6
Lock breaks: the cell blows outward and the orbs fade.
3.0
CRT scan band types THE WARDEN · LOCKDOWN IN EFFECT; boss bar fills at 4.6.

Reuses DrawPushBullet, DrawGateLock, the scan band from WaveBand.cs.

C · Riot call 6.4 s

The riot doors burst, rioters crowd a ring round the middle, and the Warden walks down from the tower with its ID card on screen.

0.5
Both riot doors burst open; rioters pour in and ring the middle.
1.8
The Warden walks from the tower, a dust puff each heavy step.
3.2
It stops and shows its baton-charge line at the party; ID card slides in.
4.4
A red roar ring: the rioters scatter back out, doors slam.
5.4
Card slides away, boss bar fills.

Reuses DrawRiotDoors, DrawRioterBody, the charge telegraph in DrawWardenUnderlay.
