CRT Backgrounds

Six animated backgrounds in the cabinet's phosphor look. Each one reacts: move over a screen and it follows you, click a screen and it answers, or fire the events below at all six at once to compare. Pick a colour to see them retint (the game gives each background its own).

What the events stand for

  • Menu click: pressing a key on the menu or lobby. Also what clicking a screen here does, at the cursor.
  • Player hit: a player takes damage. Shake, a short white flash, the background gets rough for a moment. Behind the arena this could be the only reaction, kept small.
  • Wave clear: a calm, bright release. Things settle, lock in or open up.
  • Boss warning: the whole screen goes red and pulses for a couple of seconds, whatever its own colour is.
  • Pointer: the menu cursor, or in a match the nearest player. Speed of movement also feeds the energy.

Reduced effects drops glow, the roll bar, flicker, shake and flash, like the other CRT screens do. If you like any of these they'd slot in as extra choices on the Menu background card.
