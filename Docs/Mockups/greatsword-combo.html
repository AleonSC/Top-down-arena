Greatsword: three cuts and a guard

Proposed rework, drawn in the game's Moonlit Crescent look: the big two-handed sword, the tapered sweep, the glassy hanging crescent and the sword wave. The basic attack becomes a three-hit chain: two quick cuts, then a slow overhead rend. The weapon-action key becomes Guard & Rend: hold to stop bullets and build Temper, release to fire a beam that scales with it.

Both stages below are live. Enemy bullets come from the right; one in six is a red aimed shot, which nothing here stops.

Basic attack: the chain

Cut, return cut, overhead rend. The pips over the Knight show the step. The chain restarts after 0.9 s without a swing. Each cut leaves its edge hanging as a crescent; the third hangs longest.

Alt fire: Guard & Rend

Hold the weapon-action key (hold the button below, or Space) to raise the blade: 120° arc, half speed, no swinging. Every bullet stopped adds a Temper pip, up to six. Release to fire a piercing beam for 30 + 15 per pip. Six pips also make your next chain start on step 3.

Why it works

  • Hits 1 and 2 are a light bullet screen you can keep up while moving.
  • Hit 3 is the committed beat, with the longest crescent covering the recovery.
  • Guard trades damage output for safety, then pays it back as a beam.
