Click the arena to aim a strike. It replays on its own every few seconds.
0.0 sUPLINKRed spotting line drops from the sky to your cursor point.
0.3 sLOCK-ONRed circle + spinning brackets; 3·2·1 countdown. Enemies inside get marked.
1.5 sCHARGEScreen dims, thin pilot beam, dust sucked in.
1.9 sSTRIKEWhite flash, column of light, shake, shockwave. Everything inside dies.
3.0 sSCORCHMolten glass crater burns enemies for 4 s.
