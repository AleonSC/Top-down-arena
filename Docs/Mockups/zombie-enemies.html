Zombie Infection · enemy designs

The horde

With Zombie Infection on, every regular enemy is replaced by zombies, all green, all dripping. Two kinds of melee runner set the pace, a Spitter makes you move, a Crawler punishes standing still, and later waves add a Bloater that bursts into a choking cloud, a Shrieker that whips the horde into a frenzy, and a Mender that keeps it alive. The dead still get back up (a green crack and glow before they rise).

The zombies

Green effects (every zombie shares them)

DripsEvery zombie leaves a few green drops behind as it moves; Runners leave a streak. They fade in about a second.
Spore hazeA faint green glow around each body, pulsing slowly. Spitters puff a cloud when they spit.
Hit splatsYour shots knock out a small green spray the way they came in.
Death burstA ring of green droplets and a stain on the floor that fades over a few seconds.
RisingWhere a zombie will get back up: the stain cracks and glows brighter for 3 s, then it climbs out.
Bloater cloudA thick green fog where a Bloater burst, swirling and thinning over 4 s. Inside it you're slowed and can see less.
ShriekA pale ring rolls out from the Shrieker; every zombie it touches gets speed streaks for 4 s.
Mending pulseA soft emerald pulse from the Mender, with a green thread to each zombie it heals.
Reduced effectsDrips and haze off; bursts and the rising glow stay, smaller (the setting players already have).

Notes

  • Replaces the basic version's stand-ins (Swarmers, Brutes and Lungers). Bosses, their adds and challenge rooms stay as they are.
  • Mix by wave: Shamblers from wave 1, Runners from 3, Spitters from 6 (at most 4 alive), Bloaters from 8, Crawlers from 9 (at most 3), Shriekers from 12 (at most 2), Menders from 15 (at most 2). The cap of 60 alive and the growing count stay.
  • Priority targets: the Shrieker and the Mender hang back behind the horde, so getting to them is the challenge; both are frail once you do.
  • Counterplay is the point: Shamblers punish being cornered, Runners punish standing in the open, Spitters push you off a spot, Crawlers punish standing still, Bloaters punish killing up close, Shriekers punish ignoring the back line, Menders punish spreading your damage. Together they keep you moving without bullets on screen.
  • Numbers are starting points for tuning with the bots, like the basic version's.
