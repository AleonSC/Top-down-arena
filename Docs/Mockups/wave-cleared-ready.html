Mockup · Between waves · look C3 with Awards and Ready

Awards & ready up

Look C3 with the Status column replaced by Awards, and a ready system for the next wave. Pressing Ready (or Space) says you are set; the wave starts a few seconds after everyone is ready, and until then there is no timer. The Auto-ready switch is remembered between runs: while it is on you count as ready as soon as your upgrade pick is done, and when everyone has it on the wave starts on the same countdown as today, so solo play keeps today's pace. Three places to put the controls. The buttons and the switch work; the state buttons jump to common cases (the other two players are examples).

1 · Ready bar along the bottom

A button, the Auto-ready switch and the status all sit in one bar under the tally, so the right side stays purely information. Each row gets a small tick on the far right that shows who is ready (a ring for players who are on auto-ready). The bar underneath is striped while the wave is waiting and drains once a countdown runs. The easiest to find, and the closest to the Game Over buttons.

2 · Ready under "Next wave"

The button and switch sit at the bottom of the Next wave panel, where the eye ends after reading what is coming: read the mix, then say you are ready. The tally keeps ready ticks on each row. The bar at the bottom shows the status. The panel is a bit more crowded, but the control belongs to the thing it starts.

3 · Ready on your own row

Your own row is highlighted and ends in the Ready button; everyone else's row ends in a READY / WAITING pill, so the tally doubles as the ready list. The Auto-ready switch goes in the Squad row. The bottom bar is only the status line and timer. Fewest extra elements, and it reads well in 3 players, but the button is smaller and further from the middle of the screen.
