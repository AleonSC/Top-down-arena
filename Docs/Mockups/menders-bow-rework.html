What changes on screen

  • The bow is 30% bigger, with a dark outline, gold tips and a green grip wrap.
  • A quiver sits on the back. After each shot the hand pulls a new arrow and swings it over the shoulder onto the string in 0.25 s, the bow's existing recovery time.
  • At full draw the bow glows gold and the Focus bar pops out behind you. Hit the zone and the bar bursts, the game pauses for a frame, the screen kicks, and a gold arrow flies off wrapped in two wind ribbons.
  • For Scatter, the bow snaps a wide fan of five small arrows, and a teal arc under you shows the cooldown.
