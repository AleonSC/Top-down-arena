Warhorn Volley Space · 14 s cooldown · 0.9 s horn · 3 arrows × 12 to all it passes · pinned 2 s

Eleven enemies close in and four gunners open up. You raise the horn and blow; the sound rolls out in front of you. Arrows arc in over your shoulder from behind and land in a moving front that crosses the whole arena along your aim. Everything the front passes takes three arrows and is pinned; shots in its path are knocked down. The arrows part around you and your allies.

Beats

  1. 0.00 sSpace: you lower your weapon and raise the horn. You keep walking at half speed.
  2. 0.32 sThe blast, 0.9 s: sound rings roll out ahead of you, dust kicks up round your feet, a low rumble in the screen.
  3. 0.92 sThe first arrows land 150 px behind you. Their shadows race in over the floor ahead of them.
  4. frontThe landing line moves along your aim at 520 px/s (the stage scale), a darker band of falling shafts riding over it.
  5. passEach enemy it crosses takes three arrows, 12 each, 0.07 s apart, which stay stuck in it, and is pinned: 40% slower for 2 s.
  6. 2.47 sThe front leaves the far side. The shafts stay in the floor for about 2 s, then fade.

How it plays

  • The skill is the aim: the front covers everything along a lane, so you line the pack up first. It starts behind you, so it also clears what's chasing you if you aim back.
  • It works the same with every weapon: the horn takes the swing slot for 0.9 s, nothing else.
  • Shots in the front's path are cut, so it doubles as a moving shield that sweeps the bullets off the field ahead of your team.
  • Players and allies are never hit: the arrows land round them.
  • Bosses take the arrows but aren't pinned.
  • Level 5 (capstone): a second volley sweeps back towards you 1 s after the first.

Build notes

  • Not built. The DPS Knight has three Space abilities today (Caught Steel, the Gauntlet, Bladed Dive), so this is a new AbilityId at the end of the enum, a 4th lobby key, and a protocol bump for the replicated volley (start, aim, start time: the arrows are drawn from a seed on every screen).
  • Real-arena sizes (the stage is scaled to 720 px): a 700 px wide lane from 150 px behind you to 1,400 px ahead, the front at 700 px/s, so about 2.2 s to cross.
  • Damage happens once per enemy when the front reaches it; the arrows that land in it are the look. The other ~300 shafts are cosmetic.
  • The sounds here are synthesized in the browser (a filtered sawtooth horn with reverb, a noise hiss panned across the field, whistles and thuds), as a guide for the real sound bank.
  • Open: should the front also stop red aimed boss shots? Proposed: no, only ordinary bullets.
  • Open: should the horn also rally allies within 300 px (+15% move speed for 3 s)? Proposed: no, the volley is the payoff.
  • Proposed rare card: Bodkin Points (arrows hit 25% harder and the lane is 20% wider).
