Deployable Cover

Plant a bracket of steel plate in front of you along your aim. Two charges, up to three covers standing at once, each one soaking a lot of fire before it breaks apart.

Deploy animation

The strip shows cover A in its first 0.7 s, from the throw to solid. Bullets are stopped from 0.4 s on.

Wear and break

Dents where shots land, cracks at every sixth lost, the plate darkens and the bar turns amber then red. At zero it bursts into tumbling plates, dust and sparks, and leaves bolts and scraps on the floor for a few seconds.

Rules

  • BlocksEnemy bullets and enemy bodies: they walk round it. Your team's shots pass through.
  • Fourth coverPlacing one with three standing folds away the oldest.
  • BreaksAt zero health; each enemy shot wears it by what it would have hit for.
  • Wave endCovers fold away when the wave is cleared.
