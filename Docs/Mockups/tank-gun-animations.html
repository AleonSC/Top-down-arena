Tank Gun Animations

Scattergun, Flak Cannon and Riot Repeater as they could look in play: recoil, hand work, shells, bursts and hit feedback. The gun shapes and the glowing shots copy the in-game ones (flechettes, orbs, tracers). Everything else is proposed.

Move over an arena to aim. With auto-fire off, hold the pointer down to shoot.

Scattergun 0.45 s · 5 × 7 dmg · cone 0.52 rad

    • Point-blank: the dashed ring is 120 px. Pellets that land inside it show double damage and shove the enemy.
    • Pump action: the front hand slides back and forward after every shot and a red shell drops out sideways.
    • Upgrade: 7 pellets in a 15% tighter cone.

    Flak Cannon 0.8 s · 30 dmg · burst 80 px

      • Lobbed shell: it rises and grows over enemies, with a ground shadow, then bursts at the cursor reticle.
      • Airburst: flash, shock ring and shrapnel. Red enemy bullets inside the ring pop.
      • Upgrade: 2 bomblets scatter from the burst and pop where they land.

      Riot Repeater 0.18 s · 11 dmg · spread ±0.06

        • Suppression: each hit adds 8% slower shooting, up to 50%, fading 25% a second. The meter and the red shot rhythm show it.
        • Bolt cycle: a short kick and a brass casing per round. The magazine block flickers with each shot.
        • Upgrade: two-round bursts, then a longer pause.

        Mockup only. Timings use the real fire intervals, ranges and damage from Weapons.cs. Nothing in the game has been changed.
