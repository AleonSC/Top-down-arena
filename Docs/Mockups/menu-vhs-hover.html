VHS hover, snap to focus

Hold the cursor over any menu button and it turns into a worn tape. Click it and it snaps into focus as the normal button. It stays clean until the cursor leaves and comes back. Pick a look in the strip, or press Auto demo.

Look

A · Tracking tape

  • hover scanlines, a bright band rolling down, red/cyan text split, small sideways jitter
  • click 0.24 s: drops in from above, button squashes, split collapses to nothing

B · Chroma bleed

  • hover washed out and soft, heavy colour fringing, moving grain, no band
  • click 0.32 s: fringes slam together, slight overshoot like a lens locking

C · Rewind

  • hover blue cast, fat band, flicker, and a small tape counter in the corner
  • click 0.30 s: focus pull from heavy blur with a white flash

Notes for the build

  • In Unity this is a per-button draw pass in Menus.cs (scanline quads, band rect, offset text copies for the split); no shader needed.
  • Hover VHS time is stateless (driven by Time.unscaledTime), the snap is a 0.2 to 0.3 s timer set on click.
  • Reduced effects: no jitter, band or grain. Hover just tints and the click is instant.
  • The same pass would apply to the Play picker buttons and the pause menu if you like it.
