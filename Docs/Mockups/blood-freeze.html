Animation beats

  • Charge. A rune circle spins up at your feet while blood streams in from all sides.
  • Wave. A crimson-to-white ring bursts out with screen shake, a flash and ice shards thrown off the front edge.
  • Frost. Cracks race across the floor behind the ring and stay for the whole freeze.
  • Frozen. Each enemy is sealed in a hexagonal crystal with red veins, a glint, and a draining timer ring.
  • Thaw. In the last second the crystals crack and flicker, then shatter into shards and mist.
