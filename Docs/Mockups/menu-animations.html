Menu Drawer Animations

Four ways to animate the new side-drawer menus. Each clip runs the same story on a loop: the pause menu opens, the highlight moves down to Settings, Settings opens in the drawer, it goes back, and the menu closes. The opening style also applies to Settings, Records and How to play from the main menu, and the section change to every window. Use 0.5× to slow a clip down.

Option ASlide and stagger

  • The drawer slides in from the left; its rows follow one after another, the underline draws across, the party rises and the run card drops in with a small bounce.
  • A section change slides the old rows out to the left and the new ones in from the right; the right-hand panel swaps with a short slide.
  • Closing slides the drawer back out. The plainest and quickest to read; the same opening suits Settings, Records and How to play from the main menu.

Option BGlitch in

  • Builds on today's signal-glitch opening: the drawer flickers on with red and cyan fringes and a little shake, scanlines roll in, PAUSED types itself out, and the run card is revealed top to bottom.
  • A section change glitches across: the panels jitter sideways with a colour split for a moment as they swap.
  • Closing squashes the whole thing to a thin line and off, like an old screen switching off.

Option CUnfold

  • A line of light sweeps across from the left edge and the drawer unfolds behind it; the contents fade in once it's open. The run card's corner brackets fly out from its middle to its corners, then the card fills in.
  • The highlight glides smoothly from row to row instead of jumping. A section change folds the run card shut and the next panel unfolds in its place.
  • Closing folds the drawer back into the line at the edge.

Option DCamera push

  • The arena pulls back a little, blurs and loses some colour while the drawer swings in from the side in 3D; the run card pops in.
  • A section change flips the right-hand panel over like a card, with Settings on its back.
  • Closing runs it backwards: the camera pushes back in. The most cinematic, and it makes clear the game is on hold behind the menu.
