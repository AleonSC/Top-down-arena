Force Pact

Guardian Spirit is a bullet-eating ghost that follows you for 8 seconds. Force Pact takes a regular enemy and makes it fight for you. It behaves like the Warden's Guard: it stays at your side, fights what comes near you, and when it falls it comes back after a while. Cast it again on a different kind of enemy and the pact moves to that type. The ability is the same in all three previews; only the way it looks differs. Nothing here is in the game yet. Numbers are placeholders.

All previews No look picked yet
  1. CastAim near a regular enemy and press the key. The nearest one to your cursor (bosses and boss adds are skipped) is bound in about a second.
  2. FightIt becomes a thrall of that type, with that type's attack, and takes on enemies within 240 px of you. Enemy shots and bodies hurt it.
  3. FallWhen its health runs out it breaks apart and comes back at your side 15 s later, at full health, as the same type.
  4. RecastCast on another enemy at any time. The old thrall dissolves into the new one, and the pact now respawns that type.
LOOK A

Soul Chains

What you see

A chain of glowing links shoots from you to the enemy and cuffs it. Its colour drains to grey, then violet floods in. The thrall stays tied to you by a sagging chain with a broken shackle ring turning round it. When it dies the chain snaps, the pieces are hauled back to a ring of links at your feet that fills as the timer runs, then the chain throws it back out.

How it reads

The tether shows who owns what at a glance, even in a crowd, and the sag tells you how far it has strayed. Heaviest on screen of the three: a line and a ring per thrall.

LOOK B

Blood Seal

What you see

A gold circle of runes opens under the enemy, turns, and a seal stamps down with a shockwave. The thrall keeps a small turning seal under it, three rune shards orbit it, and a thin thread links it to a matching seal at your feet. When it dies its seal cracks and burns, and the seal at your feet fills like a clock. At zero a pillar of light drops and the thrall steps out.

How it reads

The most "contract" feeling: it looks like a deal being signed. The ground seal at your feet doubles as the respawn clock, so no number is needed. Quiet during the fight, loud at cast and return.

LOOK C

Override

What you see

A targeting reticle zooms in and snaps onto the enemy while a link percentage climbs. The enemy tears into glitchy slices with red and cyan edges, flickering between its old colour and teal until the link hits 100. The thrall keeps a thin bracket and a dashed signal line to you. When it dies it bursts into pixels that fly to your hex timer, and it is rebuilt there by a scan line.

How it reads

Reads as hacking the enemy, which fits an enemy you did not summon yourself. Teal is also the colour of no existing enemy, so a thrall can't be mistaken for one. The scan-line rebuild is the cheapest to draw.
