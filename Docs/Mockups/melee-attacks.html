Melee swing styles

Today a swing is a white arc and a faint band, a thrust is a streak, and a slam is a flash. Below are four ways melee could look instead, on five Knight weapons that cover the three motions: sweeps, thrusts and slams. The arena is drawn at the game's scale, with each weapon's real reach. Notes for building it

  • All of this is drawing, on each screen: FxKind.Slash (sweeps and thrusts) and FxKind.Jab (the Lance) are drawn by DrawSlashFx and DrawJabFx in Game/WorldEffects.cs. Hits, reach and timing stay in Core/Knight.cs, so styles B, C and D need no protocol change.
  • Wind-ups (A, and C's short pull-back) are the one gameplay question. If the hit waits for the wind-up, attacks land about 0.08 s after the click and feel slower. To keep the timing, play the pull-back during the previous swing's recovery, or draw it over the first frames while the hit lands as it does now.
  • Hit-pause in A uses the hit-pause that's already in Juice.cs (held frames), but short (50 ms) and only for heavy weapons. Reduced effects already turns hit-pause, flashes and shake off.
  • The weapon model (A and C draw it) can come from the class looks in ClassLooks.cs and WorldPlayers.cs (DrawKnight already draws the sword at your side).
  • The same style should carry over to the other melee weapons: the Lance's box, Holy Mace, Lightbringer, Censer, Quarterstaff, the Ninja's Kusarigama, Bo Staff and Katana, and the Hemomancer's Blood Lash.
  • With Visual effects off, the arena should draw exactly as now (the render check stays IDENTICAL). The new look only shows with effects on, like the other effects.
