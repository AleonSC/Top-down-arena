Riot Gear: effects

Three looks for the shield, all on the same timeline: it is up at the start of a run, blocks the first hit, takes 30 seconds to come back, and a small HUD chip shows where it is. Move the mouse over a stage to aim. Bullets fly in from random sides.

Fire a single bullet to see the block, fire three to see the half second of invulnerability after it, or fire during the recharge to see a hit land.

Look 1 Halo

  • A thin ring round you. Ready: steady steel-blue with a slow dashed outer ring. Block: white flash, the ring bursts outward and throws shards from the impact point.
  • Recharging: a faint ghost ring fills clockwise, so you can read the 30 s on the player itself. When it returns it pings once.
  • Reads at any camera size and in a crowd of bullets. Cheapest to draw and the lightest on the eyes.

Look 2 Plate

  • A riot shield plate that hovers beside you and swings toward the nearest incoming bullet. It stands for the Warden's riot troopers, so it looks like the perk's name.
  • Block: sparks off the impact point, a quick white flash, and the plate breaks into three pieces that fly off. Recharging: a ghost plate fills along its length, and the plate snaps back with a small pop.
  • Most characterful, but the plate points at one bullet at a time, so with many bullets it jitters. Heavier to animate.

Look 3 Glass

  • A faint hex bubble split into six wedges. Block: a ripple at the impact point, then the bubble shatters into six shards.
  • Recharging: the wedges light up one by one, so each wedge is five seconds. The HUD shows the same six segments.
  • The recharge is readable at a glance without a number. The six-step timer is also the easiest to see in a screenshot or a replay.

Rules to confirm

  • Blocks one hit from an enemy, a bullet or an explosion. Burning, poison and hazards go through.
  • A block gives 0.5 s of invulnerability, so a burst or a shotgun can't take the shield and the health in one go.
  • The 30 s timer starts when the shield blocks, not when the hit lands on health. It keeps running between waves.
  • Open: a hit that is blocked by a Parry or Barrier first should not spend the shield. I'd say it doesn't.
  • Open: should the Warden's own shield troopers or boss slams count as "one hit"? I'd say yes, so the perk is strongest against the boss that unlocks it.
  • Open: in co-op each player has their own timer. Teammates see your shield, so the shield state goes in the snapshot (protocol bump).
