Soul Lantern

Soul Link leaves the Healer Soldier and takes the Healer Summoner's third ability slot, replacing the old zone lantern, under the name Soul Lantern. Cast it and a lantern appears beside the ally nearest your cursor (or beside you, solo). It links to that player, follows them, heals them through a tether, and spits little flames at enemies near it. Each scene loops the same moment: you at left, a hurt ally at right with your cursor on them, three enemies closing in. The lantern's lifetime is shown at real length (6 s).

Reduced motion is on: each scene shows one still frame.

Proposed numbers (same for every look)

  • Cast: 18 s cooldown. The lantern lands next to the ally nearest your cursor (any distance; a downed ally counts). Solo, it lands beside you and links to you.
  • Link: the lantern hovers about 40 px off its player, follows them for 6 s, and a tether shows the link. If the player dies, the lantern fades.
  • Heal: 8 health a second through the tether, in a pulse every 0.75 s (the real heal is smooth; the pulses are only how it is drawn).
  • Flames: every 0.6 s, a flame at the nearest enemy within 260 px of the lantern. 7 damage, so about 12 damage a second.
  • Capstone: lasts 9 s, and the linked player can't go down while it lasts (the old lantern's trick).
  • Replaces: Soul Lantern (zone) and, because Soul Link leaves the Soldier kit, the Soul Ghost goes with it. Kindred Spirits ("Soul Link heals twice as fast") would be re-pointed at the lantern's heal.

Build notes

  • Needs Core/ work: a new minion kind (the lantern, linked to a player) and a protocol bump. Nothing is built until you pick a look.
  • The Healer Soldier's slot 2 is taken over by the Deployable Medkit (separate mockup).
  • With Visual effects off, the lantern is a plain glow and the tether a plain line.
