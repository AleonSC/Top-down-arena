Adrenaline Shot

Haven is replaced by a single-target boost. Press the ability and the shot goes to the player closest to your cursor, you included. Alone, it always goes to you. For 8 seconds the target moves faster, fires faster and takes less damage. Each scene loops the same moment: you at left, two allies, your cursor drifting from one ally to the other (the bracket snaps to whoever is closest), then the shot. The ally takes one enemy hit before the shot (full damage) and one after (reduced), and the last 1.5 s flicker as the buff runs out.

Reduced motion is on: each scene shows one still frame.

Proposed numbers (same for every look)

  • Cast: instant, 14 s cooldown (Haven was 16 s). Goes to the living player closest to your cursor, measured to their position. No range limit, so a far-off ally can be reached. Downed players are skipped.
  • Solo: no choice to make, it goes to you.
  • Buff, 8 s: +30% movement speed, +40% fire rate, 30% less damage taken. Recasting on a buffed player refreshes the 8 s; it does not stack.
  • Fire rate uses the same multiplier path as Overdrive (which is +100% for 5 s), so it does not stack on top of it: the larger one wins.
  • Capstone (open, pick one): (a) the buff lasts 12 s; (b) it also shields the target for 30 health and cleanses slows; (c) it splashes half the buff to the nearest other ally within 250 px. I would pick (c): it keeps the pick-a-target choice meaningful but rewards grouping.
  • Hallowed Ground (the card that made Haven bigger and longer) would need a new home: "Adrenaline lasts 3 s longer", max 2 stacks.
  • HUD: the target gets three small icons over their head (boot, bullets, shield) and a buff row entry with the 8 s timer. They flicker for the last 1.5 s.
  • Bots: cast on the lowest-health ally when enemies are near; on themselves when alone.
