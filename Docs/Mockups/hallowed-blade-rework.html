Hallowed Blade

The Knight healer's ultimate, reworked. A giant sword drops into the arena and stays planted for the full 8 seconds, sending out a pulse every 2 seconds that burns enemies and heals allies. Three looks to compare against what ships today. Pick one and I'll build it.

Gold edge: smite, enemies in the ring burn Mint wake: restore, allies in the ring heal Numbers are illustrative
0.30 s

A · Planted Blade

Heavy and physical. The sword hits like a meteor, cracks the ground gold, and every pulse flares the cracks.

  1. 0.0 sGold telegraph fills the circle; the sword's shadow grows under it.
  2. 0.6 sImpact: screen shake, white flash, debris, 200 damage shockwave.
  3. +2 sPulse ring with a mint wake. The cracks surge with each one.
  4. 8.6 sFinale pulse, bigger than the rest. The sword dissolves into embers.

B · Pillar of Light

Clean and readable. The sword is made of light and a beam goes up to the sky. Triple ripples roll outward.

  1. 0.0 sA thin beam forms above the target while the sword falls down it.
  2. 0.6 sImpact: the beam flares, ripples race out.
  3. +2 sThree rings (gold, white, mint) with diamond sparks on the front. The beam swells.
  4. BetweenLight motes spiral in toward the blade, which is the heal.

C · Winged Seal

Divine and ornate. A turning sigil on the floor, a halo and spectral wings on the hilt that flap with each pulse.

  1. 0.0 sThe sigil's outer ring traces itself around the circle.
  2. 0.6 sImpact: the sigil snaps in, wings unfurl.
  3. +2 sSixteen light blades fire outward. Wings spread, sigil brightens.
  4. 8.6 sFinale. Wings fold and the sigil fades out.

What building it would touch

  • The sword has to stay. Today the HolySword zone lasts 0.6 s, then Minions.cs spawns a plain Consecrate zone and the sword vanishes. A planted sword needs either a longer-lived zone or a new ZoneKind at the end of the enum, so the renderer can tell the ultimate's ground from the ability's. A new kind changes the snapshot, so it bumps Session.ProtocolVersion.
  • Pulses are visual so far. The sim heals 15 HP/s and burns continuously. If the pulses should carry the numbers (say 30 heal and 40 damage every 2 s), that is a small Core change and the mockup numbers show what it would feel like. Otherwise the rings are just rhythm over the existing ticks.
  • Mastered version. The three-sword cast (cap in Knight.cs) would plant three smaller blades 220 units apart; the pulses overlap in the middle.
  • Reference pages. The Armory and Bestiary are untouched. They will be out of date once this ships.
