Arena layouts

Six fixed floor plans for the 1600 x 900 arena, drawn to scale. Pick one, then click or drag in the arena to put a shooter anywhere and see what its shots can reach. Switch between enemy fire (crates stop it) and your fire (crates don't, walls and pillars do). Each layout also walls the edges: players can only wrap through the teal player gates, and enemies only come in through the red holographic enemy gates that are part of the wall. Try the other edge sets on any layout.

How to read it

Wall24 px thick. Never breaks. Stops enemy shots and yours, blocks walking.
PillarRound wall, 56 px across. Same rules, easier to circle.
Crate44 px (heavy 54). Today's cover: stops enemy shots until worn down, your shots fly over.
Wrap linkA gate in an edge wall. Walk through it and you come out of the matching gate on the other side.
Entry edgeWhere squads walk in. Players can still wrap round every edge.

Decided

  1. Enemies come in through holographic red enemy gates that are part of the edge wall. With open edges they use the whole edge, on the sides the layout allows.
  2. Walls and pillars block your shots as well as theirs. Crates stay one-way.
  3. Each boss cycle gets one layout, picked at random, never the same one twice in a row. Boss waves are the open arena.
  4. Random crates, pots and barrels still spawn in the gaps, clear of the walls.
  5. Paths and layouts mix freely. Narrow Pass, Tiny Arena and Solid Walls close the arena, so the edge gates drop away and the inner walls stay.
