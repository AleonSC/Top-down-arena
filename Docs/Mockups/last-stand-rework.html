Tank Knight: Last Stand

Animation mockup. Visual rework only. The knight calls a cavalry to the field: three charges of mounted knights thunder through the arena while the tank holds the centre. Compare today's plain pulse with three proposed looks on the same scene.

Last Stand 70 s cooldown · 8 s (12 s mastered) · cannot drop below 1 HP · every enemy targets you · +50% damage

Today the ultimate is a tint and a pulse, so nothing tells you or your team what it does. Proposed: a cast that takes over the screen, a horn call, and three cavalry charges that sweep the lanes while a ring counts down the 8 seconds.

    What it changes

    • The cast is an event. The arena dims, a banner slams in and a heraldic circle burns into the floor.
    • Every lane is telegraphed on the floor 0.5 s before a charge, so the cavalry reads as a hazard-free ally sweep, not a surprise.
    • Three charges: left to right, right to left, then a wedge led by the Marshal from the top. They fill the 8 s.
    • The buff is readable: a draining ring, aggro lines from every enemy, and three chips (1 HP floor, +50% damage, all enemies target you).
    • Three flavours: spectral (A), crusader (B), ember dragoons (C).

    Build notes. Drawing and a new summoned-ally visual only. The charges are cosmetic in this mockup (the damage numbers show a possible 60 per trample); making them hit for real would be a design change and needs a Core change, a snapshot field and a Session.ProtocolVersion bump. The cavalry can be a client-side effect fed by the existing ability-cast message. The "Current" view is approximate. Use the scrubber to step through the charges.
