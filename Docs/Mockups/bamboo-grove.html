Proposed numbers

Radius140 px round you (about 4 Ninja widths)
Wind-up0.35 s: ring and ground cracks show where it lands
Spike hit40 damage, once per enemy
Stun1.2 s (heavies 0.6 s, bosses 0.3 s)
Bleed8/s for 4 s after the stun ends (32 total), refreshes on recast
Cooldown14 s
Tank angleStalks stay 1.5 s and block enemy bullets (B's rim fence stays 3 s)
Upgrade ideaDeep Roots: +30% radius, bleed lasts 6 s

The three takes

  • A · Grove: the whole circle bursts at once into a thicket. Instant, readable, every enemy stunned on the same frame.
  • B · Ripple: rings of bamboo race outward from your feet; enemies stun as the wave reaches them. The outer ring stays 3 s as a fence that holds enemies off.
  • C · Impale: a crossed cluster of spears shoots up under each enemy and lifts it off the ground for the stun, then drops it bleeding. Sparse grass-stalks fill the rest.
Gold stars = stunned. Red drops and red numbers = bleeding. White numbers = the spike hit.
