Tank Summoner · Golem Staff

A golem you actually summon

Today the staff lobs a boulder and a small 36 px pentagon follows you. These three options turn the staff into a summon: hold to call a golem up out of the ground, and the golem is the weapon. Pick a look and a behaviour; the drawings animate at game scale.

What stays the same in every option

  • The boulder lob goes. The staff's shot becomes a short stone bolt that the golem copies (so the weapon still fires when you click).
  • Up to 3 golems with extra projectiles, as today. A new summon past the cap replaces the oldest.
  • Golems block enemy bullets and soak boss damage like the Treant. The Titan ultimate is untouched.
  • Needs a protocol bump (new minion fields for the rise and slam draw). Tests: a new golem suite replaces the boulder test.
