Shadow Clone, reworked as a wall

Today the Ninja's Shadow Clone is one decoy that bursts for 60 damage. The rework summons a line of clones in front of you. They do nothing: no attacks, no bursts. Enemies shoot at them, and you can walk into them to shove the whole wall forward and keep it between you and the fire.

Three looks for the same mechanic. Each plays a loop: cast, the wall takes fire, the ninja shoves it forward, it wears down. Turn on Take control to move the ninja yourself and push the wall around.

5clones, 31 px apart (a 155 px wall)
50HP each, 250 in total
7 slifetime, then they fade

A · Sumi Ink Wall BRUSH STROKES, SPLATTER, MELT

The clones are ink silhouettes. A brush stroke is dragged from the ninja to each slot and the figure rises out of a splash. Bullets splatter ink and make the figure thinner. A dead clone melts into a stain that stays on the floor until the next cast.

control: move the pointer or drag, and walk into the wall
  • 0.00 s · CASTNinja flicks a brush. Five strokes sweep out and splash on the ground.
  • 0.10 – 0.50 s · RISECentre clone first, then outwards. Each stretches up out of its splash and settles.
  • HITSquash toward the bullet, ink droplets spray back. The figure gets thinner as its HP drops.
  • PUSHWalk into a clone and it slides, shoving its neighbours. Ink scuffs trail behind.
  • ENDThe clone melts into a puddle and leaves a stain. Expiry melts the wall from the edges inward.

B · Smoke Seal Bunshin HAND SEAL, POOF, PAPER TAGS

The classic. A hand-seal ring spins up around the ninja, then each clone pops into existence out of a puff of smoke with a little overshoot. They flicker faster as they get hurt. When one is destroyed, it goes out in a big poof and drops a few paper tags that drift away.

control: move the pointer or drag, and walk into the wall
  • 0.00 s · CASTA violet seal ring spins out under the ninja. Eight ticks light up in turn.
  • 0.15 – 0.60 s · POOFCentre out, a puff expands at each slot and the clone pops through it, scaled 120% before it settles.
  • HITWhite flash, a small puff and a star burst. Flicker speeds up as HP falls.
  • PUSHDust kicks up at the contact point. Clones wobble as they slide.
  • ENDBig poof, three paper tags spin away. Expiry pops the wall one clone at a time, edges first.

C · Afterimage Phalanx DASH-OUT, ENERGY FENCE, SHATTER

The ninja splits into afterimages that dash out and lock into line with a shockwave. A thin energy fence links neighbouring clones, so the wall reads as one barrier from across the screen. Hits glitch the figure with a cyan and magenta split. A dead clone shatters into shards and the fence closes the gap.

control: move the pointer or drag, and walk into the wall
  • 0.00 s · CASTFive afterimages peel off the ninja and fly to their slots with motion trails.
  • 0.35 s · LOCKEach lands with a ring and a vertical flash. Once all are down, the fence line ignites.
  • HITHorizontal slice offset and a cyan/magenta channel split. Jitter grows as HP falls.
  • PUSHThe fence stretches between clones as they slide. Scan lines crawl up the figures.
  • ENDShatter into pixel shards. The fence reconnects the clones either side. Expiry flickers out.

Rules the three looks share

Enemies target the nearest clone, as they do now. Clones have no attack, no taunt radius and no burst. The ninja pushes with its body and is slowed a little while shoving (the mockup uses 30%), and clones can shove each other, so a hard push drives the whole row. Clones stop an enemy's bullets but not its melee: grunts have to walk around the ends. The capstone could add a second row behind the first.

Mockup only: the game files are untouched. If one look wins, the front end change is in WorldEnemies.cs (DrawClone), and the logic change is in Ninja.cs and Kits.cs (Clone minion becomes pushable, no burst).
