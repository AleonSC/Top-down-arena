Expanding arena · Room building part 2 · step 3

Room fittings

Spend vectors to fit one thing into a room you built. Four fittings, three looks each, all live. Pick a look per fitting (A, B or C), or mix parts.

Turret nest

fires at enemies in its room · enemies can break it · rebuilt each round

A weak auto-turret on a pedestal in the middle of the room. It holds a flank while you fight elsewhere.

Healing well

slow regen while you stand in it · off while you shoot

A place to fall back to between fights. It only heals while you stop shooting, so it can't be tanked in.

Trap floor

a strip across the room · slows and burns enemies · never hurts players

A strip laid across the room between its doors, so a crowd has to wade through it to reach you.

Shutter gate

on a door · the team closes it for 8 s · 30 s cooldown

Shut a door to split a crowd or buy a reload. Press the use key next to it; enemies wait or go round.

Rules to settle

How you buy one
A proposal on the draw screen (click a built room, pick a fitting), voted like a room. One fitting per room.
Price
First guess: turret 14, well 12, trap 10, gate 8 vectors.
Turret
About a Grunt's damage, 300 px range, health of 3 Grunts; broken until the next building round.
Well
2% of max health a second, off for 1 s after you fire.
Trap
40% slow and a light burn while on it; bosses are not slowed.
Gate
Fitted to one door of the room; closed for 8 s, 30 s cooldown, anyone in the team can use it.
