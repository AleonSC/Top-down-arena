Lobby → Main menu return

Four takes on pressing the lobby's back key. Built from the game's own lobby and menu renders. Today the entrance plays backwards (1 s). Each loop: lobby, back press, the move, menu.

Option ACRT power-cycle 1.05 s

  • The lobby squashes to a white-hot line, like a tube switching off.
  • The line shrinks and blooms, then the menu snaps back open from it with a slight overshoot.
  • Feel: clean, fast, fits the CRT look everywhere.

Option BTape rewind 1.0 s

  • ◀◀ REW on screen, the picture rolls and tears with colour fringing and a tracking band.
  • Cuts to the menu through a burst of static (the intro-skip static), OSD flips to ▶ PLAY.
  • Feel: playful, matches the menu's VHS hover.

Option CScan line de-res 1.1 s

  • A bright scan line sweeps top to bottom; the lobby breaks into growing pixel blocks just ahead of it.
  • The menu is redrawn behind the line with a cyan afterglow and sparks; the title lands first.
  • Feel: "redrawing the screen", calmer than A or B.

Option DPanels flip away 1.2 s

  • Party column, then loadout, flip edge-on and vanish over the starfield.
  • Title, menu box and player card flip in one after another, each with a lit edge.
  • Feel: physical, staggered, most "card game".
