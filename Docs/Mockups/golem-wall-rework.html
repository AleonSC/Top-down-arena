Golem Wall rework

Today the Golem Wall is three small grey blocks (22 px radius) that stand in front of you for 6 seconds and then vanish, however much they have left. This rework makes them real golems: bigger, heavier, and they stay until they are broken. Each golem soaks 200 damage, shows its wear as cracks, and crumbles when it is spent. Nothing times out, so a wall nobody shoots at stays up. The arena below replays the same barrage against each style. Watch the timeline under it: the wall lasts as long as its golems do, not as long as a timer says.

Notes for building it

  • Size and spacing are in Core/Kits.cs (Stone Wall: Radius 22 → 38, BlockRadius 30 → 46) and the spawn spacing in Core/Minions.cs (AbilityId.GolemWall: side * (i * 55f) → 76f, and 90 px ahead → about 110 so the back of the golem clears the caster). The capstone's five golems then span 380 px.
  • Lifetime: the cast passes 6f as the minion's life. Companions use T = -1 for "stays", so the walls would pass that instead. The Wall also needs to die at 0 block health. Companions only stagger and get back up (Minions.cs around line 528), so the Wall kind gets its own rule: remove it and play the crumble.
  • Damage: the block loop in Minions.cs (around line 802) already soaks a bullet against BlockHp. The HP fraction already travels in the snapshot (GameNet.cs line 712), so the cracks and the HP bar can be drawn on every game. I don't expect a protocol change, but I would confirm it when building.
  • Drawing: a new Game/GolemWallLook.cs, called for MinionKind.Wall from DrawMinion, the same way CloneWallLook.cs is. Rise and rubble need the minion's age and the time it died. Rise is its age, and the crumble can run as an FxKind sent when it dies.
  • Open question: if you cast again while a wall is still up, should the old golems stay, or be replaced? These previews assume they stay. A cap of one wall per caster keeps the arena from filling with stone.
  • Reduced effects removes the glow, dust and ember passes. Each style falls back to its stone shape, the cracks and the HP bar.
  • README's Golem Wall line ("for 6 s") and the ability description in Minions.cs change with it. The Armory and Bestiary pages are not touched by this mockup and will be out of date.
