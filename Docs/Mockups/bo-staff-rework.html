Bo Staff Rework

The Ninja tank's staff becomes a three-beat combo: a cone slash, a jab, then a full spin. The weapon-action key (Left Alt) swaps the vault for a shove. The stage below loops both on dummy enemies. All numbers are proposed starting values; none of this is in code yet.

Build-up shows the chain rule: clicks stack up with no timer, and Alt wipes the stack. Switch Auto demo off and play it yourself with the buttons, a click on the stage, or the Z and X keys. In Combo and Shove, amber dots are enemy bullets the spin cuts, and the dashed dummies are outside the shove's width.

Build-up rule: every click adds one step (slash, then jab, then spin) and the next click always continues from there, however long you wait. The spin ends the chain and starts it over. Alt, at any point, clears the build-up so the next click is a slash again. So click, Alt, click is slash, shove, slash. Damage totals 68 per full combo, against 16 per 0.4 s spin today.

Open questions

  • Should the slash and jab also cut small bullets, or only the spin? Only the spin keeps the finisher special.
  • Should bosses and heavy enemies shrug off the shove's knockback, or take a shorter push?
  • Is losing the vault acceptable? The Ninja still has Shadow Step and Healing Dash for movement.
