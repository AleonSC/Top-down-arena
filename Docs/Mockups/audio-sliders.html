Fancy audio sliders

Three directions for the volume rows in Settings. All are live: drag, tap the speaker to mute a row, or focus a slider and use the arrow keys. Each fits the existing Master / Music / Weapons / Enemies / Effects list, and the same game panel colours.

What each one costs in the game

  • A · Level meter. Cheapest: 20 rects per row in IMGUI. Bars bounce when you move it and flash amber/red near the top, so it reads as a real meter. Bar heights stagger for character.
  • B · Glow rail. Closest to a normal slider, but with a gradient fill, tick marks every 10% and a bubble showing the value on hover or drag. Needs a small glow texture for the fill.
  • C · Waveform. Most distinctive: a waveform you "fill in". Needs a pre-baked per-row waveform array (about 64 floats) and a line mesh in GL; the waves can idle-animate on the live Music row.

Not wired into the game yet; pick a direction and I'll build it into the Settings screen.
