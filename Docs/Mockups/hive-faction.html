Hive Roster

Each Hive enemy plays the part of one Warden enemy, so the ten-wave cycle can teach in the same order. Everything is drawn as the game draws: vector lines on the dark arena. The loops show what each does; sizes and timings are proposals.

Side by side

The Queen keeps her own Drones, Bombers and Eggs (they come from her fight, not from waves). Colour does the first job of telling these apart: amber workers, orange soldiers, cream shields, gold lobbers, lime stingers, pale needlers, deep-amber bumbles, red hornets.
