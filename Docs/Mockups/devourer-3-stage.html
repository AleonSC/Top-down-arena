Boss proposal · wave 45

The Devourer, in three stages

Today it keeps 260 px from you and turns a double spiral of small shots for the whole fight. For 3 s out of every 10 it gets hungry: it fires rings of big shots, and your shots heal it for half their damage. This mockup splits it like the Warden, the Lich and the Storm Herald: three stages by health, one attack at a time with breathers between, a 2 s stage change that clears the screen, and a last stand below 10%. Each stage turns the hunger into something new: first it eats food you can deny it, then it swallows players, then its mouth heals it only from the front. The arena below plays each stage against two test dummies (you in green, an ally in blue); pick a stage to watch it, or let it cycle.

What it keeps

  • Hunger: shots that land while it's hungry heal it for half their damage. It's now a turn of its own in stage 1, then becomes the mouth in stage 3.
  • The hungry rings: 20 big shots at 90 px/s every 1.2 s, during the Hungry turn.
  • The double spiral: 2 small shots every 0.08 s at 140 px/s, turning 0.17 rad a shot. It's stage 1's opening turn and comes back slower in the last stand.
  • Keeping 260 px from you, except when it lunges, burrows or eats.
  • Its look: a 12-sided crimson body, 50 px, whose mouth opens when it's hungry. It grows each stage (50 → 58 → 64 px).

What's new

  • Morsels: food it spits out and crawls over to eat. Shoot them or step on them first. It's something to shoot while you hold fire on the boss.
  • Snap and Frenzy: lunging bites along a warning line, like the Lunger at boss size.
  • Swallow: a co-op mechanic. A cone pulls you towards its mouth, and if you reach it you're inside. Allies' shots do double damage while you're in there, and enough of them spit you out early.
  • Burrow and Bile: a ripple chases you underground and erupts. Lobbed globs leave acid pools.
  • The Maw: in stage 3 the hunger has a direction. Shots into its mouth heal it and shots on its back hurt more, so one player draws the mouth while the other flanks.
  • Jaws: two curved rows of teeth that close on you, with a gap at each side.

To build it

  • Stage, turn, timers, lunge line, burrow ripple and the swallowed player in a DevourState on the boss, synced like HeraldState; thresholds 65 / 30 / 10%. The old 10 s hunger timer in LateSpecial goes.
  • Hunger stays in HurtEnemy: Hungry(e) covers the Hungry turn, and a facing check covers the Maw (heal inside its 90° cone, ×1.25 outside). Burrowing and changing stage go in BlocksShot.
  • New: morsels as an EnemyType.Morsel with ParentId = the Devourer (60 HP, popped by walking over them). Swallowed: Player.SwallowT (synced) pins the player to the boss and hides them. The inhale pull works like the Gale's wind in LocalControl. Bile pools reuse the Queen's honey puddles.
  • Health: BossHpMult for the Devourer (2× now) retuned in the Boss Lab for 60–120 s, plus Bestiary entries, stage banners in StageShiftLook.cs, bot rules (morsels, lines, ripple, cone, flanking) and a devourer sim test per stage.
