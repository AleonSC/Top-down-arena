Who's playing, to the menu

Three looks for the move from "Who's playing?" into the main menu when you pick a profile, and back out when you press "Switch profile". Each one has its own sound cues. Press Replay or click a card in the picker to play it; click "Switch profile" on the menu to play it back. Sound starts after your first click on the page.

The looks

Click a header to play that look above. Times are from the click. The ▶ buttons play one cue on its own. The same cues play when the move runs backwards (Switch profile), with the rising sounds turned into falling ones.

Notes for building it

  • A new Game/ProfileTransition.cs with a clock like MenuIntro.cs. ChooseProfile starts the move in instead of closing the screen at once, and the menu's "Switch profile" button starts it backwards. Neither screen takes a click while it plays, and any key skips to the end.
  • The picker and the menu already share the title's place and the axis, so only the parts above, below and on the axis move. The axis never leaves the screen.
  • Decision for you: at launch the menu's plotted intro (the pen writing the title, 2.75 s) waits for the picker. In all three looks the title is written or slides in as part of the move, so I would let this move replace the plotted intro after a pick, and keep the plotted intro for the other ways into the menu (back from the lobby does not play it today). Say if you want both.
  • Sound: new Sfx values at the end of the enum, made in SoundBank.cs from the same pieces as the pause and picker sounds, played from a small ProfileSounds.cs the way PickSounds.cs does. The pen scratch is the menu intro's. They follow the Effects volume.
  • Reduced effects or Visual effects off: a 0.4 s crossfade with one soft click and a quiet chord, no flying cards.
  • Audio and drawing only: no network message or save format changes, so no protocol bump.
