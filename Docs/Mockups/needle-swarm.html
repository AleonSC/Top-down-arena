How it works

  • A thick ring of blood needles circles you all the time.
  • Missing needles are siphoned out of you one by one; each costs health.
  • A needle blocks one enemy bullet and breaks.
  • An enemy in range: the swarm slowly grows a tendril to it, then wraps it in a spinning ring that cuts until it dies (it moves at half speed while wrapped).
  • When it dies the needles flow back into the orbit.
  • Siphoning stops below 15% health (proposed safety rule).

Tuning

Mockup only: 250 health, 3 health/s regen and a free refill at 0, so the demo keeps running.
