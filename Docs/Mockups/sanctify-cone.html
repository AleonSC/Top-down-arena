Mockup · Elementalist · Healer

Sanctify Light Cone

Sanctify stops being a pulse around you. It becomes a massive cone of light thrown along your aim. Enemy bullets outside the cone slow to a standstill and hang in the air. Everyone standing in the light heals over time. Three looks for the same behaviour, all looping below.

In every preview: you are the teal healer, bottom left. The blue ally stays in the light. The amber ally keeps stepping across the lower edge. The violet ally is outside, so their health bar does not move. Enemies keep firing the whole time, so frozen bullets pile up around them.

ACathedral

Warm gold shafts of light. Long god rays shimmer through the cone and dust drifts toward the far end. The floor outside goes dark and cold. Frozen bullets get a pale gold halo, like a candle behind frosted glass. Softest and calmest of the three, so it is the easiest to read in a busy fight.

BRadiant Lens

Crisp, geometric and loud. A hard white rim with a gold and cyan split, wave arcs rolling out of the caster (each one is a healing tick), a turning sigil and a lens flare at the apex. Bullets outside lock into cold star glints with a ring, as if time stopped on them. Most readable as a "tick", least subtle.

CDawnfall

A fan of feathered beams, like a wing opening. Eleven blades of amber and rose light that sway and overlap, brightest at the caster, with golden leaves lifting off them. The dark outside is a deep violet. Frozen bullets are set in amber beads with a glint. Most distinct from Meteor, Blizzard and Tsunami, and the most "healer".

Another name for it

A cone that freezes bullets and heals over time is not really a "sanctify" any more. Candidates, with the first one my pick. The enum AbilityId.Sanctify would stay as it is, so nothing on the wire changes for the rename.

Daybreak (my pick)

Light spilling across the arena while the bullets hang in the dark. Short, fits the Dawnfall look, and nothing else in the kit is called that.

Hallowbeam

Plain and clear about the shape. Reads well on the ability tile.

Stillness

Names the bullet freeze rather than the light. Best with look B.

Lumen Sanctum

Keeps a nod to Sanctify. Longer, so it needs a smaller tile label.

Notes for building it

Netcode

Needs a cone zone in Core and a new FxKind for the light, so a protocol bump. Bullets would carry a slow factor instead of being erased, which touches the snapshot.

Reduced effects

Rays, motes, leaves and the flare drop out. The cone fill, the rim and the frozen bullets stay, so it still reads.

Left out of the preview

The ability tile, the cast sound and how a mastered cone looks. Say which look you like and I will mock those next.
