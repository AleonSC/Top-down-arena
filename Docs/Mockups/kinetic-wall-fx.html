Kinetic Wall force field

The lens as a living field: it boots up from the middle, every shot sends a ripple across it, caught shots hang in it crackling, and it strains and heats up as it fills. The unleash sucks in, then blasts.

What animates

  1. Boot: the lens draws out from its middle in 0.3 s, with a bright scan line racing to each end.
  2. Surface: A: hex cells that shimmer and flare. B: a wobbling skin of thin arcs with light streaks flowing along it.
  3. Impact ripples: each catch sends a bright pulse both ways along the field and a ring at the hit point.
  4. Drag: a shot slows to a stop over 0.3 s inside the field, leaving a fading streak.
  5. Held shots: jitter in place, each with a small flickering arc to the nearest rim.
  6. Strain: as it fills, the field brightens and shifts from violet toward hot pink, sparks run faster, and above 80% it flickers.
  7. Unleash: 0.15 s collapse to a bright core, then a cone of rings, the held shots flung as streaks, enemies flash and get shoved.
