Customization Window

Three looks for the window that opens when you click your name chip on the main menu. It holds what Rank unlocks: a frame for your party card, a trail, and a menu background. Your title comes with your rank tier and shows beside them. The chip itself is now a generic player with your title, badge and XP; the role and class are chosen in the lobby.

Click a tile to equip it (click it again to take it off). Locked tiles show the rank they need. Use the switch to see the window at different ranks. Tiles marked example are not built yet; they show how a fuller ladder would fit.

Look AWardrobe

  • You on a stage on the left, moving, with your trail and your menu background behind you, and your name, title and badge under it. Whatever you pick shows there straight away.
  • Tabs on the right for Frame, Trail and Background, each a grid of tiles with a live sample. The strip along the bottom is how the others see you in a lobby.
  • Best for trying things on. The most space for a growing ladder, because only one category shows at a time.

Look BLocker

  • Everything on one page: a row per category, each a strip of tiles, so you can see your whole look and what is left to earn at once.
  • The preview is a fixed column on the right: your lobby party card and your menu chip, exactly as they'll look.
  • Best while there are few cosmetics. Once a category has more than a row of tiles it would need scrolling.

Look CDrawer

  • Built like Records and Settings: a drawer on the left with your rank and a row per category (each shows what's equipped), and the open category on the right as a list with a large preview.
  • Reuses the game's own window pattern, so it needs the least new UI code and slides in like the others.
  • The reward ladder (rank by rank) is the last row, so progress and customizing live in one place.
