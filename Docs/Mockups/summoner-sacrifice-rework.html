Summoner Call

Sacrifice blows up the minions you already have and leaves you without them for 3 seconds. This replaces it with an ability that brings something new in and adds to your army instead of cashing it in. Four concepts, each a different kind of summon: a gate that keeps spawning, a turret beast, a bodyguard that hunts, and a marching wave. They are kept apart from Bat Swarm (a swarm) and Alpha Call (a frenzy), so the DPS row stays varied. Nothing here is in the game yet. Cooldowns, damage and lifetimes are placeholders.

CONCEPT A

Rift Gate

What happens

A rift tears open at your cursor and stays for 6 s. It spawns a ghoul every 0.35 s. Each ghoul charges the nearest enemy, claws it a few times and falls apart.

How it plays

An area you place. Put it where enemies come from and the arena fills with small attackers. Rewards good placement; the gate itself can't move.

CONCEPT B

Hydra

What happens

A three-headed hydra rises at your cursor for 8 s. Each head picks its own target and spits venom bolts, so one summon covers three enemies at once.

How it plays

A stationary turret that never needs aiming. Strong against spread-out waves, weak if you leave it behind. Gets the most from Wolf Whistle and Imp Caller style play where you keep moving.

CONCEPT C

Reaper

What happens

A spectral reaper joins you for 7 s. It lunges at the nearest enemy and spins its scythe in a full circle, hitting everything within reach twice, then goes for the next one.

How it plays

One strong minion that fights on its own and follows you. The best fit for a build that wants a single big bodyguard rather than a crowd. Hits harder per target than the others.

CONCEPT D

Bone Tide

What happens

Seven skeletons burst out of the ground in a wedge in front of you and march along your aim for about 5 s. They hit what they touch, slow it, and crumble one by one.

How it plays

A directional push, like a bowling lane of bodies. You aim it, it clears a path and buys space. The shortest-lived of the four, so it has the shortest cooldown.

  • All four add to your army. None of them removes your companions, so Imp Caller, Wisp Lantern and Wolf Whistle keep their usual crowd. Sacrifice's CompanionCd reset goes away.
  • They are meant to be separate from existing summons: Bat Swarm (many small hunters), Alpha Call (a buff), Guardian Spirit and Phoenix (support). A is a spawner, B a turret, C one big minion, D a directional wave.
  • Any of them needs a new MinionKind (and for A, an FxKind or zone for the gate), added at the end of the enum, plus a bump to Session.ProtocolVersion.
  • AbilityId.Sacrifice, its README rows, the explode icon in PixelArt.cs and its Armory page would all be renamed or replaced. The Armory and Bestiary pages are not touched until you say.
