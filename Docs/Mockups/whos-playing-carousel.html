Who's playing? · the monitor with its drive, and a disk carousel

The floppy drive is now part of the monitor, in its chin under the screen, on both screens. Who's playing? shows only the monitor and drive, with one big disk in the middle of an endless side-scrolling carousel (up to 50 profiles). Scroll with the arrows, the arrow keys, the mouse wheel or a drag; click the middle disk to load it. Manage profiles and New profile sit underneath. Loading the disk sends it up into the drive, and the B case rises in under the monitor as the main menu, now without a drive of its own.
