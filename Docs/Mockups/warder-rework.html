MOCKUP · ENEMY REWORKWarder rework

Today's Warder is a violet octagon, the same size and silhouette as the other 16 px enemies, and its tell is a thin dashed line. These three give it a shape you can pick out from across the arena, a visible cast, and a "kill me first" read. Each scene loops the same 10 s: cast, bubbles up, an ally takes a hit, the Warder dies and the bubbles pop.

What would change in the game

  • Body: new silhouette and idle motion in VarietyLook.cs / WorldEnemies.cs; hitbox, health and AI stay as they are.
  • Cast: a 1.4 s charge before each ward (today it is instant), then glyphs fly to the allies. Gameplay-neutral if the ward still lands at the end of the charge.
  • Tether: beads flowing from the Warder to each ally instead of a dashed line, so you can see which way it points.
  • Marker: a small violet chevron over the Warder while any ward is up. Optional.
