Great Star stance

A second stance for the DPS Ninja's Shuriken. Left Alt draws one huge star off your back (and rips out any stuck stars). Hold LMB and swipe to throw it; the shape of the swipe bends its flight, so it can curl round cover. It cuts through everything in its path and flies back to your hand.

Three ways the swipe could work. Pick one and play: swipe on the arena. It runs a demo throw if you leave it alone. Browser drawing, so the look is approximate.
