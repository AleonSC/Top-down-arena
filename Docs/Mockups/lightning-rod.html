Become a conductor. For six seconds you crackle with charge, take 80% less damage and pull every enemy onto you, and every enemy hit that lands calls a bolt from the sky down on whoever landed it. How it plays

  • 6 s

    You crackle with charge: a blue glow round you and small arcs jumping on your body. It ends after 6 s, or at once if you go down.

  • −80%

    Damage you take while it's up. A 10-damage shot takes 2, shown as a small grey −2 beside you.

  • Taunt

    Every enemy, bosses too, goes for you for the whole charge (the same path as Roar). Their marks turn blue.

  • 30

    Each enemy bullet that hits you calls a bolt down on the enemy that fired it, scaled by your damage. If the shooter has died, the bolt still lands where it stood.

  • 2 × 15

    The bolt arcs on to the 2 enemies nearest it, within 320 px, drawn as jagged lines.

  • +40%

    It counts as lightning: every wet enemy takes the usual extra arc from where it lands.

  • Melee

    Contact and melee hits on you call a bolt too, on whoever hit you (bodies, the Swordsman, Reavers, Riot Shields, the Devourer's bite).

  • 0.25 s

    The cap: one bolt per enemy every 0.25 s, so a shotgun volley calls one bolt, not five.

  • Lv 5

    Mastered: lasts 8 s and each bolt arcs to 3 enemies.

Calls made where the brief left room

The cap is per shooterEach enemy can call one bolt every 0.25 s. The other pellets of a volley are still grounded (cut by 80%) but call nothing. Arcs and wet arcs aren't capped.
Arcs reach 320 px"The 2 nearest" needed a limit, or a lone bolt would arc across the whole arena. 320 px is about a screen third.
Shots in your hit grace countAfter a hit you can't be hurt for a moment. While charged, a shot that touches you then is still grounded: used up, no damage, and its bolt falls.
Unknown shootersA shot fired by nothing in particular (a hazard, a ring off a bomb) strikes the enemy nearest to where it was fired from, within 160 px.
Capstone: 8 s and 3 arcsLonger pressure and one more arc per bolt, as the brief suggested. The 80% cut stays the same.
No Roar cut on topIt uses the taunt path for targeting only, so Roar's own damage cut never stacks with the 80%.
