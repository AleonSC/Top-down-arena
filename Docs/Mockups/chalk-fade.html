Mockup · Healer Summoner · Spirit Chalk

Chalk Fade

Plain ink no longer stops bullets. It burns down on its own over 10 seconds. The rare card Warding Ink makes it a wall again, but each bullet it stops takes 1.5 s off the dot it hit, so a busy wall is gone in a few seconds. Four looks for the fade. Every scene draws a line, lets it age, then an enemy at the right starts shooting at you from 3 s on. Turn Warding Ink off and on to compare.

Reduced motion is on: the scenes show one still frame each.

Shared across all four

  • Life: every dot lasts 10 s (was 4 s). A loop's ring still holds for 3.5 s, then the dots carry on fading like any other.
  • Default (no card): enemy bullets fly straight through the ink. It still burns and slows enemies and heals allies.
  • Warding Ink (rare card, Spirit Chalk only): bullets are destroyed on contact, and the dot they touched loses 1.5 s. Seven hits finish a fresh dot, and a dot gone from the line is a gap bullets pass through.
  • Hit feedback: a spark burst in the look's own colour at the dot, plus a brief flare on that dot so you can see which part of the wall is wearing thin.
  • Readout: the clock and the "ink left" bar under each scene show total life left across the line. The game itself has no readout; the line's look is the readout.
  • Reduced effects: the line thins and dims (look A) and the sparks and motes are skipped.
