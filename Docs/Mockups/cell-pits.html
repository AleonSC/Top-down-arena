Pits

Voids cut into some cell layouts. An enemy that goes in is gone. A player who goes in loses some health and climbs back out at the nearest edge. Approximate HTML mockup, not the real draw code.

Proposed rules

WhoFalls in
EnemyDies at once, counts as your kill. Coins pop out onto the rim so shove kills still pay.
Boss, mini-boss, flyersImmune: they hover over pits.
PlayerTakes 15% max HP, respawns on the rim next to where they fell, 1 s shield. Can't kill you: stops at 1 HP.
DashCrosses a pit if it lands on solid ground; a short dash that ends over the void falls.
BulletsFly over. Pits block walking, not shots.
  • Edge grace: you fall only once your centre is over the void, so brushing the rim is safe.
  • Shoves: knockback, explosions and charges are what sends enemies in.
  • Steering and bots: treat pits like walls when pathing.
  • Network: pits are layout data, seeded per cell like walls, so no new wire data beyond the fall event.
