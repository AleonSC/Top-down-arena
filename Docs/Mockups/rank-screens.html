Rank Screens

Where a cosmetic account Rank would show up: a badge in three styles, then the end-of-run screen, the lobby, the main menu chip and Records. Pick a badge style with the switch below and every screen redraws with it. All numbers are placeholders (a rank costs 200 x rank XP; a run is worth roughly 400 to 1,200 XP).

Rank is separate from the Level on the results cards, which is the in-run level you earn during a match. That is why the badge is a different shape and says RANK.

BadgeStyle

BadgesThree styles, five tiers, four sizes

  • The colour is the tier: steel (1-4), blue (5-9), teal (10-19), violet (20-29), gold (30+). The tier also has a title: Recruit, Challenger, Veteran, Champion, Legend.
  • A is the simplest to read at tiny sizes. B adds chevrons for the tier (they drop out when small). C puts pips on the ring.

Screen 1End of run

  • This is the payoff moment. The XP breakdown ticks in line by line, the modifier multiplier lands last (this is where harder modifiers pay off), then the bar fills.
  • On a rank up the bar fills, snaps and restarts, the badge pops with a ring, and the reward card unlocks. Without one, the card says how much XP is left.
  • Your party mate's rank shows smaller on the right. It would replace nothing on the existing results cards; this panel sits beside them, or opens after the stats, whichever you prefer.

Screen 2Lobby party cards

  • The badge sits on the corner of each portrait, so the card keeps its three lines. Cosmetic rewards show here: Sam has the gold frame and a title on the hover card.
  • Hover a card (shown on the first) for the title, total XP and equipped cosmetics. Everyone sees everyone's rank, which is where it matters in co-op.

Screen 3Main menu name chip

  • The bottom-left chip gets a second line (your title) and the badge, with a thin XP bar along its bottom edge. Clicking still edits the name.
  • On hover it shows the XP numbers (strip above, right). It is the first thing you see on launch, so it never needs its own screen.

Screen 4Records profile

  • The full view. Left: rank, XP and what is equipped. Right: the reward ladder and recent runs, with the modifiers each run used and the XP multiplier they gave.
  • The modifier badges from the unlock plan (one per modifier you have won with) fit on this page under the runs.
