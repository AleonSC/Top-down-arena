How it plays

RISE1 s

Rock pillars erupt around you (20 each, shove 140 px) and the earth piles onto you. You can't be hurt while it forms.

STOMPwalking

Every step (0.42 s) the planted foot hits enemies under it for 25 and flattens them for 0.25 s. Runners and fodder pop in one step.

SLAMfire · 1 s

Both fists into the ground 110 px ahead: 60 to everything in 110 px, stun 0.6 s, a ring of stone spikes and a hard shove. Replaces your weapon.

BODYpassive

70% less damage, no knockback or slows, 80% speed. Enemies are pushed aside and every enemy within 400 px goes for you.

CRUMBLEat 15 s

The golem breaks into 8 boulders that roll outward (40 each) and you step out. Also ends at once if you go down.
