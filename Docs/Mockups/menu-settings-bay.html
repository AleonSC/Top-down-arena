Main menu settings in the bay

Press SETTINGS on the case: the shutter rolls up onto a digital settings screen in the bay. Pick a group and the screen switches off to a line and back on with that group's settings, all inside the same small screen. ‹ BACK (or Esc) returns to the groups, and SETTINGS again (or Esc on the groups) rolls the shutter down. The arrow keys and Enter work too. This is an approximate browser mockup: glows and type will differ in the game.

The door

SETTINGS rolls the same shutter Play and Join use; its lamp stays lit while settings are open.

Groups

Four tiles on the screen: Audio, Visual effects, Gameplay, Keybinds. Hover or arrow keys select, click or Enter opens.

The switch

Opening a group or going back turns the tube off to a bright line and on again (0.3 s), then the header types itself.

Controls

Digital: bar sliders with − and +, ON / OFF keys, segmented choices, and key caps you click then press a key to rebind. Each change flashes SAVED.
