Katana combo rework

The Katana stops being a single repeating slash. It becomes a fixed three-step chain, swipe, swipe, lunge, always in that order, and every hit charges a meter that pays for a map-wide Alt strike.

Both stages below are playable. Everything is mockup timing, not final numbers.

Sounds start after your first click or key press on the page.

1 · The chain swipe → swipe → lunge

Press attack to start the chain. Press again while a step is playing and the next step is queued, so tapping through the two swipes lands the lunge as soon as the second swipe ends. Stop for 0.7 s and the chain resets to the first swipe. The lunge replaces today's sheathe-and-quick-draw cut.

2 · The Alt strike hold Alt, aim, release

When the meter is full, hold Alt. Time crawls, every enemy gets a bracket, and the one nearest your cursor is marked with a route line that runs through it and ends behind it. Release Alt to go: the screen freezes, you cross the map as one cut, and the enemy falls a beat after you have already sheathed.

Beat by beat

  1. holdTime drops to 12% speed, the arena dims, brackets appear. Nearest enemy to the cursor gets the crimson reticle and a dashed route to its back.
  2. 0.35 sRelease. Time stops, black bars slide in, you crouch in the sheathed stance.
  3. 0.14 sThe cut: one bright line drawn across the map from you to the point behind the target. You are gone from the start and appear at the end. Invulnerable from release to the end of the cut.
  4. 0.65 sHeld frame. The line stays, you sheathe (small ring), the enemy is still standing.
  5. 0.8 sThe enemy splits along the line, 120 damage. Anything the route crossed takes 60 and a crimson stain. Time returns.

Rules in this mockup

  • Costs the whole meter (100). Charge it with swipes (+10) and lunges (+20) per enemy hit.
  • The meter drains at 25 per second once you have not hit anything for 1.2 s, so a full bar is gone in 4 s of standing still.
  • No range limit and no line of sight. The route ignores walls and bullets.
  • You always end 52 px behind the target, facing away from where you came from.
  • Releasing Alt with less than a full meter does nothing, and the bar shakes.
  • The Alt strike is not part of the chain. Using it does not reset or advance swipe, swipe, lunge.

What changes from today

  • The 0.6 s sheathe and 220 px quick-draw (2.5× for 50) is replaced by the lunge as step 3.
  • Slashes stop being a repeat of one animation. The two swipes alternate direction.
  • A new meter on the Ninja HUD, charged by hits, drained when you stop hitting, spent by the Alt strike.
  • Alt strike is a new input. Shadow Step and Kunai Mark already teleport, so this needs a different look and a different trigger from both.

Open questions

  • Is 25 per second after a 1.2 s grace the right drain? Should taking damage drain it faster?
  • Is Alt the right key? The weapon-action key is already used by Shuriken and Kunai. Katana could use either.
  • Should the route cut other enemies for 60, or only the target?
  • Do bosses take the full 120, or a capped share?
  • Co-op: does the freeze stop time for all players, or only slow the striker's own view?
