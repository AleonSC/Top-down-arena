Panel Gradients and Glow

Real renders from the game's own drawing code (not a redrawn mockup): the current look on the left, the new one on the right. Nothing is committed yet.

Main menu

The menu box is shaded from a blue wash at the top to near-black at the bottom, with a soft blue glow round it. The same look carries through the animation where it grows into the lobby.

Who's playing?

Each profile card is shaded and glows in its rank color; the one under the mouse glows stronger. Empty and full slots stay dim and barely glow. (This preview uses three test profiles.)

Lobby

The big box behind every lobby tab (Loadout, Perks, Difficulty, Modifiers, Hunt, Practice) gets the same blue gradient and glow as the menu box, so the menu-to-lobby move doesn't change its look midway.
