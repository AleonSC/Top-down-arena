Ability tiles that answer back

The ability bar at the bottom of the screen, as the game draws it now, with four ways to animate it. Each style covers the same five moments: a cooldown finishing, a second charge coming back, using an ability, pressing one too early, and the ultimate filling up. It plays by itself; use the buttons to fire one moment, or press Space Q E F (or click a tile) to play it yourself.

Notes for building it

  • All of it lives in the HUD on your own screen (Hud.DrawAbilityBar plus a small HudTiles.cs): no simulation or network change, no protocol bump.
  • Cooldown finishes and charge comes back are found by comparing each slot's Charges with last frame's. Use and too early come from the key press: today a press while recharging does nothing at all, so the tile just has to notice it.
  • Pops, presses and shakes are a GUI.matrix scale, offset or turn around the tile's centre, the way the pause menu's flicker nudges its panel. Rings, sparks and shards are GL, like the upgrade pick's shards (PickOutro.cs).
  • Reduced effects (Settings) keeps the colour changes and drops shaking, wobbling, flashes and flying sparks. Visual effects off draws the bar exactly as today.
  • Each moment could carry a quiet sound from SoundBank.cs: a soft tick when a cooldown finishes, a dull click when you press too early.
