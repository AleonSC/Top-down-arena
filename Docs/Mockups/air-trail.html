Air Trail

A trail of disturbed air behind anything that moves, scaled by how fast it moves. Slow movers get nothing. Fast ones get a lot. Players, enemies and minions all use the same rule. Each lane runs a real mover at its real speed from the game's data.

Look

Speed to trail

One curve for everything. Trail strength is 0 at or below "Starts at", then rises slowly (squared) so the middle speeds stay light and only the fast movers get the full effect. Dots are the lanes above.

Four looks, three speeds

Each card shows the same three movers: a Grunt (85), a Healer (215) and a Hive Bomber (330). Click a card to put its look on the arena.

Build notes

  • Speed comes from how far the body moved this frame, smoothed over a few frames, so every client works it out for itself. Nothing is sent and the protocol version stays the same.
  • Drawn with the other Visual effects, under the body, and skipped when they are off. Knockback, dashes and Bulldoze count as speed too, so a dash gets the full trail for free.
  • Particle looks (B, C) cap at about 24 live bits per body and stop spawning below the start speed. Looks A and D are drawn from a short position history and cost almost nothing.
  • Bosses are slow, so they get little. The Dual Reaper (150) and the Duelist's lunges are the exceptions. Say if bosses should be left out.
