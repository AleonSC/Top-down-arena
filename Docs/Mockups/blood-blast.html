Blood Blast

Your blood is the blade. Every attack opens a vein and costs a little health, and each swing shapes that blood into a different cut. Build the fire chain out of any moves, in any order, as long as you like. Every enemy you cut gets a blood stack (one per attack), and when it dies each stack flows back into you as health.

Your chain

Plays on a loop against training dummies. Dummies die and respawn; their stacks heal you on death. Health resets each loop. Drag chips to reorder, or use ‹ to move one left.
