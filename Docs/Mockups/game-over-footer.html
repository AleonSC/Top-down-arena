Play again is the wide hero, Lobby is the neutral side button, Leave is a ghost (outlined, no fill) so nobody hits it by reflex. Closest to today, least risky.

C · Rank as the card's footer

The rank belongs to you, so it moves inside your trophy card as a footer row (one per player in co-op, each with their own rank and +XP). That removes the separate strip entirely, so the buttons get a clean centered row with a divider: Leave on its own, then lobby, then the big Play again on the right where the eye ends. Tidiest, but touches the card code too.
