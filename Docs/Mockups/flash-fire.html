How it plays proposed rules

  • Kindle0.5 s: embers stream into you and a dashed ring marks the reach. You can walk at half speed but not attack. Can't be hurt.
  • FlashA wall of flame rolls out to 320 px in 0.3 s. 150 to every enemy it passes (by your damage), bosses too, and a 40 px shove (not heavies or bosses).
  • BurnEverything flashed burns for 10 a second for 5 s (Fireball's burn rules).
  • Slow40% slower for 4 s (heavies and bosses 20%), drawn as ash dragging at their feet.
  • ScorchThe circle stays on fire for 4 s: 20 a second to enemies in it, and walking in sets them burning and slowed too.
  • Mastery380 px reach, the ground burns 6 s.
  • CardRare Backdraft (up to 2): the flash hits 25% harder and burns last 2 s longer a stack.
  • BotCasts it with 5 enemies within 280 px.
