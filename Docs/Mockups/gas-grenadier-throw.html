Gas Grenadier Throw

Today the grenadier has no wind-up: the target ring appears the moment the canister leaves its hand. These three throws add a readable tell, an arm that actually swings, and a canister that flies in an arc. Move the cursor over the arena; the grenadier marks a spot near you and throws.

Approximate: drawn in a browser canvas with the same body, mask and gas colours, not by the game's draw code.

To build the chosen one

  • Lock the spot when the wind-up starts, not at the throw. The ring marks where the canister will land, so you can leave it.
  • GrenadierFire in WardenFaction.cs splits into wind-up, throw and recovery; the gas patch spawns at the throw. The target ring (FxKind.Target) is shown from the wind-up instead of from the throw.
  • DrawGrenadier in WardenLook.cs reads a wind-up state to pose the arm, glow the lenses and show the canister in the air.
  • A new enemy state is synced to guests, so the protocol version goes up.
