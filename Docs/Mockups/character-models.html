Character models

Today every player is a coloured circle with a role mark and a small class overlay. This is one coherent set of 18 top-down models (3 roles x 6 classes) built on the same three rules, plus a socket for every weapon so a reworked weapon model drops straight in. Everything stays inside the real body circle (Tank 18 px, Healer 14, DPS 12) and the bright hit core stays on top, so dodging reads exactly as it does now.

Role = body shape and trim colour. Class = costume and head. Weapon = hands and prop. Move your pointer over the stage to aim; click any model or weapon below to load it.

The weapon socket

  • Origin and axis: a weapon is drawn in its own frame: the main grip at (0, 0), the aim direction along +x, +y to the player's right. The character places and rotates it; the weapon never knows about the body.
  • Envelope: x from -16 to 56, y from -14 to 14 (in Tank-size units; the frame is scaled down for Healer and DPS). Long weapons (Lance, Halberd, Bone Spear) use the full length; sidearms use a fraction.
  • Anchors: grip, optional off (where the second hand lands), and muzzle (shot origin, flashes, charge glow). Shots keep spawning from the body edge as now; the muzzle is only for the flash.
  • Hold poses: one (one hand, other arm free), two (both on the weapon), dual (a mirrored copy in each hand), float (open palm, the weapon hovers above it).
  • States a model can animate: idle, fire (a kick of 3 px and the muzzle flash), and the weapon-action key (Kusarigama reel, Katana sheath, Kunai blink...). The stage's "Auto-fire" shows the fire kick.

Building it

  • Visual only: no network message or snapshot changes, so no ProtocolVersion bump.
  • Each class's KitLook.Body (ClassLooks.cs) already draws over the player circle. The new body would become the shared layers (back, shoulders, role trim, arms, head) plus a per-class costume, with the weapon switch (today inside DrawKnight, DrawNinja, DrawBloodMage) moved into one DrawHeldWeapon keyed by WeaponId so Soldier, Elementalist and Summoner weapons get models too.
  • Downed players keep the grey circle; Vanish, i-frame flicker and hit-flash already scale the whole body's alpha, so they carry over unchanged.
  • The Armory, field guide and lobby previews all go through DrawPlayer, so they'd pick the models up for free. Bestiary/Armory pages stay as they are until you ask.

Open questions

  • Detail level: this is about 20-30 shapes per model. Too busy at 1x? The lineup is the test.
  • Shoulders and pauldrons stick out a little past the body circle (Tank by ~2 px). Keep that, or clamp everything inside?
  • Class colours: each class has its own palette (olive, violet, forest, steel, charcoal, crimson) with the role colour only in the trim. Alternative: tint the whole body by role like today. Say which you prefer.
  • Weapons first: which weapons do you want redrawn from scratch? The bench versions are a first pass to prove the sockets.
