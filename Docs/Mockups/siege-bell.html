Siege Bell

Plant a war bell, ring it three times, and the third toll throws a shockwave that stuns every enemy in the radius.

Look

Proposed numbers

SlotTank Knight ability, beside Leap Slam, Shield Wall and Cavalry Call
Cooldown22 s
Ringing1.3 s rooted while the bell rings, taking 25% less damage
Tolls 1–2120 px ring, 8 damage, cancels enemy wind-ups
Toll 3260 px shockwave, 20 damage, 24 px shove
Stun2.5 s (bosses 0.8 s)
MasteredStun 3.5 s; the shockwave erases enemy bullets it passes
BotsRing it with 4+ enemies within 220 px

Three looks

A Bronze: a gold bell on a timber yoke, warm rings, dust on the third toll.
B Iron: a black-iron tocsin, segmented steel sound bars, a hard white crack.
C Cathedral: a spectral bell lowered from above, rune rings, no frame.
