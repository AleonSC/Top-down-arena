Guardian's Oath rework

Today the Oath is a remote shield: you point at an ally and a beam gives them 100 damage of absorb. Downed allies are skipped. The rework turns it into a rescue. The Knight charges to the player nearest the cursor, living or downed, drags them out of the fire, and plants a Ward of Light that cuts the damage everyone inside takes.

Ward anatomy

Beat by beat

  1. 0.0 s

    Aim. Hold the cursor near any player. The nearest one within 600 px of the cursor gets a gold bracket. Downed counts.

  2. 0.0–0.4 s

    Charge. The Knight dashes in a straight line to the target, leaving afterimages and shoving small bullets aside. No contact damage on the way.

  3. 0.4 s

    Grab. A light tether snaps on with a small shockwave. Downed allies are lifted, living ones are held.

  4. 0.4–1.2 s

    Pull. Both retreat about 360 px toward the arena side with the fewest enemies. The ally can't be hurt while dragged.

  5. 1.2 s

    Plant. The Ward of Light opens, 130 px wide, and lasts 5 s. Allies inside take 50% less damage.

  6. 1.2–6.2 s

    Hold. Hits inside the ward show the reduced number. Downed allies inside bleed out at half speed.

What changes

  • It reaches downed players, which no shield ability does now.
  • It moves the Knight into danger and the ally out of it, so the cost is positional as well as the cooldown.
  • The effect covers an area, so it also protects the Knight and anyone who runs into it.
  • The shield pool is gone, so the Bulwark upgrade becomes ward strength.
  • The Capstone version follows the Knight, is wider, and stands a downed ally back up at 25%.

Open questions

  • Range cap. 600 px keeps it from crossing the whole arena. Should longer reach be the capstone, or should the capstone stay about the ward?
  • Revive. Pulling a downed ally out is already a rescue. Reviving would overlap with Lay on Hands, so it is only in the capstone here.
  • Boss attacks. Should the ward reduce telegraphed boss damage, or only bullets and contact hits? I assumed all damage.
  • Solo. With no ally, the Knight charges to the cursor and plants the ward there. Keep that, or make the ability unusable?
