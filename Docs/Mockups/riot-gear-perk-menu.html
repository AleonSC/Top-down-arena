Riot Gear: perk menu

Two looks for the lobby's Perks tab, with the first Warden perk unlocked and equipped. Riot Gear here is the reworked version: it blocks one hit, then recharges in 30 seconds. The other perks stay locked placeholders, as in the current build. The unlock rules shown come from Docs/BossHuntsAndPerks.md.

Riot Gear
Unlocked by winning the Warden Hunt.
Your shield blocks the next hit that would hurt you. Once it has blocked, it takes 30 seconds to come back. You start every run with it ready.
How it plays
Run starts: ready Hit blocked Ready again
30 s recharge (fixed)
Rules
Blocks one hit from an enemy, a bullet or an explosion.
A block gives 0.5 s of invulnerability, so you can't be chain-hit.
Doesn't block burning, poison or other damage over time.
Off in the Practice Range and the Boss Lab.

Selecting a boss row fills the detail panel. The bar shows when the shield is up and when it isn't.
  • Scales better: the roster is 14 perks across 9 bosses, and a boss row can hold all three of its perks and say how to unlock the next.
  • Has room to explain the rules and to show the recharge as a timeline, which matters because the recharge is now the whole point of Riot Gear.
  • More new UI than A (two panels, a detail view), so it costs more to build.
