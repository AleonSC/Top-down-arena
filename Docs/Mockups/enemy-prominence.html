Visual flair mockup

Enemy prominence

Players wear a wide, soft halo in their role colour (about 5× the body radius). Enemies are flat polygons with no light at all, so in a busy fight they read as the dimmest thing on screen. These looks lift them without a halo on every enemy: most use edges, shadow and a lit centre, and spend real glow only when it carries information.

Click a card to put it in the big arena. All cards draw the same fight, so you're comparing looks, not luck.

My pick: F, then dial it back if it's too much

F stacks the cheap things (a contact shadow, a dark hull with a bright rim, a hot centre) and keeps real glow for two jobs: the half second before an enemy shoots or lunges, and elites. Grunts and swarmers never glow, so a crowd of 20 stays readable and the players are still the brightest things on screen. If F reads too busy, E alone is the lightest version that still gives the "danger is about to happen" cue.

Where it would go

  • Body, rim, shadow and core: WorldEnemies.DrawEnemy (before Polygon(...) and after it).
  • Glow: the additive pass in Juice.DrawGlowPass, next to the boss disc at line 729. It already has GlowDisc, and its first-vertex trick keeps the preview tool clean.
  • Gated by the existing Visual effects setting, so the plain look stays available. No network change, so no protocol bump.
  • Not touched: bosses (they already glow), zombies and the special-cased bodies (DrawGatekeeper, DrawShadow, ZombieLook); they'd get it in a follow-up if you pick a look.
