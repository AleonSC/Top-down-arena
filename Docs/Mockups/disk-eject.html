Main menu, disk drive

Disk eject button

Your disk sits in the drive under the title. An eject button on the drive pops it out and sends it back to its cradle on the "Who's playing?" shelf, so you can pick another profile. Pick a button look and an animation below. The cursor, hover, press and sounds are part of the mock.

Button looks

A Keycapoutside the housing

A small raised key just right of the drive with the eject symbol. Hover lights its edge and shows an EJECT DISK tooltip. The drive itself stays exactly as it is today.

Good for

  • cleanNothing added inside the drive bay
  • hoverReads as a key, like the lobby's Ready key
B Labelled pillunder the readout

A small pill reading EJECT DISK under the status line, shown while a disk is loaded. The most obvious of the three, since it says what it does without a hover.

Good for

  • clearNo guessing what the symbol means
  • costOne more element in a busy spot under the title
C Round buttonbuilt into the drive

The housing grows by one end cap with a round push button, like the eject button on a real drive. The button ring takes your rank colour while a disk is loaded.

Good for

  • feelLooks like part of the machine
  • costThe housing is wider than on the picker, so it changes shape between screens

Animations

A Slide outabout 4.0 s

The existing move played backwards, started by the button. The cells drain, the latch clicks, your disk slides out of the slit flat, tips upright and flies down to its cradle. The menu fades and the shelf returns.

Beats

  • 0.6 sClick. The key sinks, the light goes amber
  • 0.8 sEJECTING. The four cells drain right to left
  • 1.3 sLatch click, slit flash, disk slides out
  • 2.3 sFlies to the shelf, clamps close at 3.4 s
B Spring popabout 3.4 s

A spring eject. The disk kicks out of the slit face-on with a bounce, hangs there while the readout says TAKE DISK, then drops out and flies to its cradle. It has a clear moment of its own that the old move lacks.

Beats

  • 0.6 sClick
  • 1.1 sThunk. The disk kicks out about 40% and bounces
  • 1.5 sHangs, TAKE DISK blinks amber
  • 2.0 sDrops fully out and flies to the shelf

Build notes

  • Art and animation only. No network message or snapshot change, so no Session.ProtocolVersion bump.
  • The button calls the same StartProfTrOut() that "Switch profile" does today, so the move to the picker, its cues (Sfx.DiskEject, DiskClick, DiskFly) and the skip-on-any-key stay. The new part is the first second: button press, the drive draining, and for B the spring.
  • DriveSlot.cs gets an eject-button draw and a hit rect. DriveAt gets an "ejecting" phase before the disk leaves, and ProfileTransition.cs gets a short lead-in clock for it.
  • Reduced effects or Visual effects off: no animation, the picker just opens, as it does now.
  • The button is disabled during a move, in the lobby, and when no profile is loaded (a command-line start).
