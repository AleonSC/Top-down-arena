Emote bar

Hold the emote key and a see-through bar slides up above the ability bar with every emote you've unlocked side by side. Its ends fade away. Scroll the wheel, press ← → or click the arrows on the sides to move along it; click any emote in view to use it. Let go of the key and it closes. In a match there's a short cooldown, shown as a thin bar under the strip.

Opening. Hold the emote key (default V, rebindable in Settings > Keybinds). While the bar is open, a left click picks an emote instead of firing, and the mouse wheel browses instead of switching weapons or zooming. Let go and it closes (a setting could make the key a toggle instead).

The bar. Transparent tiles in a row, 56 px each, the row's two ends fading out under a mask so it never looks cut off. It sits just above the ability bar and doesn't cover your character. Only the emotes you've unlocked are listed, in unlock order, and it remembers where you left it. Hover lights a tile; the first nine show their number so 1-9 work too while it's open.

In a match. The bubble is smaller (80%) and sits above the player's head for 2.4 s, seen by everyone. After using one the strip greys out for the cooldown (4 s here; the Hangout would use a shorter one) and a thin bar runs down under it. Using one never interrupts shooting, moving or abilities; it's drawing only, nothing it does affects the fight. Hidden while you're downed.

Wiring (if picked). CosmeticKind.Emote (all unlocked emotes are available, no equip step), a Msg.Emote (the emote number, from client to host, then relayed to everyone) so a protocol bump, the bar drawn by the HUD next to Hud.cs's ability bar, and the bubble drawn over players in the world renderer and over avatars in LobbyRoom.cs. The host enforces the cooldown.
