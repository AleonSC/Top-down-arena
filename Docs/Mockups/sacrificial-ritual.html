Sacrificial Ritual

Kill yourself on a blood sigil. Your body stays behind and you rise as a blood spirit enemies can't see for 10 s. Each kill stores health, and when time runs out you return to your body with it. No kills: you come back with 1 HP.

How it plays

  1. Sacrifice (1.4 s). A pentagram bleeds under you, five spikes close in and you die. Bullets already in the air miss.
  2. Spirit (10 s). Your body stays on the sigil with a timer ring. Enemies lose you: they stop aiming and wander (a ? over each).
  3. Hunt. The spirit moves faster and passes through enemies and shots. Fire slashes with spirit claws (pick a look on the right). Each kill sends a stream of blood to your body: +10% max health stored.
  4. Return. At 0 s the spirit flies back and you stand up with the stored health (up to 100%), or with 1 HP if you got no kills.
