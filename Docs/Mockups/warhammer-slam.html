Warhammer: a slam you can feel

The Knight's Warhammer today is a stick with a block on it and a flat ring. These three looks keep every number (the 160 px circle 100 px ahead, the 0.9 s charge, the 0.08 s swing, the aftershock 0.2 s later and 40 px further out) and rebuild how it looks and lands: a hammer with weight, a head that heats as you charge, a swing with a trail, and an impact with a hit-stop, a shake, a mark that stays, enemies that reel and stun stars for as long as the stun lasts.

The charge control picks how long you hold: a tap (20 damage, 0.25 s stun) up to full (80 damage, 0.9 s stun). A harder hit makes a bigger flash, deeper cracks and a taller pillar.
