Rules in this mockup

Chill → Freeze

Hits add chill (the pips under an enemy). At 3 it freezes for 2.4 s (bosses would take less).

Shatter

A finisher hitting a frozen enemy shatters the ice: 2.5× damage, a burst of shards, a longer hit-stop.

Chain timing

Each Fire plays the next step. A press during a step is buffered. 1 s without Fire resets to step 1.

Parry cancel

Parry at any moment breaks the current step into shards and keeps your place in the chain. A perfect parry (a shot caught) reflects it as an ice shard, and the next step skips its wind-up and adds +1 chill.
