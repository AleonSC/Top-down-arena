Smoke Bomb Rework

Tank Ninja, Space ability. Today it is a flat grey disc. The proposal gives it a thrown pellet, a crack and flash, a cloud with real volume, a lantern seal that shows the time left, and a clear read of which bullets are slowed.

Proposed

Beat by beat

  1. 0.00 sPellet drops from the ninja's hand with a lit fuse and shrinks as it lands.
  2. 0.16 sCrack: a white-violet flash, 14 amber sparks and a shockwave ring out to the cloud edge.
  3. 0.16 to 0.8 sThe cloud blooms outward in layered puffs, each with a dark body and a lit upper rim. The ninja sits half veiled.
  4. to 4 sThe cloud churns slowly, outer puffs turning faster than inner ones, with thin wisps circling. Bullets crossing it stretch short and cool to violet.
  5. last 1.2 sThe seal ring blinks, the edge frays and the puffs lift and thin out.

What it fixes

  • The cloud now has depth, so it reads as smoke and not as a debug circle.
  • An amber seal ring drains with the time left, like the Spirit Ward, so allies know when cover ends.
  • Slowed bullets visibly change: short trails and a violet tint inside, so the half speed is readable at a glance.
  • The cast has a cause and an effect (pellet, crack) instead of a ring appearing from nothing.
  • The fade-out warns before it ends, where today it just vanishes.

Dev notes. Visual only, so no protocol change. The cloud replaces the ZoneKind.Smoke case in WorldEnemies.cs; the pellet and crack ride on the existing FxKind.Ring broadcast plus a small particle burst on the cast. The puff layout is seeded from the zone id so every peer draws the same cloud. Gameplay numbers (140 px, 5 s, half speed, 30% less damage) are unchanged. Capstone and Thick Smoke toggles above show the larger cloud and the slowed enemy.
