Upgrade Card Layout

The level-up cards redrawn like the milestone perk cards: rarity chip centered on top, the icon in a cutout tile, then name and text. Cards with a stack cap get circles that fill with how many you hold. Each card glows in its rarity colour and bobs slowly. The "THIS PICK" line moves off the bottom of the screen and onto the picked card. Same offer as your screenshot (level 2, Soldier DPS).

A. Picked card has a cap (Thick Skin)

  • Reinforced Guard (cap 4, you hold 2) and Thick Skin (cap 3, you hold 1) show circles: filled for what you hold, a ringed circle for the one this pick would add.
  • The change line takes the circles' place under them, only on the picked card: "Damage taken 100% → 90%".
  • Focus has no cap, so it shows no circles.

B. Picked card has no cap (Focus)

  • With no circles to hold the line, "THIS PICK" is an overlay strip at the bottom of the picked card.
  • Unpicked capped cards keep their circles but not the change line. The old line under the cards is gone, so the buttons move up.

C. Glow by rarity

  • Common whitish, Uncommon green, Rare blue, Very Rare purple, Legendary orange (the game's existing rarity colours). The glow is soft on idle cards and about twice as strong on the picked one.
  • Bob: 5 px up and down over 3.4 s, each card offset so they don't move together. Skipped when reduced motion is on. The picked card also lifts 12 px.

D. Buttons under the cards

  • The hint line under the buttons is gone. Both labels are uppercase with a little letter spacing; "Pick a card" becomes CHOOSE UPGRADE.
  • Reroll needs two clicks. The first click arms it: the button turns amber, reads CLICK AGAIN TO REROLL and a bar along the bottom drains over 3 seconds. A second click inside that time rerolls. If the bar runs out, or you pick a card, it goes back to normal.
  • Three looks for the idle Reroll label. Each row's buttons below are live: click Reroll to try the confirm.
  • The R key would follow the same rule: first press arms, second press rerolls. When you have no rerolls left the button is greyed and can't be armed.
