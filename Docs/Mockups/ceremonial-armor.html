The three layouts

  • Halo: one even ring that spins steadily. Easiest to read; gaps open as plates break.
  • Mandala: the first 4 plates spin fast close in, the next 6 counter-spin further out. Two layers are hard to slip through; the showiest.
  • Bulwark: plates fan out in an arc toward the nearest incoming shot and close into a full ring at 10. Blocks the most; feels the most like a tank.

Each arena runs the same fire pattern, so the Blocked and Hits taken counters compare the layouts directly.
