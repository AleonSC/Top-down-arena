WARDEN · NET & CELL

Mockup: replaces the shackle ring in the Count phase's searchlight turn.

P1's screen

Bot plays by itself. Take over: WASD move · Space dash · Q E skills. Each skill in the cell takes 1 s off.

P2's screen

The rule

  • Trigger: stunned or ~1.8 s in the searchlight (same as the shackle ring today).
  • Net: the Warden fires it (0.45 s flight), it wraps you, screen fades to black.
  • Cell: one-cell room, a gap in each wall; Guards pile in from all four sides, faster over time.
  • Hold out 30 s; every skill (dash, Q, E) takes 1 s off.
  • Co-op: only the caught player leaves; a locked cell with the countdown marks their spot.
  • Shoot the lock: the teammate shoots that cell's padlock; each hit takes 0.25 s off.
  • Both caught: the second player's cell is next to the first's, both in view; the fight pauses.
  • First out resumes the fight and can shoot the other's lock.
  • Solo: the fight pauses until you're out.

Open questions

  • Downed in the cell: out early and downed, or kept at 1 HP (as here)?
  • Lock value: 0.25 s a hit (as here), or a 6-hit lock that frees outright like the gate lock?
