Vanish

For 3 seconds enemies lose track of you and shoot at the spot where you vanished. Your next hit deals double. Today the ninja just turns see-through with a purple ring. These three looks give the cast, the invisible body, the decoy spot and the ambush hit their own treatment. Mechanics are unchanged.
