Hangout emotes

Short gestures your character plays in the Hangout (and, if you want, in a match): a bubble and a little movement over your head for a couple of seconds, seen by everyone. Twelve ideas, unlocked by rank. Click one on the bar (or press its number) to play it on your character; the other two characters play a random one now and then. Drag the rank to see what's locked.

Others emote

Playing one. Hold V for a wheel of your eight equipped emotes (flick toward one), or tap 1-8 in the Hangout. One at a time per player, 2.4 s long, and a 1.5 s pause before the next, so nobody can spam. Moving cancels it.

Look. Each is a bubble above the head plus a small effect (confetti, hearts, a spin ring). No new art: the same fills and discs the frames and card backgrounds use, driven from the clock. With Visual effects off the bubble shows still.

Wiring (if picked). A CosmeticKind.Emote (the wheel slots are an equipped list, not one slot), a Msg.Emote with the emote number and the lobby avatar id, drawn in LobbyRoom.cs over the avatar (protocol bump). In a match it would be a small bubble over the player with a longer pause, off by default.
