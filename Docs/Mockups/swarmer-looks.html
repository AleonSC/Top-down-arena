Swarmer Looks

The current Swarmer becomes the Spiker. The new Swarmer is the simplest melee enemy: no spikes, no tricks, it walks straight at the nearest player and bites. It comes in big packs and each one is weak. Four looks below, each with a pack of seven chasing a target. Move your pointer over an arena to steer the target.

Notes

Numbers are placeholders: the new Swarmer is weaker than the Spiker on purpose (14 health against 22, 6 contact against 10), with speed just under the Spiker's but a straight path, so it arrives sooner. All four have the same stats, so only the look differs. Colours avoid the existing orange, blue, red, pink and brown enemies.
