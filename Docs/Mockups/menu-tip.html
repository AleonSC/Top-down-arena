The line under the title

Five ways to show the changing line under CARTESIAN COLOSSEUM, against today's plain text. The lines are the game's own (your best run and the How to play tips); they change every few seconds here so you can see the switch. The second strip tries shorter wording, which suits a splash better than full sentences.

Notes for building it

  • All in Menus.cs (MenuTip picks the line, DrawMenuTitle draws it). Pills, chips and the leader line are the UI kit's pills and the menu's GL lines; the splash's tilt is a GUI.matrix turn.
  • Each line gets a kind (Tip or Record) so the tag and icon can follow it; the short wording would be a second column in the tips table, kept next to the long one How to play uses.
  • Reduced effects: no bounce, wobble or typing; lines just fade.
