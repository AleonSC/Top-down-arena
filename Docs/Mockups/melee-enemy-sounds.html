Melee Rusher Sounds

Mockup sounds for the enemies that never shoot and run at you: Swarmer, Spiker, Brute, Lunger, Buzzsaw and Spike Block. Each sound is built in the page with the same primitives as SoundBank.cs, so the recipe under every card can be pasted into Set(Sfx.<Name>, ...).

Press Play encounter on an enemy to hear its whole routine in order, or play single sounds. Headphones help: the telegraph cues (Lunger, Spike Block) must read clearly under gunfire.

Open questions

  • Do packs of four Swarmers each chomp, or should bites share one voice with a min gap so a pack doesn't turn to noise? The mockup uses a 0.08 s gap.
  • Buzzsaw touches land about eight times a second. A tick per touch may be too busy; an alternative is one looping whirr while it is in contact.
  • Should the Lunger and Spike Block telegraphs be heard from across the arena, or only when near a player?
  • Deaths: one shared splat pitched per enemy size, or the distinct deaths here?
