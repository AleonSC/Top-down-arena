Soldier DPS Gun Animations

Twin Rifle, Railgun and Burst SMG as they could look in play: recoil, hand work, casings, charge-up and spin-up. The glowing shots copy the in-game ones (orange tracers, the Railgun's slug with rings of air and its ion trail). Everything else is proposed.

Move over an arena to aim. With auto-fire off, hold the pointer down to shoot.

Twin Rifle 0.1 s · 4 per barrel · barrels 60 px apart

    • Two guns, held wide: each rifle is angled in so both streams cross at your cursor's distance (never closer than 120 px).
    • Crossfire: the dashed ring is the 45 px crossfire zone. Hits inside it do double and show gold. Aim at the X.
    • Hand work: both guns kick together, the bolts flick back and brass drops out the outer side. Barrels warm up the longer you hold fire.
    • Upgrade: a third rifle fills in the middle of the same 60 px.

    Railgun 0.7 s · 45 dmg · pierces 3

      • Charge: three coils light up one after another over the 0.7 s between shots, and a ring closes in on the muzzle when it is ready.
      • Discharge: a white flash, arcs jumping between the rails, a hard 0.2 s kick that slides the rails back, and steam from the coils.
      • Ion trail: the path glows for 1 s and ticks 25 a second on anything standing in it. The slug is the in-game one.
      • Upgrade: 2 shrapnel shards fly sideways from every enemy it pierces.

      Burst SMG 0.055 s · 5 dmg · spin-up 2 s

        • Spin-up: the three barrel tubes turn faster and the metal heats from steel to white. A ring fills round you, white-hot when full.
        • Spray: the faint cone is the real spread. It tightens from 9° to 2° and damage climbs from 5 to 8 as it spins up.
        • Wind-down: let go and everything cools over 0.6 s. Auto-fire holds for 4 s, then rests so you can see it.
        • Upgrade: +1 bullet per shot at half damage, with a spray 35% wider.

        Mockup only. Timings use the real fire intervals, ranges and damage from Weapons.cs. Nothing in the game has been changed.
