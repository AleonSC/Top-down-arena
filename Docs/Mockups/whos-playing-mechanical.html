Floppy shelf: handwriting and machinery

Your pick (Shelf 4, the display shelf) again, with the shelf rebuilt as a machine in three ways, plus a font picker for the names on the labels. The first stage is the shelf as it is in the game now, for comparison.

Name font

Pick a font and every stage updates. Hover a disk, click one to play as it, press Manage profiles for Rename and Delete.

NowDisplay shelf

Merged in the game today.

Mechanical 1Riveted rail

A heavy steel shelf: riveted face, a hazard-stripe lip, gusset brackets with bolts, and a bolted bezel round the wall. A lamp by each slot plate lights for the profile you used last.

  • The bezel and bolts are the same ones the game already draws round the runs screen (KeyUi.Bezel), so this reuses its look.
  • The hazard stripe is the loudest part. It could be a plain dark lip if it is too much.

Mechanical 2Clamp cradles

Each disk sits in its own cradle: a pedestal under it and a clamp post either side with a lamp. Hover a disk and the clamps open and release it as it lifts.

  • The most moving parts. The clamps open and close, so the click-to-pick has a physical beat.
  • The shelf face is plain, with vent slots at the ends.

Mechanical 3Console deck

The shelf is a control deck. Each slot has a dark well like the Play keycap's, holding a small green CRT readout (LOADED, READY, NO DISK) with a lamp. Vent grilles and corner screws finish it.

  • The green readout matches the main menu's CRT screens, so the picker and the menu feel like one machine.
  • It adds state text per slot (LOADED for the profile in use).
