Animation mockup for the rework of Unbreakable. Two lancers ride in beside you and charge across the arena along your aim, shoving enemies out of the lane, slowing and hurting them. Two looks on the same scene. Drag the aim slider to see which enemies the lane catches.

Cavalry Call proposed 18 s cooldown · 2 lancers · 900 px charge · 35 damage · 150 px shove · 45% slow for 3 s

Nine enemies stand in the arena, three of them Heavy (purple, with a health bar). Heavy enemies take the damage but only a short shove and a lighter slow. The aim line shows the lane while the knights are called in.

    What it changes

    • Unbreakable was a defensive timer. Cavalry Call is an engage tool: it clears a lane, so a tank can open a path to a squishy ally or break a pack up.
    • You stay put and keep fighting. The knights do the moving, so the cast has no animation lock and no risk.
    • The lane is readable before anything happens (aim line, two sigils), so a cast is placed, not guessed.
    • Heavy enemies and bosses take the hit but barely move, so it cannot be used to skip a boss.
