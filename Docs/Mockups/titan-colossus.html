Titan: Colossus

Today's Titan is a 32 px stone minion that slams in a plain ring. This version calls up a colossus more than four times as wide. Rubble flies in from the ground and stacks into a titan, it stomps after the nearest enemy, winds up before every slam, and its body walls off every bullet. When the time runs out it falls apart into a rubble pile.

Beat by beat

  1. 0.0 sCast. A rune circle burns into the ground 60 px ahead of you. Twelve chunks of stone lift off the arena floor around its rim. (Today: a small hexagon pops in with a ring.)
  2. 0.0 – 1.4 sThe chunks spiral in and stack into legs, torso, shoulders and a head. Dust rolls out. Bullets that cross the circle early still hit the ground, not the titan.
  3. 1.4 sStands up. A thud, a short screen shake, and a dust ring knocks small enemies back. It now blocks bullets with its whole body.
  4. 1.8 s onChases the nearest enemy at a walking pace. Both fists rise in a 0.4 s wind-up before each slam, so players can see the hit coming and keep enemies where it will land.
  5. every 1.4 sSlam: a shockwave ring, cracks that stay on the floor for a few seconds, flying debris, and 90 damage to everything in reach with a short shove outward.
  6. last 1.2 sEyes flicker, the body shakes, and the titan falls apart. The chunks burst outward, settle as a rubble pile, and fade after 3 s.

Pick a look (A, B or C). Say if the wind-up before each slam is a fair trade for the bigger hit, and if the colossus is too big for the arena.
