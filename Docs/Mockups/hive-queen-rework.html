The Hive Queen, reworked

Three directions for her body, each shown live and through every phase (Swarm, Honeycomb, Frenzy, Last Stand) plus airborne in a royal dive. Same palette and body plan as her Hive bees, so she reads as their queen.

Today (Tools/Preview render)

A · Monarch

regal · long body · four wings

A big queen bee: long tapered abdomen with a stinger, four wax wings, a gold tiara and a glowing wax gem on her back. Phases change her armour: wax scales grow over her abdomen in the Honeycomb, she burns red with torn wings in the Frenzy, and loses a wing and leaks honey in the Last Stand.

B · Brood Mother

grotesque · egg sac · stubby wings

A small head and thorax dragging a huge, see-through egg sac that pulses with eggs and larvae. It ties straight into her Eggs turn and drones. The sac is wrapped in wax in the Honeycomb, glows red and boils in the Frenzy, and hangs half-empty and leaking in the Last Stand.

C · Comb Throne

armoured · orbiting comb · readable health

A slimmer queen inside a ring of six comb cells she carries like armour. The ring tells the fight's progress at a glance: brood cells with drones in the Swarm, sealed wax walls in the Honeycomb, three cracked red cells in the Frenzy, and none left in the Last Stand.
