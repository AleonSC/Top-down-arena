Shadow Clone Medics

A hand seal, three puffs of smoke, and every player gets a shadow clone medic of the Healer for 10 s. Each clone sticks to its player, heals them and steps into the shots aimed at them. Shown with a party of three: you, a Tank and a DPS.

Seal

The Healer flashes a cross-shaped hand seal. A puff of ink smoke bursts beside each player and a clone steps out of it, kneeling.

Intercept

When a shot is heading for its player, the clone dashes onto its line and takes it. A hit leaves a dark ripple; the clone fades as its health drops.

9.0 – 10.0 s

Fade

Clones flicker, then vanish in smoke with a falling leaf. Each gives its player a last heal of 20% of what it soaked.

Rules

  • Blocks every enemy shot aimed at its player, red aimed shots included; shots at other players pass through it.
  • Broken at 0 health: it pops in smoke and its player keeps the fade heal.
  • Melee enemies ignore clones: they guard against shots only.
  • Solo: one clone on you, with 50% more health.
  • Ends at 10 s, when its player leaves the run, or between waves.
  • Your own shots and allies' pass through clones.
