Bullet Rain

Today the DPS Soldier's ultimate is Bullet Storm (F, 60 s cooldown): 6 s of triple fire rate and +2 projectiles, with no visual of its own. These three looks give it one. Look A keeps the mechanics exactly and only adds flair. Looks B and C turn it into a literal bullet rain that falls on an area, which is a design change and needs its own balance pass. Every scene loops: activate, six seconds of fire, wind-down.

All previews No look picked yet
LOOK A

Storm Surge

What you see

The Twin Rifle becomes a firehose. Three tracers per shot, the two extra projectiles drawn hotter, brass flying, a heat aura and an orange edge glow while it lasts.

Gameplay

Unchanged: 6 s, ×3 fire rate, +2 projectiles. Cheapest to build, works with every weapon.

LOOK B

Rain Barrage

What you see

A red zone locks on at your cursor, you fire straight up, and bullets come down inside it. Each lands with a ripple, sparks and a scorch mark. Every falling round shows a growing dot first, so allies can read it.

Gameplay

Area denial. The zone follows your cursor within 40 px a second. Close to an airstrike, so your own fire rate stops mattering.

LOOK C

Skyfall Curtain

What you see

Volleys leave the barrel straight up, then a slanted curtain of cold white-blue bullets sweeps across the arena in a 140 px band. Chevrons mark the leading edge. It reads like real rain.

Gameplay

A sweeping wall. Enemies caught in the band take the hits, so you steer it by positioning. The band is a clear shape to dodge for friends standing in it.
