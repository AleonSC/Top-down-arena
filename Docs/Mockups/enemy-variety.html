Enemy proposal · waves 1–49

More variety between the bosses

Today a regular wave is a random pick, one enemy at a time, from everything unlocked so far. Nothing new joins after wave 31, melee is gone by wave 13, and elites are only bigger (2.5× health, 1.25× size, 35% faster fire). This proposal adds three things: four new enemies for the second half of the run, elite traits that give each elite one twist, and squads, so a wave is made of small teams that work together instead of a random crowd. Pick a tab in the arena to watch each one against a test dummy (you, in green) that shoots back.

Four new enemies

one every few waves from 18 to 36

Each fills a gap in the roster: nothing regular fires beams, nothing leaves the floor dangerous, there's no melee after wave 12, and the Medic is the only enemy that helps the others.

Elite traits

one trait from wave 12, two from wave 35

Elites keep today's 2.5× health and bigger body, and each also rolls one trait from the table, shown as an icon over it and a coloured rim. The traits change how you fight that one enemy, not just how long it takes.

Squads

about 70% of a wave, the rest single enemies as now

A wave is built from a budget (today's enemy count). Squads that are unlocked by that wave are drawn at random and paid for until about 70% is spent, then the rest is filled with single enemies as now. A squad arrives together from one edge, keeps its formation for a few seconds, and its members look after each other: a Shielder stands between you and its Snipers, a Medic follows its Brutes. Melee squads keep rushers in every wave of the run.

What the wave on the slider is made of

What it keeps

  • All 17 regular enemies, their numbers and their unlock waves.
  • Today's wave sizes (4 + 2 × wave, up to 32, then up to 40 after wave 20), the extra quarter for a third player, and the paths' own wave mixes.
  • Elite chance (4% a wave from wave 12, up to 35%), health and size. The flat 35% faster fire moves into the Hasted trait.

To build it

  • New EnemyTypes BeamSentry, Scorcher, Reaver, Warder after BallLightning, with Defs, AI and look. The sentry's beam is a line hazard like the Lich's sweep; the Scorcher's trail reuses PatchKind.Fire; the Reaver's swipe is a cone like the Halberd's; the Warder's bubbles reuse the Shielder's ShieldHp.
  • Enemy.Traits (a byte of flags, synced) rolled in SpawnEnemyAt; hooks in HurtEnemy (Shielded, Mirrored), OnEnemyKilled (Volatile, Splitting) and the fire timer (Hasted, Commander).
  • A Squads table (members, cost, first wave, formation) and BuildWave spending a budget on it; spawn groups get a shared edge and a SquadId for the escort rules.
  • Bots: the beam's line, fire patches, the Reaver's cone, Volatile rings. Bestiary entries, Practice Range picker, a variety sim test.
