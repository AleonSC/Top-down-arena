Mockup · Soldier · DPS abilities

Dash, Grenade & Mark

Three looks for each of the Soldier DPS's three abilities, as looping scenes at game scale. The blue circle is you, red diamonds are enemies, red dots are enemy bullets. Pick one look per ability (or mix: they are independent). Dash is only 0.18 s long in the real game, so use Slow motion to study it.

Reduced motion is on: each scene shows one still frame. Press Pause to start them.
Ability 1 · 3.5 s cooldown

Dash

A 216 px burst (1200 px/s for 0.18 s) in your move direction, invulnerable for 0.30 s (the dash and 0.12 s after). Capstone: +1 charge, so nothing new to draw. The scene shows three enemy bullets that would have hit; they pass through you during the invulnerable window.

Ability 2 · 6 s cooldown

Cluster Grenade

A lobbed grenade lands at your cursor after 0.55 s (up to 380 px), deals 80 in a 110 px radius, and bursts into 8 shrapnel. The circle is the real blast radius. Tick Mastered to see the second grenade 80 px further.

Ability 3 · 12 s cooldown

Mark for Death

Marks the enemy nearest your cursor for 6 s: it takes 50% more damage (a 12 hit becomes 18). The scene casts at 0.8 s, shoots every 0.7 s and lets the mark expire at 6.8 s. Tick Mastered to mark the three nearest.

Shared across all looks

  • Colours: Dash is cool blue-white (it's you), the Grenade is orange-yellow like fire, Mark is red like the existing Mark ring (1, 0.25, 0.25). No two abilities share a colour.
  • Drawing only: none of these change gameplay or the network. Dash and the Mark ring already broadcast FxKind.Ring; the Grenade already carries Splash and Burst on the bullet. The Mark timer reads Enemy.MarkT, already synced.
  • Where it would go: a new Game/SoldierLooks.cs, hooked from AbilityLooks.cs (Dash and Mark) and the shot drawing in ShotLooks.cs (Grenade, detonation).
  • If it needs a new effect kind (e.g. the afterimages), that is a new FxKind at the end of the enum and a Session.ProtocolVersion bump. Most looks can be built from local state and avoid that.
  • The Armory and Bestiary pages are not touched unless you ask.
