Boss proposal · wave 35

The Lich, in three stages

Today it's immune while its phylactery stands (a new one rises 15 s after each breaks), raises three skeletons every 9 s, and swaps between an aimed fan of 5 piercing shots and slow rings of 16 orbs, for the whole fight. This mockup splits it like the Warden, the Queen, the Singularity and the Colossus: three stages by health, one attack at a time with breathers between, a 2 s stage change that clears the screen, and a last stand below 10%. Its phylacteries get bigger roles, then break for good, and the Lich sheds its body. The arena plays each stage with a test dummy that shoots whatever can be hurt; pick a stage to watch it, or let it cycle.

What it keeps

  • The phylactery (600 health): the Lich can't be hurt while one stands, and a soul tether from it to the Lich shows that. Stage 1 plays it as now, with a 12 s window after you break it.
  • Raising the dead: skeletons climb out of graves and walk at you. They're today's grunts.
  • The soul fan (5 aimed piercing shots, about 9° apart, every 0.8 s) and the slow grave rings (16 big orbs at 90 px/s, turning a little each ring).
  • Its colour, size (36 px), seven sides and slow drift.

What's new

  • Graves: six headstones in fixed places. The dead rise from them, and in stage 3 the Lich blinks between them.
  • Three phylacteries: in stage 2 they stand in a triangle joined by soul beams that hurt to cross. Breaking one cuts its beams; break all three to open a window.
  • Soul sweep: a long draining beam that swings toward you. A line attack the game doesn't have yet.
  • Bone prison: spikes rise in a ring round you with one gap, then fire inward. Get out through the gap.
  • Wailing spirits and dead hands: ghosts that dive at where you were, and hands that burst up under you, one after another.
  • Undeath: its body crumbles, and the spectre blinks from grave to grave, bursting rings of orbs each time it appears.

To build it

  • Stage, turn and timers in a LichState on the boss, synced like WardenState; thresholds 65 / 30 / 10%.
  • Reuse: Phylactery enemies and the immunity check for stages 1 and 2; Hazard warning circles for dead hands and grave blinks; the Reapers' lasers for the soul sweep and the phylactery beams; the Bone Guard's cage look for the prison.
  • New: graves as fixed props, the bone-prison ring of spikes, spirits that circle out then dive, and the blink between graves.
  • Health: BossHpMult for the Lich (3.2× now) should come down and be retuned in the Boss Lab for 60–120 s, plus Bestiary entries, a Boss Lab stage lock and sim tests per stage.
