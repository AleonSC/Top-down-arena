Arc Lightning

Hold to charge a strip of ground in front of you (90 px wide, 160 px long for a tap, out to 800 px, half the arena, at full charge after 0.9 s). Let go and everything in the strip takes 40 and is stunned for 0.4 s. Today that's a ring filling round you, a flat outlined strip, and three jagged lines. Below are four other ways it could look, each covering the whole sequence: charge, release, aftermath. Drag the charge length to see how each look grows, and turn on Barrage for the extra strips.

Notes for building it

  • Charge: DrawArcCharge (WorldEffects.cs). Release: FxKind.ArcField (DrawArcFieldFx) plus a FxKind.Bolt to each enemy hit. The effect already carries the strip's start, end, half width and colour, so there is no protocol change. Everyone sees the same crackle because it's seeded by where it went off.
  • Only your own charge shows the strip preview today (mine); other players get the ring. Every look below keeps that: allies see the build-up on the caster but not the aim strip.
  • The enemy stun (0.4 s) is shown as a small crackle that stays on each enemy for the stun, in the looks that have one (a per-enemy timer would be needed on remote screens; the host already tracks FrozenT).
  • Colours: the game's BoltShot pale blue, with white cores and a gold "full" cue as today. Chain Light's warm light and the Storm Hammer's arcs are not touched.
  • Glows go in the additive glow pass like the other upgraded looks. With Visual effects off it draws exactly as now (render check IDENTICAL).
  • The preview is drawn at 0.55 of the game's scale; the strip, its width and the timings (0.15 s minimum draw, 0.9 s full, 0.35 s effect) are the real numbers.
