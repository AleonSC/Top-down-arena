Hangout upgrade

Lobby to Hangout

The Loadout tab's preview window (the square with your ship in it) becomes the Hangout room. Three ways to do it, each about 0.9 s like the Play button's scan wipe. Each one plays forward, holds, then runs backward the way the return to Loadout would. The room is the 790 x 565 one you have now, with the layout B extras: minimap top left, the PvP toggle under it.

Details in all three

  • The loadout panel fades out in the first 40%. Everything else on the left (party, Ready, Start) stays put, and the Hangout button swaps to "< Loadout" halfway.
  • Your ship scales to the room's 1.5x size as it moves from the window to its spot; the room extras (name plates, minimap, PvP toggle, glowing border) arrive in the last 40%.
  • No clicks during the animation. With reduced effects on it snaps, like the Play wipe.
