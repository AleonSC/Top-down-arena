Summoner DPS: Bat Swarm

Animation mockup. Visual rework only. Compare today's plain ring of bats with three proposed looks on the same scene: you stand on the left, four enemies walk in from the right, and 8 bats hunt them for 6 s.

Bat Swarm 12 s cooldown · 8 bats (14 with capstone) · 6 s · hunt enemies

Today: 8 bats appear in a ring around you and fly at the nearest enemy. Proposed: the cast has a wind-up, the bats read as one swarm, every bite lands with feedback, and the remaining time is visible.

Beats

    What it fixes

    • The cast has a wind-up and a burst, so the player sees the ability fire.
    • Bats leave motion trails and flap, so a swarm reads as one living thing instead of 8 loose dots.
    • Every bite has an impact: a red flash, sparks and a floating hit number.
    • Time left is shown on a rim around you, and bats fade out as they expire instead of vanishing.
    • Enemies being hunted are marked, so you can see what the swarm is after.

    Build notes. Drawing only, so no protocol change. Bat damage (6 here), bite rate and bat speed are stand-ins for the real Minion stats. The capstone's 14 bats are not shown. The "Current" view is approximate.
