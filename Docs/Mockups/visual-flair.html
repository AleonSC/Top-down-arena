Notes for building it

  • All of it lives in the front end (WorldRenderer.cs and a new Juice.cs): particles, shake, hit-pause and numbers are local to each screen, so there's no protocol bump and the host's simulation never waits on them.
  • Hit-pause freezes only what you see (the drawing and your screen's interpolation), never the host's simulation, so co-op stays in sync. The sim keeps running; the picture catches up over the next frames.
  • A Visual effects section in Settings: screen shake (off / low / full), damage numbers on/off, and one "reduced effects" switch that turns off shake, hit-pause and flashes for anyone sensitive to them.
  • The render check (Tools/RenderCheck) would prove that with every effect off, the arena draws exactly as today.
