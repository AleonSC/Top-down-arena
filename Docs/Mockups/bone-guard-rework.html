Tank · Summoner · weapon rework

Bone Guard

Now: darts (7) every 0.3 s; the 3rd in one enemy within 3 s cages it 1.5 s; 2 orbiting skeletons block bullets and shoot caged enemies. No Alt. Three directions below, each with an Alt. Click a canvas to pause it.

A · RIB LOCK

Keep the loop, make it read, detonate it

Same dart → cage loop, made legible: three pips over the enemy, a rib cage that claws up out of the floor, skeletons that fire bone shards. Alt cashes in every dart you've stuck.

Fire
Unchanged numbers 7 dmg · 0.3 s · cage 1.5 s. Pips fill 1/2/3; the stuck darts fuse into the cage.
Alt
Shatter 6 s: every dart you have stuck explodes in bone shards 10 each; every cage bursts 30 in 60 px. Rewards stacking many enemies before cashing in.
Skill
Spread darts over a crowd, then Shatter. Low risk, closest to today.
B · MARROW TETHER

Darts stitch the crowd together

Enemies you hit within 1 s of each other get threaded with marrow. Cage one and the cage reels its threaded neighbours in and slows them: a Tank tool for bunching a wave. Alt drops a bone palisade.

Fire
Darts as now. Consecutive hits on different enemies within 1 s link them. A cage yanks linked enemies 40 px toward it and slows them 30% while it holds.
Alt
Ossuary Wall 8 s: lob a bone to the cursor; a 110 px palisade of femurs rises across your aim for 3 s, blocking enemy shots. Enemies pressing on it take a dart every 0.6 s (so it cages them).
Skill
Group, then wall off. Most crowd control; overlaps a little with Golem Wall.
C · GRAVE SENTINELS

Skeletons with shields and spears

The skeletons become the weapon's teeth: they carry bone shields while they circle you, then leap to a cage and spear it. While they're away you're open. Alt trades places with one.

Fire
Darts as now. On a cage, both sentinels leap to it and jab 3 × 12 each, then run back. Shields block bullets only while orbiting.
Alt
Bone Swap 7 s: a sentinel dashes to the cursor (260 px max) and you swap places. Bone spikes erupt where you stood: 20 + one dart to each enemy within 48 px, shoved out 22 px.
Skill
A Tank escape that punishes the crowd on you. Highest skill ceiling, biggest change.
Notes
  • Alts mix freely: any Alt works with any Fire (e.g. A's look + C's Bone Swap).
  • Numbers are starting points for the balance pass; Ossuary would also add to the Alt (e.g. Shatter shards +20%).
