Blood Ritual

You bleed a pentagram onto the floor at your cursor. It tears open into a portal and a flood of blood imps pours out for 8 s, swarming enemies, clawing them and pulling their fire off you. When it shuts, the imps that lived dive home and give your blood back.

The beats one cast, 11 s

  1. 0.0 s
    InscribeA rope of blood runs from your hand to the cursor and draws the circle, then the five strokes of the star, then runes between the rings. You can walk and shoot.
  2. 1.2 s
    OpenThe sigil flares and the floor inside it caves into a red pit. Enemies on it are slowed 40%.
  3. 1.6 s
    Flood8 imps burst out at once, then 4 a second for 8 s. Each climbs out, flaps up and dives at the nearest enemy that has the fewest imps on it.
  4. 9.6 s
    CloseThe portal shuts. Every imp still alive dives back into it, and the sigil sends their blood to you as a stream.

Proposed rules numbers to tune

  • SlotTank Hemomancer ultimate, key 3 (after Bloodbath and Blood Golem), 80 s cooldown.
  • Cost15% of your max health, paid over the 1.2 s inscribe. Can't be cast under the blood floor (20%).
  • SigilAt the cursor, up to 320 px away; 90 px radius; slows enemies on it 40% while open.
  • Imps40 over 8 s, at most 24 alive. 30 health, 260 px/s. Claw 6 every 0.4 s, each claw adds a bleed stack.
  • Tank partA clawed enemy is taunted onto that imp for 1 s, so shots go at the swarm instead of you and your team.
  • Imp deathPops in a splash: 10 to enemies within 40 px.
  • PaybackEach imp alive at the close heals you 1% of your max health (up to 24%, more than the cost if the swarm survives).

Approximate: the glow and soft trails are browser effects; in Unity they'd be layered strokes and additive sprites.
