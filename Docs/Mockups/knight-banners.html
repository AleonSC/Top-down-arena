Knight Banners

One planted standard per role, as an ultimate. It stands for 60 s, or until you press the ultimate again; the cooldown starts when it comes down. (The demo lifts it early.) Allies standing in its ring get the buff; step out and it fades. Pick a banner to watch it in play.
