CRT HUD MODES

Themed versions of the two partial HUD modes on H: Corner text and On the player. Both keep only what you act on mid-fight (health, ability cooldowns, enemies left) and now speak the console's language: VT323 phosphor text, segmented cells, inverse-video keycaps, bracket corners and scanlines. Every number carries the key it belongs to. Drawn over a real preview render (wave 6, two players) with the HUD hidden.

Moment
CRT colour

1 · Corner text

  • System font, no glow: it reads as a debug overlay sitting on top of a CRT game.
  • Health is yellow-green from the generic health ramp, unrelated to the CRT colour.
  • "SPACE 11 · F 25": the ult shows 25 (seconds left), while the full HUD shows the same ult as 66%.
  • Wave and enemies left sit far apart from everything else, in a third style.

2 · On the player

  • "127" floats above you with no label, in the spot where your name was.
  • "11 25" under you are bare numbers: nothing says which key they belong to, and 25 is the ult in seconds rather than its charge.
  • The arc hugs the body and blends into the Aeroaegis shield and your own bullets.
  • "23 LEFT" at the top has no wave beside it, so it's unclear what it counts.
