The Larder

Eight enemies for the waves before the Devourer, one per Warden enemy so the lessons carry over. Brood are its spawn: mouths with no eyes, the Devourer in miniature. The Larder are the butchers who keep it fed. Each one teaches a turn of the fight before you meet it.

The Feast

last wave

Mirrors the Riot Line. Four Carcass Bearers as a wall, two Gulpers behind it, two Bile Spitters lobbing over. Within ~190 px of the wall, or with half the slabs broken, the Gulpers push through the gaps and inhale. Broken slabs drop morsels, so shoot them before the Gnawlings that follow can eat them.

In the Devourer fight (optional)

  • Hungry: two Gnawlings crawl out with the morsels and race the Devourer to them.
  • Inhale: Gnawlings in the cone are pulled in too and heal it 1% each when swallowed.
  • Ravenous: a Maw Priest at each side door; shots into either maw heal the Devourer.

Gutworm, not Burrower: that name is already the Earthquake minion (Burrower.cs).
