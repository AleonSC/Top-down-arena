DPS Hemomancer ability rework · replaces Hemo Nova (6 s)

Vein Rush

The Hemomancer opens a vein and becomes the blood. For a fifth of a second it is a stream that cannot be touched, cutting through everything in its way. Every enemy it passes through pays a little of the price back.

Live preview

Click anywhere in the arena to rush toward that point (280 px at most). Left alone, the Hemomancer rushes through the enemy line every 2 seconds. Orange shots pass through it during the rush. The 6 s cooldown is off here, and health comes back at 1.5 HP a second so the preview keeps looping.

Three looks

AStream

The body melts into a liquid ribbon that snakes along the path, thick at the head and thin at the tail. At the end the ribbon pools and the Hemomancer rises out of it with a splash.

BNeedle

A straight crimson streak with a white-hot core. Afterimages hang along the path, every enemy cut gets a sharp slash line, and each hit freezes the frame for a few hundredths of a second.

CSerpent

A blood serpent with an open jaw lunges along the path and bites through each enemy. The Hemomancer reforms from its tail as the coils pull back into the landing point.

The relic

Blood finds the shortest way through. So does the Hemomancer.

The DPS Hemomancer has no way to move quickly today. Vein Rush gives it one, and makes it pay for the escape unless it goes through enemies instead of away from them.

Cost
10% of max health, paid at the start. It cannot take you below 1 HP.
Rush
280 px toward the cursor over 0.22 s. You are untouchable and pass through enemies and their bullets.
Each enemy
25 damage (raised by Blood Price) and 3 bleed stacks, 18 damage over 3 s.
Refund
2% of max health for each enemy you pass through. Five enemies pay the whole cost back.
Trail
A blood trail stays on the floor for 2 s and slows enemies on it by 30%.
Mastery
Crimson Tide (the renamed rare card): 1 s after the rush the trail bursts for 20 to every enemy on it.
Cooldown
6 s. AbilityId.HemoNova is kept and renamed.

Open questions

  • Would 2 charges of the rush be a better mastery than the trail burst? Charges help it escape twice; the burst rewards rushing into packs.
  • Should a boss count as several enemies for the refund (for example 3), so rushing through a boss is worth it?
