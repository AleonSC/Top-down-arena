Who's playing? on the B machine

The same case as the main menu. Here its shutter starts rolled up, and the bay is a disk rack holding your three disks and a blank "New profile" disk. Hover a disk: it lifts, the right-hand screen shows that profile, and the drive reads "INSERT: NAME". Click it: the disk flies into the drive, the shutter rolls down over the rack, and the same machine becomes the main menu (the monitor retypes, the left keys turn into Play / Join / Settings).
