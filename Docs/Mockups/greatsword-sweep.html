Greatsword: one big cut, left hanging

The Greatsword is one heavy 170° sweep, 125 px out, every 0.7 s for 60 damage, and the edge of each sweep stays in the air for 0.5 s as a crescent that stops enemy bullets. Today that is a rose-coloured band and a thin arc. These three looks keep the numbers and make the cut read as a weight: you gather the sword back in two hands, it comes across in one stroke, and the crescent it leaves is a solid thing you can see bullets die on.

Enemy bullets are on by default so you can watch the crescent work. One red bullet passes straight through it: aimed red shots are the only ones it does not stop. The sword beam is the +1 upgrade (a piercing crescent at 40% damage).
