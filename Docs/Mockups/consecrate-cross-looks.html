Healer Knight · Mockup 2

Consecrate: three looks

The same ability in three different visual takes. The cross flies in a line, grows as it goes, heals allies for 35, gives them Swiftness for 4 s, and now hits enemies for 25 damage. Each target is hit once per cast.

Click or tap any arena to aim and recast.

A · Radiant Cross solid light, the straight read

Two glowing bars with a white-hot core. It stays level with your aim and is the easiest to read in a crowded fight.

B · Turning Cross quarter-turn as it flies

The cross rotates a quarter turn over its flight, with a halo ring at the center. The hit area turns with it, so late targets are caught on the diagonal.

C · Stained Glass tiled cross with afterimages

A cross built from colored glass tiles, leaving fading echoes of itself behind. It looks heavier and more ceremonial, and the echoes show the path it took.
