Boss proposal · wave 30

The Duelist, in three stages

Today it strafes, alternates two volleys and blinks away every 3.5 s, leaving decoys that pop into rings. This mockup turns it into a duel in three stages by health: it fights you straight, then hides among lookalikes, then draws a dueling circle and dares you to parry. One technique at a time with breathers between, a 2 s stage change that clears the screen, and a last stand below 10%. The arena below plays each stage with a test dummy; pick one to watch it, or let it cycle.

What it keeps

  • Its strafing at 280 px and both volleys: the fan of 11 small shots and the three red aimed shots, every 0.9 s.
  • The blink and its decoys that pop into rings. The blink closes each stage 1 loop; the decoys become the heart of stage 2.
  • Its look (a silver diamond) and its health (6000, ×2.8 for a boss), wave 30.

What's new

  • Lunge: a telegraphed dash along a line, leaving blade marks that fire sideways.
  • Guard: its blade turns your shots back at you from the front. It turns slowly, so you can get behind it.
  • Masquerade: three decoys circle you with it. Only its shots are solid; the decoys' are hollow.
  • Crossing: an X of blade lines across the arena.
  • Dueling circle: a round wall of blades for stage 3, shrinking in the last stand.
  • Salute: the first boss move that rewards the parry. Parry the lunge and it's open for 2.5 s at +50% damage. Dodging is fine too; you just don't get the opening.
  • Flurry: five quick slashes of shot arcs, left and right of you.

To build it

  • Stage, turn and timers in a DuelistState on the boss, synced like WardenState; thresholds 65 / 30 / 10%.
  • Lunge and Salute reuse the Warden's charge (warning line, contact damage). The Salute's hit is melee, so the parry already blocks it; a parry during it sets a 2.5 s stagger and a +50% damage-taken multiplier (like the searchlight's LitMult).
  • Guard: in Collide, player bullets that hit within 70° of its facing become enemy small shots. Its facing rides the snapshot.
  • Hollow shots: a bullet flag (harmless, drawn as a ring) for the decoys.
  • Dueling circle: a round version of the Narrow Pass and Tiny Arena walls in KeepInArena; touching it hurts 15 and pushes you in.
  • Bots don't parry, so the fight must stay beatable by dodging; the stagger is a bonus. Retune BossHpMult in the Boss Lab for 60–120 s, then Bestiary entry, Boss Lab stage lock and sim tests per stage.
