What the log holds

Everything needed to tune a number

Weapons

  • Shots fired, hits, accuracy
  • Damage per hit, per second of use, per wave
  • Share of total damage, kills, overkill
  • Status effects applied (burn, bleed, slow, stun) and time spent
  • Healing or shielding done, for support weapons

Abilities & ultimate

  • Casts, cooldown uptime, time cast was idle
  • Damage, healing, shield soaked, enemies affected per cast
  • Kills per cast, damage per cooldown second
  • Whiffs (cast that hit nothing)

Enemies

  • Spawned, killed, time alive, time to kill
  • Damage they dealt to you, by attack
  • Health at spawn after scaling, elite count
  • Distance they died at, how often they reached you

You

  • Health every second, low-health time, downs
  • Damage taken by source and by wave
  • Dash, parry and dodge use, time standing still
  • Upgrade offers, picks and the level at each wave

The report builds on the existing session recorder (format 3), so a test file also opens in Tools/SessionReport/report.py. New fields are per-weapon and per-ability counters, time-to-kill per enemy type and the findings list.
