DPS Hemomancer ability rework · replaces Frenzy Pact (16 s)

Blood Debt

Borrow your own blood. Your health drops to a quarter at once, which puts Blood Price near its cap. The health you gave up becomes a debt on your health bar. Kill fast for 8 seconds and you earn it back. Whatever is still owed when the time runs out is gone.

Live preview

Starts on its own and loops. The Hemomancer fights normally for a moment, casts, then has 8 s to pay the debt back. Move the mouse over the arena to walk the Hemomancer. Raise or lower the kill rate to see a good and a bad outcome.

A · LedgerThe debt is a hatched ghost segment on the health bar. Each kill spits blood coins that arc into the bar, and a ledger in the corner counts owed, paid and left. Paying in full stamps the bar. A forfeit cracks the ghost segment into shards.
B · ChainsShackles of blood hang from the Hemomancer, one per chunk of debt. Each kill throws a chain from the corpse that hooks the Hemomancer and reels the blood home. At timeout the leftover shackles rust brown and snap.
C · Pawned HeartA cracked heart floats over the Hemomancer with its pieces hollow. Each kill sends a drop that fills the next piece. At timeout the hollow pieces break off and fall. A full heart beats and glows.

The relic

The vein lends freely. It always collects.

A gamble only the Hemomancer can make. It turns Blood Price from something that happens to you into something you choose, and gives you a clear goal for the next 8 seconds. The icon follows the look picked above.

Cast
Your health drops to 25% of max at once. If you are already at or below 25%, it costs a flat 5% instead. The health lost becomes the debt.
Blood Price
At 25% health it is about +33% damage (the cap is +40% at 10%).
8 s window
Lifesteal goes from 3% to 15%. Each kill pays back debt: a Grunt 4% of max health, a Heavy 10%, an elite 15%, and each hit on a boss 1%.
Paid in full
A "Debt paid" flourish, and the 15% lifesteal lasts 2 s longer.
Timeout
Whatever is still owed is gone for good. The ghost segment leaves the bar.
Mastery
Rare card, renamed from Bloodrush to fit: kills repay 1.5×.
Cooldown
16 s, starting at the cast. AbilityId.FrenzyPact is kept.

Open questions

  • Cost floor: is a flat 5% right when you cast at or below 25%, or should the ability simply not work there? A flat cost lets you keep casting it for the lifesteal alone.
  • Boss repayment: 1% per hit could pay off the whole debt on a single boss with Blood Bolts. Should boss hits cap at, say, 30% of the debt, or should only boss damage thresholds count?
  • Other healing: should health orbs, allies' heals and lifesteal also pay the debt, or only kills? In the preview only kills repay, and other healing fills the bar in front of the ghost segment.
  • Should the debt ghost segment be visible to allies, so a healer knows not to waste heals on you?
