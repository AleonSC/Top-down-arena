Katana streak examples

Stills and loops for the chain streak: how the meter behaves, what the empowered strike looks like next to a normal one, and three ways to show the count. The playable version is in the Chain Streak artifact.

The meter with a streak

Grey: no streak, the full meter drains 1.2 s after the last hit. Rose: each strike kills, the meter goes back to full and the chain climbs. The fifth kill makes the next strike empowered.
