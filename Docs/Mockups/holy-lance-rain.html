Holy Lance Rain

A Healer Knight ultimate. Spears of light fall all around you. Each one hurts the enemies near where it lands and leaves a small healing glow on the ground. It's a third option beside Hallowed Blade and Pilgrim's Standard. This mockup is approximate: the browser's glow and blur are softer than Ui.cs can draw.

Scatter: random spots, kept spread out. Spiral out: spears land from your feet to the rim. Seek enemies: half the spears go to the nearest enemies, the rest scatter. The knight walks during the rain, and the circle follows them.

Build notes

  • Built with the Scatter pattern, on key 2 of the Healer Knight's Ultimate row (Core/HolyLanceRain.cs).
  • The host picks each spot. Clients draw from two effects: FxKind.HolyLanceRain (the circle round the caster) and one FxKind.HolyLanceSpear per spear (warning, fall, impact, glow).
  • Look: Game/HolyLanceRainLook.cs. Landing sound: Sfx.LanceImpactSfx.
  • No protocol bump: it uses the existing effect messages.
