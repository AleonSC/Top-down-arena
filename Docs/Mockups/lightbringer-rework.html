Lightbringer: Dawn, Mercy, Noon

Today the Lightbringer swings the same 100° cut every 0.5 s and throws a crescent of light that heals allies it passes. Twelve hits fill a light gauge that makes the next crescents heal three times as much.

This rework turns it into a three-step healer chain, and each step does a different job: heal one ally, shield the next, then heal everyone around you in a burst. The light gauge now powers that burst, and the weapon-action key gets an alt attack. The Knight swings one longsword through every move, with wind-up, strike, follow-through and back to guard, like the Twin Blades rework. The blue figures are allies with health bars, so you can watch the heals land. Watch the replay, or switch to Try it and play it.

The sword

A longsword with a sun-disc pommel and a gold guard. The fuller glows brighter as the gauge fills; at 12 the whole blade burns and leaves a wider trail, so you can see a Radiant Sunburst coming.

What stays

  • Reach stays at about 80 px for the cuts. The crescents still fly past the cut and pierce 2, so you can heal allies who aren't next to you.
  • The light gauge is still 12 pips round you, filled by enemies your swings hit.
  • Shot upgrades apply to every hit, as on every melee weapon. The parry's animation cancel sends the chain back to Dawn.

What changes

  • A full gauge powers the next Noon instead of the next swing, so you time it by where you are in the chain.
  • Mercy's ward reuses the Energy Ward shield and takes the larger of the two; it doesn't stack.
  • Three pips over your head show the chain step. The third glows when the next click is Noon.
