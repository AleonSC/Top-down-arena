Lightbringer rework

The Lightbringer stops being one cut on repeat. It becomes a three-step chain where each step does a different healer job: patch up, protect, then heal everyone around you. The light gauge now powers the finisher, and the weapon-action key (Left Alt) gets an alt attack. Two candidates for it are mocked below; pick one.

The chain: Dawn, Mercy, Noon

Always in this order. Fire while a step plays queues the next. If you don't swing for 0.8 s the chain starts again at Dawn. Every crescent still pierces 2 and still flies out past the cut, so you can heal allies who aren't in melee with you.

Radiant Sunburst

The light gauge (12 pips round you, as now) no longer powers "the next swing". It powers the next Sunburst, so you choose when to spend it by timing your chain. Full gauge: the ring grows to 220 px, heals 3× (30 HP), puts a Mercy ward on every ally inside and spends the gauge. Tapping fire all the way through gives you a radiant group heal about every 3 to 4 chains in a crowd.

Alt attack: pick one

On the weapon-action key (Left Alt), like the Twin Blades lunge and the Katana strike. Both spend or build the same light gauge, so the alt and the Radiant Sunburst compete for it. That trade is the healer's decision.

A · recommended

Sunlance

Tap to throw a lance of light at the ally nearest your cursor, anywhere within 450 px. It heals them for 4 per light pip and burns every enemy along the line. One big heal on one ally, at range. Use it to save someone across the arena instead of waiting for the group heal.

B · alternative

Dawnguard

Hold to raise the blade. A 120° arc of light in front of you blocks enemy bullets, so allies standing behind you are covered too. Each bullet blocked adds a light pip. Let go after blocking 3 or more and you fire a Sunburst straight away.

Open questions

  1. Sunlance or Dawnguard? (Or both: tap for Sunlance, hold for Dawnguard. That's more to learn and more to balance.)
  2. Should Sunlance be able to target yourself when no ally is in range? The mockup says no, to keep it a support move.
  3. Mercy's ward reuses the Energy Ward shield (ShieldHp). Should it stack with a real Energy Ward or take the larger of the two? The mockup takes the larger.
  4. All numbers are first guesses for a balance pass after it's built.
