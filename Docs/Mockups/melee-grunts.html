Melee Grunt Variants

Three melee grunts. The Buzzsaw replaces the Swarmer, the Swarmer moves in a new way, and the Spike Block is new. Each arena has a target (the teal dot) to chase. Move your pointer over an arena to steer the target yourself.

Buzzsaw

A round body ringed by spikes that spin at a blur.

  • Steers with momentum, so it swoops in on curves and loops past you instead of turning on a spot.
  • Fastest grunt. Low damage per hit but a hit lands about 8 times a second (about 33 damage per second).
  • Spikes spin at roughly 3 turns a second, which is purely visual. It hurts on any contact.

  • Heads for you but swings 40 to 65 degrees off line, then flips to the other side every 0.2 to 0.45 s.

Spike Block

A square with one big spike on each face.
Live
Damage taken0Hits0
Health60
Speed65 + side
Lunge35 dmg
Radius14
  • Approaches slowly, rocking side to side, with one spike always turning to face you.
  • Inside about 135 px it stops and winds up for 0.55 s: it shakes, the spike glows and a red line shows the lunge.
  • Then it dashes about 170 px in a straight line for 35 damage on a hit. It is slow and dim for 0.8 s afterward, which is your window to shoot it.
Notes

Names and numbers are placeholders for you to change. Sides and radii follow the existing grunt scale. The Buzzsaw keeps the Swarmer's slot in wave design and in the melee-heavy modes (Swarm Fields, Defend the Crystal, Zombie waves). The Spike Block would need a new enemy type added at the end of the enum, plus a protocol version bump.
