Bear Totem attacks

Today the Bear Totem is two quick claw swipes that make enemies bleed, plus a Bear companion that mauls what's nearby. In the game both swipes come from one totem rod held in both hands, the claws are three pale arcs, the Bear is a plain brown seven-sided shape, and the bleed is a red tint with drops. Styles A, B and C below put a claw on each hand instead: the left hand rakes across, then the right rakes back, and the totem itself rides on your back and flares on each rake. The moves are the same: two swipes (85 px reach, 110° arc), the Bear running out to bite every 0.6 s, and bleed stacking up to five hits. Pick one, or mix parts of several (the claws from one, the Bear from another).

Notes for building it

  • Claws: the swipes are FxKind.Claw, drawn by DrawClawFx in Game/WorldEffects.cs (reach, arc and rake direction already arrive with the effect). Every style here replaces that one function (the hands are drawn with your character), so the claws need no protocol change. Hits, bleed and timing stay in Core/KitWeapons.cs (FireBearClaws).
  • The hands (A, B and C) replace the held totem rod, which is the WeaponId.BearTotem case in Game/CharacterModels.cs. Which hand is out, and how far along its rake, comes from the same FxKind.Claw effect the claws use: its rake direction (the sign of the radius) says which hand, and its age says how far. The first swipe is the left hand and the second the right, so no network change. The claws would stay centred on you, as the hit arc is; only the drawn arm and claw reach out from the hand. The totem moves to your back as a charm.
  • The Bear is drawn today by the generic polygon at the end of DrawMinion (Game/WorldEnemies.cs). A new DrawBear would be called for MinionKind.Bear the way DrawHiveDrone is. Legs and trail come from how far it has moved since last frame (kept per minion on your own game), so a walk cycle needs nothing from the host.
  • The bite wind-up and chomp (A, B and C) need to know when a bite lands. The bite timer (Minion.Cd) is host only, so there are two ways: guess it on each game (the Bear is next to an enemy, so open the jaw), or send a small effect when it bites. A new effect kind goes at the end of FxKind and bumps Session.ProtocolVersion (now 128).
  • Bleed stacks: the five-notch counter (C) and the scar count (A) need the stack count on every game. Today only BleedT and BleedDps exist, on the host, and the enemy just has a bleeding flag. Showing stacks means adding one value to the enemy snapshot, which is also a protocol bump. The tint, drops and drip (all styles) work with the flag we have.
  • Reduced effects already turns off the glow pass and particles. Each style degrades to its base shapes: the claws, the Bear body and the red tint.
  • Nothing here changes damage, reach or timing, so the sim tests are unaffected. The Armory and Bestiary pages aren't touched by this mockup.
