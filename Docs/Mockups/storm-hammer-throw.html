Storm Hammer: a throw you can see coming

Today the Elementalist's Storm Hammer is an 18 px head on a short stick that appears at the muzzle. The new version is more than twice the size, and you see the player throw it: the hammer in their hand rises over the shoulder as the lightning builds, snaps forward, and that same hammer leaves the hand. Then it flies, strikes and electrifies.

Every number stays as the game has it: 460 px/s, 1.1 s of flight, 0.7 s between throws, 40 damage, three arcs of 5 over a second. Pick a look, press play or drag the scrubber. "Now" is the current hammer for comparison.

Five moments

How the held hammer becomes the thrown one

  • Hold (0 to 0.45 s). The hammer sits in the right hand at about 1.5x today's size, head forward, with a few sparks on the head.
  • Wind-up (0.4 s). The arm draws back, the hammer swings up over the shoulder and swells to 2x as it rises (it is closer to the camera), its shadow slides away, the body leans back and lightning gathers along the haft.
  • Throw (0.08 s). The arm snaps forward to full reach. A flash and a ring of sparks mark the release.
  • Hand-off. The held model is hidden the frame the projectile appears, at the hand, in the same pose and size. No pop.
  • Re-form. 0.7 s after the throw (the weapon's fire interval) sparks pull in to the hand and a new hammer fades in over 0.3 s, even while the first is still flying. That is the cue that you can throw again.
