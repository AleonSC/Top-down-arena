Boss proposal · wave 50 · the final boss

The Eclipse, in five stages

Today it orbits the middle through three phases: a 4-arm spiral and 24-shot rings, then two Phantoms, a gravity pull and 4-way triple shots, then two Hexers, a strike on each player and a five-armed spiral. Every other boss now has three stages and a last stand. The final boss gets five, and a last stand that has to be won on damage. A sun and its moon line up as the fight goes on: Corona, Penumbra, Umbra, Echoes of the nine bosses before it, Last Light as the arena burns in from the edges, and Totality below 5%. Each stage keeps a mechanic from the last one, so the fight gets harder as it goes. The arena below plays each stage against two test dummies (you in green, an ally in blue); pick a stage to watch it, or let it cycle.

What it keeps

  • The 4-arm spiral (0.07 s, 150 px/s) and the 24-shot ring (every 1 s, 110 px/s): Corona's first two turns, unchanged.
  • Phantoms and the gravity pull in the Penumbra (2 Phantoms on arrival, a 120 px/s pull for 3.5 s), Hexers in the Umbra, as today's phases 2 and 3.
  • Today's final five-armed spiral that switches direction every 3 s, now Last Light's first turn.
  • Orbiting the middle 140 px out, its 16-sided gold body (52 px) and the corona that grows wilder each stage.

What's new

  • The Moon: from the Penumbra on, a moon orbits the sun 300 px out. It can't be hurt and it soaks your shots, but its shadow is the only safe place from an Eclipse flash.
  • Solar flares: long lanes of fire from its rim at each player, with a 1 s warning line.
  • Darkness and sunspots: the Umbra puts the arena in the dark. Standing outside a drifting pool of light drains you, and its bullets only show in the light.
  • Crescents that fly out and come back, and closing umbra rings with gaps to find.
  • Echoes: four turns borrowed from the bosses you've beaten: the Colossus's push-block stomp, the Lich's soul sweep, the Storm Herald's strike rain and the Singularity's accretion disk.
  • Last Light: the edges of the arena burn inward. Totality: a 12 s nova timer with no shadow to hide in. Kill it, or take the nova and the timer starts again.

To build it

  • EclipseState on the boss (stage, turn, timers, moon angle, flash, sunspots, closing rings, nova timer), synced like HeraldState; thresholds 80 / 60 / 40 / 20 / 5%, 2.5 s stage changes in StageShiftLook.cs.
  • Reuse: Hazard for strikes; the Colossus's push-block rings, the Lich's sweep (SweepOn/SweepA), the Herald's strike rain and the Singularity's DiskOrb for the echoes; Phantom/Hexer adds; Nightfall's darkness grid (DrawNight) for the Umbra; TinyInset for Last Light's closing walls.
  • New: the moon as a body that blocks player shots (in BlocksShot) and a shadow test (InEclipseShadow) shared by the flash, the bots and the drawing; flare lanes as line hazards; crescents as returning bullets.
  • Health: BossHpMult 2.7 → about 4.5, tuned in the Boss Lab for 180–260 s solo, about twice any other boss. Bestiary entry with six stage locks, sim tests per stage, preview ECLIPSE=<health> ECLIPSETURN=<turn>.
