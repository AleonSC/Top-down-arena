How it fits design notes

  • Animations: drum indexes 60° each shot, barrel kick and heat, muzzle flash and smoke ring, grenade spins in an arc over its shadow with a trail, hop, fireball, shockwave, debris, embers, scorch.
  • Swap: the drum spins out to the side, the other slides in with its colour band (amber HE, teal smoke) and clacks home with a ring. A label pops over the player.
  • Reload: drum swings out, six hulls spill, rounds click in one by one.
  • Smoke reads clearly: jammed enemies get a grey gun, a crossed-bullet icon and coughs; slowed bosses get a SLOWED tag and keep firing.
  • Overlap: the Riot Repeater's Tear Gas suppresses 50%. Smoke is the hard counter (no shots at all), so it should live on a different weapon or replace Tear Gas.
