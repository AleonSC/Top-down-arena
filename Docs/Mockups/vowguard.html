Vowguard · sword & shield

An arming sword and a heater shield with two stances. Valor fights with both: cuts, thrusts and shield bashes. Sanctuary holds the shield up front, blocks shots and turns blocks into healing. Each stance plays a chain of up to 10 steps you build yourself from its nine moves, in any order, repeats allowed.

Live stage

Your two combos from the builder below, played in a fight. Shots come in from the right; watch the shield.

Space or click the stage: next step · Q / Alt: shift stance · the diamonds over the knight are the chain (gold-edged: finishers) (top row: this stance, dim row: the other), the dots under it are Faith.

Build your combos

Click a move to add it to the end of the chain (up to 10 steps). × removes a step, the arrows or dragging reorder it, and clicking a step first makes the next move you pick replace it. The stage plays your edit straight away.

Presets

Valor

Sword forward, shield on the arm. Full speed; the shield only blocks during shield moves. Hits light Faith.

Sanctuary

Shield up front, sword tucked. 80% speed; small shots in a 100° front arc are always blocked, and each block lights Faith.

How it plays

Fire

Plays the next step of this stance's chain (1 to 10 steps). Hold to keep going; a press counts once a step is 70% through. The chain starts over 0.8 s after a step ends without one, and after its last step.

Shift (weapon-action key)

0.25 s to change stance. Each stance keeps its own place, like the Nodachi. Going into Sanctuary blocks at once; going into Valor shoves with the rim (6, 40 px).

Faith

Up to 6. Valor links light one per enemy hit, Sanctuary one per shot blocked. A finisher (anywhere in the chain) spends it all: +2 healing and +3 damage per Faith. A perfect parry fills it.

Parry

Raising the parry starts both combos over (as on the Arc Knuckles), so you can reset into your opener.

Building a combo

In the lobby, the weapon's detail box gets a Combo row per stance: up to ten DkKey steps (× to remove, drag to reorder) and the move list. Saved with the loadout (10 bytes a stance); bots use the Crusader preset.

Mastery card

Proposed: the last step of each chain hits 25% harder and heals 25% more.

Numbers are first guesses sized against the Lightbringer and Censer (about 45-55 damage a second, 8-15 healing a second in a full rotation). Moves, names and numbers are all open to change.
