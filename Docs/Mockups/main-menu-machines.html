Main menu machines

Three fresh layouts. Click Play on each: a physical part opens onto the saved-runs screen; click it again (or Back) to close. Approximate browser mockup: the game draws with its own widgets, so glows and type will differ in Unity.

A · Console deck: a hatch flips up into a screen

The monitor sits on a low, wide deck. The small keys and the drive are on the deck's front. Play unlatches a hatch in the deck's top, which hinges up into a screen standing between the deck and the monitor.
