Sound cues, in order
 

1 · Hydraulic

  • Heavy and slow (1.5 s). A valve hiss, then the doors unlock with a thunk and glide apart on a long steady hiss.
  • Steam puffs out at the chassis walls when the doors stop. The three keys drop with a gravity fall and a thud each. The legend rolls like a reel.
  • New run slams down with a big thunk and a last hiss.

2 · Servo

  • Quick and clean (1 s). Beeps on the open and close, a rising whine while the doors move, and a clamp click.
  • The keys slide down their rails smoothly, each with a falling whirr. The legend crossfades with a key press. New run rises from below instead of slamming.

3 · Relay

  • All clicks (1.7 s). The doors open in six notches, each with a ratchet click.
  • The keys drop in four notches. The legend is a split-flap, like the header. New run slams with a plain thunk.

4 · Blast door

  • Slowest and loudest (2 s). Amber warning lamps flash outside the box and a klaxon sounds before anything moves.
  • The doors grind open with a low rumble and a small shake, then boom. The keys tilt like trapdoors before they fall. New run lands with the biggest slam and a steam blast.

Sound

  • All sounds are made in the page (no audio files), so they only approximate the real kit. Each one is a candidate for a real sample.
  • The strip under the stage shows each cue's position in the transition. Letters in the header and legend click as they flip, in every version.
  • Back plays everything in reverse with the same sounds, so the doors close with the same thunks.
