Boss proposal · wave 20

The Singularity, in three stages

Today it loops four attacks for the whole fight. This mockup splits it like the Warden, the Hive Queen and the Twin Reapers: three stages by health, one mechanic at a time with breathers between, a 2 s stage change that clears the screen, and a last stand below 10%. The arena below plays each stage with a test dummy; pick one to watch it, or let it cycle.

What it keeps

  • Every attack it has today: the Pull and its spiral, the Collapse rings, the sweeping lasers, the Cages and the corner Gravity Wells. They're just spread over the stages so each one gets the screen to itself.
  • Its bullet kinds and damage (small shots and big orbs, with the new +15%).
  • Gravity Wells' shot bending and drift, but only in the Event Horizon now: they collapse into the core when Accretion starts.

What's new

  • Accretion disk: two rings of orbiting debris that turn opposite ways, each with gaps. A new bullet motion (orbit a moving centre) that needs replicating.
  • Slingshot: debris leaves the outer ring at a tangent, aimed so it crosses where you are.
  • Event horizon: a black disc round the core that grows through stage 3; touching it hurts (20 a second) and its pull never stops.
  • White hole: the pull flips to a push and every enemy bullet on screen turns round.
  • Split: three shards orbit and fire; the core can't be hurt, the shards pass their damage to it.

To build it

  • Stage, turn and timers in a SingularityState on the boss, synced like WardenState; thresholds 65 / 30 / 10%.
  • Disk bullets: an orbit centre, radius and angular speed on the bullet; clients move them from those three numbers between snapshots.
  • Health: with three stages and breathers the fight runs longer at the same health, so BossHpMult for the Singularity (3× now) should come down and be retuned in the Boss Lab for 60–120 s.
  • Bestiary and Armory entries, a Boss Lab stage lock, and sim tests per stage, as the other staged bosses have.
