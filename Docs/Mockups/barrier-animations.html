Barrier animations

Today the Barrier is a faint disc with a ring that thins as it soaks damage and flickers when it is about to go. Nothing shows the cast, a bullet hitting it, or it breaking beyond a puff. Below are three reworks next to today's look, in the three situations that matter (hold against regular fire, a boss wearing it down, the reflect capstone), at the game's real size and timing. Radius, duration, soak and every hit rule stay as they are: this is drawing only.

Build notes

  • Drawing only: the Barrier block in WorldRenderer.cs (the p.BarrierT > 0f part of the under-player layer) would call a new small look file like BarrierLook.cs. Nothing in Core/ changes for A to C; no protocol bump.
  • With Visual effects off the arena should draw exactly as now (the render check stays IDENTICAL); the new looks only show with effects on.
  • Real numbers are used: radius 105, lasts 4 s, soaks 360 of boss damage, 0.4 s cast ring, flicker under 1 s left or under 25% strength. Regular bullets never wear it down; boss bullets do. The 45 damage per boss bullet here is only illustrative (eight hits to break it) so the break is easy to see; the real wear comes from BlockerWear.
  • Already replicated and enough to drive every look: BarrierT and BarrierFrac. Hit ripples, dents and plate recoil are cosmetic, triggered where Juice.cs already spots a blocked shot. Which plate or crack goes is chosen locally from the hit angle, so players could see slightly different cracks but never different results.
  • Reflect tint needs one new bit. BarrierReflect is host-only today, so other clients can't tell a mastered Barrier from a plain one. Showing the mirror look on everyone's screen means replicating that flag, which is a snapshot change and so a Session.ProtocolVersion bump. Without it the tint would show only for the host and the owner; the rest of each look is unchanged.
  • Shatter and the expiry pop play on BarrierT falling to 0, so a late join or a dropped packet just skips them.
