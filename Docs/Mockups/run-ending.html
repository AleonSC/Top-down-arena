Run Ending Mockups

Three ways to get from the moment a run ends to the results screen. Each one has a Game over version (the last player goes down) and a Victory version (The Eclipse dies at wave 50); switch between them above each screen. They play on a loop, and Replay starts one again. All three end on the current results screen.

Option ALast breath

  • Time slows almost to a stop on the killing blow, and the camera eases in on it.
  • Game over: the colour drains out and a red vignette closes in, then GAME OVER slams down with a shake. Victory: a white flash, the boss bursts into light rays, every enemy shot turns into a gold spark, then VICTORY.
  • The title glides up to the top of the results; the stat tiles drop in one by one and the player cards rise.

Option BShatter

  • The frame freezes on the killing blow and cracks spread out from it, like glass.
  • Game over: the frozen screen breaks into shards that fall away. Victory: the shards blow outward from the boss with gold edges.
  • Behind them is the results screen. The title lands with an impact ring, and then the tiles and cards come in. It echoes the shatter you see when you pick an upgrade.

Option CThe tally

  • Slow motion into a freeze, then letterbox bars close in to a band across the middle with the title in it.
  • A big wave counter ticks up to the wave you reached and lands with a thud (WAVE 11, or WAVE 50 with a burst of gold confetti).
  • The bars open onto the results: each stat tile counts up in turn, then the player cards flip over and their trophies pop in one at a time.
