Knight DPS Abilities

Four replacements for Whirlwind, Lunge, Battle Fury and Blade Tempest. Each one works the same with the Twin Blades, the Greatsword or the Rapier, asks for a skill (a graze, a throw, a read of the field, a lane), and ends in one big moment. Every stage plays on its own; pick a look under each one.

Caught Steel Space · 16 s cooldown · 6 s to catch · up to 12 shards · 22 each

Six gunners keep up a crossfire. Bullets that skim past you (inside the dashed ring, without touching you) snag and hang in the air, then drift into orbit. Press Space again and a sweeping cut hurls the whole halo out as a fan of steel that cuts the bullets in its way.

Scrub0.00 s

Beats

  1. 0.00 sSpace: the graze ring shows round you for 6 s. Gunners on both sides open up.
  2. grazeA bullet whose closest pass is inside the ring but outside your body freezes where it was closest, with a snap, then eases into orbit 40 to 54 px out. Bullets that would hit you still hit you.
  3. 12 heldThe pips in the corner fill. At twelve the ring stops catching and the rest fly past.
  4. 3.60 sSpace again: the orbit spins up, then a sweeping cut throws each shard off the blade as it passes, fanned across 70°.
  5. releaseShards fly at 1,150 px/s, cut any enemy bullet they touch and stop in the first enemy for 22.

How it plays

  • The skill is standing close to the bullets on purpose. A careful player who stays out of the pattern catches nothing.
  • The release is aimed: the fan follows your cut, so you choose which side gets the steel.
  • A hit while you hold shards knocks a third of them loose (they clatter to the floor and fade).
  • Works the same with every weapon: it only reads where bullets pass and where you aim.

The Gauntlet Space · 9 s cooldown · 380 px throw · 5 s Challenge · resets on the kill

Throw down the gauntlet at a gunner behind a pack. It hits, your crest burns over the enemy and you shoulder-charge in full plate, bowling the pack out of the way. Kill the Challenged enemy within 5 s and the gauntlet flies back to your hand, ready to throw again, so the scene does it twice.

Scrub0.00 s

Beats

  1. 0.00 sWind-up: the gauntlet comes back past your shoulder (0.22 s). The throw line glows.
  2. 0.22 sIt spins out at 850 px/s, an aimed shot that stops at the first enemy it meets.
  3. 0.66 sClang. 12 damage, the crest stamps over the enemy with a 5 s timer ring, and a ring marks the ground under it.
  4. 0.86 sYou charge at 1,000 px/s. Enemies in the way take 18 and are bowled 55 px aside. You can't be hurt while charging.
  5. 1.22 sImpact: 30 and a short hold on the hit. Then your normal swings.
  6. on the killThe crest shatters, the gauntlet flies back to your hand with a ring of steel and the cooldown resets.

How it plays

  • Two skills: landing the throw past the pack, and picking a target you can actually finish in 5 s.
  • Throw, charge, kill, throw again: a good player crosses the arena in a chain of Challenges.
  • A miss, or a Challenge that runs out, costs the full cooldown.
  • The charge is your gap closer, so the kit still has a way in after Lunge goes.

Misericorde Space · 12 s cooldown · executes under 25% · 200 px between leaps

Wounded enemies (under 25% health, from anyone's damage) show a small dagger. Your teammate in the corner wears one gunner down, and it gets its dagger too. Press Space: you leap onto the wounded enemy nearest your cursor and drive the mercy dagger home, then leap on to the next wounded enemy within 200 px, and the next, until none are left in reach.

Scrub0.00 s

Beats

  1. 0.00 sThree enemies already show the dagger. The ally's shots bring a fourth under 25% at 1.10 s and its dagger lights.
  2. 1.35 sSpace: a 200 px ring flashes, the enemy nearest your cursor is bracketed and you leap (0.2 s, untouchable).
  3. stabThe dagger goes in with a hold on the hit. The enemy is executed whatever health it has left.
  4. chainA thread runs to the next wounded enemy within 200 px and you leap along it. 0.41 s a link.
  5. 2.83 sNo wounded enemy in reach: the ring flashes grey, you flick the dagger and rise.

How it plays

  • The skill is reading the field: spread damage, notice what your team has softened, then cash it all in at once.
  • You pick the start with your cursor, so you choose which way the chain runs.
  • Healthy enemies are ignored even if they're closer. The two in the middle are never touched.
  • Any enemy at low health counts, so it's strongest next to a teammate who sprays damage.

Drawbridge Ultimate · 75 s · 500 × 100 px lane · 140 crush · stands 5 s

A gatehouse rises behind you, the chains snap and a huge drawbridge falls along your aim, crushing everything in the lane and throwing the edges aside. Then it lies there as a raised walkway: enemies can't step onto it, and their shots stop at the rails. You walk out along it and cut at whatever presses against the sides.

Scrub0.00 s

Beats

  1. 0.00 sCast: the lane is marked on the floor and two towers rise behind you with a rumble.
  2. 0.42 sThe bridge tips from upright. Its shadow sweeps the lane ahead of it, so everyone can see where it will land.
  3. 0.72 sBoth chains snap, links flying.
  4. 1.05 sSlam: 140 to everything in the lane, 45 and a 40 px shove to enemies at the edges, dust rolling out both sides.
  5. walkwayFor 5 s you and allies can walk on it. Enemies are kept off (they press against the rails) and their shots stop at the rails.
  6. 6.10 sThe planks give way from the far end and the bridge is gone.

How it plays

  • The aim is the skill: a lane through the thickest part of a wave crushes the most and gives you the safest perch.
  • The walkway is a position, not a shield: shots from the open far end can still reach you.
  • With the Gauntlet, the bridge becomes a runway: throw from the deck, charge off the end.
  • Bosses take the crush damage but aren't killed outright, and they can't climb on either.

Build notes

  • Not built. The AbilityId slots would be kept and renamed, as with the DPS Hemomancer rework: BattleFury → Caught Steel, Lunge → the Gauntlet, Whirlwind → Misericorde, BladeTempest → Drawbridge. New replicated state (held shards, the Challenge, the bridge zone) means a protocol bump.
  • Sizes are scaled to the 720 px stage. Numbers are first guesses for the balance pass.
  • Open: should Caught Steel release on your weapon's next swing instead of a second press? Knights swing almost all the time, so a second press keeps the release deliberate.
  • Open: bosses. Proposed: the Gauntlet Challenges a boss and the charge stops at it, but there's no refund. Misericorde never executes a boss; a wounded boss takes a heavy stab (120) and ends the chain.
  • Open: should the Drawbridge rails also stop bosses' red aimed shots? Proposed: yes, since you can still be shot from the open far end.
