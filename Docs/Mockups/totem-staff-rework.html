Totem Staff rework

The Sprite Staff is getting the Firefly Hop, and the Treant Rod the Root Lash with a root tether. That leaves the Totem Staff, which today is the same seed bolt plus a totem that sits still. You want something entirely different, so these are five whole-weapon ideas and not new bullets. Each one heals through its own mechanic, and none repeats a hopping mote, a root thrust or a standing totem.

Replacing the Totem Staff

Build notes

  • What goes away. The totem zone and its charge (ZoneKind.Totem, TotemCharge, TotemPulse, the 10 s throw, the totem branch in SeedHit, the charge shown on the totem), plus its tests, its lobby text and its README rows. That is a deletion, so I would ask before removing it.
  • What each concept needs. Nimbus: a zone that follows your cursor, with its position sent to everyone. Stag of Dawn: a Summoner minion like the Bear, with a dash and trail zones. Tollbell: an orbiting hitbox like the Knight's swings, plus a toll effect. Rolly: a bouncing minion. Spirit Chalk: a line zone whose points are sent to everyone, the biggest change to the snapshot.
  • Network. Every concept adds new effect, zone or minion values, so the protocol version goes up. New values go at the end of their enums.
  • Upgrades. Multishot and Barrage need an answer for the new weapon, as the Sprite Staff has (+1 Sprite). Suggested: Nimbus +1 cloud, Stag +1 stag, Tollbell +1 bell, Rolly +1 Rolly, Spirit Chalk +50% ink.
  • Numbers are a starting point, not measured. Each is sized to about 26 damage a second on one target. Build one, then tune it with the DPS meter and a target dummy (F1 → DPS), and rerun weapondps write. The dummy stands still, so it flatters the stag, Rolly and the chalk.
  • Reference pages. The Armory and Bestiary entries for the Totem Staff go out of date after the change. They stay as they are until you ask for them.
