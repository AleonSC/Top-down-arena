Boss wave teleport

When the boss wave starts, anyone in a side room is moved to the hub. Today that is an instant snap. Three ways to make it a moment. Blue and orange are two players starting in the side room.

Loops every 3.6 s

A · Beam up

Clean

Rings close in, a light column lifts the player into a thin streak. A second column drops at the landing spot and the player stretches back into shape.

B · Rift

Dramatic

A spinning portal opens under the player and swallows them. Another opens in the hub and pushes them out with a small bounce.

C · Data stream

Fits the CRT look

A scan line digitises the player into pixels that fly across the map and rebuild at the landing spot, with a short glitch.

Shared timing (all three)

0.0 sWarning“BOSS INCOMING” banner. A landing ring shows in the hub for each player, so teammates see where they land.
0.0 – 0.9 sChargePlayer is locked and takes no damage. Only players outside the hub do this.
1.1 – 2.1 sTravel and arriveCamera cuts to the hub. Players land a fraction apart so it does not look like one blob.
2.3 sBoss introStarts once the last player has landed. Total delay is about 2 s, only when someone was in a room.
