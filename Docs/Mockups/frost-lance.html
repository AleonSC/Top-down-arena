Frost Lance visuals

The Elementalist Tank's Frost Lance juts a 230 px icicle out along your aim (18 damage), then splinters off its sides into enemies up to 60 px beside it (50%). Both slow by 30%. Today that's a flat blue wedge and a few small triangles. Below are four other looks, drawn at the game's real reach and timing, with today's look for comparison. Hits, reach and timing don't change: this is drawing only.

Notes for building it

  • All of this is drawing, on each screen. The icicle is FxKind.Icicle (DrawIcicleFx) and the splinters are FxKind.Splinter (DrawSplinterFx), both in Game/WorldEffects.cs. Hits, slow, reach and the 0.15 s splinter delay stay in Core/Knight.cs and Core/KitWeapons.cs, so none of these need a protocol change.
  • The hit frost on enemies (the pale tint and frost spikes that fade over about 1.5 s, showing the 30% slow) needs a small per-enemy timer the drawing code can read. Enemies already show a frozen look (WorldEnemies.cs); this would reuse it. The toggle above shows each look with and without it.
  • The ground frost in B and C lingers about a second on each screen as a purely visual effect (no gameplay), like the other floor decals.
  • Look D matches the glowing arc direction picked for the melee weapons, so it would sit with them best. B and C are the most distinctly "ice".
  • With Visual effects off, the arena should draw exactly as now (the render check stays IDENTICAL). The new look only shows with effects on, like the other effects.
  • Extra projectiles make the icicle longer and the splinters fly further: every look scales with the real reach and spread, shown here at the base 230 and 60.
