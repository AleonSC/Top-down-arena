    The kit

    Weapon · Vector Forge

    Strider

    Fire is a light rivet gun. The weapon's real job is the Strider, a walker drawn line by line at your side. It hunts the enemy nearest you, punches, and every 4 s slams the ground. Only one ever, so it is built to carry a fight: it draws aggro and takes hits for you.

    Alt on an enemy locks it on: it drops everything, sprints there and hits +50% until that enemy dies. Alt on the floor posts it there to guard.

    Drag Pylon

    Plant a pylon at your cursor (up to 320 px away). It projects a vector grid on the floor: enemies walking through it are slowed 45% and the grid bends under their feet. It stays until you place the next one, which folds the old one up.

    Radius
    130 px
    Slow

    Ultimate · 70 s

    Breach

    Aim at a wall within 220 px. A vector hammer draws itself and smashes a 3-tile hole: enemies by the wall take 80 and are thrown back. Behind it a random room draws in, with one thing inside: vectors, gold, a repair bay, a workbench, or an ambush.

    Hole
    3 tiles · permanent
    Blast
    80 in 140 px · knockback
    Room
    6 shapes, 8-15 tiles, free
    Fallback
    no wall: shockwave only

    Breach room roller same generator as the bench

    Every room is rolled from a shape and a content table, placed so its floor covers the hole. Rolled rooms join the map like a Stash: no vote, no vector cost.
