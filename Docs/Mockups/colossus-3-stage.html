Boss proposal · wave 25

The Colossus, in three stages

Today it walks at you, stomps a ring with one gap every 4 s and throws a 3-rock volley, for the whole fight. This mockup splits it like the Warden, the Hive Queen and the Singularity: three stages by health, one attack at a time with breathers between, a 2 s stage change that clears the screen, and a last stand below 10%. It breaks down as it goes: armour, then shell, then a molten core. The arena below plays each stage with a test dummy; pick one to watch it, or let it cycle.

What it keeps

  • The stomp: a 48-shot ring with one gap, at 150 px/s. It's stage 1's main turn, and it also lands every Charge.
  • The rock volley: 3 aimed piercing shots, about 10° apart, every 1.2 s, between turns in stages 1 and 2.
  • Walking at you, 200 px off. It speeds up as it sheds armour (35 → 55 px/s), then kneels for the last stand.
  • Its bullet kinds: small shots, piercing rocks and big orbs.

What's new

  • Boulders and meteors: thrown at where you're heading, with a shadow that grows before they land, then a burst of shots.
  • Fissure: a crack races toward you, then erupts along its whole length. A line hazard the game doesn't have yet.
  • Fallen plates: cover that blocks shots both ways, so hiding costs you damage. Charges smash them, and smashing one dazes the Colossus (+50% damage).
  • Rockslide: rows of rocks roll in from an edge with a moving gap. Plates shelter you from them.
  • Lava: in stage 3 everything leaves burning pools, so the arena fills up and you keep moving.
  • Cave-in: pillars fall at marked spots, stand as cover for 3 s, then shatter into shots.

To build it

  • Stage, turn and timers in a ColossusState on the boss, synced like WardenState; thresholds 65 / 30 / 10%.
  • Reuse: Hazard warning circles for boulders, meteors and the cave-in; the Warden's baton charge for Charge; path fire patches for lava pools.
  • New: plates and pillars as zones that stop bullets from both sides (Flame Wall only stops enemy ones); the fissure as a line hazard; rockslide rows.
  • Health: BossHpMult for the Colossus (3× now) should come down and be retuned in the Boss Lab for 60–120 s, plus Bestiary entries, a Boss Lab stage lock and sim tests per stage.
