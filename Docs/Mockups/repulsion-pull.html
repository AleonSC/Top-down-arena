Healer · Soldier · weapon rework mockup

Repulsion Cannon + pull

Fire stays exactly as it is: a 70° cone, 240 px, 6 damage, 90 px shove, health orbs. Alt becomes a pull: the same cone sucks enemies toward you. The loop shows Alt, then Fire, so you see the pull-then-blast rhythm. Pick an option below.

Pull

The options

A · VACUUM

One sharp yank

Alt fires the cone in reverse. Smoke and the shock ring rush into the muzzle, and every enemy in it slides 110 px toward you, stopping 60 px in front.

Feel
Mirror of the blast. Simple, readable.
Use
Drag strays into your team's fire, pull a runner off a downed ally.
B · UNDERTOW

Hold to reel in

Hold Alt for up to 1.2 s: a swirling current fills the cone and drags enemies in at 140 px/s, slowed 40% while caught. Let go to stop.

Feel
Channelled, a tractor beam.
Use
Hold a crowd in place for teammates; you can't fire while holding.
C · RIPTIDE

Pull, then punch

Same yank as A, and it gathers enemies on a glowing knot. A Fire within 1.2 s is overcharged: +50% push, +4 damage, amber ring, and wall slams get likely.

Feel
A two-button combo with a payoff.
Use
Gather a pack, then blast it into a wall.
