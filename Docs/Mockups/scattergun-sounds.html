Scattergun Sounds

The Scattergun shot is the loudest thing in a Soldier's kit and it fires every 0.45 s. These four options keep the same synth recipe as SoundBank (Sfx.ShotShotgun) but turn down the crack, shorten the tail and lower the peak.

Press Fire loop to hear each one at the real fire rate. Peaks are shown against the current shot, so you can compare how much quieter each one is.

Notes

  • All four options are drop-in: they only change the numbers passed to SetGun(Sfx.ShotShotgun, ...). No new code, no protocol change.
  • Each shot plays 4 takes with a little pitch variation, as in the game. Press a card's Play repeatedly to hear them cycle.
  • Sfx.ShotShotgun is also the sound for any other weapon that points at it, so the change applies to all of them.
  • Browser playback here is a close port of the C# synth, not the game's own audio. Final levels depend on the game's Weapons volume slider.
