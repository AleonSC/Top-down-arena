Rocket Drop

Call in a crate holding a heavy rocket launcher with infinite ammo. For 10 s you fire massive rockets that blow up in a 170 px radius. Pick a drop style and a warhead below.

Drop
Warhead
Auto off: mouse aims, hold click to fire, WASD moves, Space calls the drop.

Three drops

A · 2.2 s to land

Chute crate

Red flare on the cursor, a cargo plane's shadow passes over, the crate floats down on a chute. Walk to it and grab the launcher. The most ceremony; you commit to a spot.

B · 1.1 s to land

Drop pod

A target ring locks on, a steel pod streaks in and slams down (90 px, 120 damage). Its petals open and the launcher rises on a rack.

C · 0.7 s to fire

Kick drop

The crate falls at your feet; you kick it open and shoulder the launcher in one move. Fastest, no walking, no slam.

Beats

  1. Call: the drop marker goes down (flare, ring, or your shadow growing).
  2. Land: dust ring and a thump; the pod also hits what's under it.
  3. Take: step on it. The launcher swings onto your shoulder, a gold ∞ pops over your head and the 10 s bar fills.
  4. Fire: each rocket kicks you back, blows a backblast cone behind you, and accelerates from 300 to 950 px/s.
  5. Blast: it bursts on the first enemy, a wall, or the cursor: white flash, fireball, shockwave ring, debris, screen shake, a scorch that fades over 8 s.
  6. End: at 0 s you drop the empty tube and your weapon comes back.
