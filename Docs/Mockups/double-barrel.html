Double Barrel

A side-by-side shotgun for the Soldier DPS's locked fourth weapon slot. Short range, wide pellet cone, pellets that pass through up to two enemies. Two shells, then a break-open reload.

Move over the arena to aim. With auto-fire off, hold the pointer down to shoot.

Waiting for the first volley.
  • Falloff wedge: the faint wedge on the floor is the damage range. Full damage inside the inner arc, down to 35% at the outer arc. Tracers dim as the damage drops.
  • Pierce: every pellet passes through 2 enemies. A spark flies out of each far side, so a line of enemies visibly gets raked.
  • Hand work: each shot kicks the gun back and twists the muzzle away from the barrel that fired. The hammer drops, the lever pops, the front hand pulls the barrels open and the hulls flip out.

Model sheet drawn at 5× game scale

  • Silhouette: two parallel barrels with a ventilated rib, a long walnut fore-end and a brass hinge. It reads as a shotgun from a distance and is clearly wider than the Railgun and the SMG.
  • Hot barrels: a barrel that just fired glows orange from the muzzle back and cools over 2 s.
  • Break-open: the barrels shorten and slide forward as they tip down, the breech opens on two chambers, and a drop shadow grows to show the height.
