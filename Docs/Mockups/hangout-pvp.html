Hangout upgrade

Hangout PvP layouts

Three ways to lay out the new Hangout: a bigger room that scrolls to a border, a background slider, a PvP checkbox, and shooting. All three show the same scene: you (gold frame) are shooting Mira, who has PvP on and loses health. Rook has PvP off, so Rook is drawn faded with a dashed plate and your shots pass through. The panel is 790 x 520, the same space the room has now.

Behaviour in all three

  • Scrolling: the world is bigger than the panel (default 2400 x 1600). The camera follows you and stops at the border, so the glowing teal rim is as far as anyone can go. A small minimap shows where everyone is.
  • Shooting: aim with the mouse, hold left click to fire your own loadout weapon with its normal fire rate. Hangout hits do a little damage, health refills in a few seconds, and nobody dies for real (a short knock-out flash, then respawn at a spot along the bottom).
  • PvP off: you can't fire (the cursor shows a crossed-out reticle), your shots from others pass through you, and you're drawn faded with a dashed name plate and a "PEACEFUL" tag. Everybody sees each other's setting. It starts off.
  • Background slider: I drew it as brightness of the floor, from a dim void to a bright floor, since the lobby backdrop is already your menu background. If you meant a slider that steps through different backgrounds, say so and I'll swap it.
  • Network: a new message for shots and a PvP flag on each character, so the protocol version goes up. The host decides hits.
