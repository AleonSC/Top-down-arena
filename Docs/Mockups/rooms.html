What to look at

  • Hallways on vs off. Off, every room is a dead end with one door, and the safe play is to stand in the hub and shoot through the doorway. On, the rooms join into a loop: enemies can come at you two ways, and you can run the loop to kite.
  • Spills. A room with two doors picks one at random for each spill. Hallway spills take longer to reach the hub, but they come from where you aren't looking.
  • The vault (gold). Off a hallway, with two elite guards and a rare-or-better chest. Optional: the wave ends without it, and the rooms keep spilling while you're in there.
  • Hallway width. 260 px, wide enough to dodge a volley. Narrow corridors would turn back into face-tanking.
  • Zoom 2x. In a room or hallway, enemies are in front of you instead of just off-screen.
  • The HUD. Minimap (corner), a room strip (top middle, with spill timers), or both.
