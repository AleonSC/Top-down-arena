What to look at

  • The map. Wings: two big rooms, one door each. Cross: four rooms, the hub becomes the crossroads. Annexes: four smaller rooms, two doors on each side.
  • Zoom 2x. In a room, enemies are in front of you instead of just off-screen.
  • Split up vs stay together. Split clears faster, but the hub fills with spills while you're both away.
  • The HUD. Minimap (corner), a room strip (top middle, with spill timers), or both.
  • The doors. Red while uncleared, flashing amber 2 s before a spill, teal once cleared.
