The Bazaar as hologram panels

The shop before a boss, in the Tab screen's hologram look: panels projected over the arena from the beam along the bottom. The shelf keeps its holo cards; the header, wallet, services, Done shopping, your teammates and the popups move onto the glass. Gold stays the Bazaar's colour.

You are
Effects

Click a card to see what it changes (your stats, left), click it again to buy. Try Restock, Remove upgrade, Give and Done shopping. 1280 x 720, scaled to fit.

Shelf

The same six holo cards in their rarity colour. The rarity is an inverse-video tag; the price a gold tag (red when you can't afford it, BUY · n on the picked card); a bought card gets a green BOUGHT stamp.

Services and wallet

The four services are soft keys with their price; one that does nothing (full health, a token already) is dashed. Your gold sits in the header as a tile; gifts arrive as a gold toast under it.

Done and teammates

Done shopping is the lobby's green key. Each teammate is a key with their state and gold and a GIVE key that opens a small panel. The host also gets Close the shop for everyone, in red.

Building it: the panels, beam and text helpers are TabHolo.cs's and the cards already use DrawHolo, so this is mostly ShopUi.cs moving its pieces onto them; YOUR STATS would be the Tab screen's panel with the shop's green preview lines.
