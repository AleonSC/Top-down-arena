Four enemies do the same thing, a wind-up then a straight lunge or charge, and each shows it differently. This page shows today's four looks, then three candidates for one shared look. Scrub the wind-up or press play.

A. Lane: a filled strip that fills out from the enemy

Reads as a floor hazard: you see how wide and how far. Fills over the wind-up, flashes white for the last 0.1 s.

B. Rail: dashed rail with marching chevrons and an end ring

Thin and light on a busy screen. The chevrons speed up as it gets close, the end ring marks where it stops.

C. Lane and landing mark: the strip plus a closing ring at the end

Same ring language as the Fuse Runner and Gas Grenadier blasts, so "ring = it lands here" stays one rule. The most to read.
