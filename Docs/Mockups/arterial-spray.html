DPS Hemomancer ability rework · replaces Blood Boil (AbilityId.BloodBoil kept)

Arterial Spray

The Hemomancer opens a vein and points it. Hold Space to spray a cone of pressurized blood at your cursor. It costs health every second you hold it, and because of Blood Price it hits harder the longer you keep going. You choose how deep to cut.

Live preview

It plays by itself: the Hemomancer sprays until 15% health, sputters, waits out the 8 s cooldown and starts again (the preview refills health during the cooldown). Move the mouse over the arena to aim. Hold the mouse button, hold Space or press Hold to spray yourself; Replay goes back to the automatic loop.

Three looks

AJet

A tight, pressurized stream leaves the wrist and holds together for about 120 px, then bursts into a fan that fills the cone. Drops that land stay on the floor as spatter, and soaked enemies are covered in heavy red flecks.

BMist

A wide, fine mist. The whole cone glows with a red haze full of tiny drifting droplets, and the edges of the arena tint red while you spray. Soaked enemies take on a red film instead of spots.

CHeartbeat

The blood comes in spurts on your heartbeat, each one a thick arc that curls as it flies. The spurts speed up as your health falls, so you can hear and see how deep you are. Damage lands with each spurt.

The relic

Every Hemomancer learns where the big vessels are. The bold ones learn to aim them.

A held channel in the style of a flamethrower, made of blood. The cost is visible on your own health bar, and the reward is visible in the damage readout, which climbs as the bar empties.

Input
Hold Space. The spray leaves your hand toward the cursor and follows it while you hold.
Shape
A 60° cone, 200 px long. It hits every enemy inside it, not just the first.
Cost
4% of max health per second while held. Nothing up front.
Damage
30 per second to each enemy in the cone, times Blood Price. That is 30/s at full health and about 41/s just before it sputters. Look C deals the same amount per second in spurts.
Soak
Enemies in the spray are slowed 20% (and for 1 s after) and gain a bleed stack for each second they stay in it, up to 5. Grunts and shooters are pushed back a little; Heavies and bosses are not.
Ends
When you let go, or when your health reaches 15%: the spray sputters out and can never take you lower.
Cooldown
8 s, starting when the spray ends.
Mastery
Rare card High Pressure (renamed from Boiling Point): the spray also washes away enemy bullets it touches.

Open questions

  • Is 200 px the right length? Longer makes it safer to use against shooters; shorter makes it a close-range commitment.
  • Should it go through enemies, or should a Heavy at the front shelter the enemies behind it?
  • Should the drain rise the longer you hold (for example 4%/s growing to 6%/s), so long sprays are a bigger gamble?
  • The class's 3% heal from hits partly pays for the drain against big packs (tick the box above to see it). Should Arterial Spray's damage be left out of that heal?
  • Blood Boil's level 5 capstone (bursts hit within 80 px) needs a replacement. A wider cone, or bleed stacks that cap at 8?
