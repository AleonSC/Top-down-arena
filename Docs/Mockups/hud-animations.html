HUD mockup · Elementalist, wave 1

HUD animations

Every panel on the HUD now has the upgrade cards' shading: a rich top fading to near-black, tinted by the panel's colour. That part is built. Below are five ways to make the ability bar move on top of it. Style A is the one you asked for: ready abilities glow, used ones burn out and warm back up as they recharge.

Style

Click a ready tile to use it, or press Use every ability. Every ability is class orange and the ultimate is gold, as in the game. Cooldowns are shortened so you see a full cycle.

The five styles

Building it

  • Done already: Ui.HudPanel shades every HUD panel like CardArt.Body. Plain panels are slate, accented ones take their colour (class, gold for boss waves, red when downed). The lit rim wash is lighter because the gradient carries the colour now.
  • All five styles slot into the existing TileLook in HudTiles.cs. They add a few fields (burn, heat, sparks) and draw in DrawAbilityTile. No network or snapshot change, so the protocol stays put.
  • Particles (sparks, ash) would reuse the HUD's existing dot and glow helpers. Reduced effects keeps the glow and the colour change but drops sparks, ash, the glitch and any movement. Visual effects off draws the bar as it does today.
  • Style A needs the most new drawing: a scorch overlay with a moving burn line, hand-placed cracks per tile and a handful of particles. B and E are the cheapest.
