The Burrower

Your slam opens the ground, a Burrower breaks out at the cursor, dives, tunnels through the pack for about 2.7 s and sinks again. Same numbers as before (25 slam, then 30 to each enemy it passes under). What changes here is how it looks and moves: a moving ridge of soil, a scar that fades behind it, a snap-up under each enemy it hits, and screen shake on the big moments. Pick a look, then scrub the timeline.

Build notes

  • The Burrower is a minion that cannot be targeted while tunnelling. Only the cursor telegraph, the breach and each snap-up need to be sent to clients as effects. The ridge and the scar can be drawn client-side from the minion's position and a short history of it.
  • The soil scar is a fading line of dark blobs, so it is cheap: about 70 blobs at the longest. Dust puffs are drawn from the same history. Reduced effects should drop the puffs, the chunks and the shake, and keep the ridge.
  • All three looks use the same hit logic. The looks differ only in how the ridge is drawn, so choosing one is a pure art decision.
  • Adds a MinionKind and effect kinds at the end of their enums, so Session.ProtocolVersion needs a bump when built. The Bestiary and Armory are untouched.
