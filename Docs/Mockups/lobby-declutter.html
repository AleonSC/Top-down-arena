Opens from the ascension button on the weapon. Esc or ✕ closes it.

One detail box

The separate Weapon and Ability boxes merge into one full-width box. Click any weapon or ability and it shows that item's text and numbers. Only Katana and Shadow Step have real copy; the rest is placeholder.

Smaller text

Lobby text drops to about 80% of today's size. Panels keep their size, so the loadout card gets room to breathe. Title bar, mode tabs, party rows and picker buttons all shrink together.

Role and class title

Three options for "Ninja DPS". A puts a class badge and a red DPS tag beside the name. B sets a small role line above the class name. C joins a solid role slab to the class name.

Ascension button

The right quarter of every unlocked weapon button is its own button with a rank icon and 0/3 count. It opens the ascension window. The old Ascensions row is gone, which frees a row of height.

Glowing bars

Bars get a lit gradient, a soft halo, a bright end cap and a slow pulse. The pulse stops when reduced motion is set. Turn glow off above to compare.
