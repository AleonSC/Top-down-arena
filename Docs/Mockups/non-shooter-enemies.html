Non-shooter enemies

Three new regular enemies that never fire a bullet: one Hunter, one Disruptor and one Zoner. Each has three looks to choose from. The arenas run the planned behaviour against a stand-in player that answers it (and sometimes doesn't, so you see what happens). Distances in the arenas are half the game's, so they fit.

Arenas With "Steer the player" on, the player follows your mouse or finger inside an arena.
Hunter · from wave 15

Fuse Runner

Sprints at you, plants its feet within 120 px, and a 0.8 s fuse runs down before it blows up. The blast hurts enemies too, so it's a threat you can also use.

Numbers
35 health, 200 px/s. Blast 90 px for 35 damage (+3% a wave). Comes in pairs.
Warning
It stops dead and the fuse shows the blast's full size, closing over 0.8 s.
Answer
Step out of the ring, or lead it into its friends first. Shot during the fuse, it blows up early where it stands.

Disruptor · from wave 17

Hookman

Hangs back about 260 px with its squad, swings a chain, shows a line for 0.5 s and throws a hook along it. A hit yanks you 220 px toward it and the enemies beside it. It does no damage itself: the danger is where it drags you.

Numbers
70 health, slow walker. Hook reaches 320 px, yanks 220 px over 0.3 s. Every 4 s.
Warning
The chain spins up (1 s), then the line locks where you stood and the hook flies 0.5 s later.
Answer
Sidestep or dodge, or perfect-parry the hook. A teammate's shot on the chain cuts it. A miss leaves it open for 1 s (+50%).

Zoner · from wave 18

Sapper

Circles at mid range, planting a mine every 1.6 s (up to 4, each lasting 12 s). A mine arms after 1 s. Step near an armed one and it beeps for 0.3 s, then blows. Shoot a mine and it blows at once, hurting the enemies near it.

Numbers
55 health. Mines: trigger at 40 px, blast 70 px for 28 (+3% a wave), for players and enemies alike.
Warning
A mine is dim while it arms, then shows its trigger ring. A triggered one beeps and flashes for 0.3 s.
Answer
Walk round them, kill the Sapper to stop new ones, or shoot a mine next to enemies to use it.
