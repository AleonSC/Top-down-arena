Swordsman

A long-bladed melee enemy. It walks up, raises its sword and cuts across the cone in front of it. The cone is the exact area the blade covers: a curved band from the hand out to the tip. The shield variant holds a shield in front that stops your bullets until it commits to a cut.

Live

Reach: the area the blade covers Committed: the cut lands when the red reaches the edge Swept: where the blade has already passed Shield: blocks bullets while it is up

Press and drag on the arena to move the player yourself. The player fires at the Swordsman on its own. Get out of the cone before the red fill reaches the edge.

One attack, four phases

Close in until in reach

Sword held low at its side. The dashed cone is the area the blade will cover. The shield is up in front and the spark shows a bullet being stopped.

Wind-up 0.55 s

The blade swings out to one edge of the cone and the cone fills red from the enemy outward. It stops turning. The shield stays up.

Cut 0.15 s

The tip sweeps edge to edge along the rim. The bright wedge is what the blade has already passed through. The shield drops the moment it swings.

Recover 0.80 s

Sword low and slow. This is the window to shoot it. The shield stays down for the first 0.75 s, then comes back up.

Notes

  • It sits between the Brute (slow, heavy) and the Lunger (one dash). The Swordsman is about timing a sidestep, not about running away.
  • Because it stops turning during the wind-up, circling it works. Standing still next to it does not.
  • The shield turns the fight around. Shooting it from the front does nothing, so you have to go round the side or bait the cut and shoot in the recover.
  • It moves slower and recovers longer, so the shield costs it speed in return for being hard to shoot.
  • The cone is drawn on the floor, below the enemy, so it never hides bullets.
  • Numbers are a starting point for tuning. The code is not written yet.
