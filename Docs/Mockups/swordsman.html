Swordsman

A new melee enemy with a long blade. It walks up, raises its sword and cuts across a cone in front of it. The cone is the exact area the sword covers: a curved band from the hand out to the tip, swept from one side to the other. It comes in two variants, a single cut and a two-hit combo.

Live

Press and drag on the arena to move the player yourself. Get out of the cone before the red fill reaches the edge.

One attack, five phases

Close in until in reach

Sword held low at its side. The dashed cone is the area the blade will cover.

Wind-up 0.55 s

The blade swings out to one edge of the cone. The cone fills red from the enemy outward and it stops turning.

Cut 0.15 s

The tip sweeps edge to edge along the cone's rim. The bright wedge is what the blade has already passed through.

Return cut 0.30 + 0.15 s

Combo only. The cone refills red for a short beat, then the blade cuts back across the same area.

Recover 0.80 s

Sword low and slow. This is the window to shoot it.

Notes

  • It sits between the Brute (slow, heavy) and the Lunger (one dash). The Swordsman is about timing a sidestep, not about running away.
  • Because it stops turning during the wind-up, circling it works. Standing still next to it does not.
  • The combo's second cut comes only 0.30 s after the first, so stepping sideways inside the cone does not work. Leave the cone entirely.
  • The cone is drawn on the floor, below the enemy, so it never hides bullets.
  • Numbers are a starting point for tuning. The code is not written yet.
