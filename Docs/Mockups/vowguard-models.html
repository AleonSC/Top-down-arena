The looks

A · Crusader

  • Shield: thick curved plate seen from above, navy field, stance-colour rim stripe, gold boss.
  • Sword: broad two-tone bevelled blade, curved gold quillons, wrapped grip.
  • Trail: faint white smear.
  • Closest to the Lance's steel. Most grounded.

B · Templar

  • Shield: heater silhouette tilted toward the camera, stance-colour cross on navy, gold trim. Tilts more face-on when raised.
  • Sword: fuller glows in the stance colour; gem in the guard.
  • Trail: stance-colour crescent.
  • Most readable at game zoom.

C · Radiant

  • Shield: faceted steel frame round a glowing core; projects a light lattice while guarding.
  • Sword: white core with stance-colour edges, winged guard.
  • Trail: three afterimages.
  • Most "holy", loudest.

New motion (all three looks)

  • Anticipation: a short pull back before each strike.
  • Follow-through: sword and shield on springs, so they overshoot and settle.
  • Hit-stop: 60 ms freeze on contact (90 ms on finishers).
  • Body twist: shoulders turn with the swing.
  • Trail: the look's smear, crescent or afterimages.
  • Stance switch: a sword twirl while the shield swings across.
  • Idle: slow breathing sway instead of a frozen pose.
