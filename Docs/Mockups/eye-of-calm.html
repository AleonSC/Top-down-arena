    Spin-upWind gathers round you over 0.8 s; the wall grows out from the eye. Already shoves enemies out of the eye.

    HurricaneThe full storm follows you. Enemies in the wall are dragged round it, slowed and ground down; their shots are torn away.

Rules

  • Eye90 px round you. Allies in it (you too) heal 12 HP/s. Enemies can't walk in; any enemy shot reaching it, red aimed shots too, is gone.
  • Wall90 to 280 px. Enemies in it take 22 a second, move 45% slower and drift round it counter-clockwise (heavy enemies and bosses take the damage but aren't moved).
  • BulletsSmall enemy shots entering the wall are swept round once and scattered; red aimed shots pass through but slowed 50%.
  • YouMove at 80% and can still shoot; your shots ride the wind and curve with it.
  • EndsAfter 10 s, on a second press, when you go down, or between waves. Always ends in the final gust.
  • Cooldown85 s, starting when the gust ends.
