Run Launch Mockups

Three ways to go from the lobby's Start button into wave 1, each about five seconds. They play on a loop; Replay starts one again. The game already waits 3 seconds before the first wave, so each of these fits inside that wait rather than adding to it.

Option ADrop in

  • The lobby's panels slide out to the sides; the arena's grid lights up in a ripple from the middle.
  • Each player beams down as a pillar of light in their colour, lands with a ring, and their class icon flashes above them.
  • The HUD slides in, then GET READY counts down to a gold WAVE 1. Enemies flicker in at the edges as it ends.

Option BInto the preview

  • Everything but your loadout's live mini-arena fades; the mini-arena grows to fill the screen and becomes the real arena, your character in it.
  • Your friend streaks in from their party card to their spot.
  • Ties the lobby to the match: the little arena you've been watching is where you land.

Option CTitle card

  • Letterbox bars close in; a title card names the run (difficulty, modifiers) and the party slides in from both sides, each with their class icon and loadout.
  • The bars split open on the arena, the players already standing in it, then a FIGHT! stamp.
  • Most cinematic; nice for a fresh run or a continued save ("WAVE 14 · CONTINUED").
