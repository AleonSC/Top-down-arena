Storm Hammer: slam the ground, send the storm out

An alternate attack for the Storm Hammer. Instead of throwing it, the Elementalist holds the hammer in both hands, hauls it overhead while lightning builds, and drives it into the floor just ahead of them. A shockwave of electricity rolls out from the crater and electrifies everything it crosses, using the same arc rule as the throw.

Three looks for the wave itself. Charge picks how long you held: a tap makes a small wave, full charge a big one. The numbers in the table are placeholders to react to, not tuned.

Five moments

Sequence

  • Hold (0 to 0.4 s). Same held hammer as the throw, now in both hands.
  • Wind-up (0.55 s, longer if charging). Hammer overhead and swelling to 2.2x, shadow sliding away, body leaning back. Sparks are pulled in from the floor round the target circle, so the charge reads before the hit.
  • Slam (0.08 s). The hammer comes down fast and stops in the floor 58 px ahead. The picture holds for about 0.06 s and shakes, as the Warhammer's slam does.
  • Wave (0.5 s). A flash, a crater, and the shockwave runs out to full radius. Each enemy it crosses flinches outward, flashes white and is electrified.
  • Aftermath. Cracks cool from white to dark violet over about 2 s. The hammer stays in the floor crackling, then breaks into sparks and re-forms in the hand.
