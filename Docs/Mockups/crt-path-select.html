Choose your path, CRT mockups

Four ways to dress the post-boss path choice in the lobby's CRT language. Same content in each: two offered paths, the host-only pick, the "no path" option, bonus and prize. Hover, click a card or tab to select it, and press the take button to lock it in (as host or as a guest).

CRT fx

A. Twin tubes

Each path gets its own green phosphor tube, like two monitors on a desk. The closest to today's cards, so the least code to change. The live floor preview stays in colour; all the text is phosphor.

B. Route radar

One amber tube with a branching route map on the left: the stretches you've taken, the fork you're at, and the boss ahead. Pick on the map or the tabs. It makes the "journey" idea from the design doc visible.

C. Route console

A cyan terminal: a scan log lists the routes with danger bars, a dossier prints the rules, and a live feed shows the floor. Number keys 1-3 select, Enter confirms. Most compact, and the most "hacker" of the four.

D. Switch panel

The tactile blue chassis with two small green feed screens and real push buttons with LEDs. A lamp tape along the top is the run's route so far. It matches the lobby's mix of tactile buttons and digital screens.

Mockup only: nothing here is wired into the game. Paths, rules, bonuses and prizes are the real ones from Core/Paths.cs.
