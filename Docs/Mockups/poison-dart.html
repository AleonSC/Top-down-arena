Mockup · Soldier · DPS ability 1 (replaces Dash)

Poison Dart

A dart flies at the enemy nearest your cursor and poisons it. If it dies while still poisoned, the poison jumps to the enemies around it, and on from them when they die. Three looks, each playing the whole chain: the dart (0.5 s), the first enemy dying (2.2 s), the spread, and a second death that carries it on (3.9 s). The blue circle is you, the white cross is your cursor, the diamonds are enemies.

Reduced motion is on: each scene shows one still frame. Press Play to start them.
Ability 1 · 8 s cooldown

The whole chain, three ways

The dart hits for 20 and poisons for 18 a second over 5 s. A poisoned enemy that dies spreads it to the 3 nearest unpoisoned enemies within 180 px, each at 80% of the strength it had, with the full 5 s; they can spread it again. Tick Mastered: it spreads to 5 and doesn't weaken.

Shared across all looks

  • Colour: toxic green, so it doesn't clash with Mark for Death's red or the Grenade's orange. Medicine Darts' poison (the Ninja healer) stays its softer green bubbles; this one is louder so you can tell whose poison will spread.
  • Network: the venom is a new replicated flag on each enemy, and the dart and the spread are two new FxKinds (dart: you → target; spread: corpse → each new victim), so Session.ProtocolVersion goes up.
  • Status icon: a green drop with a dart through it in the row over the enemy, next to Medicine Darts' bubbles.
  • Phase Step (the Dash card) becomes Virulence: the poison deals 30% more and spreads 60 px further, two stacks.
