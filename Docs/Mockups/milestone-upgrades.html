Milestone Upgrades

A second pool of upgrades, offered every 10 levels. The cards use the same layout as the new level-up cards, in gold, so they read as a different kind of pick. A very rare regular card (Milestone Compass) lets a milestone offer turn up between the milestone levels. Numbers are placeholders for you to tune. Nothing here is built yet.

A. Milestone level (level 10)

  • At levels 10, 20, 30 and so on you get a milestone pick. The title turns to "MILESTONE!" and the three cards come from the milestone pool only.
  • Every card shows its circles (the next one ringed) and what the pick changes, all the time, since every milestone card has a cap. The picked card gets corner brackets and a stronger glow.
  • No Reroll button, like boss rewards. Only CHOOSE UPGRADE.
  • The milestone pick comes on top of that level's normal pick, so reaching level 10 is never worth less. It shows first, and the normal cards follow ("1 MORE AFTER THIS").

B. The very rare card in a normal level-up

  • Milestone Compass is a Very Rare card with a cap of 3. Each stack gives every level-up a 5% chance of also offering a milestone pick (5%, 10%, 15%).
  • It is a normal card, so it only turns up in normal offers and it competes with two other cards for your pick. The circles show it at 0 of 3, and the line on the card shows the new chance.

C. A lucky milestone (any other level)

  • When the Compass roll hits, you get the same milestone screen, titled "LUCKY MILESTONE!". It is an extra pick on top of that level's normal one, like a boss reward.
  • It never takes the place of a real milestone level, and it draws from the same pool, so maxed cards stay out.

D. Level bar marker

  • A small gold diamond on the level bar shows the next milestone, so people know it is coming. The count under it ticks down.

Things I assumed

  • Milestone picks are extra picks, not replacements. If you would rather the level-10 pick be the milestone instead of the normal pick, that is a one-line change.
  • Everyone in a co-op run gets their own milestone pick at their own level.
  • The Compass chance is rolled once per level-up, not per pick, so a double level-up can roll twice.
  • Milestone stacks are listed on the Tab screen with your other upgrades.
