Acupuncture Needles

Healer Ninja weapon rework, replacing Medicine Darts. Built in the game (protocol 516). Fire places healing needles in teammates' pressure points. Alt drives bloodletting needles into enemies that cut and bleed them. Animated sketch; shapes and speeds are approximate, not the game's draw code.

Green-tipped needles fly through enemies and stick in teammates; five in one teammate open the meridian. Red-tipped needles stick in enemies; five in one enemy burst at the pressure point. With "Needles return" on, a bleeding enemy's needles fly to the most hurt teammate when it dies.
