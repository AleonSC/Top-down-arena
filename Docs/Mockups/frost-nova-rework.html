Elementalist Tank: Frost Nova

Animation mockup. Visual rework only. Compare today's single ring with three proposed looks on the same scene. Enemies are placed so six are inside the radius and two are outside, and the outside ones keep walking.

Frost Nova 12 s cooldown · 220 px radius (scaled 0.78) · 40 damage · frozen 3 s

Today: one cyan ring, then enemies turn a flat blue. Proposed: a wind-up that marks the radius, a layered wave, a lasting frost on the ground, and ice that visibly holds each enemy and counts down.

Beats

    What it fixes

    • The radius is hard to judge from a single fading ring. The wind-up ring and lasting rim show it.
    • Today nothing says how long enemies stay frozen. Each one has a draining ring.
    • The end of the freeze is sudden. Enemies now shatter or thaw with a clear cue.
    • The cast has no weight. The flash, edge frost and kick give it a tank-sized hit.

    Build notes. Drawing only, so no protocol change. The capstone (erases enemy bullets inside the radius) would reuse the wave and is not shown. The "Current" view is approximate.
