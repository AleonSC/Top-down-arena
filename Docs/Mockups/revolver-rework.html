REVOLVER // REWORK MOCKUP

Single-action Revolver: one click fires, the next click pulls the hammer back (a quick 0.15 s pull). Fire rate is your click speed. Alt switches to the Fan stance: click to Pistol Whip, click again to Fan the Hammer. Holding parry and clicking also fans; tapping parry resets the combo to the whip.

Approximate HTML mockup to try the feel; the in-game look would reuse the existing Revolver and Pistol Whip draw code.

At 4 clicks/s (2 shots/s) the shot does 100 DPS; at 6 clicks/s, 150. Today's Revolver ≈ 82. The pull (0.15 s) plus 0.08 s after a shot caps it at about 4.3 shots/s.

Rules

  • Gunslinger no cylinder, no auto-fire: holding does nothing. Clicks alternate FIRE and COCK; the hammer swings back over 0.15 s, and a click during the pull fires as soon as it's back.
  • Whip click in the Fan stance. Wide and forgiving; every enemy in the arc is stunned, so a tight pack all freezes.
  • Fan click again within 0.5 s of a whip, or hold parry and click at any time (the only time you can shoot with the parry up).
  • Reset tap parry after a whip and the combo goes back to the start: your next click is another Whip. Parry during a click-started fan cuts the rest.
  • The shield stays up while you fan, so you block bullets in front while firing; the 1.2 s fan reload stops that being a permanent wall.
