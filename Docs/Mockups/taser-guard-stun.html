My pick

A · Dart & Wire. It is the only option with something no other bullet can have at any size: a line back to the shooter. You can find the source, trace the shot to you and judge the timing, all from one glance. It also survives grayscale and squinting best.

  • Give the charge and the fire a distinct sound, so it is never only a visual cue. Sound is the one thing clutter can't hide.
  • D is the fallback if you want a shot that reads as "danger" with no learning at all, at the cost of size.
  • Wiring it in would replace DrawStunShot in WardenLook.cs for this enemy. The Warden's own stun shot can stay as it is or take a second option's look, so the two are never confused.
