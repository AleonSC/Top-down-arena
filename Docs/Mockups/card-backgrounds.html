Card backgrounds

A fourth cosmetic kind next to Frame, Trail and Menu background: the fill of your lobby party card (and your results card), seen by everyone. Ten ideas, each unlocked by rank. Pick one for the card on top; drag the rank to see what's locked. They are drawing only: the lobby entry would carry one more byte, like the frame.

Effects

Readability. Every background gets a left-to-right scrim (dark behind the portrait and name, lighter on the right) and is kept low-contrast, so the name, role and badges read the same on all of them.

Cost. Plain GL quads and lines drawn from the clock, like FrameLooks.cs; nothing synced beyond the chosen id. With Visual effects off each one falls back to its still base colours.

Wiring (if picked). A CosmeticKind.CardBackground at the end of the enum, a Rank.cs table row each, a byte on LobbyEntry and in Msg.Cosmetics (protocol bump), drawn in DrawPartyCard before the portrait, and a new section in the Cosmetics drawer.
