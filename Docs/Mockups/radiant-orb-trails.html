Radiant Orb Trails

Hold fire and the orb flies from you to your cursor at 750 px/s, then sits on the cursor and burns enemies (8 damage) and heals allies (4) five times a second while you drag it around. Today it has no trail at all. Below are five ways to give it one, in the arena at the game's scale. The loop shows the three moments: the cast (the orb flies out), the drag (it chases a moving cursor through the pack) and the release (it fades). The two Light Orbs that Multishot and Barrage add are on by default, and share the trail style at half size.

Your system asks for reduced motion; these previews are animated on purpose, so use Pause to hold a frame.

Build notes

  • Drawing only. The trail is the client replaying where the orb has been: DrawRadiantOrbs in Game/WorldRenderer.cs would keep the last ~0.5 s of p.RadiantDisp per orb (and per Light Orb) and draw from that. Nothing is sent over the network, so no protocol bump, and every player sees the same trail on everyone's orb.
  • Still orb, still alive. The orb sits on the cursor most of the time, so a pure motion trail would vanish whenever you hold still. B, C and D keep a little life going at rest (stardust drifting off, echoes pulsing, three coloured motes circling); A is a pure comet tail and only shows when you move.
  • Glow pass. The soft light (ribbons, halos, sparkles) goes in the additive glow pass like the melee energy and Chain Light's looks, so it also shows, clipped, in the lobby's loadout preview.
  • Scales with the orb. Width follows RadiantSize (Homing Rounds grow the orb, so the trail widens with it); a Light Orb gets the same trail at half size.
  • Cost. A and D are one polyline each, around 30 segments per orb. B and C spawn small particles or rings (roughly 30 alive per orb) from the existing Spark and ring helpers. With Visual effects off it draws exactly as now (render check IDENTICAL).
  • My pick: E, Radiance. The comet tail gives the drag its sense of speed and a readable shape for where you've swept, and the sprinkle of stardust keeps it alive when you stop. It stays in the orb's own pale gold, so it still reads as light and as the Healer's (the Mage's fire and frost have their own colours). Say which you want and I'll build it.
