Tortoise Oracle

An ancient tortoise settles on the floor at your cursor and slows time in a wide circle around it. Enemies and their bullets drag at half speed. Allies standing inside run at double tempo: twice the healing, twice the fire rate, cooldowns ticking twice as fast.

Ult
View

Watch the enemy bullets crossing the rim: they halve speed as they enter and pick it back up when they leave. Allies inside show a jade tempo ring and their cooldown pips refill twice as fast as the ally outside.

What it does

Enemies inside×0.5

Move, attack wind-ups and fire rate at half speed.

Enemy bullets inside×0.5

Travel at half speed while over the circle, normal again once out.

Allies inside: healing×2

Every heal they receive (regen, heals, lifesteal) counts double.

Allies inside: fire rate×2

Weapons fire twice as often; damage per shot unchanged.

Allies inside: cooldowns×2

Ability cooldowns recharge twice as fast. The ult's own cooldown is not sped up.

Open questions

  1. Bosses at ×0.75 or the full ×0.5? Full slow can break timed boss attacks.
  2. Should allied bullets also speed up inside, or only fire rate? The mockup keeps bullet speed normal.
  3. Does the field stay where cast, or follow the caster? Mockup: stays put, the tortoise is planted.
  4. Minions (the Summoner's own) inside: double tempo too? Would make it a strong Summoner synergy.
