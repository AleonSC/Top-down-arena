Menu Window Mockups

Three directions for one window style shared by the pause menu, Records and How to play. They're built from the newer pieces: the trophy cards of the upgrade picker and results, the lobby's icon-chip buttons and corner brackets, and the run's new parts (paths, modifiers, difficulty, challenge rooms). In every option, Settings moves out of the pause menu into its own window of the same style. Use the switch above each screen to see the three windows.

Option ARun dossier

  • One wide window with a left column that sums things up and a right side to act or read. Paused: the left is your run (wave, path, modifiers, difficulty, the next boss, this stretch's challenge room) and your party as small trophy cards; the right is five big buttons, each with a line saying what it does.
  • Records: the left is your profile at a glance, the right shows trophy rows for bosses, classes and challenge rooms. How to play: the left lists topics (Challenge rooms is one of them), the right shows the open topic as icon cards.
  • The closest to today's layout, so every window keeps its current content and just gets the frame.

Option BCard deck

  • The upgrade picker's trophy cards, used for everything. Paused: a run strip of chips under the title, then one tall card per choice with a hatched art panel and a big pixel icon. The highlighted card gets the brackets and the fading bar, and 1–5 picks one, like the picker.
  • Records: one card per category (Runs, Bosses, Classes, Challenge rooms), each with a big number at the top and its list below. How to play: topic cards three at a time with page dots, and a keycap strip of the controls along the bottom.
  • The boldest look, and the most consistent with the level-up screen and the results.

Option CSide drawer

  • A tall drawer slides in from the left with a vertical menu (keys on the right of each row). The frozen arena stays visible on the right, with a floating "this run" card and your party over it. It works with the signal-glitch opening: the glitch tears in, then the drawer.
  • Records and How to play reuse the drawer for their sections. The open section fills the right as one large panel, and How to play gets a small live diagram per topic (here, the portal and the five rooms).
  • The lightest. It hides the least of the game and reads like a console menu.
