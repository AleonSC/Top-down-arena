Lightbringer looks

New looks for the Lightbringer's moves, drawn in the game's style next to what the game draws today (dashed frame). Each card loops the move and shows four still frames under it, at the same moments for every option in a section, so they compare like screenshots.

Noon's ring was the weakest: it grows once and fades. Every option below gives it movement after the blow. Pick one per section, or mix and match; the chips switch each section between a normal and a Radiant (full gauge) version where that applies.

Noon Sunburst: the ring

The sword is planted 0.35 s after the click. The ring reaches 140 px (220 when Radiant), shoves enemies and heals the allies it passes.

Dawn and Mercy: cuts and crescents

Dawn's crescent heals the ally it reaches; Mercy's wards them. Today both are the plain white sword wave every Knight weapon throws.

Sunlance

Light gathers in the off hand for 0.36 s, then the thrust sends it to the ally nearest the cursor.

Mercy's ward

A 12 HP shield for 3 s. An enemy shot hits the warded ally a second in.

Mockup only: none of these looks is in the game yet. The game's current look is drawn from Game/LightbringerLook.cs.
