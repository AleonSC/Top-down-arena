Rapier: every hit counts

The Rapier is fast and narrow: a thrust every 0.28 s, 130 px long, 18 damage, always at the nearest enemy in front. Each hit in a row on the same enemy deals 10% more, up to +60% at six, and every enemy bullet the blade skewers on its line adds a stack too. Today that is a thin line and a flat flash, so you cannot see the streak you are building.

These three looks keep the numbers and make the streak visible: you see the thrust (a hand and arm that actually reach, a blade that flexes at full extension), and you see how many stacks you have without looking at a HUD. Switch the bullets and the second target on and off to see what each does.

    • With the +1 upgrade on, a thrust that hits two enemies runs the streak check once for each.
