The Squall

The Storm Herald's faction, the same size as the Warden's: 8 enemies, 4 squads, a set piece and a 10-wave Campaign cycle. A storm front that walks ahead of the Herald, and each enemy previews one of its attacks.

Members

The Warden's slot each one fills is in brackets. Weight and first round copy the Warden's table in Approaches.cs.

Squads

Four squads and the wave-4 set piece, like Breakout / Yard / Patrol / Cell block and the Riot line.

Campaign cycle

One lesson per wave, building to the Storm Herald.

#TeachesMix

Notes

  • Dust Devil, Squall Gunner, Grounder, Stormcaller and Pylons fade at the first boss after (Fade 0–1); Thunderhead and Ball Lightning stay two waves longer (Fade 2, like Thugs and Shivs).
  • Ball Lightning already exists (EnemyType.BallLightning, the Herald's summon). The faction version is the fix in Docs/Approaches.md: works with no Herald, slows and freezes skip it, its pop shots count as boss shots.
  • "Lightning Rod" is already an ability, so the paired posts are called Pylons. Their arc reuses the Herald's rod arc.
  • No arena-wide wind outside the fight: the Dust Devil's shove and the Squall Gunner's own drift stand in for the Gale.
  • Strikes, arcs and the Conductor's chain can reuse Herald.cs's warning and hit code at smaller numbers.
  • New EnemyType values would go at the end, before None, with a protocol bump.
