Gauntlets

A brawler's melee weapon. Fire throws a five-hit combo that ends in a ground slam. Parry cancels any punch's windup or recovery without losing your place in the combo, and a perfect parry jumps straight to a stronger finisher. Alt has three moves: a rocket dash, a charged ground pound, and a grab-and-throw.

Combo rules

  • Hold or tap Fire: each punch can start during the last one's recovery. The full chain takes about 1.8 s, roughly 60 damage per second.
  • 1.2 s without a punch resets the combo to the jab.
  • Raising parry during a windup or recovery cancels that punch. The combo stays where it was, unlike the Katana, which goes back to its first step.
  • A perfect parry fires the Counter Hammer on the next Fire press, from any step.
  • The five pips under the player show the combo step. They turn gold when the counter is ready.
