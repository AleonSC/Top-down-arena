Mockup · main menu

Tip box

The tip in its own box under the menu card, with a gap between them, instead of a tray pulled out of the card. Six looks. Each is 680 px wide like the card unless noted, and the tip changes every 6 s with a fade (B, C and F can be clicked to skip).

A · Plain box

Bulb, topic, text, a timer line along the bottom

The simplest: the card's own box style, two lines of text, and a thin line filling along the bottom edge until the next tip.

  • Good: matches the card exactly; the timer shows it will change
  • Watch out: nothing to click
B · Narrow, with arrows

Smaller than the card, centred, ‹ › on each side

560 px wide so it reads as a lighter box than the card. Round arrow buttons let you step through the tips.

  • Good: compact and easy to browse
  • Watch out: text is centred, so long tips look ragged
C · Picture and controls

A small drawing, the tip, and Prev / Next

Each tip gets a little picture on the left (the hitbox one here). The right side has Prev and Next buttons.

  • Good: the picture explains the tip at a glance
  • Watch out: needs a drawing per tip (the How to play page already has them)
D · Tab on the border

A TIP tab sits on the top edge, like Switch profile

The same trick as the Switch profile tab over the name card: a small label straddling the top border, and the text centred inside. Corner brackets echo the card's.

  • Good: looks like part of the menu's set
  • Watch out: the tab and brackets are extra drawing
E · Category

A coloured tag says what kind of tip it is

Basics, Combat, Co-op, Bosses or Items, in a colour, with a divider and the tip beside it.

  • Good: lets players spot the kind they still need
  • Watch out: needs a category on each tip
F · Two boxes

The tip, plus a separate Next button with a countdown ring

The tip box is a little shorter than the card and a square Next box sits beside it, with a ring that fills until the next tip.

  • Good: a big, obvious button to skip ahead
  • Watch out: two boxes, so more to look at

Notes for building it

  • The box is drawn after the card now (no hiding behind it), at MenuCard.yMax + 16. It fades out while the Join row is open.
  • It reuses Ui.BodyBox, so it matches the card. The change from the built tray is only its position, the gap and what's inside.
  • C and E need a little data per tip (a drawing, a category). The rest need nothing new. No protocol change.
