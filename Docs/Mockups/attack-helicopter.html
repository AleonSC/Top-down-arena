Soldier DPS: Attack Helicopter

Second ultimate (picked on the lobby's Ultimate row instead of Artillery Assault). F calls in a gunship that flies in, locks one enemy at a time and chews it up with a nose chaingun for 10 s, then leaves. Approximate look: browser canvas, not the game's draw code.

Orbit

Beats

  1. 0.0 sF: gold radio ring round you, callsign "VIPER 1-1 INBOUND".
  2. 0.0–0.9Flies in from off-screen, dropping from altitude (shadow tightens).
  3. +0.25 sRed brackets close on the target (lock), barrels spin up.
  4. 10 s20 rounds/s on one enemy until it dies, then the next nearest to you.
  5. endPulls up, climbs and flies off.

Proposed numbers

  • Cooldown70 s (Artillery Assault: 60 s)
  • Duration10 s on station, after a 0.9 s fly-in
  • Chaingun20 rounds/s × 8 = 160 dps, × your damage multiplier
  • TargetsNearest enemy to you within 560 px; one at a time
  • HelicopterCan't be hit; ignores walls (it's airborne)
  • CapstoneA second gun on the wing: two targets at once
