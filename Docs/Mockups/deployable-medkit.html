Deployable Medkit

With Soul Link gone from the Soldier kit, the Healer Soldier's third slot becomes a thrown medkit. Aim and throw it like the Cluster Grenade; where it lands it bursts into healing orbs that lie on the ground for a few seconds. Any player who walks over one picks it up and is healed (so allies have to move to it, and enemies can't be healed by it). Each scene loops the same moment: you at left, two hurt allies, your cursor between them. One orb is never picked up so you can see it expire.

Reduced motion is on: each scene shows one still frame.

Proposed numbers (same for every look)

  • Throw: 14 s cooldown. Flies to your cursor in 0.5 s (max reach 360 px, like the grenade's arc), landing where you aim.
  • Orbs: 6 healing orbs scatter within about 70 px of the landing spot. Walking over one heals 15 health. The kit itself does no healing and no damage.
  • Pickup: anyone can take them, including you. Within 40 px an orb drifts toward the nearest player, so grabbing one is easy. Each orb lasts 6 s and flickers for its last 1.5 s.
  • Downed allies: an orb that lands on or drifts to a downed ally picks them up at 20% health (a small revive, so the kit has a use in a crisis).
  • Capstone: 9 orbs, 20 health each.
  • Total if all are taken: 90 healing from 14 s of cooldown, about 6.4 a second, in bursts you have to position for. Mend Pulse is 60 on a 10 s cooldown for comparison.

Build notes

  • Needs Core/ work (a thrown projectile that spawns pickups, a new pickup kind replicated to clients) and a protocol bump. Nothing is built until you pick a look.
  • Pickups would reuse the Bone Spear pickup code path (TickSpearPickups) as a template.
  • Soul Link's removal from the Soldier also removes its tether drawing and the Soul Ghost; the lantern takes over the idea in the Soul Lantern mockup.
