Wind Clash

Four-step combo of air waves that hurt and shove: two crossing crescents, a gale lance, then a clash ring. Alt holds a storm of wind around you that shreds enemy bullets. Approximate: browser canvas, not the game's draw code.

Notes

  • Each wave hits an enemy once; shove points away from you (along the line for step 3).
  • Alt catches every enemy shot that enters: it spins round for 0.3 s, then bursts into a puff.
  • Alt is centred on you, unlike Aeroaegis (a vortex sent to an ally), so the two read apart.
  • Waves don't stop bullets; only the alt does.
