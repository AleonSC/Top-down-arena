Last Stand Rampart

A straight energy wall 400 px wide, planted 110 px ahead of you between two pylons. It blocks the whole lane in front of it, so teammates can spread out behind it instead of huddling. You stay behind the middle of it, holding the line.

It plants by itself 0.7 s in. Press Q or the button to cancel early, or let the fire wear you down. Move the pointer over the arena to aim. The three coloured dots are teammates; their shots pass straight through.

How it plays

  • Plant: two stakes drive in, the pylons rise and the wall unfolds outward from the middle in 0.5 s.
  • A shock band runs along the wall from every hit and the cell under it flares. The wall flickers when you are under 25% HP.
  • It turns with your cursor at 1.7 rad/s, like Aegis.

Why pick it

  • Biggest sheltered area in a lane fight, and teammates do not need to stack on you.
  • Easiest for teammates to read: a line they stay behind.
  • Reads as a different ability from Fortress and Shield Wall.

Watch out for

  • Only blocks the front. Enemies that walk round the pylons ignore it, and shots from the sides leak.
  • Not obviously you holding it: the wall is ahead, not on you.
  • A boss that targets the wall's end can shoot past the pylon.

Build notes

  • Knight.cs: AbilityId.LastStand keeps its enum value; its def (name, desc, no timer, cancel on second press) and the cast branch change. LastStanding(p) and its 1 HP clamp in Combat.cs go away, and so does the +50% in KnightDamageMult.
  • The 10% share is a damage redirect where the barrier consumes a shot: the shot is removed and the knight is hurt for a tenth of its damage. The Fortress dome and Shield Wall already stop shots, so the hit test has a precedent.
  • Rooting needs a planted flag on the player that blocks movement, dash and leap. Clients need it, and the barrier's shape and aim, so it is a snapshot change and a Session.ProtocolVersion bump (now 171).
  • The 400 HP in this mockup is a stand-in so the collapse can be seen. In the game the real value is the knight's own max HP; every number above scales with it.
  • This is a mockup only. Nothing in the game changes until one of the three is picked.
