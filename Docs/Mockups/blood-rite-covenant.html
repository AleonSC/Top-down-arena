Hemotank ultimate rework · replaces Blood Rite (75 s)

Bloodbound Covenant

The Hemotank draws a seal in blood and swears every enemy into it. Thorned chains lash out from the seal, and each enemy is crystallised inside its own binding ring. Nothing bound can move or fire until the oath ends.

Live preview

Starts on its own and loops. Move the mouse over the arena to walk the Hemotank; it wanders by itself otherwise. Bound enemies freeze where they stand, shooters keep their bullets hanging in the air.

The relic

What is signed in blood is paid in blood.

Occult look: a rotating sigil under the Hemotank, thorned bramble chains with a second strand winding round each one, and a gold seal ring plus red crystal shards on every held enemy. A broken oath bursts the seals.

Cast
The tendrils reach every enemy on screen in about 1.2 s, nearest first.
Duration
30 s, or until the Hemotank dies.
Cost
A fixed amount of HP per second for each enemy held. The preview uses 1.5, so 8 enemies cost 12 HP/s and 360 HP over the full 30 s. The preview Hemotank has 600 HP.
Output
The Hemotank can still move and attack, but deals 60% damage (placeholder).
Held enemies
Cannot move or fire. Bullets already in the air stop where they are.
Ends
At 30 s every tendril lets go and the enemies resume. If the Hemotank dies first, they are freed at once.
Cooldown
Starts when it ends. Kept at 75 s for now; tune after the drain is settled.
