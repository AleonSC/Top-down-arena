C1 · Full stat table

The tally becomes a table: kills, damage, damage taken, accuracy, healing and revives for each player, plus a squad total row and the run total (kills and time). The chip row on top is the team result for the wave. Damage taken is shown in amber when it is the highest, green at zero. Most of this is in the end-of-run stats already; it only needs a per-wave snapshot (the numbers at the start of the wave subtracted from now). The densest option, so it needs a 800 px wide box.

C2 · Progress per player

Fewer numbers, but each player's row shows what they gained: an XP bar with the new XP as a striped section, level badge, "to LV 7", and a gold LEVEL UP tag when the bar fills (the pick tag stays). The class sits under the name. The footer adds how far the next boss is. It answers "what did I get for that wave?", which is the question players ask at this moment. XP and levels are already replicated; the XP gained per wave is a new per-player number.

C3 · Tally and what's next

The tally on the left (compact: kills, damage, taken, a squad row) and a "Next: wave 5" panel on the right: the enemy mix by count in each enemy's colour, and a segmented road showing how far the run is from the next boss (the Approach system already knows this: ApproachLine). Good for players who like to plan, such as picking a defensive upgrade before a Brute wave. The wave list is known to the host; clients would need the next wave's mix sent with the Cleared state (a protocol bump).

C4 · Awards and ready-up

Less table, more story. One headline number per player (kills) and award tags for who did what: Most damage, Most kills, Most healing, Untouched, Revived Sam, plus the pick tag. On the right of each row, a ready tick: players can press Space to skip the wait once everyone is ready (2 of 3 here). The awards are computed from the per-wave counters; the ready-up is a new mechanic, a new message and a protocol bump, so it is the biggest change of the four. Awards alone could be added to any of the other looks.
