Ultimate Ideas

Two new ultimate ideas for every role and class, 36 in all, for the three locked ultimate slots in the lobby. Each loops in the arena: you are the ringed player on the left, your two allies sit behind you, enemies come from the right. Numbers are first guesses, sized against the current ultimates. A Wildcard would use the DPS ultimates as it does today.

Build notes. Mockup only, nothing built. Each idea would be a new AbilityId at the end of the enum (before None) and a protocol bump for its replicated clock, as Vein Tithe and Crimson Harvest did. The lobby already draws three locked slots beside the ultimate key, so a pick here would fill one of them; how a player chooses between ultimates (lobby pick, unlock by rank, or mastery) is still open.
