Per cast+3 senbon, up to 9 (3 casts). Cast is greyed out at 9

LifetimeNo timer: they stay between waves, lost only when you go down
Seek range170 px round you (faint ring, pulses on a launch)
Flight820 px/s, one launch every 0.12 s at most, two senbon per enemy at most

The three takes PICKED: C · KOI, NEW 4TH CHOICE, GREYED OUT AT 9

  • A · Halo: one ring that spreads out evenly as it fills (3, 6, 9 round you). Needles fly dead straight. Plainest to read.
  • B · Tiers: each cast adds its own ring of 3, the rings turning opposite ways. You can count your casts at a glance.
  • C · Koi: the needles school round you in a loose, breathing ring and curve in on the enemy with an ink tail. Most flair, a little busier.
White numbers = the needle hit. Green numbers and bubbles = poison. Green tint deepens with poison stacks.
