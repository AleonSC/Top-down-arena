WHAT IT DOES

A big field bursts out around you and moves with you. Every enemy inside, and any that walk in, is wired into the circuit: three conduits leave your hand and branch from enemy to enemy, drawn like Static Shock's.

Wired enemies are held stunned and shocked each pulse; the bolts thicken and whiten as the shock grows. Teammates in the field get a gold bolt and a heal each pulse.

TUNING

Pulse every 0.5 s. Bosses: shocked, not stunned. Approximate look: the bolts copy Static Shock's recipe (StaticShockLook.cs), but the game's draw code will differ a little.
