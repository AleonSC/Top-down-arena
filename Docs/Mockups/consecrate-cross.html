Healer Knight · Mockup

Consecrate, reworked

Instead of blessing the ground at your feet, you throw a Holy Cross. It leaves you in a straight line, grows as it travels, and heals and hastens every ally it passes through.

See it in motion

Click or tap the arena to aim and recast. The cross flies toward where you click.

How it plays

1. Cast

A short flash at your feet, then a small gold cross leaves you toward your cursor. It passes through allies and enemies without stopping.

2. Grow

The cross flies 480 px in 1.2 s. Its arms stretch from 36 px to 156 px and thicken as it goes, so it is easiest to catch an ally late in the flight.

3. Bless

Each ally the cross touches is healed once and gets Swiftness for 4 s: a cyan afterimage trail and faster movement. Hitting several allies at once is the point.

Open questions

  • Does it heal you? Mockup says no, so Lay on Hands and your weapon stay your self-heals. Half heal for the caster is an easy change.
  • Should it hurt enemies? The old zone burned them. A pure support cross is simpler, but a small 25 damage per enemy would keep some of that role.
  • Sacred Ground upgrade. Today it makes the zone 40% bigger and 3 s longer. It could become 40% longer range and 2 s more Swiftness.
  • Hallowed Blade capstone. The giant sword uses the same blessed ground. It should keep its own zone and not change.
  • Captain (Follow) version. The cross could split into three at 20 degrees apart.
