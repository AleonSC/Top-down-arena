Revolver & Mender's Bow animations

Both Healer sidearms draw very little today: the Revolver is a flat gun with a muzzle dot and a row of pips; the Bow is a ring that fills and a plain arrow. Below, each one is shown in the actions you actually do (tap, fan the hammer, reload; tap, full draw, perfect shot), in today's look and three alternatives, at the game's real size and timing. Hits, damage, rounds and timing don't change: this is drawing only.

Build notes

  • Drawing only: CharacterModels.cs (the gun and bow in hand), WorldShots.cs (bullets, arrows, the cylinder pips and bow ring) plus a new small look file like FrostLook.cs. Nothing in Core/ changes, so no protocol bump.
  • With Visual effects off the arena should draw exactly as now (the render check stays IDENTICAL); the new looks only show with effects on.
  • The Revolver's real numbers are used: tap every 0.45 s, fan after 0.2 s then a round every 0.07 s within 5° of aim, last round heavy, 6 rounds, 1.2 s reload, top-up one round every 0.25 s.
  • The Bow's real numbers are used: 0.15 s minimum draw, full draw 0.9 s, perfect shot = release within 0.25 s of full, tap arrow 8 damage and 5 heal, full 40 and 20, perfect 50 and 25. A fire-rate upgrade only shortens the draw.
  • Multiplayer: other players' guns and bows would animate the same from the data they already sync (DrawT, AmmoUsed, ReloadT); the recoil, casings and string wobble are cosmetic, driven by the local clock.
