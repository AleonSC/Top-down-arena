Two
Stances

The Katana's Alt no longer strikes; it switches stance. In Hayate every cut is a dash that comes back on a kill. In Senbon your swipes leave marks. Each stance fills its own bar, and switching with the bar full spends it: leave Hayate at 5 Flow and you chain through the nearest enemies; leave Senbon with 6 marks and every marked enemy falls as you sheathe.

Synthesized sounds. Only the arena you last used makes sound.

疾風

Hayate stance

Blade trailing low behind you · moon steel
  • Fire: dash-cut 150 px toward your aim in 0.1 s, untouchable, 28 to everything you pass.
  • 0.55 s cooldown, none if the dash killed; each killing dash adds a level of Flow (up to 5): +8% length, +4 damage.
  • Flow fades only while you are in Hayate (a level every 0.7 s after 2.2 s without a kill).
Leave at 5 Flow: Kage Chain. Time slows to 35% and you hop through the 6 nearest enemies, 70 each, then land in Senbon.
千本

Senbon stance

Blade forward, edge lit rose
  • Fire: swipes, 16 damage, 120° out to 120 px. Every enemy that survives one takes a mark (up to 3 each).
  • Marks stay on enemies while you are in Hayate, so you can come back for them.
  • The ring round you fills toward 6 marks.
Leave with 6+ marks: Zanshin. The world stops, you cut through every marked enemy and return; as you sheathe, 34 per mark, every enemy bullet cut. Then you land in Hayate.

The switch

  • Alt (Left Alt in the game). With the bar empty it's a 0.22 s flourish: the blade swings from one guard to the other and the stance's name flashes over your head.
  • With the bar full, the switch is the cash-out: no extra key, no meter to aim.
  • 0.4 s before you can switch again. The parry cancels a dash or a swipe, but not a chain or Zanshin already under way.

One full loop

  1. HayateDash through the grunts; each kill resets the dash. Five kills in a row: 5 Flow.
  2. AltKage Chain through six; land in Senbon, blade forward.
  3. SenbonSwipe the armored ones; marks pile up, the ring fills.
  4. AltZanshin: the field stops, every mark falls at the click. Back in Hayate.

Against the game: replaces swipe, swipe, lunge and the strike meter. New replicated state: the stance, Flow and marks per enemy (protocol bump). Kage Chain can ride the existing strike's path (DashKatana over Msg.WeaponDash, landed by the host like TickKatanaStrikes). Zanshin can't stop the world in co-op; on other screens only the marked enemies would freeze. Both cash-outs sit near the Ninja's own Shadow Step and Death Lotus, so those may want a look later.

Open questions

  1. Should a full bar always cash out on the switch, or should a tap switch and a hold cash out, so you can keep a full bar for later?
  2. Do marks fade after a while, or stay until the enemy dies?
  3. What should the +1 card do: a 7th Kage Chain hop and a 4th mark per enemy?
  4. Should a dash through a marked enemy in Hayate add to its marks, so the stances feed each other?
