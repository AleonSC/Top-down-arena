Mockup · main menu

Record and tip, together

You're right: the Best run line and the tip are one chip today, taking turns. Here the whole thing leaves the space under the title. A and F keep them as one thing, B to E show both at once. The card moves up a little in each, since the chip's gap is free. All of them use the real record line and the real tips.

A · Alternating shelf

Same chip logic, slides out from the bottom

One shelf rises from under the bottom edge, shows the record, crossfades to a tip, then slides away. Same "record, then tips" order as now.

  • Good: the least change from today; big readable text
  • Watch out: you only see one of the two at a time
B · Split shelf

Record on the left, tip on the right

The shelf is split in two. The gold record block stays while the tip on the right changes. Both are always visible.

  • Good: record never goes missing; clear who is who by colour
  • Watch out: the tip has less width, so long ones wrap
C · Corner pair

A gold record plate and a blue tip card

Two plates rise from under the bottom edge, one after the other: the record bottom left, the tip bottom right. They stay clear of the card.

  • Good: each has its own place and shape, easy to hide one later
  • Watch out: busiest of the set; two things moving
D · Pull-tab drawer

The tab shows the record, the drawer has both

The closed tab reads "Wave 26 · Tips". Click it (try it above): a drawer with the record on the left and the tip with Prev / Next on the right. It peeks open once.

  • Good: nothing in the way when closed, the record still shows
  • Watch out: tips are hidden until someone opens it
E · Tray under the card

Slides out from behind the main card

A tray hangs off the bottom of the card, as if it were pulled out of it. The record is the first line and the tip is the second, so they belong to the card.

  • Good: both together, close to the buttons, stays inside the card's width
  • Watch out: only two short lines, so long tips get cut
F · Ticker with a pinned record

Record fixed on the left, tips scroll

A thin strip along the bottom. The record is pinned at the left end and the tips run past to its right.

  • Good: always there, tiny, both visible
  • Watch out: scrolling text is easy to ignore

Notes for building it

  • The record line only shows once Profile.BestWave is above 0. In A, the record step would just be skipped for a new profile; in B to F the record block hides.
  • MenuTip() already gives topic, body and the record flag, so these mostly change where DrawMenuTipChip draws. No protocol change.
  • A, B, C and E slide in on the menu intro timer (MenuIntroK), as the chip does now.
