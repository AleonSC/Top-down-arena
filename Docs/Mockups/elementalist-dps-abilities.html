Meteor · Blizzard · Tsunami

Meteor and Blizzard keep their numbers and only get new visuals. Arcane Surge is replaced by Tsunami: a wave rolls out from where you stand in the direction you aim, nicks everything it crosses and leaves it wet. Wet enemies are slowed, and every lightning hit anywhere on the map arcs to every wet enemy (Arc Lightning, Storm Hammer, Chain Light, Nimbus), for a little extra damage. The wave itself barely hurts: the payoff is the lightning that follows. Pick one look per ability.

Tsunami

The loop: 1 the wave rolls out and soaks everything in its path; 2 wet enemies drip and crawl; 3 an Arc Lightning strip hits one dry enemy and the bolts jump to every wet enemy, near or far; 4 a Storm Hammer hit does the same on each of its pulses; 5 they dry out. The three enemies off the wave's line stay dry.
