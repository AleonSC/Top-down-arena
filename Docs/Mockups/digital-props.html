Digital crates and jars

Four ways to move the crate, heavy crate and clay pot away from wood and clay and toward something that reads as a construct. Each card shows the same six states: a crate at full, half and low health, a heavy crate, a pot, and the pot smashing (looping). The strip underneath draws the look you pick at game scale, with bullets, so you can judge it as it will actually be seen.

Barrels and the chest

The red barrel is a red ring with a hoop, an inner band and an amber hazard triangle; lit, it shakes, flashes and spits sparks as before (the fuse here loops slowly so you can watch it). The boss-fight health barrel is green with a glowing white cross. The treasure chest is a gold wireframe with brackets, bands and a diamond lock; opened, its lid lifts away as an outline and light pours up out of it.

Scale

What stays the same. Footprints, hit boxes, the drop-in and dust puff, the white hit flash and the shadow are untouched. Only the drawing inside DrawCrate and DrawPot in PropsLook.cs would change. Crates wear through damage in all four looks (the three crack stages become dropouts, dead cells or missing glyphs).

Readability. Enemy shots stop at crates and yours fly over, so cover has to stay obvious against bright bullets. A and D are the lightest on the eye; B and C are the most solid. Barrels and the chest follow look A in round two.

Visual effects setting. Reduced would drop the scanlines, glitch slices and blinking. Off would keep the current wood and clay.
