    The rules

    • Cost15% of your max health, paid up front like the Hemomancer's Space abilities. It never takes you below 1 HP, and at 1 HP it still goes off.
    • The poolFloods out from where you cast to 170 px over 0.9 s, stays 10 s and doesn't follow you.
    • WadingEnemies in it move 50% slower, Heavy enemies 35%, bosses 20% (Heavy enemies and bosses ignore other slows, not this one).
    • Bleed15 a second to everything in it, bosses too, every 0.5 s, scaled by your damage and Blood Price. It stops the moment they step out.
    • HealEach tick heals you 25% of what it dealt, wherever you are. That replaces the class's 3% on those ticks; it doesn't add to it.
    • GuardAllies standing in it, you included, take 20% less damage.
    • EndsAfter 10 s, or between waves. Going down doesn't end it; it just can't heal you while you're down.

    MasteredThe pool spreads to 220 px and gives back 35% of the bleed.

    In the lobby the Ultimate row

    ULTIMATE

    🔒 🔒

    Key 0 is the class's default and stays Vein Tithe. Key 1 holds a second ultimate where the class has one (so far the Tank Hemomancer's Bloodbath and the Tank Soldier's Juggernaut Suit); the other keys stay locked. The pick travels with you to the host, the other players, saved runs and the Practice Range. Bots use key 0, a Wildcard ignores it, and changing role or class puts it back to key 0.
