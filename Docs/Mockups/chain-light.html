Chain Light

Chain Light throws a 320 px cone of light (about 70° wide) every half second, and its 40 damage is shared between every enemy inside it. Today that's a flat flash and a bolt to each enemy. Below are four other ways it could look, in the arena at the game's scale. Change the number of enemies to see the damage split, and turn on Barrage for its extra cones.

Notes for building it

  • All drawing, on each screen: the cone is FxKind.LightCone (DrawLightConeFx, WorldEffects.cs) and the light to each enemy is FxKind.Bolt in the light's warm colour (DrawBoltFx, and DrawJuicyBolt with effects on). No protocol change: the effects already carry the cone's aim, angle and reach, and a bolt's two ends.
  • The damage lands at once on everything in the cone, as now. B's chain (links 40 ms apart) and C's wavefront (0.16 s out to the reach) light enemies up a moment after they're hit. If that reads as lag, they can play faster.
  • Only Chain Light's bolts change. Chain Lightning, the Storm Hammer's arcs and other bolts are a cooler blue (BoltShot) and keep their look.
  • The glows (the cone's light, beams, halos) go in the additive glow pass like the melee energy, so they also show, clipped, in the lobby's loadout preview.
  • With Visual effects off it draws exactly as now (render check IDENTICAL).
