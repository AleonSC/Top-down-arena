Bullet Rain

Bullet Rain takes over the Elementalist DPS ultimate slot from Meteor Storm (F, 70 s cooldown). A zone locks on at your cursor, you raise the staff, and magic rounds fall inside it for six seconds. Four ways to make it read as magic rather than gunfire. Nothing here is in the game yet, and the damage and rate numbers are placeholders.

All previews No look picked yet
LOOK A

Arcane Barrage

What you see

A rotating rune circle with a hexagram, glyphs round the rim and drifting sparkles. Violet bolts with a bright core fall through it. Each lands with a flare, a ring and a scorch of light.

Magic feel

The most classic spell circle. Reads instantly as a ritual, and the circle doubles as a clear edge for allies to avoid.

LOOK B

Starfall

What you see

The zone fills with twinkling stars. Slanted gold and pale blue shooting stars with long glittering tails fall in. Each lands as a starburst and leaves motes drifting up.

Magic feel

Soft and beautiful. The gentlest look, and the heaviest on glow, so it has the highest effect cost.

LOOK C

Elemental Volley

What you see

Three kinds of round fall mixed together: fire with ember tails, ice crystals that shatter into shards, and jagged lightning that arcs to the two nearest enemies on impact. Three coloured arcs mark the rim.

Magic feel

Ties into the rest of the class: the lightning fits Tsunami and its wet enemies. Easiest to read at a glance, because each element has its own colour and shape.

LOOK D

Rift Rain

What you see

A swirling rift opens above the zone, teal and pink. Ghost rounds with comet tails pour out of it in a cone, and each landing releases soul wisps that float up.

Magic feel

The strangest and most summoned-feeling look. The rift is a strong silhouette, and it is the only one where the rounds visibly come from one place.
