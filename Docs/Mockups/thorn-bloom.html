Thorn Bloom

Blood spikes burst out of you in every direction. Each one that lands whips a tendril to the next enemy, and that one chains on. It cuts you too, so it hits hard.

Click the arena to aim the spike ring and recast. HTML approximation; the real look would be drawn in Ui.cs.

Rules (proposed)

Slot
DPS ability (beside Arterial Spray, Blood Debt, Vein Rush)
Cost
12% max health, taken at the burst
Cooldown
8 s
Spikes
12, evenly round you, 240 px, stop on the first enemy
Spike hit
45 + a bleed stack
Tendril
to the nearest enemy not yet tendrilled, within 180 px: 30, then −20% a jump
Jumps
3 per spike (mastered: 5)
  • Point blank on a crowd: up to 12 spikes + 36 tendril hits.
  • Each enemy takes one tendril per cast, and never one if a needle struck it, so chains fan out instead of looping.
  • Several spikes can land on one big enemy (Heavy, boss).
  • Blood Price applies to both spikes and tendrils.
  • Your hits heal 3% as usual, so landing a lot pays some cost back.
  • Can't down you: the cost never takes you below 1 health.
