The slot, three looks

Where the line was

A Slim slot170 px slit

A thin machined slit under the title, with a bolt at one end and a status light at the other. The quietest option. After the disk is in, the light stays on in your rank colour as a small "signed in" mark.

Beats

  • 0.9 sThe disk lifts out of its clamps and flies to the slit, others dim
  • 2.0 sIt tips flat from its top edge, shutter first
  • 2.5 sIt slides in, the lip flashes, the light turns your colour and blinks
  • 3.3 sTitle writes on, then the arc, then the box
B Drive bay330 px housing

A wider housing that matches the shelf's hardware: status light, four read cells and a small readout underneath that goes from DRIVE A: EMPTY to DISK DETECTED, READING ANTHONY, then the name with READY. It tells the story of the load, so it is the busiest option.

Beats

  • 1.2 sLight pulses amber as the disk approaches
  • 2.5 sDisk slides in, readout switches to DISK DETECTED
  • 2.95 sFour read cells fill while the title types
  • 3.9 sReadout settles on the name
C Origin port52 px socket

Keeps the idea of the origin point of the axis. The disk shrinks all the way down into a small square socket exactly at the origin and the ring pulses once more. Closest to how the move works today, with the dot swapped for a real socket.

Beats

  • 0.9 sDisk shrinks as it flies to the origin
  • 2.0 sTips flat over the socket
  • 2.5 sDrops in, socket lights in your colour
  • 3.3 sTitle writes on, arc, box
The arc, three vector looks

From dashes to a path

1 Pen pathsolid 1.5 px

A smooth solid curve like one drawn with the pen tool: hollow square anchors at both ends and the peak, short handles with round knobs. The shot rides it with a solid tapered tail instead of a chain of dots.

2 Plotted curvefades at the ends

The arc as a function plot. It fades toward its ends, has ringed end nodes labelled x1 and x2, and a small f(x) label at the peak. The shot drops a thin plumb line to a baseline tick as it moves, so it reads as a point being plotted.

3 Tapered stroke0.6 to 3.8 px

A filled shape instead of a line: thin at the ends, fuller at the peak, with a soft glow under it, like a brush or variable-width stroke in a vector editor. The shot is a pointed comet that follows the curve. The most graphic of the three.

Building it

What changes in the game

  • DrawAxis (Menus.cs) goes. It is called from Menus.cs, ProfilesUi.cs, LobbyCrt.cs and LobbyEnter.cs, replaced in the picker and menu by one DrawDriveSlot.
  • DrawMenuArc and ArcAt are redrawn as the chosen vector style. The intro pen that traces the arc keeps working because the curve itself does not move.
  • DrawProfTrWho (ProfileTransition.cs) loses the plot-point dots and the shooting trails. The other disks fade with the shelf, the picked disk takes the flight, tip and insert. The clock keeps its length so the sound cues and menu intro stay in step, with new cues for the lift, the slot click and the read.
  • Switch profile plays it backwards: the disk pops out of the slot, tips up and drops back into its clamps.
  • No network or snapshot change, so the protocol version stays as it is.
