Blood Wall

A toggle. Press it and you bleed a wall behind you wherever you walk: it stops enemy bullets and enemies can't cross it, while you and your allies walk straight through. It drains your health, and the drain doubles every few seconds. A healer can keep you going longer, but the ramp always wins. Press again to stop, or ride it until it takes you down.

The tank walks a figure eight on their own; hold the mouse (or a finger) on the arena to lead them. Space or the button toggles the wall. The ring round the tank and the number over them are the drain, heating through the four stages below. Untick the healer to see how much sooner the ramp takes you down alone.

Blood Wall

"Every step a brick, every brick a pint. Walk until the floor runs dry."

Tank Hemomancer, ability slot 2 (where Crimson Pact is). Proposed numbers, all tunable:

Press
Toggles it on. No upfront cost. Press again to stop.
Wall
Laid behind you as you walk, a 12 px thick line along your path. Standing still lays nothing. Each piece stands 6 s, then crumbles.
Blocks
Every enemy bullet, red aimed shots included. Enemies can't walk through it. You and allies pass freely, and your shots fly over it.
Drain
1% of your max health a second, doubling every 4 s: 2% at 4 s, 4% at 8 s, 8% at 12 s, 16% at 16 s. No hit grace, and it can take you down.
Healer
Any healing still lands, so a healer stretches it. A steady 4% a second carries you from about 17 s to about 20 s; nothing outheals 32% a second.
Ends
On a second press, or when you go down. The wall you laid stays up for the rest of its 6 s either way.
Cooldown
8 s, starting when it ends.
Upgrade
Blood Oath (rare, 2 stacks): the wall stands 2 s longer and the drain doubles 1 s slower.
Capstone
When a piece of wall crumbles it bursts into spikes: 20 to enemies touching it.

Open questions

  1. Which look? A Rampart, B Thornvein or C Garnet (or a mix, like the Rampart's ridge with Garnet's crystal crumble).
  2. Does the wall wear down? Proposed: no, it just times out. Alternative: each bullet stopped chips the piece it hit, and a piece breaks after 6 hits.
  3. Should enemies that walk into it take damage? Proposed: no (the capstone's spikes cover that). Alternative: 4 a second while pressed against it.
  4. The ramp. 1%/s doubling every 4 s takes a full-health tank down in about 17 s alone. Shorter (doubling every 3 s, about 13 s) or longer?
  5. Taunt. Crimson Pact made every enemy target you. Should the wall keep a weaker taunt (enemies within 200 px go for you while it's up), or is the wall enough?
  6. Bots. Proposed: a bot tank turns it on when 3+ enemies are near and off at 40% health.

Today's Crimson Pact: pay 10% of your max health, every enemy targets you for 4 s, then the damage you took bursts back out at 150% within 220 px (Blood Oath: 50% harder, 30% farther; capstone: 30% less damage while it lasts). The AbilityId.CrimsonPact slot would be kept, as with the other Hemomancer reworks.
