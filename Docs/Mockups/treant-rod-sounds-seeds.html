Treant Rod Sounds and Seeds

Part 1 lets you hear three options for each Treant Rod sound: the root lash, the new seed shot, and the seed landing. Part 2 shows three looks for the tiny seeds your Treant fires at enemies.

Browser playback here is a quick port of the kind of synthesis SoundBank uses, so it shows the character of each option. Once you pick, I tune it in SoundBank and check it with Tools/SoundCheck/render.sh.

Part 1: Sounds

The lash is Sfx.SwingLight today (the same swish as other light melee). The seed sounds are new, so they need to be quiet: seeds fire about 2.5 times a second all wave.

Part 2: Seeds

A small tree stands in the middle and flicks seeds at the nearest enemy. Switch looks to compare. The root lash fires too, so you can see the seeds next to it. Reduced effects drops the trail and the hit sparks in every look.

Proposed numbers

  • Damage 3 per seed, every 0.4 s, so about 7.5 damage a second. The Treant's slam is 15 every 1.2 s, and the Wisp shoots 7 every 0.5 s, so seeds stay a side job.
  • Range 320 px, speed 520 px/s, nearest enemy, fires only while a wave is fighting. Seeds are blocked by walls like other player bullets.
  • Damage uses your damage multiplier. The Treant growing 30% with Extra projectiles also makes the seed 30% bigger; it does not add more seeds.
  • Look A reuses the existing minion shot with a new colour, so no protocol change. Looks B and C need a new bullet kind, which means a Session.ProtocolVersion bump.
