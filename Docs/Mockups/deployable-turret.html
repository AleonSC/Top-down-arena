How it plays

  • Cast: crate drops at your feet, unfolds in 0.5 s, you mount automatically.
  • Mounted: you are rooted, the turret turns toward the cursor and takes hits for you.
  • Hold fire to spool the barrels up. Releasing winds them down.
  • Press the key again to dismount early.
  • At 0 s or 0 HP it overheats and detonates (160 px, 80 damage).

Fire visuals

  • Alternating twin barrels with recoil, muzzle star and ground light flicker.
  • Gold tracers, hit sparks, brass casings, smoke trail.
  • Barrels heat from steel to orange to white.
  • Overdrive at full spool: shock rings, screen-edge glow, harder shake.
  • Kills leave scorch marks. Brutes pop and chain-damage neighbours.
