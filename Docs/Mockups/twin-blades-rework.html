Twin Blades: a five-step chain

Today the Twin Blades swing a 90° slash every 0.16 s for 15, left and right in turn, and the Knight's model holds one short sword that never moves. Ten hits in a row prime a dash-strike on the weapon-action key.

These three reworks keep the weapon fast and close (about 75 px reach) and give it a five-step chain plus an alt attack on the weapon-action key. The Knight holds a sword in each hand and drives them through every move the way the Halberd mockup does: wind-up, strike, follow-through, back to guard. Pick one, watch it loop, or switch to Try it and play it.

Damage a second assumes every hit of the chain lands on one enemy, clicking as early as each step allows. Today's figure is 15 every 0.16 s, without the dash-strike.

What stays

  • Reach stays close (the sword tips sit about 75 px out), and the first two steps are as fast as today's swings.
  • Shot upgrades work as on every melee weapon: damage, swing speed, crits, lifesteal and the elemental rounds apply to every hit, including thrusts, waves and shock rings.
  • The parry's animation cancel sends the chain back to step 1, as it does for the Katana and Bo Staff.

What changes

  • The 10-hits-in-a-row priming goes. The alt attack runs on its own cooldown, and the chain is the reward for keeping up the rhythm.
  • +1 cut becomes: every slash strikes once more for 60%, thrusts reach 20 px further, and the finisher gets a bigger area.
  • Five pips over your head show where you are in the chain; the fifth glows when the next click is the finisher. A thin ring under you fills as the alt attack comes back.

Build notes

  • A TwinBladesMotion like HalberdMotion: each step is a few key poses for both hands and the body, and the host times each hit from them.
  • The chain step and alt cooldown go in the player snapshot (the Combo fields can be reused), so the protocol version goes up.
  • The Bestiary and Armory pages are not touched by this mockup.
