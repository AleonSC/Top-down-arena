Effects

Where it shows. Everywhere a player's name is drawn in Ui.Label with the name text: the party card, the Hangout plate, the world tag over a player (Hud.cs) and the results strip. Chat and the pause menu's player list stay plain so they read at a glance.

Readability. Every effect keeps the letters solid and high-contrast; glows and outlines are thin, and the moving ones (shimmer, glitch, fire) are limited to a small area so a name never gets hard to read. Names longer than 12 letters just stretch the effect.

Cost. Per-letter fills and a few discs from the clock, like FrameLooks.cs. With Visual effects off each one is its still base colours. In a match the tag is small (12 px), so the motion effects tone down to colour and glow only.

Wiring (if picked). CosmeticKind.NameEffect at the end of the enum, a Rank.cs row each, one more byte on LobbyEntry and in Msg.Cosmetics (protocol bump), a DrawName helper the four places call instead of Ui.Label, and a section in the Cosmetics drawer.
