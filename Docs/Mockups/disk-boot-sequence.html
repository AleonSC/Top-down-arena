Disk boot sequence

You pick a disk in Who's playing?. It travels up to the drive in the monitor's chin and slides into the slot. The drive reads it while the monitor retypes the title. Then the main menu's case drops out from under the monitor, lands, and powers on. The sequence is 4.8 seconds; scrub the bar or slow it down to study each beat. This is an approximate browser mockup: glows and type will differ in the game.

Your system asks for reduced motion, so it won't autoplay. Press Play or scrub the bar.
