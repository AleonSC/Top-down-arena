Fix

      The new firing animation, beat by beat (same for A, B and C)

      WhenWhat you seeWhy
      0 – 0.04 sA flash on each real muzzle, turned along that gun's barrel: a hot core, a 4-point star (one of three shapes so it never repeats exactly), two short puffs out of the muzzle brake's side ports, and a warm pool of light on the floor.The flash rides the gun, so the stream visibly comes out of it.
      0 – 0.08 sEach gun slides back 3 px along its own barrel and the muzzle climbs 6° outward, then springs back with a small overshoot. The arm bends with it, and the body jolts back 0.6 px.Recoil follows the real barrel angle instead of the aim, and reads as two separate guns.
      0 – 0.03 sA glint at the ejection port, then a brass casing flips out of the outer side, lands, bounces once, slides and fades after 1 s.Floor brass builds up while you hold fire, so the ground tells you how long you've been shooting.
      held fireThe barrels heat from the muzzle back: steel, then orange, then white at the tip. Past half heat a shimmer of hot air rises off them.Kept from today's heat, now visible as a gradient.
      after you stopSmoke curls out of both muzzles for up to a second, more the hotter they were.A small payoff for a long burst.
      0 – 0.03 sThe round's first frame is a bright streak from muzzle to round, then the normal tracer.Joins gun and round in the eye.

      What each fix changes. A moves the spawn point to the drawn muzzle (34 px ahead instead of 12, still ±30 px apart), so Weapons.cs fires from there, WeaponRange adds the 22 px and TwinTubeMuzzle moves to the tubes. The streams and crossfire are unchanged; rounds start 22 px further out. B does the same and also narrows TwinBarrelGap from 60 to 36. That is a balance change: up close the streams are less likely to pass either side of a small enemy. C changes drawing only: hitboxes start where they do today, and the tracer is drawn from the gun and slides onto its real line in 0.06 s (about 50 px of flight). Still open: with the "+1 barrel" upgrade the middle stream has no gun under it. One idea is that each rifle gets a second barrel.
