Host to Lobby Mockups

Three ways to go from pressing Host game on the main menu to the lobby, each a little over a second. Today the screen just swaps. The menu's animated background keeps running underneath in all three, since both screens share it. Each loop shows the menu, the press, the move, and the lobby settling.

Option AThe card becomes your loadout

  • The menu card empties and grows into the loadout panel, its plot corners riding out with it to the panel's corners.
  • The title shrinks and flies to the top left, turning into LOBBY on the way, with its x-axis underneath.
  • The party column, tabs and bottom bar slide in around the panel; your party card lands last with a ring and its HOST badge pops.

Option BThe axis opens

  • The x-axis under the title brightens and stretches across the whole screen.
  • It splits into two lines, one sweeping up and one down, with sparks and tick marks. Between them the lobby is already there; outside them the menu is still showing.
  • The most "Cartesian" of the three: the axis the menu is built on opens the way into the lobby.

Option CThe launch in reverse

  • The menu card drops away and the title lifts off the top; a ripple runs out across the grid from the Host button.
  • The lobby's panels slide in from their nearest edges with a small overshoot: the party column from the left, the loadout from the right, the tabs from above, the bottom bar from below.
  • The mirror image of the run launch, where these same panels slide out to the sides, so going into the lobby and leaving it feel like one pair.
