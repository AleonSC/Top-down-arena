Mockup · main menu

Tips only

No record line, so the tip has the space to itself. Six placements on the main menu. They all play on a loop with the real tips from How to play. B is clickable.

A · Bottom shelf

Slides up from under the bottom edge

A full-width bar with a small picture, the tip in big text and a dot for each tip. It holds for about 8 s, slides away, and comes back with the next tip.

  • Good: readable, has room for a picture, nothing near the card
  • Watch out: it moves a lot
B · Pull-tab drawer

A TIPS tab you open yourself

Click the tab above. A drawer slides up with a picture, the tip and Prev / Next. It peeks open once by itself.

  • Good: never in the way, and you can read as many as you like
  • Watch out: people who never click it never see a tip
C · Chip with a timer

Today's chip, with arrows and a countdown ring

The same spot and shape, but with Prev / Next arrows and a ring that fills until the next tip. Hover would pause the ring.

  • Good: almost no change to build, and you can skip ahead
  • Watch out: one line only, so long tips get cut
D · Tray under the card

Pulled out from behind the main card

A tray slides down out of the bottom of the card, with the tip on up to two lines. It reads as part of the menu card.

  • Good: right next to the buttons, same width as the card
  • Watch out: pushes into the space under the card; fine here, tight on small screens
E · Caption

No box at all, like a film subtitle

The tip fades in over a soft dark floor at the bottom, with a small TIP label above it, then fades out. No panel, nothing to dismiss.

  • Good: the quietest option, looks part of the scene
  • Watch out: no way to go back or skip
F · Side card

Slides out from the left edge

A card comes in from the left edge with a small handle, so it can be pulled back out later. The tip wraps to a few lines.

  • Good: uses the empty side space, leaves the bottom clear
  • Watch out: sits near the left edge on narrow windows

Notes for building it

  • MenuTip() would drop the record branch and just rotate HowToTips; Profile.BestWave is no longer read here.
  • With the chip gone from under the title, the card can move up (A, B, E and F) or stay (C and D).
  • No protocol change.
