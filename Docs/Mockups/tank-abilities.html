Tank Soldier: Roar and Bulldoze

Animation mockup. Flip each stage between what the game draws today and the proposed version. Both play on the same scene, so the difference is only the effects. Slow-mo helps read the beats.

Roar 14 s cooldown · taunt 5 s · 40% less damage

Today: one orange ring, then a thin pulsing arc for five seconds. Proposed: a wind-up, a layered shockwave that visibly turns enemies, and a plated shield that counts down the taunt.

Proposed beats

  1. 0.00–0.20 sWind-up. Soldier swells, red sparks are sucked in. Telegraphs the cast.
  2. 0.20 sRelease. Body slams to 125%, screen edge flashes red, camera kicks.
  3. 0.20–0.90 sThree staggered rings plus a filled blast disc and 28 speed streaks.
  4. as wave hitsEach enemy flashes, pops a "!" and swings round to face the soldier.
  5. 0.20–5.20 sSix armour plates orbit the soldier. A rim arc drains as the taunt runs out. Dashed tethers show who is aggroed. A soft pulse goes out every second.
  6. last 1 sPlates flicker so the player knows the 40% damage cut is about to end.

What it fixes

  • Today nothing shows that enemies actually turned. Now each one reacts when the wave reaches it.
  • The old arc says "taunted" but not for how long. The draining rim does.
  • The damage reduction has no visual. The plates are it.
  • The cast has no weight. The wind-up and shake give a tank-sized hit.

Bulldoze 9 s cooldown · 60 damage · 0.35 s charge · invulnerable

Today: the soldier slides forward and a small ring appears at the destination the moment you cast. Proposed: a lane telegraph, a bow wave that plows through, and a stomp where the charge ends. The big enemy in the lane is heavy, so it takes the hit but does not move.

Proposed beats

  1. 0.00–0.12 sLane telegraph with moving chevrons. Soldier crouches back and kicks dust.
  2. 0.12–0.47 sCharge. Afterimages, speed streaks, dust off both heels, skid marks that fade over 1.6 s, and a shield flicker for the invulnerable window.
  3. on contactEnemy flashes white, shows "60", spins off sideways with a streak. A 50 ms hit-stop sells the weight. Heavy enemies throw sparks and stay put.
  4. 0.47 sStomp at the end point: double ring and a ring of dust, small camera kick.

What it fixes

  • The ring now lands when the charge lands, not at the cast.
  • The path is readable before and after the charge, so players learn the 60 px sweep width.
  • Hits have feedback beyond the slide: flash, number, hit-stop.
  • Heavy enemies visibly resist, which explains why they do not move.

Build notes. The "Current" view is reconstructed from Abilities.cs and WorldRenderer.DrawPlayerLinks, so treat it as approximate. Roar's plates, tethers and wind-up read TauntT, which is already synced, so they are drawing-only. The Bulldoze trail, hit sparks and end stomp need either a client-side check of the soldier's dash (as Ambience.cs does) or a new FxKind at the end of the enum, which would bump Session.ProtocolVersion. Nothing here changes gameplay numbers.
