Arcane Missiles → elemental

Arcane Missiles today are purple rocket-shaped bolts. The mechanics stay exactly as they are: each cast conjures 3 into orbit round you (cap 6), they launch one every 0.08 s at the nearest enemy within 300 px, homing, and with the orbit full a cast flies at your aim. Below are five elements for them, drawn at the real orbit radius, launch rate, speed and homing, with today's look for comparison. Fire and lightning already belong to the Fireball and Arc Lightning, so the leading ideas are frost (three takes) and a stone one that gives the Elementalist's DPS trio three different elements.

Notes for building it

  • Drawing only. The shot stays Defs.ArcaneShot / WeaponId.ArcaneMissiles, so damage, homing (MissileTurn), orbit and launch timing in Core/KitWeapons.cs are untouched and there is no protocol change. The looks live in Game/ShotLooks.cs (the missile and its trail), Game/WorldRenderer.cs (the orbit) and Game/CharacterModels.cs (the staff icon).
  • If you pick one, the name, Desc and README row change with it ("Ice Shards", "conjures 3 shards into orbit..."), and Defs.BulletColor(ArcaneShot) moves from violet to the element's colour so the hit sparks and damage numbers match. A rename of the enum value is optional: WeaponId.ArcaneMissiles can stay as the internal id to avoid touching saves, tests and the Armory build.
  • The frost looks (A–C) are purely visual: they do not slow enemies, because that would be a mechanics change. If you want a chill on hit later, that is a separate decision (Frost Lance already owns slowing).
  • Per-shot sparkle trails are cheap particles like the existing arcane smoke (EmitFromShot). With Visual effects off, the arena should draw exactly as now (the render check stays IDENTICAL).
  • Not done here, on purpose: the Armory and Bestiary reference pages stay as they are until you ask for them to be updated.
