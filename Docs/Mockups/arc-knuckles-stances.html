Arc Knuckles: Brawl & Rocket

Two stances on one weapon. Brawl is a seven-move combo where every punch sends the enemies the way your movement keys point and every dash goes there too. Rocket is today's dash chain as the default Fire combo, with no timing windows; the parry still cancels it. Play it below or watch a demo.

WASD move and steer Click an enemy: next move Hold on move 7: charge Q swap stance (Alt in the game) Space / Right-click parry

Stance 1Brawl

Damage is a multiple of the weapon's 22. The steer direction is the movement keys at the moment you press; with no key held, a punch carries on along the line from you to the target and a dash goes toward the cursor.

Shared rules

  • Swap stance on the weapon-action key (Left Alt in the game, Q here). It's free now that the Rocket Dash lives in the Rocket stance. Swapping resets both combos.
  • Parry cancels the current move in either stance and resets that stance to move 1.
  • Perfect parry arms the gold Counter Hammer, as today.
  • Wall slam: an enemy knocked into a wall fast takes ×0.5 and is stunned briefly. It rewards steering groups into walls.
  • Pips under the arena show the stance and the next move; blue for Brawl, ember for Rocket.
