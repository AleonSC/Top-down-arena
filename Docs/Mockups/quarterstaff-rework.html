Quarterstaff Rework

The Healer Knight's staff becomes a two-handed weapon held like the Halberd. The primary button alternates a left swipe and a right swipe. Every swipe that connects stores a Jab stack, up to 3. The alt fire spends stacks on a cone-shaped jab. Multishot raises the stack limit.

All three staff models are new. All numbers are proposed starting values and none of this is in code yet.

Jab stacks

Each swipe that hits at least one enemy adds one stack, however many it hits. Stacks show on the staff and as pips behind you. They fall off 6 s after the last connecting swipe.

Open questions

  • Is a stack per connecting swipe right, or should it be one per enemy hit? Per enemy fills the bar in one swipe against a crowd.
  • Charges (one jab per stack) or Power (one big jab for all stacks)? The stage shows both. I lean Charges because it keeps the alternating rhythm.
  • Does Multishot only raise the limit, or also add a stack per swipe? Only the limit keeps +0 and +2 filling at the same speed.
  • Which look: Oak and Brass (plain, sockets), Pilgrim's Ash (cloth and prayer beads) or Beacon (iron, with a crystal head that lights)?
  • Should the 6 s decay exist, or do stacks last until spent?
