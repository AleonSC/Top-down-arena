Tank Soldier: Fortress dome

Rework: the ult drops a fixed, fully impenetrable dome (radius 240 px, arena scale) on the player's spot. It stays put. Only players and player bullets pass through the shell; enemies, enemy bullets, boss attacks and lasers cannot enter or shoot through. Pick a look. The simulation loops: enemies (red) press on the wall, enemy shots (orange) burst on it, your shots (blue) pass out.
