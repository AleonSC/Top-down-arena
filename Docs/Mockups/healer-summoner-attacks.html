Healer Summoner attacks

All three Healer Summoner weapons fire the same small green bolt, so they feel like one weapon with three names. Below are three new attacks for each, built so each weapon keeps what it does for the party. Every scene runs the attack on a loop at the game's scale.

Weapon 1 of 3

Sprite Staff

Today: seeds (9 damage, every 0.35 s) stick in an enemy for 4 s. Two Sprites fly to whoever is hurt and heal them. Killing a seeded enemy frees another Sprite for 6 s, up to 4 more.

Keep: seeding on every hit, so a kill still frees a Sprite. Upgrades stay as they are (+1 Sprite).

Weapon 2 of 3

Treant Rod

Today: bolts (8 damage, every 0.3 s) that heal an ally they pass through. Your Treant roots where it stands and blocks bullets. A vine from you to it heals allies crossing it (8 a second) and slows and stings enemies (50%, 6 a second).

Keep: the attack heals allies it touches, and the Treant and vine are untouched. The ally in each scene is there to show that. Every few shots the weapon aims at them.

Weapon 3 of 3

Totem Staff

Today: seeds (9 damage, every 0.35 s). Every 10 s your next shot also throws a healing totem to your cursor (up to 400 px). Six seed hits on enemies near a totem make it pulse 25 healing to allies near it.

Keep: the thrown totem, and every attack hit on an enemy within 150 px of a totem counting as a charge. The totem in each scene shows its charge as six pips and its 150 px reach as the faint ring.

Build notes

  • Numbers are a starting point, not measured. Each attack is sized to about 26 damage a second on one target, which is what the three weapons do now. Build one, then tune it with the DPS meter and a target dummy (F1 → DPS) until the lobby's Damage stat reads about the same.
  • Upgrades don't need new work. On these three weapons Multishot and Barrage already add utility (+1 Sprite, more totems, a bigger Treant) and not extra bullets, so no attack has to handle extra projectiles. Damage, fire-rate, crit and mod cards apply to whatever the attack hits.
  • Where the utility hooks in. Seeding and charging are called from a seed hitting an enemy (SeedHit), and the Treant's heal comes from a bullet's HealAlly. Each new attack calls the same functions for every enemy or ally it touches, so the Sprite, Treant and totem code stays as it is.
  • Network. Anything drawn that isn't a plain bullet needs a new effect or bullet kind, which means a protocol version bump. New values go at the end of their enums.
  • Before building: the lobby preview, the Armory text and the Bestiary descriptions for these weapons will be out of date after the change. The reference pages stay as they are until you ask for them.
