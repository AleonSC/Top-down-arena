Kinetic Wall

Space: a wall appears in front of you. Enemy bullets that touch it slow to a halt and hang in it, each one charging the wall. Space again (or when it expires): the wall unleashes everything in a big cone forward. Damage grows with the charge.

Approximate: browser glow and blur stand in for the game's additive draws. Pick a look, then I'll build it with real draw code and send Tools/Preview shots.

—

A · Stasis Pane flat slab

Straight cyan slab with hard edges. Bullets freeze in place and glow brighter as the charge climbs. Release: a wedge of light, frozen bullets flung out with it.

B · Gravity Lens curved arc

An arc bending around you; bullets spiral to a stop and orbit slowly inside the lens. Release: the lens snaps flat and a ripple cone rolls outward.

C · Kinetic Grid hex cells

A row of hex cells; each cell that catches a bullet lights up (cells = charge readout). Release: every lit cell fires a beam, merging into the cone.
