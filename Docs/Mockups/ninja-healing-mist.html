Healing Mist

Today a mist follows the ninja for 6 seconds and sheds a drop of dew twice a second. The rework turns it into a sprint. The ninja runs faster for a few seconds and leaves a trail behind. Anyone who steps into the trail heals for as long as they stand in it. Three looks for the trail; the numbers are the same in each.

Beat by beat

  1. 0.0 sThe ninja walks. Two allies are hurt (45 and 30 HP) and standing off to the side.
  2. 0.5 sCast. A ring of mist, and the ninja speeds up by 45%. Speed lines trail the body.
  3. 0.5 – 4.5 sEvery 0.15 s the ninja drops a patch of mist on the ground. Together the patches make a continuous trail. (Today: dew drops fall inside a mist that follows you.)
  4. 2.4 – 4.4 sBoth allies walk into the trail. Each heals 12 HP a second while standing in it, and the ninja heals too while the trail is under its feet.
  5. 4.5 sThe sprint ends and the ninja drops back to normal speed. The trail stays.
  6. 5 s after each patchThat patch fades. The oldest part of the trail goes first, so the trail shortens from the tail.
