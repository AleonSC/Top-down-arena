Bastion

A kite shield. It always guards your front half. Left click chains three shield strikes; hold Alt to brace, soak bullets into Ram, then left click to bash.

WASD move · mouse aim · LMB chain / bash · hold RMB or Shift brace

The kit

Guard

always on

The shield covers your front 180°. Every bullet that hits it is blocked, red aimed shots included. Your back stays open.

Shield Chain

LMB · LMB · LMB

Rim Cut, Backhand, then Boss Slam: a thrust of the boss that shoves 200 px and stuns.

Brace

hold Alt

Plant the shield: slower, guard widens to 220°. Bullets are soaked into the boss and fill 5 Ram pips.

Shield Bash

LMB while braced

Ram sets the dash, damage and stun. At full Ram the stored shots burst out as steel shards.
