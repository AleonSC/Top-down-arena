Bo Staff: a staff you can see being swung

Today the Ninja tank's Bo Staff appears as a flat line and a glow over the hit, with no hands on it and no pose before or after. These animations keep every number (the 130° slash, the 140 px jab, the full spin, the 100 px shove) and change what you see: the Ninja holds the staff in two gloved hands, winds it back, swings it through with the grip sliding, follows through, and brings it back to a ready carry.

Frame by frame

What moves

  • Two hands stay on the staff. The rear hand pushes, the front hand guides, each joined to a shoulder in your colour. The shoulders turn with the swing.
  • The grip slides. The staff is carried short, runs forward through the hands as it is driven out, and comes back to the carry afterwards.
  • Slash: a quick draw-back behind the shoulder, then the sweep across the 130° cone with your body leaning into it, then a follow-through that settles.
  • Jab: the staff squares up to your aim, pulls back to the hip, then thrusts 140 px with a 12 px step, a shock ring at the tip and speed lines behind you.
  • Spin: a coil to the side, then your whole body turns with the staff for one full circle. A ring lands on the floor at the end of the spin, then a short recovery.
  • Shove: the staff turns crosswise in both hands, is drawn in, driven out as a bar with pressure arcs rolling off the front, held, then drawn back.
  • Enemy bullets (amber) are cut where the staff meets them, with a small spark.

Kept as it is

  • Slash 16 damage, 130° cone, 105 px. Jab 22, 140 px line, 28 px wide. Spin 30, 100 px. Shove 6, 100 px wide, 78 px deep, pushes 70 px.
  • Step times 0.32, 0.30 and 0.80 s (the spin includes its recovery); the shove lands 0.1 s after the press.
  • The chain has no timer, and the three pips over your head are unchanged.
  • Client-side drawing only. Session.ProtocolVersion stays.

Needs your call

  • Look. Sage ribbon is one soft energy sweep that matches the game's glowing melee trail. Afterimages leaves three fading copies of the staff, which shows the staff itself better in a crowd.
  • Spin turns the body. In the mockup the model turns a full circle with the staff. In the game that would be drawn only; your aim stays where it is.
  • Arms in the shove. The bar ends 72 px out, so the arms are fully stretched at the front. A shorter drive would look more natural but would change the push.

Not in the mockup

  • Hit-stop and screen shake (the other melee looks have them; the same values would apply).
  • The Alt strike's sounds and the upgrade (+20 px reach) look.
