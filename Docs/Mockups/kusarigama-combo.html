Kusarigama: sickle, then chain

Proposed remake for the Tank Ninja's Kusarigama. The basic attack becomes a two-piece combo: a fast sickle cut, then the iron weight thrown down the lane, which drags its target back to you. The weapon-action key becomes Whirl & Drag: hold to spin the weight in a circle around you and pull every enemy nearby into the chain.

Both stages are live. Move the pointer over a stage to aim, click to attack, or leave Auto on. Nothing in the game has changed yet.

Basic attack: cut, then throw

Piece one is a 144° sickle cut that leaves a lilac crescent hanging in the air while the weight whips behind you. Press again inside 0.7 s for piece two: the weight spins overhead, flies out 280 px, bites the first enemy in the lane and hauls it back to your feet, stunned. The cut then lands on it.

Two pips over the Ninja show the step. Pointer aims; with no pointer it aims at the nearest enemy.

Weapon action: Whirl & Drag

Hold Left Alt. The weight winds up to speed in 0.35 s and circles you, its radius growing from 70 to 150 px. Anything within 200 px is dragged toward you in a slow swirl, and the spinning chain cuts everything it crosses. Let go (or run out of the 3 s) and the chain snaps in: a shockwave knocks the crowd back and stuns it.

The ring around the Ninja fills as the 3 s whirl runs out. In the demo the lockout after release is cut to 1.2 s.

What changes from today

  • The throw no longer needs a second key press to be useful. Its own return drags the target in, so the hook-and-reel on Left Alt goes away.
  • The stunning reel slash (52 damage) is replaced by the cut, which lands on the dragged enemy.
  • Left Alt now does the whirl for every Kusarigama owner, with no hook needed.

How it would be drawn

  • Cut: a tapered lilac ribbon from handle to blade tip that hangs 0.4 s, the same glassy crescent the Greatsword uses.
  • Throw: the weight whirls once, streaks with a glow, and the chain draws taut with a dashed pattern. Impact gets a ring, sparks and a 0.05 s hit-stop.
  • Whirl: a speed-blur arc behind the weight, a dashed pull ring that rotates, and inward chevrons that show the pull.

Questions for you

  • Should the spinning chain cut small enemy bullets, like the Bo Staff? Easy to add.
