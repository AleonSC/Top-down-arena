HUD modes

Three modes on H: Full (today's HUD), Partial and Hidden. Partial keeps only what you act on mid-fight: your health, ability cooldown numbers, enemies left. Pick a look for Partial below, then press H to see all three.

Dropped in Partial. The console and its CRT glass, wave plate and score, XP strip, level chip, class names, teammate rows, path/gold/modifier chips, tile art and ability icons. Names over heads stay off. Hidden is today's H: only slim boss bars remain.

A · Corner text. Plain text in the corners with a shadow, no boxes. HP bottom left, cooldowns in a row bottom centre, enemies left top right. The calmest; reads fine on busy backgrounds.

B · Key row. A compact row of four small keycaps with the number inside; a dimmed cap with a cooldown fill while recharging, lit when ready. Thin HP bar above it, enemies left centre top.

C · On the player. The info rides on your character: a health arc, tiny cooldown numbers orbiting. Nothing at the screen edges but a top-centre enemy count. Least eye travel, but busiest near you.

D · One line. A single thin strip at the bottom: W12 · 14 LEFT · HP 180/240 · Q 3.2 E · ⇧ 1.1 ␣ · Smallest footprint; denser to read.

Wiring (if picked). hudShown becomes a three-step hudMode (saved in Settings, no protocol change as the HUD is local). Partial draws the chosen look in DrawHud in place of the console groups; mini boss bars and the boss bar group stay as they are.
