Glacial Tomb

Today the cast is a bare ring. These three versions turn it into a moment: every enemy bullet is erased, every enemy freezes solid for 4 s, and the boss takes 250 instead. All three run the same scene on a loop: the cast, the wave, the 4 s hold, then the thaw. Frost Nova already uses small faceted ice blocks with a countdown ring, so none of these reuse that. Nothing here is in the game yet.

LOOK A

Sarcophagus

What you see

A rune circle spins up at your feet. On the cast, cracks race across the floor and a carved ice slab thrusts up around every enemy, lid seam and glyph on its face. Bullets burst into snow glints. The boss is not entombed: ice lances punch through it.

Timer and thaw

A cyan band along the slab's foot drains as the freeze runs out. In the last second the slab cracks, then bursts into slabs of ice and mist.

Feel

The literal reading of "tomb". Heavy, ceremonial, easy to read from far away. The slab hides the enemy a little, so it is drawn glassy.

LOOK B

Absolute Zero

What you see

A clock dial winds up at your feet, then stops dead. The wave drains the colour out of the arena behind it, split into red and cyan at its edge. Bullets lock in mid-air as ice beads and shatter a beat later, in wave order. Frost creeps in from the screen edges.

Timer and thaw

A ring of 24 ticks around each frozen enemy goes dark one tick at a time. The colour returns as a second wave, in the same order the enemies froze.

Feel

The most dramatic. It sells "everything stopped" better than the others, and it changes the whole screen for 4 s, so it needs the Reduced effects fallback.

LOOK C

Crystal Reliquary

What you see

A hex lattice of frost spreads outward from a turning mandala, cell by cell, like a crystal growing. Each enemy is caged by a cluster of tall spires with a glowing core. Bullets are replaced by tiny crystal sprouts that bloom and fade.

Timer and thaw

The spires pale and pulse faster as the freeze runs out, then crack and fall in a shower of shards. The lattice recedes with them.

Feel

The most magical and the most ornate. It reads as a relic or an altar. The spires are the biggest silhouettes of the three, so a crowd can get busy.
