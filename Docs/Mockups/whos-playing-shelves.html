Floppy profiles: more shelves

You picked the tray, so here are four other ways to shelve the same disks, with the tray first for comparison. Every one keeps the Play-style Manage keycap and the same disk, so only the furniture changes.

Hover a disk to lift it. Click one to play as it. Manage profiles shows Rename and Delete (Delete asks twice).

Your pickTray

The earlier option C: a panel with corner brackets, the lip covering the bottom of each disk.

Shelf 1Index drawer

A card-file drawer. Each disk has an index tab in front of it with its slot number, and the drawer front has a pull groove. The lip is lower than the tray's, so more of every disk shows.

  • The tabs give each disk a clear slot name even if the label is busy.
  • Rename and Delete sit on the drawer front above the pull.

Shelf 2Drive bays

Each profile is its own disk drive with a drive letter (A:, B:, C:, D:) and a lamp. The disk sticks up out of the slot. Hover to eject it a little, click to push it in.

  • The lamp is lit on a drive that holds a profile. The empty drive is unlit and takes the New profile disk.
  • The most game-like of the set: the click has a physical result.

Shelf 3Lidded box

A disk box with its smoked-plastic lid propped open behind the disks, plastic ribs between the slots and a front lip with slot numbers.

  • The ribs make four clear compartments, which suits an empty slot.
  • Takes the most vertical room: the lid sits between the axis line and the disks.

Shelf 4Display shelf

Disks stand on a wall-mounted metal shelf in front of a pegboard, each under its own spotlight that brightens on hover. Nothing is hidden, so the whole disk always shows.

  • The only layout where the bottom corners of the disk (the write-protect and hub holes) show.
  • Rename and Delete go under the shelf, so the shelf face stays clean.
