New enemy note

When a wave brings an enemy type for the first time, the between-waves banner says so: its name, its job, and one line on what to do about it. Three places to put it, shown on three sample waves. The enemy glyphs are the job marker shapes, not the real enemy art. The one-line descriptions are drafts.

A. In the band: a line under the title

Reads as part of the announcement and costs no extra space. With two new enemies it gets crowded fast, so it stacks to two lines at most.

B. Chips under the band: one card per new enemy

Room for the job marker, a name and a full sentence per enemy, and several fit side by side. The band stays clean. This is the clearest when a wave brings two or three.

C. Corner toast: after the band, in the corner

Stays up a few seconds into the fight so it is read at your own pace, and never covers the banner. It sits near the HUD, which is already busy.

Rules in every version

Only for types you have not met yet (from the same profile record as the name tags, so it appears once). A wave that unlocks nothing shows nothing. Job color and shape match the marker under the enemy. Wave number comes from the type's unlock wave in the wave recipe; a path with its own enemy mix can introduce a type earlier, and that counts too.
