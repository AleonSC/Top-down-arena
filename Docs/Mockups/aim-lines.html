Aim line cosmetics

Ten looks for the line from your weapon to the crosshair. Move the cursor over the arena to try one, or pick a card. All of them keep the rules of today's line: it runs from you toward the crosshair, never past your weapon's range, and ends in a cap when the crosshair is farther than that. Unlock ranks are placeholders.

Notes for building it

  • Only your own line is drawn, so this is a local cosmetic: store the pick in the profile like the other cosmetics and switch inside DrawAimLine. No network message changes, so no protocol bump.
  • Every look is tinted by the class colour, except Prism, which cycles through every hue, and Storm and Ember, which keep their own palette and use the class colour for the glow only.
  • Ruler counts from the muzzle, just past your body, so its numbers are the distance a shot has travelled.
  • Pixel Beam snaps to a 4 px grid, which is the one look that should not be anti-aliased.
