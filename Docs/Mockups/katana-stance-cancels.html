No more held specials. Each stance is a basic-attack combo with a finisher worth reaching: Hayate dashes and slices, four steps ending in a zig-zag through the crowd; Senbon is the showy one, five steps ending in a sheathe that drops every cut you left. Switching is instant and is the skill: switch once a hit has landed and you skip the rest of that step and hit harder with the other stance's next one. Each stance remembers its place for a second, so you can weave between them.

Everything in the arena is drawn at game scale with only the shapes the game itself draws (filled triangles, lines, arcs, soft glows, an additive light pass, hit-stop and shake; no canvas blur). What you see here is what it can look like in Unity.

疾風 Hayate

The dash combo

Blade trailing low · moon steel · every step moves you
  1. KiriDash-cut 120 px along your aim, 22 to all on the line. 0.04 + 0.09 dash + 0.16
  2. KaeshiDash-cut 120 px, angled 20° to the other side (a zig). 22. same timing
  3. TsumujiA short 70 px dash into a full spin, 85 px round you, 26. 0.04 + 0.08 + spin 0.14 + 0.18
  4. Shippū (finisher)Four hops, each to the nearest enemy ahead (60° cone, 230 px), cutting through it: 18 a hop. Then a skid, 0.38 s, that you'll want to cancel. 4 × 0.065 + 0.38

Untouchable during every dash. A dash that kills skips its own recovery, so killing keeps you moving.

千本 Senbon

The fancy combo

Blade forward, edge lit rose · every hit leaves a cut
  1. AgariRising cut, 130° at 120 px, 16. 0.05 + 0.12 sweep + 0.15
  2. KaeshiThe return cut the other way, 16. same timing
  3. EngetsuFull-moon spin with a 30 px step, 105 px round you, 20. 0.04 + 0.2 + 0.18
  4. SenbonSix crossing slashes in a 70° cone over 0.3 s, 7 each. 0.3 + 0.12
  5. Nōtō (finisher)You sheathe. Every cut you left on an enemy lights up, and at the click each enemy takes 10 per cut (up to 6). 0.3 still, then the click, 0.3

Every Senbon hit leaves a glowing cut on the enemy (the marks of the last version, now drawn as the cuts themselves).

The switch

  • Alt switches stance at once. No wind-up and a 0.3 s cooldown.
  • Cancel: switch after a step has hit (from the end of its dash or sweep until it ends) and the rest of that step is skipped. You get a small burst (30 px push within 90 px), a gold flash and +25% and no wind-up on the next attack, if it comes within 0.8 s.
  • Switching before a step has hit drops that step: no bonus.
  • Each stance keeps its place for 1 s after its last step, so Senbon 1-2-3-4 ⇄ Hayate 1 ⇄ Senbon goes on to Nōtō.
  • Mashing one stance waits for each step's recovery; cancelling skips it. That's the reward.

Why the built version looked flat: its hops were 2 px lines with no hit-stop, its cuts were drawn after the fact rather than as you dashed, the halves were plain red discs, and nothing marked the moment to act. This version puts the hit, the split and the hold on every step.
