What changes

  • Window 0.22 s to 0.30 s. At 230 px/s that is 69 px of shot travel instead of 51. Riposte still adds 0.12 s (0.42 s with it).
  • The animation, for everyone: the shield flashes white along its whole arc, sparks in your colour, a bright ping, a 50 ms hold, and the shot goes back as a white needle with a trail in your colour.
  • A short slow-down (time at 30% for 0.3 s) on the first perfect parry in 0.6 s, so a burst slows once, not three times. Solo only; see below.
  • While the window is open, a thin white line runs inside the shield and fades as it closes, so you can learn the timing by eye.
  • Unchanged: the 0.5 s rearm, the 150 the shield soaks, the damage (12, double with Riposte), Counterstance, Mirror Guard.

Feel beats, from the catch

  1. 0 msShield arc flashes white; ring and sparks in your colour; ping.
  2. 0–50 msThe picture holds (your own screen).
  3. 50–350 msSolo: time at 30%, easing back; the needle leaves.
  4. impactThe needle cuts its target with a split if it kills.

Against the game: the window is one number in Parry.cs (PerfectParry). The look needs its own FxKind.PerfectParry carrying the player's colour and the shield's arc, sent where ParryHit sends the white spark today, plus a new Sfx, so that's a protocol bump. In co-op the game can't slow time for one player, so there the hold and flash are only on your own screen (the way Juice.cs already holds the picture for your own cuts) and nobody slows.

Open questions

  1. Is 0.30 s the right size, or 0.36 s? Both are on the toggle.
  2. Straight back (today) or toward your aim? Aim lets you pick a target but makes the parry stronger.
  3. Keep the solo slow-down, or the hold only, so solo and co-op feel the same?
  4. The returned shot: keep its speed (520 px/s) and only change the look, or make it a fast needle like here?
