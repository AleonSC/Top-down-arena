Tips and hints

Today the menu shows one tip at a time in the chip under the title, and it changes every 8 s. These six look at other places and shapes. A to C are on the main menu, D and E are in a match. Each plays on a loop, with real tips from How to play. A1 and A2 are the slide-out-from-the-bottom ones.

A1 · Slide-out shelf

Rises from under the bottom edge

A full-width bar slides up, holds for about 9 s, then slides back. The menu keeps its empty bottom third, and the tip gets room to be a full sentence. Seen once per visit and rotates on each return.

  • Good: big readable text, doesn't touch the card, lots of room for a picture
  • Watch out: moves a lot; players can miss it if they click Play fast
A2 · Pull-tab drawer

A TIPS tab you open yourself

A small tab sits on the bottom edge. Click it (try it above) and a drawer slides up with a picture, the tip and Prev / Next. It peeks open by itself once, then stays put and only opens on a click.

  • Good: never in the way, and the new player can browse all the tips
  • Watch out: people who never click it never see a tip
B · Corner toast

Slides in from the right, counts itself down

A card at the bottom right with a thin timer along its base. It's the pattern players already know from every launcher, so it reads as "optional".

  • Good: familiar, easy to dismiss with ×, can carry a "Don't show again"
  • Watch out: far from where the eyes are on the menu
C · Ticker

One line scrolling along the bottom

A thin strip with a TIP label on the left and every tip running past, endlessly. Pausing on hover would stop it for reading.

  • Good: always there, takes almost no space, shows how many tips exist
  • Watch out: scrolling text is hard to read; it gets ignored like a news crawl
D · In-match callout

Hint at the moment it matters

The first time a bullet gets close, a pulse ring marks the hitbox and a callout points at it. It has a close button and "Don't show again". Each hint can fire once per profile (hitbox, downed, boss shots, status icons).

  • Good: the tip arrives when it's useful, so it's remembered
  • Watch out: needs a trigger per hint; keep it off the screen during boss fights
E · Wave-cleared footer

A tip for the pause between waves

The Wave cleared box already makes everyone wait for Ready. A footer on it cycles a tip every 3 s while they wait, so nobody reads one in the middle of a fight.

  • Good: players have time to read, and no new window to build
  • Watch out: auto-ready players never see it

Notes for building it

  • All six use the existing HowToTips list (Menus.cs) and the blue bulb icon, so no new text to write.
  • A1, A2, B and C replace the title chip; the "Best run" record line would stay under the title on its own.
  • D needs a small seen hints list on the profile (a new Profile field, no protocol change). E and A to C need nothing new.
  • You can combine them: A2 on the menu plus D in matches covers both ends.
