Healer Elementalist: Energy Ward

Arcane Ward becomes Energy Ward. Same numbers (60 damage soaked, 8 s, 300 px, capstone 120): each shielded player gets a big bubble of energy. As it soaks damage it shrinks and turns green to say "the heal is coming"; when it breaks or runs out, half of what it soaked flows back as healing. The loop casts at 0 s, the left player (caster) takes hits until the bubble breaks, the right player (ally) takes fewer and the bubble times out. Pick a look; drag the slider to scrub.
