Mechanical buttons

The menu's tactile buttons rebuilt as real mechanical controls, in three styles, on the main menu and on the Play screen. Press and hold a button to feel the travel, and turn on Click sound to hear it. The green CRT controls (Continue, DEL) stay digital.

A. KeycapsEach button is a keycap with a dished top, sitting in a dark well. It has a lamp in the corner and drops about 7 px when pressed. The most keyboard-like, and the closest to what you have now.
B. Industrial platesA riveted steel plate with stencil lettering and an amber indicator bar. Play gets a hazard stripe. It's heavier and more machine-like, with less glow.
C. Dome and rockersPlay is a big arcade dome button. The smaller buttons are rocker switches that tilt when pressed. The most varied, and the most playful.
