Level-Up Drop-In Mockups

The picker opens with option B from the first round: a gold pillar out of your character and the three cards flying out of you. These three loops try different ways to close it once you've picked, in place of today's shatter. Each plays a moment of the fight, the level-up, a few seconds of choosing, the pick, and back to the fight.

The same in all three: the HUD slides down out of the way while the picker is open and back up after the pick. The Reroll and Take buttons rise from the bottom and drop away when you take a card. Each close takes under a second, and the next pick (if one is waiting) can open as soon as it ends.

Close 1Back into you

  • The opening played backwards. The two cards you passed on dim, fold shut and fly back into you along the arcs they came out on.
  • The one you took flashes, then follows them in last, and lands with a ring in its rarity colour and a short gold pillar. Its icon pops up over your head.
  • The whole level-up starts and ends on your character.

Close 2Fold away

  • The two cards you passed on turn edge-on and blink out, a thin line of light where each was.
  • The one you took shrinks down to its icon, which hops over to above your head and drops into you with a ring and a short gold pillar.
  • The quickest and quietest of the three: little moves on screen but the thing you took.

Close 3Into a spark

  • The cards you passed on sink and fade, sliding down out of view with the buttons.
  • The one you took pulses once and collapses into a ball of light in its rarity colour, which streaks into you along an arc.
  • It bursts on you with a ring and a few sparks, and what you gained rises off you ("+1 projectile").
