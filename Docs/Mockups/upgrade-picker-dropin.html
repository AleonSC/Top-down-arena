Level-Up Drop-In Mockups

Three ways for the upgrade picker to open, built from the run launch's "drop in": the grid ripple, pillars of light, landing rings, the gold banner that overshoots, and panels that slide in like the HUD. Each loop plays a moment of the fight, the level-up, a few seconds of choosing, then today's pick animation (the other cards shatter and yours flies into you) and back to the fight.

Shared by all three: the HUD slides down out of the way as the picker opens and back up when you've picked, like it does when a run launches. The Reroll and Take buttons rise from the bottom the same way. Keys and clicks work as soon as the cards have landed (about 0.9 s in), and a pick made earlier skips the rest of the intro.

Option ACards drop in

  • The closest to the run launch. A gold ring runs out from you across the grid and the screen dims.
  • One pillar of light per card, in its rarity colour, beams down left to right. Each card lands with a small overshoot and a ring, and its icon flashes above it before settling into the card.
  • LEVEL UP! pops in first on a dark band like GET READY, then slides up to its place at the top as the pillars start.

Option BOut of you

  • A gold pillar shoots up out of your character and a ring bursts off you.
  • The three cards fly out of you along arcs, small and turning, and open out as they land, each with a ring in its rarity colour.
  • The mirror image of the pick: the card you take flies back into you the way it came, so the whole level-up starts and ends on your character.

Option CBuilt from the floor

  • The ripple runs out from you across the grid. Where it passes under a card's spot, the arena's grid squares light up in the card's rarity colour, row by row from the bottom.
  • Once the squares fill the card's shape, they flash and the card appears in their place, with its rarity tag and name typing on.
  • The most "part of the arena" of the three: the cards come out of the same grid the launch lights up.
