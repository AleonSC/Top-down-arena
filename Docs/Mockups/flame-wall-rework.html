Elementalist Tank: Flame Wall

Animation mockup. Visual rework only. Compare today's ring of dots with three proposed looks on the same scene: orange shots come from the right and red enemies walk toward you through the fire.

Flame Wall 14 s cooldown · 90 px ring (scaled 0.78) · 5 s · burns · blocks bullets

Today: 18 flickering dots on a faint disc. Proposed: the cast throws a fireball, the ring ignites from the near side, and the wall shows how much time and bullet-blocking it has left.

Beats

    What it fixes

    • The wall now shows two things the player cannot see today: time left (outer rim) and blocking left (inner segments).
    • Blocked shots have an impact: sparks, smoke and a flare where they hit.
    • Burning enemies are obvious, and keep burning briefly after they leave.
    • Casting has a visible arc from the staff to the target, so the placement is clear.
