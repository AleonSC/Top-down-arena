Vector Pickup

A rare enemy drop that adds vectors (the Expanding arena's drawing budget) when walked over. Four looks, all cyan so they never read as health or gold. Click a card to see it in the arena strip. Small drops give +6, big ones (elites, bosses) +20, never past your cap.
