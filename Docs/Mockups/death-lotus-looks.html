Ninja · DPS · Ultimate

Death Lotus looks

Today the ultimate is a chain of flat pink cuts hopping from enemy to enemy, with a ring on the Ninja. All five arenas below play the same cast: the cursor sits on the cross, up to 8 enemies inside the 350 range ring are struck nearest first, 0.11 s apart, and the Ninja is untouchable for 1 s. Enemies, timing and range are identical, so only the visuals differ.

Reference

Pink Chain

Look
One flat pink line per hop, 0.12 s each, then a small ring on the Ninja.
Gap
No windup, no sign of the range, and nothing marks the enemies that were hit.
Look A

Petal Bloom

Look
A lotus bud opens over the cursor while diamonds mark each target. Every strike lights one petal, cuts a quick X and sheds petals that drift and fall. The last hit blows the lotus open and sends a ring out to the edge of the range.
Feel
Graceful and readable. The petal count equals the target count, so the capstone shows 14.
Build
One new petal-burst effect and one bloom effect. The Slash and Ring effects are reused. No gameplay change.
Look B

Ink Flicker

Look
The arena dims and the targets freeze under white reticles. Ghosts of the Ninja flicker past each one and leave black ink cuts. Then a white flash breaks every target at once and all the 100s pop together.
Feel
Fast, cold and precise. The all-at-once payoff is the strongest of the five.
Build
The reveal only works if enemies still stand until it lands, so either hold the damage about 1.5 s or fake the freeze on the client. A screen dim and a ghost sprite are new.
Look C

Lotus Seal

Look
A rotating sigil is drawn on the ground at the cursor, sized to the real 350 range. Threads lock onto each enemy inside it. Each strike fills one petal and cracks an X on its target. The seal then shatters outward.
Feel
Tells the player exactly who will be hit before the cast resolves, and which enemies are out of range.
Build
A ground-decal effect with a lock-on thread per target. The thread list is already known on the cast tick, so no new state.
Look D

Crimson Reaping

Look
The arena darkens. Each target takes three white-hot cuts with a crimson glow and a small screen kick. Every kill leaves a crimson lotus stain on the floor that stays until the cast ends.
Feel
Heavy and violent. Leaves a visible scoreboard of who was killed.
Build
Kill-site decals and a short camera shake. The shake is the only new Unity-side piece.
Notes
  • Every look keeps the Ninja where they stand. Ghosts and afterimages are visual only.
  • Timing is the game's: 8 strikes at 0.11 s is 0.88 s of cuts inside the 1 s invulnerability. At 14 targets the cuts run 1.54 s, so the last 0.5 s would land after the invulnerability ends. Worth speeding the stagger up at the capstone.
  • Any new effect kind or network message means a protocol version bump.
