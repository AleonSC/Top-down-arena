Proposed rules

Cast70 s cooldown, lasts 12 s. Same slot as Thousand Clones / Dragon's Dogma (3rd pick on the Ultimate row).
Rise0.8 s: the spirit pulls in round you; can't be hurt, 30 + a shove to enemies within 95 px.
Avatar60% less damage, walk at 85%, every enemy goes for you. Enemy shots that hit the spirit's body are swallowed.
Moon CleaveFire (weapon put away). 0.9 s wind-up with a floor arc filling in (you turn slowly), then a 200° sweep out to 170 px: 80 damage, 0.5 s stun, pushed back ~150 px (heavies half, bosses not). One every 1.6 s while Fire is held.
ReleaseAt the end the spirit shudders, then bursts: 40 and a shove within 130 px. Also ends if you go down or the wave ends.
Mastered15 s, cleave reaches 20% further. Rare card Kami Vessel: +2 s, cleave wind-up 0.2 s faster.

Vs Stone Colossus

  • Slower, wider hit: one big telegraphed arc instead of a 1/s fist slam.
  • Pushes, not stuns: clears space round you, so allies behind you get room.
  • Tank + DPS: damage cut and taunt keep the tank job; 80 per cleave on a crowd is the damage.
  • Readable: the floor arc tells teammates where enemies will fly.
