Katana chain streak

An Alt strike that kills its target keeps the meter full, so you can strike again at once. Each kill is one chain. While the chain is above zero, the meter does not drain. At five chains the next strike is empowered.

Playable. Enemies have different health on purpose: swarmers (50) and grunts (100) die to a strike (120), brutes (220) survive it and break your streak, unless the strike is empowered (240).

Rules in this mockup

  • A strike spends the meter as before. If the target dies, the meter is set back to 100 and the chain goes up.
  • If the target survives, the meter stays spent and the chain resets to 0 (streak lost).
  • 1 kill = 1 chain. By default the enemies the line cuts through also count when they die (switch off "Route kills add chain" to count only the target).
  • While the chain is above 0 the meter never drains. At 0 it drains at 25 per second after 1.2 s without a hit, as now.
  • At 5 chains the next strike is empowered: 120 to everything on the line (up from 60) and 240 to the target (up from 120). The cut is wider and white-gold.
  • After an empowered strike that kills, the meter stays full and the chain starts again from 0 (or stays at 5+ with the option on).

Open questions

  • Should a streak time out (for example after 5 s without striking)? As built here, a full meter with a streak can wait forever.
  • Does taking damage end the streak, or only a survived target?
  • After the empowered strike, reset the chain or stay empowered for as long as the streak lasts? The last checkbox shows the second option, which is much stronger.
  • Should route kills count? They make the chain climb faster in a crowd, so 5 comes quickly.
  • Bosses survive 120 and 240 for a long time. Should a boss hit count as a kill never, or after a big percentage of its health?
