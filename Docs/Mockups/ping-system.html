Ping System

With 1-3 players and no voice, a ping lets you say "look here", "run!" or "kill that one" without typing. Tap the Ping key (or middle mouse) to drop a plain "Here" at your cursor; hold it for a six-slice wheel. Everyone sees it, in their own colour, for 5 seconds.

In the demo, click is the Ping key: tap = the selected ping, hold 0.2 s = wheel (drag to a slice, let go; let go in the middle to cancel). Spam is capped at 3 live pings each; a 4th replaces your oldest.

The six pings

Where a ping shows up

On screen

  • Three ripples spread out from the spot, then a ring with the glyph and a thin beam of light going up, so it reads over a busy screen.
  • A small chip in the pinger's colour: name and, once, the word (P2 · DANGER). After 1 s the word drops and only the glyph stays.
  • Fades over its last second. Bullets and enemies draw over the ring, never under the glyph.

Off screen

  • The glyph pins to the screen edge (36 px in) with a chevron pointing the way and the distance beneath (640, in px like the rest of the game).
  • Because the arena wraps, it points the short way round.
  • Minimap (top-right) shows the same dot, so the party can see the whole picture at a glance.

On an enemy or pickup

  • Focus snaps to the enemy within 60 px of the cursor and follows it until it dies; the ring becomes a crosshair that sits on it.
  • Enemies a teammate has marked get a thin outline in their colour, so you can find "the Hexer P3 meant" in a crowd.
  • Loot on an orb, card or shop item tags it "mine"; the item gets a small pinger-coloured corner mark until taken.

Rules

  • Lasts 5 s (Danger 4 s). Max 3 live per player; 0.4 s between pings, so mashing doesn't flood the screen.
  • A Help ping also shows on the pinger's HP bar for everyone, and the Healer's abilities highlight them.
  • Works in matches only; the Hangout has emotes. Mute a player's pings from the pause menu if they spam.
  • Spectators and dead players can still ping, with a dim ring (nice for "boss is going left").

Questions for you

Wheel or tap-only? The wheel gives six meanings but is slower in a bullet hell. Alternative: tap = Here, double-tap = Danger, nothing else.

Own colour or class colour? Shown here: player slot colour (teal / amber / violet). Class colour is prettier but two Knights would clash.

Ping vs. emote key? Emotes use hold-T. Could merge: T bar gets a "Ping" tab. I'd keep them separate: pings are about the map, emotes are about you.
