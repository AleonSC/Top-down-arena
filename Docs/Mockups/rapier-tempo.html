Rapier: Tempo and Flourish

Today the Rapier thrusts every 0.28 s and each hit in a row on the same enemy deals 10% more, up to +60%. This rework swaps the damage streak for speed. The first thrust is slow. Every enemy you hit, any enemy, adds a stack of Tempo, and every stack makes the next thrust come sooner. Stop hitting and Tempo holds through a short grace period, then drains slowly.

The weapon-action key spends it all on a Flourish: the rapier whips back and forth in front of you, and every swing throws a crescent wave down your aim. More Tempo, more swings. Afterwards you are back to a slow first thrust, so the choice is to keep the speed or cash it in.

Open questions

  • Drop the same-target damage streak? The mockup drops it: every thrust deals a flat 18 and all the build-up is speed. Keeping both would make a full-Tempo Rapier on one boss far above the other DPS weapons.
  • Do skewered bullets still count? Today each one adds to the streak. Here it's the toggle above (on by default), so thrusting into a bullet stream still builds Tempo.
  • Hits or thrusts? With the +1 upgrade a thrust can hit two enemies. The mockup gives a stack per enemy hit, as you asked ("every hit on an enemy"), so the upgrade also ramps you faster.
  • Battle Fury and fire-rate cards would multiply on top of the Tempo interval, so the fastest thrust needs a floor (0.08 s is the game's usual melee floor).
  • The six diamonds behind you become one per Tempo stack on the same arc, the top one draining while Tempo falls. With 12 or more they sit closer together; a 6-diamond meter with half steps is the fallback if it reads too busy.
  • Network: the Tempo stacks replace RapierStreak in the snapshot, and the Flourish needs its own waves; both mean a protocol bump.
