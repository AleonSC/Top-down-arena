Lifeline

Lifeline revives and fully heals the whole party, then gives everyone 3 s of invulnerability (90 s cooldown; the capstone adds 4 s of Overdrive). Today that is one green ring per player and a thin beam from you to each ally. These are four new looks for the same cast. Each loops one scene at game scale: you (blue, centre), two hurt allies, one downed ally (grey X), and an enemy firing at the ally on the right so you can see the 3 s of invulnerability turn bullets away.

Pause and drag the slider to step through the stages: idle, cast, invulnerable, fade.

Idle

What stays the same

  • Numbers: 90 s cooldown, revive at full health, full heal for everyone, 3 s invulnerability, 4 s Overdrive with the capstone.
  • Nothing about the network message changes, so the protocol version stays put. The look would live in AbilityLooks.cs as a new set of FxKind effects, played on every client.
  • The last 0.6 s of invulnerability blinks in every look, so players know when the cover ends.
