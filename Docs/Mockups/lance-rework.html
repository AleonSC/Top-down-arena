Lance: drive them back

A rework of the Tank Knight's Lance, drawn in the game's look. Today it is one heavy jab that shoves 140 px. This page keeps that shove as the heart of the weapon and builds a four-step chain around it, with a proper held lance, a shield on the off hand, and an alt attack on the weapon-action key.

Every moveset shares one new rule, Impact: an enemy you shove that hits another enemy or a wall takes extra damage. Shove one into a pack and it bowls them; shove one into a wall and it's stunned. The chains are built to set that up.

Impact: the push combo

  • A shoved enemy still sliding fast (about the last 35 px of a shove) is armed.
  • Armed into another enemy: both take 10, and the one it hits slides on with 70% of its speed, armed too. One good shove can bowl a line.
  • Armed into a wall or layout block: 18 and a 0.8 s stun.
  • Orange numbers on the stage are Impact damage. Heavy enemies and bosses aren't shoved, so they never take it, but you can still bowl small enemies into them.

How it plays

  • Hold the button and the chain plays on by itself, like the Twin Blades. A press counts once a step is 80% through.
  • The chain starts over 0.7 s after a step ends without a press.
  • Four diamonds over your head show the chain; the fourth glows when the finisher is next.
  • Each step carries you a little forward, so the chain walks you into the pack you're pushing.

2 · Alt attacks

The weapon-action key (Left Alt), like Twin Fang or the Greatsword's guard. Pick one; each moveset has a default that suits it, but any of them works with any chain.

Each move on its own

The four steps of the chosen chain and its alt, each looping against a small pack in front of a wall.

3 · The lance

Today the lance is a flat steel wedge that only appears during the jab. Each of these is a held model: it stays in your hand between attacks, couched along your right side, with a heater shield on your left arm. The shape changes only the look.

    Open questions

    • Should Impact also work with the Shield Wall, Leap Slam and Cavalry Call shoves? It would make the whole Tank Knight a pushing class.
    • The +1 projectile upgrade: today it adds reach and width. With a chain it could add a second shove wave on the finisher.
    • Hook & Haul on a boss: pull yourself to it instead, and prime the finisher anyway?
    • Pinning Throw leaves you without a weapon for about 1.5 s. Too long for a tank, or the right price for a 45-damage wall pin?
