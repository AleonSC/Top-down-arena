Hemomancer · Tank · ability rework mockup

Exsanguinate

Blood tendrils burst out of you and out of every enemy in a wide ring. They reach for each other, touch, knot, and reel the enemies in to you while their blood runs down the strand into you. Three takes on it, plus today's version for comparison.

Vein Nettake A

Click the arena to move the Hemomancer there and cast. The two enemies past the ring are out of reach and stay put. Tick Heavy in range to see a brute that can't be moved: the tendrils go taut and strain, but it is still drained. Slow it down to 0.25× to see the tendrils touch.

Take C · 3.6 s show

Heartstrings

Slow and ritual, set to your heartbeat. Each enemy's heart glows and a tendril creeps out of it as yours reach out like seaweed. They tie on a beat, then three heartbeats tug them in a third of the way each, draining a third each time.

  1. 0.15 s first beat: a ring to 220 px

  3. 0.85 s second beat: they tie
  4. 1.45 / 1.95 / 2.45 s tug, tug, TUG

What would change

Cost, reach, drain
Kept: 10% of max health, 220 px, 20 drained from each and healed to you, 8 s cooldown. Deep Veins still reaches 30% farther and drains 50% more.
The pull
Today it moves enemies 60 px in at once. The rework reels them all the way in to touching you, over the show (A 0.5 s, B 0.26 s, C three tugs). They are stunned while held, so they can't shoot on the way in.
When the drain lands
On arrival (A, B), or a third on each tug (C), not at the cast. An enemy that dies on the way in lets go of its tendril and its blood splashes into you.
Heavy enemies, bosses
Not moved, as today. The tendrils go taut and strain against them, and they still get drained.
Mastered
Kept: drained enemies are slowed 50% for 2 s. They now arrive in a clump at your feet, which sets up Blood Lash and Crimson Pact.
Network
A new FxKind at the end of the list (one per held enemy, with its id, so the client can draw the tendril to wherever it is now), and a protocol bump. The pull itself is server-side movement, already replicated.

Open questions

  • Which take? B feels best in a fight; C looks best but holds enemies for 2.5 s, which is a lot of crowd control for an 8 s cooldown.
  • Pull everything to touching you, or stop at Blood Lash range (about 100 px) so the follow-up lash's tip hits them for double?
  • Should allies in the ring get a thin tendril too and share the heal (Blood Gift style)?
  • Should the cast lock you in place while you reel, or can you keep walking and drag them with you?
