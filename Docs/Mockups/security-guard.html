Security Guard

A prison security officer with a service pistol. It stops, locks onto where you are heading and fires one round, then another, semi-auto. Seven shots empty the magazine, and the 2.2 s reload is your window to rush it. The red lane on the floor is the locked shot.

Live

Ready: where it would shoot now Locked: the round leaves when the red reaches the end Reload: the guard is open

Press and drag on the arena to move the player yourself. The guard leads with a straight-line guess from your speed, so changing direction in the 0.25 s of red beats it.

One magazine, four phases

Ready 0.55 s

Pistol up, pips show the rounds left. The dashed lane is where it would shoot right now.

Lock on 0.25 s

It plants its feet and the lane fills red toward the lead point. The aim no longer follows you.

Fire 1 round

One shot, slide kicks back, a brass casing flies out. Seven times, 0.8 s apart.

Reload 2.2 s

Empty: it drops the magazine, seats a new one and racks the slide. The cream ring fills. Rush it now.

Notes

  • It sits between the Grunt (fast bursts) and the Sniper (one slow shot). Its threat is rhythm: a steady drip, then a gap.
  • Leading punishes running in straight lines and rewards sudden turns. Circling it works.
  • Two or three guards with offset reloads make the "gap" harder to find, which suits the Patrol squad.
  • The lane is drawn on the floor, under bullets, so it never hides them.
  • To decide: a Patrol squad (three guards and two Rioters); whether a perfect parry returns the round; two guards behind the Riot Line on the last wave.
  • Numbers are a starting point for tuning. The code is not written yet.
