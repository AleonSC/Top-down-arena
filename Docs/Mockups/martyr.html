Nothing about the rules changes: cost, range, 50% revive, the 12 s ward and the invulnerability all stay as they are. This is drawing only.

Martyr 40% of max health · revive the downed ally nearest the cursor, or ward the most hurt ally for 12 s

Pick a situation and a look; it loops. Revive: an ally is down, you pay and they stand up. Ward: nobody is down, so the most hurt ally is warded, then takes a lethal hit and the ward spends itself. The 12 s ward clock runs at 2× here so the whole story fits in one loop.

Beat by beat

  1. 0.0 sPress the ability. A mark opens on your chest (cross, sigil or heart, by look) and the 40% leaves you.
  2. 0–0.45 sYour blood travels to the target: a string of beads (A), a line that paints the sigil (B), an artery that beats (C).
  3. 0.0 sRevive: the ally stands the instant you press, as today. The rise is dressed: halo and petals (A), light spokes (B), two defibrillator thumps (C).
  4. wardThe ward stays on the ally and shows time left: 12 beads (A), a ring that unwinds (B), a heart that beats faster (C).
  5. last 3 sIt flickers and speeds up, like today's pulse but on the clock itself.
  6. lethal hitThe ward spends itself: the ally never drops, they pop back at 50% with a short invulnerable shimmer.

Rules as drawn

  • Revive lands at the press, as in the game now. The stream is a cosmetic that follows.
  • Ward lasts 12 s; the drawn clock is exactly that timer, so it replicates from WardT and needs no new field.
  • No snapshot change: every look reads state that already exists (WardT, Downed, InvulnT) and the existing Drain and Ring effects. Protocol version stays as it is, unless the sigil or tether want their own effect kinds.
  • The invulnerable shimmer after a revive or ward pop is new but cosmetic: it reads InvulnT.
  • The ward sits under the ally's feet (B) or above their head (A, C), so it never covers their bullets or aim line.

Left out: sound, the real sprites for the Hemomancer and allies (circles stand in), and how each look draws when two or three wards are up at once (they stack on different allies; one ward per ally).
