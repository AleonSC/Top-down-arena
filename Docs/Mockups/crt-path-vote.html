Path voting, CRT mockups

Three ways to turn "Choose your path" from a host-only pick into a squad vote, in the blue CRT language of the lobby and the Wave cleared tube. Click a path (or the no-path key) to cast your vote, change it as often as you like, and watch the table lock once everyone has voted. Teammates are simulated.

Who votesEvery player in the run, 1 to 3. One vote each, changeable until the table locks.
Three optionsPath 1, path 2, or the plain arena. The plain arena is a ballot option, not a host-only key.
WinnerMost votes wins. A tie goes to the host's vote, so the host keeps the casting vote and a 1-1-1 split can never stall.
Locking3 second countdown after the last vote (same as the Ready countdown). Any change restarts it. The host can force the result after 60 s for a stalled player.

A. Twin tubes with ballot lists

Closest to the shipped cards, so the least code to change. Each tube lists who voted for it, with a big vote count in the corner and a lit border on the leader. The no-path key sits under the tubes with its own count, and a roster row shows who has still to vote.

B. Ballot console

One tube. Each path is a row with a block-bar tally, number keys 1 to 3 cast the vote, and a short dossier for whichever row you are on prints on the right. Most compact, and the closest to the terminal look of the lobby. The floor preview becomes a small window in each row.

C. Tug of war

Two full tubes, and between them a long tally bar that slides toward the leading path as votes land. Ties show as a centred bar with a gold casting-vote marker on the host. Best at making the split visible from across the room.

Mockup only: nothing here is wired into the game. Paths, rules, bonuses and prizes are the real ones from Core/Paths.cs. Needs a Msg.PathVote and a protocol bump if built.
