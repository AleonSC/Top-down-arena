Kusarigama Rework

Four new looks for the Tank Ninja's sickle and chain. The moves, timings and numbers are the game's own (96 px cut over 144°, 280 px throw, a whirl from 70 to 150 px out, a 200 px pull, a 110 px snap); only the drawing changes. Every look drops the old glowing swipe band.

What each look does

Shared by every new look

A real sickle and chain. The sickle is a curved blade on a wooden grip (today it is a 3 px arc), the chain is drawn as links that sag when slack and pull straight when taut, and the weight is a small iron fundo with a ring, not a spiked ball in a glow disc.

The cut follows the sickle. The sickle flies out on its chain and the effect is drawn along the path its blade takes, so it reads as a chain weapon rather than a sword.

Less screen. The whirl drops the turning dashed rings and the chevrons; the pull is shown on the enemies being drawn in and at the 200 px edge only.
