Embolus Spears

Arms out, a hardened blood spear in each hand. Click throws one, click throws the other, a third click rips both back. You pay in blood and get it back on the catch, or blow them up for half.

Three looks

AClot Javelin

Dark congealed shaft, a faceted ruby head. Leaves a drip trail; lodges with a crack and a splat. Blast: one heavy ring and a spray of clots.

BBarbed Thrombus

Thicker, veined, barbs swept back like a harpoon. A pulsing vein runs its length. Blast: the barbs fly out as spikes.

CHemoglass

Translucent crimson crystal with a hot white core and a glow. Clean light streak. Blast: a flash and a burst of glass shards.
