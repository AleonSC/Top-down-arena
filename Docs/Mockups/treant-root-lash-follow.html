Treant Rod: root lash

The lash now stays where you cast it: walk and you leave it behind, and it starts 20 px out from you as a flat, full-width stump. Here the root rides with you for its whole half second, and three ways to grow it out of the ground instead of out of thin air.

The hits don't change: they still land at the swing, along the line you aimed. Only the drawing follows you.
