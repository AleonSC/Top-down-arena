Cloud looks

AThunderhead

  • Two-tone puffs: lit tops, dark belly
  • Lightning flickers inside the cloud
  • Rain slants with drift, splashes land
  • Belly crackles when the strike is ready
  • Green + motes rise off healed allies

BStorm Spirit

  • Cloud is a character: eyes track the nearest enemy, blink, squint on strike
  • Squashes and stretches as it drifts
  • Charge ring fills round it; sparks orbit when ready
  • Teal healing drops and puddle ripples

CAnvil

  • Bigger three-tier cumulonimbus, slow swirl on top
  • Rain falls as streaked curtains
  • Forked bolt with branches
  • Strikes leave a scorch mark for 1.5 s

Hurricane Alt

H1Eye of the Storm

  • Cloud spins up into a 140 px hurricane for 4 s, follows the cursor at half speed
  • Spiral arms drag enemies round and in; lightning lashes from the arms
  • Swallows enemy bullets that enter it
  • Allies in the calm eye heal double
  • Ends in a burst that shoves enemies out
20 dmg/s · bolts 10 ×3/s · eye 16 heal/s · 12 s cd

H2Cyclone Run

  • Cloud twists into a funnel and races 600 px along your aim, growing 50 → 100 px
  • Drags enemies along with it, bouncing off walls
  • Leaves a trail of healing puddles for 3 s
  • Reforms where it stops, drifts back to the cursor
25 dmg/s · puddles 6 heal/s · 10 s cd

Numbers are starting proposals, sized against today's 16 dmg strike every 0.6 s and 8 heal/s rain. Demo cooldown is 3 s so you can spam it.
