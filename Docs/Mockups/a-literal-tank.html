A Literal Tank

You climb into an actual tank for 15 seconds. The hull follows your movement, the turret follows your cursor, and the main gun fires big explosive shells. When time runs out you pop out of the hatch and the empty tank blows up behind you.

WASD drive · mouse aim · click or Space fire. Idle = demo.

Approximate browser mockup: the game draws with Ui.cs shapes, so glow and soft shadows will look simpler in Unity.

Beats

  1. Drop-in (0.6 s). A crate shadow grows under you, the tank lands with a dust ring and a 120 px shove. You're inside.
  2. Drive (15 s). Hull turns toward your move direction (no strafing), turret tracks the cursor freely. A tread trail is left on the floor.
  3. Main gun. Heavy shell with recoil kick, muzzle flash and a short screen shake. Explodes on hit or at the cursor's range.
  4. Crush. Small enemies you drive into are run over; heavies and bosses are shoved instead.
  5. Bail out. Last 3 s the hull blinks and beeps. You pop out of the hatch, the empty tank explodes 0.5 s later.
