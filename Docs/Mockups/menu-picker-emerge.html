The header text rewrites itself, the Quit key becomes the Back key, the Play key turns into the runs screen and the three small keys fold into New run. Pick a look, then change the header and key styles on their own. Back plays the same thing in reverse.

A · Morph

  • The Play key's rectangle grows into the runs screen: the blue face dims out while the green screen powers up inside the same box.
  • Join, Records and Settings slide down and slide together until they are one bar, which is New run.
  • Calmest of the three. Nothing disappears, everything changes shape.

B · Power-on

  • Play dips like a pressed key, then collapses into a bright line, the way a CRT switches on.
  • The line opens up into the screen with a flash, and the run details flicker on row by row.
  • The small keys sink into the chassis one after another, then New run rises.

C · Hatch

  • The Play key swings up on its top edge like a lid and the screen is already there underneath, opening as the lid goes.
  • The three keys drop away and New run unfolds from the middle outwards.
  • Most physical of the three. It reads as a compartment opening.

D · Bay doors

  • The Play key splits down the middle and the halves slide apart into the chassis walls, showing the screen through the gap.
  • The screen opens up to full height in a riveted metal bezel (new: the bezel is only in D and E).
  • Join, Records and Settings turn edge-on like louvres and vanish. New run drops in from above, bounces once, and the whole box gives a small thunk.

E · Piston lift

  • Play dips, then slides up into the header slot. The three small keys drop through the floor one after another.
  • The screen comes up from under the chassis on two rails, overshoots by a few pixels, and settles with a thunk. New run rises after it.
  • Heaviest of the five (1.5 s). Rails and the bezel are new for this look.

Header text

  • Split-flap: each letter is a flap cell that steps through the alphabet to the new letter, left to right, like a station board. Used by D and E.
  • Backspace: MAIN MENU is deleted letter by letter, then YOUR RUNS is typed. The caret stays, as it does on the picker today.
  • Scramble: every letter scrambles, then settles into the new text left to right.
  • Overwrite: a block cursor sweeps across and replaces each letter in place. Both titles are 9 characters, so this fits.

Quit to Back

  • Morph: the key grows taller and wider, turns from dark to blue, and the power icon turns into the arrow.
  • Flip: the key turns over to show Back on its other side.
  • Reel: Quit scrolls up out of the slot and Back scrolls in from below.
  • Ratchet: like Flip, but it turns in five separate clicks, each one snapping into place. Used by D.

Building it

  • One progress value drives everything, so Back is the same animation played backwards, and the header rewrites to MAIN MENU in the same style.
  • A to C run about 1 s, D and E about 1.4 s (they have heavier parts to move, so trim them if that feels slow). Clicks are ignored during it. With reduced effects it snaps.
  • Not built yet. Pick a look, a header style and a key style.
