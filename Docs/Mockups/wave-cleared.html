Mockup · Between waves

Wave cleared & the transition

Today the box is a plain 560 x 96 card that pops in once the last enemy dies: "WAVE 4 CLEARED" and one line under it ("Wave 5 starts in 4"). Nothing marks the moment itself, and nothing on it says how the wave went. Same navy card, blue accent (gold after a boss); three ways to dress it. Each plays the same beat: last enemy pops, a soft flash, leftover shots clear, the box arrives, the countdown drains, the box leaves. Press Replay to watch it; the little toggle switches to the boss (gold) version.

A · Result plate

The smallest change: same spot, same card, but taller (128). A check mark draws itself next to the title, a light sweeps across, three chips fade in with how the wave went (clear time, damage taken, score gained), and the "starts in N" line gets a draining bar instead of just a number. Boss waves turn the whole card gold. Front end only; clear time and damage are already tracked for the end-of-run stats.

B · Full-width band

A cinematic strip: it wipes open from the centre across the whole screen, the title tracks in from wide letter-spacing, and a thin timer bar runs under it. The "next wave" line moves out of the box into its own small chip below, with a taste of what's coming (enemy names from the wave list). Loudest of the three, so best kept to the quiet beat between waves; it never covers the HUD top or bottom.

C · Squad tally

Built for co-op: the box grows one row per player (kills, damage, and a green tag for an upgrade pick waiting or a revive, since clearing a wave stands everyone back up). Rows slide in one after another. Solo it shows a single row, so it's still fine. The tallest box, so it sits a bit higher than today's banner to stay clear of the players. Needs the per-wave kills and damage counted per player (a small new counter, and a protocol bump if clients must see them).
