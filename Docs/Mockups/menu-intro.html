Notes for building it

  • A new Game/MenuIntro.cs with an introT clock, run from Update on the first time the main menu shows. Coming back from a run or the lobby skips it, and so does any key or click.
  • Lines, the pen, dots and the regression line are GL (BeginUiGl, like DrawAxis today). The title's reveal is Ui.Fancy inside a clipped GUI.BeginGroup that widens as the pen moves; the card grows with GUI.matrix, the way the HUD's pieces scale in.
  • Hidden until their turn: the card's fields and buttons ignore clicks while the intro plays, so nothing can be pressed half drawn.
  • Reduced effects or Visual effects off: a 0.4 s fade of the whole menu instead.
  • Sound: a soft pen scratch while the title is drawn, a tick per axis mark, and a low chord as the card opens (from SoundBank.cs).
