Twin Rifle Grenade Launch

Animations for the under-barrel grenade tubes (the gameplay is in twin-rifle-grenade-rework.html and TwinGrenades.cs). Today a launch is a 16 px ring and a sound; the gun doesn't move. Each look below adds a kick on the firing gun, a breech that opens and spits out the spent shell, and a reload when your hits fill a tube. The guns are drawn in Twin Rifle Firing's pose A, with the tubes where the grenades really start. The flight path and burst point match the game.

    Point at the arena to aim. The grenade bursts at the crossing (your cursor's distance, 120 px to full range). The tubes take turns, left then right.

    Thump

      Reload (every look)

      • 40th hit
        The empty tube's breech drops open.
      • 0.05–0.17 s
        A fresh olive shell slides in from behind and seats.
      • 0.2 s
        The breech snaps shut with a spark. A green ring pulses off the tube, its muzzle light comes on, and the pip behind you fills. The game's ring and pick-up sound play here.
      • held
        A loaded tube is olive with a faint glow. The next to fire has a blinking light at its muzzle.

      Drawing only: no timings change. The firing gun's kick, the opened breech and the shell are client-side, started by the launch ring the host already broadcasts (HostTwinGrenade) or by a TwinNades drop, which lets other players' launches animate too. A load is a TwinNades rise. Lob draws the grenade higher than its hitbox mid-flight (its shadow is the real position), the same trick as the Flak Cannon's shell. Rocket assist adds a backblast behind the gun that is only a look and doesn't hurt anyone.
