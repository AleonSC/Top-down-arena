Last Stand Bastion

A full dome 190 px in radius with you at the centre. Every enemy shot from every direction stops at the rim. Teammates stand anywhere inside it and fire out. Turning is free because the shape does not care which way you face.

It plants by itself 0.7 s in. Press Q or the button to cancel early, or let the fire wear you down. The three coloured dots are teammates; their shots pass straight through.

How it plays

  • Plant: six anchor stakes drive into the floor, the rune ring spreads and the dome rises over 0.5 s.
  • Hits heat the rim segments they land on and flash the hex cell. Cracks creep in from the rim as your health drops.
  • Rim colour goes blue, then amber below 50% HP, then red and flickering below 25%.

Why pick it

  • Covers every direction, so nothing flanks it.
  • Simplest to read and to build: it is one circle and the Fortress dome already works this way.
  • Teammates can move around freely inside.

Watch out for

  • Overlaps Fortress, the Tank Soldier's dome. Strongest idea here for co-op, weakest for a distinct identity.
  • 190 px is a lot of the screen; in 3 player play it shades the whole arena centre.
  • Teammates who step outside the rim lose it entirely.

Build notes

  • Knight.cs: AbilityId.LastStand keeps its enum value; its def (name, desc, no timer, cancel on second press) and the cast branch change. LastStanding(p) and its 1 HP clamp in Combat.cs go away, and so does the +50% in KnightDamageMult.
  • The 10% share is a damage redirect where the barrier consumes a shot: the shot is removed and the knight is hurt for a tenth of its damage. The Fortress dome and Shield Wall already stop shots, so the hit test has a precedent.
  • Rooting needs a planted flag on the player that blocks movement, dash and leap. Clients need it, and the barrier's shape and aim, so it is a snapshot change and a Session.ProtocolVersion bump (now 171).
  • The 400 HP in this mockup is a stand-in so the collapse can be seen. In the game the real value is the knight's own max HP; every number above scales with it.
  • This is a mockup only. Nothing in the game changes until one of the three is picked.
