Energy Ward: shrink animations

Look A (the glassy energy bubble) is locked in. This page is only about how it shrinks. Each idea reacts to every hit on its own, instead of sliding smoothly down, and all of them turn green at under half shield and pay back half of what was soaked when the bubble breaks or the 8 s timer ends. Use the slider to scrub, and flip between Current and the ideas to compare.
