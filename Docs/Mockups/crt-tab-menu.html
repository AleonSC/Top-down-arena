The Tab screen in the CRT style

Hold Tab during a run: Run so far, Your build and Team. Same pages and numbers as today, rebuilt from the lobby's CRT parts: soft keys, phosphor text, scanlines, corner brackets. Colour stays where it means something: rarity, roles, health and bars.

The tab keys on the screen switch pages too (in the game: click them, or Q / E while holding Tab). 1280 x 720, scaled to fit.

A · Like the lobby

One full CRT screen with the lobby's glass frame: your stats in the left column where the party sits, the page in the big screen on the right, the three tab keys above it. Reads as the same place as the lobby's loadout.

B · Rack of tubes

Separate CRT tubes over the dimmed arena, like the run results: a stats tube, then one tube per part of the page (abilities, upgrades; totals, route, your fight; one per teammate). The arena shows between them.

Building it: both use pieces that exist: RoundTube and RoundBackdrop (RoundWindows.cs), LobbyKit keys, headings and corners, CrtKit.LoadoutBox, the colours in CrtStyle.cs, so the CRT colour setting carries over. The tooltips already have their CRT look.
