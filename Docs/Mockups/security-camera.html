Security Camera

Approximate HTML mockup (glow and fonts won't match Unity exactly). Each room loops: the player walks into the light, the camera locks on and calls a squad, then the player shoots it down and it calls a smaller one.

Proposed rules

  • Spawns in an inner room corner, ~1 in 3 rooms, from wave 4.
  • Lock-on: the light stops and follows you; stay in it 0.7 s and it raises the alarm. Step out to drop the lock.
  • Alarm: 4 of the faction's basic enemy through red spawn rings near the camera (0.9 s telegraph), then a 3 s cooldown.
  • Shot down (6 hits): it still calls 2, then stays a wreck. Coins on kill.
  • Never blocks bullets; enemies ignore it.
