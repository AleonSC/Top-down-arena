Hemomancer DPS: Crimson Harvest

Animation mockup for a rework of Crimson Apotheosis (65 s). The Hemomancer rips blood out of every enemy in the arena, each one taking a little damage. The blood gathers in a floating ball above you, bigger the more enemies fed it. Then you pick a spot and throw it for a big area blast.

Crimson Harvest rip · hold · aim · throw

Press Replay to cast. Tendrils reach every enemy, droplets stream back and the ball swells. When the rip ends, move the mouse to aim (the rings show the blast and the full-damage core) and click to throw. If you wait 5 s it throws at your cursor on its own. Change the enemy count to see the ball and the blast scale.

Ball size same look, four hauls

The ball's area follows the blood, so its radius follows the square root: doubling the haul makes it about 40% wider, not twice as wide. The dashed ring is the blast, drawn at half scale so it fits. Blood per enemy: Grunt 1, Heavy 3, boss 8.

Beat by beat

  1. 0.00 sCast. The floor darkens and a seed of blood rises over your head.
  2. 0.35–2.5 sRip. A tendril grows to each enemy, 0.35 to 0.9 s apart by distance. Each enemy takes 4 ticks of 5 and turns pale. Ten droplets stream back per enemy.
  3. ≈2.5 sThe last droplet lands. The ball is at full size and the aim rings appear.
  4. Hold, up to 5 sThe ball floats and breathes. Move at 60% speed. Rings show the blast and its full-damage core; enemies inside get an amber outline.
  5. Throw 0.65 sThe ball lobs to the target with a ground shadow and droplet trail.
  6. ImpactThree shock rings, a flash and a short shake. Enemies take damage, get flung, and the dead burst. A blood pool stays 4.5 s.

Looks

  • A · Membrane. Smooth veins that pulse, a wobbling skin with a heartbeat. Closest to Blood Shell.
  • B · Whirlpool. Dotted threads and bigger droplets; the ball swirls inside and carries orbiting drops that grow with its size.
  • C · Fissure core. Jagged beams. A dark crust with glowing cracks that burn brighter the longer you hold.
