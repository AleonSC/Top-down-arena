FIELD HOSPITAL

Healer Soldier · second ultimate (picked instead of Lifeline) · press Q, pick a door, a medical cell opens behind it.

Approximate HTML mockup: glow and fonts are browser effects. The real look comes from the game's draw code once it's built.

HOW IT WORKS

  • Q: every free door spot lights up as a green hologram door (the mystery door look, a white + instead of ?), with a ghost of the cell behind it.
  • Door spots whose cell is already taken (a built room, a mystery door's room, a sealed mini-boss room) show a dim red ×. You can't build over them.
  • Fire on a lit door: the cell slides in and slams into the wall. Q again cancels; nothing is spent.
  • No free door spot in reach: nothing is spent and the key shows NO ROOM.
