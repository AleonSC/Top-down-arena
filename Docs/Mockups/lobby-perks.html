Lobby Perks Mockups

Where the Perks menu could live in the lobby (design in Docs/BossHuntsAndPerks.md §2: 1 slot to start, up to 3; perks unlock per boss; equipped per player). Every option keeps the lobby exactly as it is today and adds as little as possible.

All three put your equipped perks on your party card (teammates' show on theirs, as the design asks). The options differ in where you change them. In Option A you can click the perk cards to equip them; the tabs work in all three.
