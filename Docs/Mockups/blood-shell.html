Hemomancer Tank: Blood Shell

Animation mockup. Blood Shell stops being a plain shield and becomes a big blood bubble you hold. Bullets and enemies stop at the skin, allies sit inside and get healed, and you pay for every hit with your own health until you reach a third.

Blood Shell hold to keep up · pops at 1/3 health · each blocked bullet costs you, heals allies inside

The bubble follows you. Enemy bullets burst against the skin and the shot's cost comes out of your health; every ally inside gets a share back. Enemies cannot walk in. Allies cross the skin freely. The ring around the bubble is the health you can still spend before it pops. Pick a look, then press Release early to let go before the third.

Beat by beat

  1. 0.0 sPress Space. Blood rushes in from the ground and swells into a bubble 96 px out.
  2. holdThe bubble stays on you with no timer. The ring shows health left to spend.
  3. each hitThe bullet bursts on the skin and the skin dents and ripples. A bead of blood runs from you to the impact and beads run to each ally inside.
  4. +0.35 sBeads land: your health drops 7, each ally inside gains 4.
  5. 1/3 HPThe skin shudders, then bursts. Enemies nearby are shoved back and a stain stays on the floor.
  6. releaseLet go early and it collapses back to the ground.

Rules as drawn

  • Blocks every enemy bullet that reaches the skin, red aimed shots included.
  • Enemy bodies cannot enter. They bunch up outside and slide round it.
  • Allies and you cross the skin freely and can stand inside.
  • Every blocked bullet costs you 7 HP (about 3% of 240).
  • Every ally inside gets 4 HP back per blocked bullet.
  • It ends when you let go, or when your health reaches 1/3.
