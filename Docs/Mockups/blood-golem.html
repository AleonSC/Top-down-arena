Blood Golem

Tear the blood stacks off every enemy near you and pile them onto yourself, paying for the rest with your own health, until 12 coats harden into a golem. For 10 s you are a walking wall: the coats soak every hit, everything near you is taunted, and your weapon becomes two huge fists. When it ends, whatever is left of the golem bursts and pays you back.

    Rules proposed numbers

    • Cast80 s cooldown, nothing up front. You can move at 50% while it gathers.
    • Gather2 s. Every blood stack on an enemy within 300 px is torn off and becomes a coat on you (it hurts them 6). Coats the stacks don't cover are paid from your health, 2.5% of max health each, up to 12 coats (at most 30%).
    • Golem10 s. Each coat is 8% of your max health in blood armor (12 coats: 96%) that takes every hit before your health does. Twice your size, 75% speed, can't be stunned or knocked back; taunts every enemy within 240 px once a second.
    • FireGolem Fist: left, right, then Slam. Fists hit 45 in a 100° cone out to 90 px; the Slam hits 70 to everything within 120 px and stuns 0.5 s. Every hit adds a blood stack.
    • AltHurl Clot (2 s): tear off a coat and throw it up to 400 px. It splashes 60 within 80 px and leaves a 4 s pool slowing enemies 40%.
    • SheddingEach 8% of armor lost falls off as a chunk. An enemy the golem kills regrows a coat (up to 12).
    • CollapseAt 10 s, at 0 coats, or press again. Each coat left bursts for 20 within 180 px and gives you back 2% of max health; the splash heals allies in it 4 a second for 2.5 s.

    SynergyBlood Blast and Blood Lash put blood stacks on enemies, so a fight that's already bloody makes the golem nearly free.
