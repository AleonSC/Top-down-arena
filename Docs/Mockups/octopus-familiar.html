Octopus Familiar

Press the ability, click a player. A spirit octopus floats round them for 60 s and slaps every enemy that comes close, up to 8 at once, one per arm. It goes early if it takes enough damage.

Press Q, then click a player

Browser mockup: the glow and soft edges are approximate; the game version is drawn with the arena's own shapes.

How it works

  1. Press

    Pick mode: reach ring round you, a ring on each player, tether to the one under the cursor. The key again cancels, nothing spent. Alone, it goes to you at once.

  2. Click

    Octopus pops out of an ink splash beside that player (you included) and floats round them.

  3. Guard

    Enemies inside 130 px of the player get slapped: 8 arms, one enemy each, so up to 8 at once. Each slap heals the player 3.

  4. Soak

    Its body blocks enemy shots and takes bites. Health bar over it, timer ring round it.

  5. Gone

    After 60 s, or at 0 health, it bursts into ink. Casting again moves it.
