Blood Lash: chain combo

Proposed rework for the tank Hemomancer's Blood Lash. Fire becomes a four-step string. Every lash that lands hooks the enemy with a blood chain (up to 4 links each), so the combo builds a web of chains back to you. The fourth step, Reap, yanks every chained enemy in and turns the spent links into Clot.

The weapon-action key (Left Alt) spends or uses the chains. Four takes on it are below, each live. Move the pointer over a stage to aim, click to lash, right-click for the alt fire, or leave Auto on. This is an approximate browser mockup: the look in Unity would be drawn with the game's own primitives.

Fire: Crack, Backhand, Snap, Reap

Each press inside 0.8 s of the last lash moves the string on; wait longer and it starts over at Crack. Chains stay on an enemy for 3 s after its last hit, then drop a link every 0.5 s. The chain counter top left is the total links out.

Four pips under the Hemomancer show the step; the thin bar under them is the 0.8 s window.
1 · FIRE

Crack

110° sweep, 130 px, 24. The outer 30% cracks for double and taunts. +1 link on each enemy hit (+2 from the tip).

2 · FIRE

Backhand

The same sweep back the other way, 24. +1 link each. Two quick lashes chain most of a crowd.

3 · FIRE

Snap

A straight 36° lash out to 190 px, 36. +2 links. The reach shot for the one that's hanging back.

4 · FIRE

Reap

Yanks every chained enemy within 340 px in to touching you: 20 + 12 a link, stunned 0.6 s. Each link spent becomes 1 Clot (4% less damage taken, up to 5).

Alt fire: four takes

In the demo the Hemomancer runs Crack, Backhand, Snap to build chains, then uses the alt fire instead of Reap. The alt can go off at any point in the string and does not reset it.

      What changes from today

      • Clot is no longer built straight from hits: Reap (or Sever) turns links into Clot, so a tank wants to finish the string.
      • The Clot Burst at full Clot goes; Reap is the payoff now.
      • Closer hits are no longer flung out to the tip: that fought the pull. Tip cracks and taunts stay.
      • Crimson Whip upgrade: +20% reach and +1 max link per enemy.

      How it would be drawn

      • Lash: the slack whip from the held pose, a tapered red ribbon trailing its tip, a crack spark and ring where the tip lands.
      • Chains: oval links of clotted blood from your hand to each enemy, sagging and swaying, thicker per link; red diamonds over the enemy count its links. They flicker as they're about to drop.
      • Reap: the chains go taut, shorten, and each enemy lands with a splash and number.
