Fireball Sounds

The Elementalist's Fireball: a big, slow ball (430 px/s, 1.4 s) fired every 0.5 s that rolls through enemies and sets them burning. Today it uses Sfx.SpellFire, shared with other spells.

Each option is a Launch plus a Burn (the crackle when it ignites an enemy). Loop fires at the real rate, with a burn on every other shot.

Notes

  • Mockup only: nothing in the game changed. A pick becomes a dedicated Sfx.ShotFireball / FireballBurn pair in a new Game/FireballSoundBank.cs (a protocol bump, since Sfx is a byte enum).
  • Browser playback is an approximation of the game's synth (noise + filters + sine sweeps).
  • Each play varies pitch a little, like the game's takes.
