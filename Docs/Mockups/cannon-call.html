Cannon Call

Fire a flare at your cursor and a battery behind the lines drops a cannonball on the mark 0.95 s later. Pick the spot, hold the pack there, and the slam does the rest. Pick a look under the stage; tick Mastered to see the three-ball barrage.

Cannon Call Space · 12 s cooldown · 420 px reach · 130 px blast · 70 / 35

A pack closes on the knight. Space fires a flare to the cursor; the mark shows the blast ring and a closing inner ring as the countdown. The ball's shadow grows on the mark, then it slams: 70 inside the core, 35 to the edge, everything thrown outward and stunned, and every enemy shot in the ring knocked out of the air.

Beats

  1. 0.00 sSpace: the knight raises the sword and fires a flare at the cursor (up to 420 px).
  2. 0.30 sThe mark lands: dashed blast ring (130 px), core ring (70 px), countdown arc. Enemies can see it too.
  3. 0.35 sThe ball enters from behind you, its shadow growing on the mark.
  4. 1.25 sSlam: 70 in the core, 35 to the edge; shove 160 to 540 px/s outward and a 0.6 s stun. Heavies take a third of the shove.
  5. afterCrater for 3 s (cosmetic in Iron Round and Royal; Hellfire burns 8/s inside it).

How it plays

  • The skill is the lead: you aim where the pack will be in a second, and herd it there with your swings.
  • Kill 3 or more with one call and half the cooldown comes back, like the Gauntlet's and Bladed Dive's resets.
  • Allies take nothing; the shove can push enemies into walls or another ball.
  • Mastered: two smaller balls (92 px, 49 / 25) land 0.22 s apart on either side along the aim.
  • Bosses take the damage, aren't shoved or stunned.
