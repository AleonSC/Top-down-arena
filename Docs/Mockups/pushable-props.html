Pushable props

Lean on a crate or barrel and it slides. Tanks shove it fastest, Dps second, Healers slowest, and enemies move it at a crawl. Even the Healer is more than ten times faster than an enemy.

The race

One crate per lane, pushed for ten seconds at the real speeds (1 canvas pixel = 1 arena pixel). Use the speed buttons to watch the enemy move at all.

Where it stops

A pushed prop stops at walls, pillars, the arena edge and other solid props. The pusher is held to the prop's pace, so pushing a blocked crate just stops you. Enemies path around crates and barrels when any way past exists, and only shove one when boxed in. Pots still smash, health barrels still break, chests never move. Bosses do not push.
