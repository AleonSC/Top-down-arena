Fuma Shuriken: two stances

Three reworks for the Tank Ninja's Fuma Shuriken. In all three, Left Alt swaps between Throw stance (today's loop) and a new Blade stance, and the parry RMB does something special mid-attack.

Each stage plays its moves on a loop. Click a move to repeat it. Nothing in the game has changed yet.

ARecall & Buzzsaw

Parry pulls the thrown star back to your hand. Blade stance holds it out as a grinding saw.

BTwin Halves

Blade stance splits the star into two blades with a 3-step chain. Parry skips ahead and leaves ghost halves behind for the finisher.

COrbit

Blade stance sets the star circling you; Fire lashes it out. Parry flips the orbit or turns shots into extra stars.
