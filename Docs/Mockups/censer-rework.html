Censer: the Thurible Rite

Today the Censer does one 360° spin on repeat (14 damage, 80 px). Each spin heals allies within 140 px for 3, and walking leaves a trail of incense. There is no combo and no alt.

The rework turns it into a healer's chain on the primary button. A Sweep and a Return Sweep each heal the allies around you and drop incense. The Benediction finisher lets the chain out for a full turn and heals more for every enemy it hits. Each enemy you hit lights an Ember, and the alt attack spends Embers to put healing somewhere the swings can't reach. The knight holds the censer on a real chain that whips, lags and trails smoke. Each move on its own

The censer

A bronze thurible with a pierced body, a domed lid and a cross finial, on a 10-link chain held in the right hand. The coals glow through the piercings and flare when the head connects. The left hand rests on the chest and opens in a blessing whenever a heal goes out.

The chain is simulated. It sags at rest, goes taut and whips in a swing, and drags behind when you walk. Smoke streams off the head all the time and thickens as it speeds up.

Time is how soon the next click is taken. All numbers are proposed starting values, and none of this is in code yet.

What stays

  • Close reach and a heal on every swing. The sweeps heal for 3 within 140 px, as the spin does today.
  • Incense still heals the allies standing in it (4 HP/s) and still spoils the aim of enemies who shoot from inside it.
  • Shot upgrades work as on every melee weapon: damage, swing speed, crits and lifesteal apply to every hit.

What changes

  • Incense comes from your swings: a puff where each sweep ends and a cloud from the Benediction. The walking trail goes (see open questions).
  • Embers: each enemy a step hits lights one, up to 5. They never fade. The alt spends them.
  • +1 projectile becomes +15 px reach, +1 HP on every heal and +1 Ember cap.

Build notes

Open questions

  • Which alt: Censer Cast, Vigil or Holy Tether? Cast is the recommendation; it gives the Healer Knight reach, which it has nothing else for.
  • Can the walking incense trail go? With puffs, clouds and the alt all making incense, a fourth source mostly adds clutter. Removing it means deleting code, so it needs your yes.
  • Should a Cast or Tether on a downed ally also speed up the revive, the way Guardian's Oath's ward does?
