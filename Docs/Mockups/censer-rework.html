Censer Rework

The Healer Knight's Censer stops being one spin on repeat. The primary button runs a three-step rite: a sweep, a return sweep, then a full Benediction spin. The sweeps heal the allies around you a little, and the Benediction heals more for every enemy it hits. Each enemy you hit stores an Ember. The alt (weapon-action key) spends the Embers to send healing somewhere you can't reach.

Two alts are mocked up below; Censer Cast is the recommendation. Every number is a proposed starting value, and none of this is in code yet.

Alt attack options

Recommended Alt A · tap

Censer Cast

Throw the censer head out on its chain to the cursor. It lands in a burst that hurts enemies, then leaves a big incense cloud that heals allies inside and makes enemy shots fired from it stray. The head reels back on its own. All your Embers are spent, and the cloud grows with each one. This gives the Censer reach: you can heal the ally at the far end of the arena without leaving the melee.

Vigil

Hold the key to whirl the censer over your head. You walk at half speed inside a ring of incense that heals allies in it, chips enemies that touch it and bends enemy shots that cross it. Each 0.7 s burns one Ember; it ends when you let go or run out. Strong for holding a revive, but it keeps you where the fighting is.

Open questions

  • Censer Cast or Vigil for the alt? Cast gives reach, which the Healer Knight lacks; Vigil overlaps with Guardian's Oath's ward.
  • Remove the walking incense trail? With puffs from sweeps and clouds from the Benediction and Cast, the trail is mostly noise. Keeping it would mean three sources of incense on screen.
  • Should a Cast that lands on a downed ally speed up the revive, the way Guardian's Oath's ward does?
  • Should the Benediction always land on the third click, or only when the first two sweeps hit something?
