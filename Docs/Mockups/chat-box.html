Chat box

A small text chat in the bottom right, just above the HUD. Press Enter and the box slides up with a text line; type, press Enter to send, Esc to cancel. When nobody has typed or sent anything for a few seconds it fades away by itself, and a new message from a teammate brings it back, only for a moment. Three looks below: pick one (or mix).

Look
Hide after5 s

Opening. Enter opens the entry line and takes the keyboard: movement, abilities and the emote key are ignored while you type, so a typed Q doesn't fire your ability. Enter again sends and closes the line; Esc closes it without sending. Your character keeps being drawn and the fight keeps running, nothing pauses.

Auto-hide. The box stays up while you type or someone speaks, then fades out after a few quiet seconds (5 s here; a setting could change it, or keep it always on). With it hidden, an incoming line pops it up for about 4 s so you don't miss it, then it fades without grabbing the keyboard. Older lines fade first, one by one, so a busy chat never fills the corner.

Where. Right edge, 16 px in, its foot just above the HUD strip so it never covers the ability bar or your card. 300 px wide, at most about seven lines; the player's name in their own colour, system lines (joins, leaves, revives) in grey italics.

Wiring (if picked). A Msg.Chat (player index and up to 120 characters, client to host, relayed to everyone) so a protocol bump; the host trims, rate-limits (about 1 line a second) and drops control characters. Session keeps the last few lines, drawn by a new ChatBox.cs next to Hud.cs. Solo play shows only system lines.
