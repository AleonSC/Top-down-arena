Ninja DPS sounds

Sound effects for the Ninja DPS: the Kunai weapon, its three abilities (Shadow Step, Vanish, Blade Fan) and the ultimate, Death Lotus. Two sets of the same sounds so you can pick a character. They are built in this page from the pieces the game's SoundBank uses, and each card shows the recipe that would go into it.

Putting them in the game

  • One new Sfx value per sound, added at the end of the enum, each with a Set(...) line in SoundBank.Build(). Names are in the recipe boxes. Two helpers join the existing ones: Delay and Room.
  • Throws, hits, the Blade Fan and the Death Lotus are raised by the host through the existing fx path, so every player hears them, quieter with distance. The mark fade and the "empty" tick play on your own screen only.
  • The Blade Fan whirl is a short looping sound that restarts while the fan is active (4 s), not one long buffer.
  • Nothing here changes the network format, so Session.ProtocolVersion stays as it is.
