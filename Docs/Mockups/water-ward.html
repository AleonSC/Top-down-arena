Healer · Elementalist · 4th Space ability · mockup, approximate lookWater Ward

Each cast calls 2 drops of water down from above; they land beside you and circle you, up to 10. A drop that touches an enemy splashes: small area damage and wet. A drop that meets an enemy bullet eats it and bursts into a wider wet mist (no damage).

Pick a look

Turn off Auto-cast and press Space (or click the arena) to stack drops by hand. "No enemies" lets you watch the orbit fill to 10.

At 10 the key greys out ("FULL"); at 9 a cast adds 1. Wet is the existing Tsunami status, so a DPS Elementalist's lightning chains through every soaked enemy.

Beat by beat

  1. 0.00 sCast: a ripple at your feet, two landing rings marked on the orbit.
  2. 0.00–0.45Two drops fall from above (0.12 s apart), stretch as they fall, and splash down onto the ring.
  3. 0.45 →They join the orbit; every drop eases to even spacing round you and trails behind as you move.
  4. touchDrop hits an enemy: crown splash, a 52 px ring, 12 dmg and wet on everything inside, a puddle left behind.
  5. bulletDrop meets a shot: the shot hisses to steam, the drop bursts into a 72 px mist that soaks everything inside.
  6. 3.00 sKey ready again; the pips under it show how many drops are circling.
