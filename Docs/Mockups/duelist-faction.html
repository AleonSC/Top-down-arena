Wave 30 boss faction · mockup (approximate, not the game's draw code)

The Masked Court

The Duelist's faction, the same size as the Warden's: 8 enemies, 4 squads, a set piece and a 10-wave Campaign cycle. A fencing salon at a masked ball: every enemy wears a mask, carries steel, and previews one move the Duelist uses in its three stages.

Members

The Warden's slot each one fills is in brackets. Weight and first round copy the Warden's table in Approaches.cs. The Approaches plan's Blade Dancer and the Decoy rework are both here (the Decoy became the Mummer's copies).

Squads

Four squads and the wave-4 set piece, like Breakout / Yard / Patrol / Cell block and the Riot line.

Campaign cycle

One lesson per wave, building to the Duelist at wave 30.

Notes

  • Fencer, Second, Parrier, Marshal and Gallant fade at the first boss after (Fade 0–1); Harlequin and Mummer stay two waves longer (Fade 2, like Thugs and Shivs).
  • Most rules already exist on the boss: the Lunge's hanging blades, the Guard's bounce-back front, hollow decoy shots (Defs.HollowShot), the dueling circle and the Salute's perfect-parry stagger. The faction would call them on regular enemies, smaller.
  • Shielders and Reavers (the plan's "regular" fits) stay in the normal pool; Parrier and Gallant replace their themed role.
  • New EnemyType values would go at the end, before None, with a protocol bump.
