Bone Spear: Marionette

Rework of the DPS Hemomancer's Bone Spear. Click an enemy up to 520 px away and the Hemomancer works its blood from range: each press of Fire lands the next move of a string on that enemy, as a phantom blow from inside its body.

Below: ten candidate moves, a builder to try strings of four, and four takes on the alt fire. Click a move card to add it to the string. Browser mockup only: in Unity it would be drawn with the game's own primitives.

Move library

Each card loops its move on one target, with three bystanders for the moves that hit others. Opener and Link moves are quick; a Finisher ends the string and starts it over.

Build a string

Up to four moves. Fire with your cursor on an enemy marks it and lands step 1; each press within 0.9 s lands the next step. Click a slot to clear it. On the stage, click an enemy to fire yourself, or leave Auto on.

The blood thread runs from your hand to the marked enemy; it fades 1.4 s after the last move.

Alt fire: four takes

Each demo runs a short string around the alt so you can see how they work together.

      Shared rules

      • Fire needs the cursor on an enemy within 520 px (Implosion's targeting). Clicking a different enemy starts the string over on it.
      • 1 health a move, 3 for the finisher. Hits heal the usual 3%.
      • A move on an airborne enemy (Heart Lift) hits 25% harder.
      • Marrow card: +1 bystander hit by Bone Spear, Marrow Burst and Haymaker; finisher costs no health.

      How it would be drawn

      • A beaded blood thread from the hand to the target, slack between moves, taut on Reel.
      • Each blow is a translucent red phantom (fist, crescent, cut) that hits from your side, with blood thrown out the far side.
      • The hand mirrors the blow: push, sweep, clench. The marked enemy carries a ring of three bone ticks.
