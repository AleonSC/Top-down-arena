Healing Rain

A rain zone cast at your cursor. Allies standing in it are healed over time; enemies in it get slowed. Four looks to pick from. Each loops the same scene: cast, about 5 s steady, then the fade. The circle is the real radius (130 px, same as Blizzard). Allies are the two blue figures (the left one is hurt), enemies are the red diamonds.

Reduced motion is on: the scenes show one still frame each. Press Restart to step through them.

Shared across all four

  • Cast: a ring (the game's FxKind.Ring) expands to 130 px in 0.4 s while the first drops land; a soft chime plays.
  • Heal tick: every 0.5 s each ally inside gets a small green + floater and a brief glow, so you can read who is being healed.
  • Enemies inside: slowed 30%; a blue-teal tint and falling drops on them so the slow is readable.
  • Expiring: the last 1 s the disc fades and the rim dashes break up. Drops thin out. No timer ring (like Blizzard).
  • Colour: a green-teal (0.35, 1, 0.7) so it never reads as Blizzard's pale blue or Sanctify's gold.
  • Capstone (if it lasts 10 s): same look for longer; nothing new to draw.

  • The icon is a new entry in PixelArt.cs (a cloud with drops); the Time Rewind hourglass2 mapping goes.
