Design notes

Always readable

Every cursor keeps a clear centre point and sits between 20 and 36 px wide, so none hides enemies or bullets. Fire pulses the cursor outward for about a quarter second.

Rank ladder

Ranks shown are placeholders, chosen to avoid the ranks that already give a trail, frame or card. Ten cursors, one new reward about every four ranks.

Visual effects off

With Visual effects off, animated cursors (embers, storm, void motes, prism spin, light trail) fall back to a still version of the same shape and colour.
