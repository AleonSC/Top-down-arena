Legion

Today Legion drops 6 wolves and 6 bats around you for 10 s (10 and 10 when mastered) and your other minions frenzy. It works, but it reads as "more of the same minions". Four ways to give the ultimate its own shape. Each preview shows the same moment: the cast, the pack going in, and the fade-out. The teal wolves are your normal companions, which frenzy (red glow) in every look. Damage, counts and timings here are placeholders.

All previews No look picked yet
LOOK A

Howling Pack

What you see

The current ultimate, finished properly. A howl ring, then 6 wolves and 6 bats burst outward from you and hunt. Wolves leave an ember trail, bats a flutter of sparks. They fade out together at 10 s.

Feel

Safest and cheapest. Reads instantly, no new rules, easiest to balance. It is still just a bigger swarm.

LOOK B

Spirit Stampede

What you see

Ghostly wolves and bats surge out in a fan, wave after wave, and pass straight through everything. Each spirit hits each enemy once, then dissolves at the far edge. Nothing stays behind.

Feel

A burst of damage you aim by facing. Plays like a piercing line attack, so it suits crowds and long corridors but does little against one target that stands still.

LOOK C

Alpha

What you see

One huge spectral Alpha wolf, three times normal size, with a ring of bats orbiting it. The Alpha charges the nearest enemy and howls every 2 s for a small shockwave. A bat peels off every 0.4 s to hunt on its own.

Feel

A single big thing to watch and root for. Strongest silhouette of the four, and the Alpha could be a target for enemy fire. Fewer bodies on screen than today.

LOOK D

Blood Moon

What you see

A red moon rises over the arena and tints it. The pack circles you for a second, then is released all at once, running faster than normal. When the moon sets, the pack goes with it.

Feel

The most dramatic, and the only one that changes the whole screen. The moon could also buff allies in range. Biggest effect cost, so it needs a Reduced effects version.
