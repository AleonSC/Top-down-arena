Boss proposal · wave 40

The Storm Herald, in three stages

Today it drifts along the top of the arena, calls a lightning strike on each player and two random spots every 2.2 s, and fires 3 piercing shots at you every second, for the whole fight. This mockup splits it like the Warden, the Hive Queen and the Colossus proposal: three stages by health, one attack at a time with breathers between, a 2 s stage change that clears the screen, and a last stand below 10%. The storm builds as it goes, from a gathering storm to a gale to a supercell. The arena below plays each stage against two test dummies (you in green, an ally in blue); pick a stage to watch it, or let it cycle.

What it keeps

  • Lightning strikes: a 70 px circle on each player, 1.1 s warning, then 25 + 0.3 × wave damage (37 at wave 40). They're stage 1's main turn and come back faster and smaller in stage 3.
  • The piercing volley: 3 shots, 0.15 rad apart, at 330 px/s, every 1 s, now only in breathers (and stretched to a 5-shot fan in the Gale).
  • Drifting side to side along the top, out of reach, until the last stand pulls it down to the middle.
  • Its look: a 9-sided storm-blue body, 40 px.

What's new

  • Lightning rods: rods fall at marked spots and arcs jump between them. A line hazard, like the Colossus proposal's fissure.
  • Wind: the whole Gale stage blows one way, then the other. It pushes you and bends every bullet, its own included.
  • Ball lightning: slow orbs that drift at you on the wind and arc to each other when they're close.
  • Downburst and storm front: rows of strikes that sweep across the arena, with a gap to find.
  • Conductor: a co-op mechanic. One player carries a charge for 3 s, then it jumps to every ally near them, so the party has to split up.
  • Rod cage: six rods around you, arcs between them, and one open side to get out through.

To build it

  • Stage, turn, timers, wind and the Conductor in a HeraldState on the boss, synced like WardenState; thresholds 65 / 30 / 10%.
  • Reuse: the Hazard strike circles for strikes, downbursts and the storm front; EnemyType adds or bullets for ball lightning.
  • New: rods and their arcs as a line hazard (shared with the Colossus fissure if both are built). Wind works like Frozen Lake's momentum in LocalControl: each game pushes its own player from the synced wind, and bullets drift by the same amount on both sides.
  • Health: BossHpMult for the Storm Herald (4× now) retuned in the Boss Lab for 60–120 s, plus Bestiary entries, a Boss Lab stage lock and sim tests per stage.
