# Weapon Variants (was "Ascensions"): 4 tiers

**Status: design in progress, nothing built.** The lobby's ascension button is still a placeholder ("0/3", `LobbyUi.AscensionKey`); it becomes "Variants" with 4 tiers.

## Decisions (owner)

- Renamed **Ascensions -> Variants**. 4 tiers per weapon, matching the 100-wave Campaign.
- Variants are unlocked by spending a **currency** (name TBD), saved in the profile like Rank and unlocks.
- The currency is **earned** from: beating a Campaign (and its 25/50/75/100 wave milestones), beating Boss Hunts, and **surprise enemy drops** (rare, random).

## Proposed (not agreed)

- Milestone waves 25/50/75/100 pay a bigger lump of currency.
- Tier cost 10 / 20 / 40 / 80; boss kill ~3 (+2 no-death); surprise drop 1-3 from a rare marked enemy.

## Open

- Currency name; which sources are in; amounts.
- What each tier does per weapon (one twist growing per tier, or a new twist each tier).
- Co-op: whose profile receives the currency.
- Profile save format and protocol bump when built.
