Drop audio files (wav, mp3, ogg, aif...) in this folder and they show up in the game's
Settings > Audio > Music player on their own, as "Custom" tracks. No setup needed.

Tips: for long songs, select the clip in Unity and set Load Type to "Streaming" in its Inspector.
To replace the menu, battle or a boss's music, or to give tracks titles and moods, make a Music Pack:
Assets > Create > Arena > Music Pack, and save it as Assets/Resources/MusicPack.
