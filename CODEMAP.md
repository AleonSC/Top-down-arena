# CODEMAP

Quick index of the source. Paths are under `Assets/Scripts/` (`Core/` = plain C# logic, namespace `Arena`, no Unity;
`Game/` = Unity front end). `Game` (Core) is one big `partial class` split by feature; `ArenaApp` (Game/) is the
front-end `partial class`. Older, more detailed per-file notes (not maintained): `Docs/CodeNotes.md`. Player-facing reference: `README.md`.

## Engine and match flow (Core)
- `Game.cs`: match state, lifecycle, frame loop (`FixedStep`, `HostTick`), picks, local movement/dash
- `Entities.cs`: data types (`Player`, `Enemy`, `Bullet`, `Orb`, `Zone`, `Minion`, `Effect`, `PlayerInput`)
- `Waves.cs`: wave ticking, wave cleared, game over
- `TimedWaves.cs`: timed waves (`TimedWaves.Share` arrival curve with three surges and lulls (`Surges`, `Pulse`, `InSurge`), `BeginTimedWave`, `TickTimedSpawn`, alive cap; off in sim tests except suites `timed`, `portals`)
- `SpawnPortals.cs`, `Game/SpawnPortalLook.cs`: spawn portals (Docs/Spawns.md; the look: a "loading" gate, its ring fills while it opens): a timed wave's singles come in bursts through `EnemyType.Portal` (`TickPortalSpawn`, `OpenPortal`, `TickPortals`, `portalCargo`); shoot one closed to cancel the rest (`PortalSealed`, coins via `DropGold`); flanks behind the party (`FlankSpot`, red arrow `DrawFlankArrows`); `SpawnSpot` in `EnemyAI.cs`; preview `PORTALS=1 shot.sh x hud 8`; test suite `portals`
- `WaveReady.cs`: the between-waves Ready / Auto-ready system (`Msg.WaveReady`) and the cleared wave's numbers and awards (`Msg.WaveSummary`); box drawn by `Game/WaveClearedUi.cs`
- `BossGate.cs`, `Game/BossGateLook.cs`: the boss gate: in the break before a boss a summoning circle opens mid-hub and the fight waits until every standing player is in it (`GateUp`/`GatePos`/`GateFill` in the snapshot, `TickBossGate` in place of the Ready countdown, `BotGate`, `GatesOn` off in sim tests but `bossgate`); the box's Gate tab and strip (`DrawGateBar`, `WaveClearedUi.cs`); tinted by the coming boss (`BossGateInk`), on the minimap (`DrawGateOnMap`) and the zoom arrows (`DrawGateMarker`); the open toast (`DrawGateToast`) and hum; preview `GATE=<fill> GATEIN=<n> [GATEBOSS=<EnemyType>] [ZOOM=2.6] [ROOMS=800,500] shot.sh x hud 10 12.2 3`; test suite `bossgate`
- `WaveDesign.cs`: wave editor designs, `.waves` format, playing them in Practice
- `DesignedWaves.cs`, `DesignedWaves.Generated.cs`: the wave sets built into the game from `Data/Waves/*.waves` (generated by `Tools/WaveGen/build.sh`)
- `Practice.cs`: Practice Range plan and picker list
- `PracticeRespawn.cs`: the Practice Range's Respawn and Restart wave (`Msg.Respawn`, `RequestRespawn`, `HostRespawn`, `RestartPracticeWave`); a Practice run never ends in a wipe (`CheckGameOver`)
- `Training.cs`: the Training section's drills: `TrainingDrill` (name, group, goal, steps, a `PracticePlan`; the `.training` text format), `TrainingLibrary` (groups, built-in drills); `Docs/Training.md`
- (`Rooms.cs` `ArenaKind` picks the arena: the Campaign is Expanding, `RunMod.SimpleArena` the open arena, the Rooms arena a dev-dock prototype.)
- `Checkpoints.cs`: checkpoints and lives (Docs/Checkpoints.md): `LivesUsed`, `LivesMax`/`LivesLeft` (DiffKnob.Lives), `OfferRetry` (a wipe with a life left: respawn at the wave's start, `SaveStore.Read`, the checkpoint as fallback), `TakeRetry` (Session.RetryRun), `GiveUpRetry`/`SettleRetry` (Game over, or an untaken retry, ends the run), `Deathless`; the checkpoint file is SaveStore.WriteCheckpoint. Test suite `checkpoints`
- `Hunts.cs`: Boss Hunt mode (ten waves ending in a boss); the 100-wave Campaign's legs (`LongRun`, `Leg`, `ShownWave`, `ShownEnd`, `NextTrackWave`, `Pace`; test suite `longrun`); the boss between waves of a run (`BossSlot`, `BossNow`, `BossNext`, `IsBossWave`: the boss follows every fifth regular wave and keeps its number; HANDOFF §2)
- `HuntMap.cs`: the hunt map (Docs/HuntMap.md): `HuntMap.Generate(seed)` makes ten rows of 2-3 nodes on three lanes (non-crossing links, Skirmish/Elite/Boss so far), `Choices`/`Pick`/`Clear`/`Clues`/`Path`/`Restore` for progress, up to 5 boss clues each a health or damage clue (`ClueIsDamage`, from the seed), `Length` 10 or 20 and the Miniboss node at step 10 of 20 (Docs/LongHunt.md); played through `HuntMapGame.cs` (test suite `huntmap`); its step-10 checkpoint save and Continue are in `HuntMapGame.cs` + `RunSave.IsHunt` (suite `huntsave`)

- `HuntMapLayout.cs`: where the hunt map's right-angle lines run (`HuntMapLayout.Links`: ports along each node's edge, a height per link picked to cross the fewest others; tested in suite `huntmap`)
- `HuntMapGame.cs`: a Boss Hunt played as a map (the lobby's Hunt map switch, `Session.HuntMapMode`): the host rolls the map, everyone votes on the next node (`SendHuntVote`, `Msg.HuntMapVote`; a tie goes to the host, the 20 s timer picks), the node's wave is tougher for an Elite, a Rest heals and a Shop opens the Bazaar with no wave, an Ambush is a fast short wave paying XP and a chest, a Contraband is dark and pays a prize card, a Gamble is rolled into one of the fights (`HuntBosses.NodeAt` in `BossSchedule.cs`), clearing the wave clears the node; state goes out with `Msg.HuntMapState` (test suite `huntmap`)
