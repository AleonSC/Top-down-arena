# CODEMAP

Quick index of the source. Paths are under `Assets/Scripts/` (`Core/` = plain C# logic, namespace `Arena`, no Unity;
`Game/` = Unity front end). `Game` (Core) is one big `partial class` split by feature; `ArenaApp` (Game/) is the
front-end `partial class`. Older, more detailed per-file notes (not maintained): `Docs/CodeNotes.md`. Player-facing reference: `README.md`.

## Engine and match flow (Core)
- `Game.cs`: match state, lifecycle, frame loop (`FixedStep`, `HostTick`), picks, local movement/dash
- `Entities.cs`: data types (`Player`, `Enemy`, `Bullet`, `Orb`, `Zone`, `Minion`, `Effect`, `PlayerInput`)
- `Waves.cs`: wave ticking, wave cleared, game over
- `WaveReady.cs`: the between-waves Ready / Auto-ready system (`Msg.WaveReady`) and the cleared wave's numbers and awards (`Msg.WaveSummary`); box drawn by `Game/WaveClearedUi.cs`
- `WaveDesign.cs`: wave editor designs, `.waves` format, playing them in Practice
- `DesignedWaves.cs`, `DesignedWaves.Generated.cs`: the wave sets built into the game from `Data/Waves/*.waves` (generated by `Tools/WaveGen/build.sh`)
- `Practice.cs`: Practice Range plan and picker list
- `PracticeRespawn.cs`: the Practice Range's Respawn and Restart wave (`Msg.Respawn`, `RequestRespawn`, `HostRespawn`, `RestartPracticeWave`); a Practice run never ends in a wipe (`CheckGameOver`)
- `Training.cs`: the Training section's drills: `TrainingDrill` (name, group, goal, steps, a `PracticePlan`; the `.training` text format), `TrainingLibrary` (groups, built-in drills); `Docs/Training.md`
- `Hunts.cs`: Boss Hunt mode (ten waves ending in a boss)
- `Contraband.cs`: Contraband (the cycle chest on waves 6, 11, 16... when anyone has it; its holder's chest items are rare or better, in `Props.cs`)
- `RiotGearLook.cs` (Game): Riot Gear's Plate look: the plate that swings toward incoming shots, the recharge ghost, the block effect (`FxKind.RiotBlock`) and the HUD chip above the XP strip
- `RiotGear.cs`: Riot Gear's shield (`RiotBlocks`, called first in `Damage`; `TickRiot`; `PerksFor` picks who brings what at match start, `GivePerks` applies it on every screen); burns, gas, acid and lava ticks pass `overTime` and go through
- `Perks.cs`: perks (`PerkId`, `Perks.All`): unlock rules, slots, equip/unequip (one per boss), cleaning the profile's `perks` line; the effects come next (`Docs/RiotGear.md`)
- `Challenges.cs`: challenge rooms (portal, crystal, gatekeeper, kill rush, altar)
- `Paths.cs`: choose-your-path offers, path enemy mixes, hazards, arena rules
- `Props.cs`: crates, pots, barrels, treasure chests; pushing crates and barrels (`ShoveProps`: Tank 70, Dps 50, Healer 34, enemy 3 px/s; `PropDir`: enemies path round them first)
- `ExpansionGame.cs`, `Game/ExpansionUi.cs`: the Expanding arena in a run: team purse (`Purse`, `AddPurse`), the rooms bought (`Expanded`), the offer and team vote (`OfferExpansion`, `CastExpandVote`, `ResolveExpansion`, `Msg.ExpandState`/`ExpandVote`), `ExpansionScale` (more enemies on a bigger map), `CurrentWorld` picks Single/Wings/the built map; the vote window, mini-maps and purse chip
- `Expansion.cs`: the Expanding arena's rooms: `RoomKind` (Combat, Shop, Floor), `ExpansionRoom` (a bought box + its parent), room shapes, `Offers` (seeded offers for a milestone, same on every machine), `PriceFor`, `Build` (hub + rooms -> a `World`); `DetRandom` (platform-independent RNG). Test suite `expand`
- `World.cs`: an arena's geometry as data (`World`: areas, doors, portals, wall spans, cover, steering floor plan `With`), `ArenaLayout` (Single, Wings, Expanding later), `World.Single` (the plain box); the Wings map is `Rooms.Wings`, `Game.CurrentWorld`/`ArenaKind` pick the one in play (Docs/ExpandingArena.md)
- `Rooms.cs`, `Game/RoomsLook.cs`: the Rooms arena (the Arena setting, Docs/Rooms.md): the open map (random spawns `RoomsRandomSpot`, free camera from 1.5x zoom with a prompt), the vault (the Gatekeeper) and the minimap; the parked room-by-room version (`Rooms.RoomByRoom`: nests, spills, locked doors, room rewards); the Wings world (hub, side rooms, hallways, doors; built as a `World`, the `Rooms.Areas/Doors/...` members read it), its walls added to the cycle's floor plan (`Rooms.With`), `PickRooms`/`RoomsOn` (regular waves), `PullIntoHub` (boss waves); the floor and door look. Camera follow in `CameraZoom.cs`
- `Layouts.cs`, `LayoutRules.cs`, `Game/LayoutLook.cs`: the six arena layouts (walls, pillars, player and enemy edge gates, per boss cycle): the data, how they play (push-out, shots, spawns, steering), the look (phosphor vector, Docs/Mockups/arena-layouts-digital.html)
- `Shop.cs`: the Bazaar (gold, shelves, shop actions)
- `Difficulty.cs`, `Modifiers.cs`, `Balance.cs`: difficulty presets, run modifiers, general balance numbers
- `Pause.cs`, `DevTools.cs`, `Showcase.cs`: pause sync, F1 dev tools, lobby loadout preview game (with stand-in teammates for ally abilities)
- `Tsunami.cs`: the Elementalist DPS's Tsunami: the wave, wet (slow) and lightning that arcs to every wet enemy (`WetArcs`, called from each lightning source)
- `Nimbus.cs`, `Chalk.cs`: the Nimbus (cloud minion, rain, strikes) and Spirit Chalk (ink dots, burn/heal/bullets, loops that pen enemies in)
- `DpsTools.cs`: DPS meter data (`DpsTrack`, `Game.Dps`, host only) and target dummies (`DevSpawnDummy`, `TickDummy`, `Foes()`)
- `MathTypes.cs`: `V2`, `Rgba`, `MathX`

## Combat (Core)
- `Combat.cs`: bullets, collisions, damage to players/enemies, on-hit/on-kill, burn, orbs
- `Weapons.cs`: fire rate, firing, volleys, range, Revolver, Mender's Bow
- `Abilities.cs`, `Ultimates.cs`, `Mastery.cs`: Soldier abilities, the 12 ultimates (second ultimates: `LightningRod.cs`), ability XP/levels
- `Sanctum.cs`: the Healer Elementalist's Sanctum ultimate: seven healing pulses from the dome's centre, each ally healed as the ring reaches them (`SanctumPulse`, called from `TickZones`; `SanctumLook.cs` draws the bubble)
- `HallowedBlade.cs`: the Healer Knight's Hallowed Blade ultimate once it lands: a planted `ZoneKind.HallowedBlade` zone (ring heals allies and burns enemies) plus an arena-wide pulse every 2 s at a third of the ring's strength (`HallowedPulse`, called from `TickZones`; `HallowedBladeLook.cs` draws the sword, beam and ripples, and the falling sword)
- `Aegis.cs`: the Elementalist tank's Aeroaegis vortex (goes to the player the cursor is nearest, enemies held out; `AeroaegisLook.cs` draws it)
- `Dome.cs`: the Tank Soldier's Fortress dome (enemies and their shots held out, `DomeLook.cs` draws it)
- `Juggernaut.cs`: the Tank Soldier's second ultimate, the Juggernaut Suit (picked on the lobby's Ultimate row, `LobbyEntry.Ult` / `Player.UltPick`; `Upgrades.Ultimate(role, kit, pick)`, `AltUltimate`, `ClampUlt`): 10 s of armour soaked before health (`JuggSoak`, from `Damage`), 25% slower, a taunt (`PickTarget`), a flak shard back at the shooter per bullet (`JuggShot`/`JuggFlak`, from the bullet loop; `Bullet.Shooter`, `FxKind.JuggShard`), ends on time, armour 0, down or wave end (`TickJugg`); `Game/JuggernautLook.cs` draws it; `Docs/Mockups/juggernaut-suit.html`
- `PoisonDart.cs`, `Game/PoisonDartLook.cs`: Poison Dart (DPS Soldier ability, took `AbilityId.Dash`'s place and number): `CastPoisonDart` darts the enemy nearest the cursor, the venom (`Enemy.VenomT`/`VenomDps`, `TickVenom`, replicated `Enemy.Venomed`), `SpreadVenom` from the kill loop (Combat.cs) passes it on; Virulence (`Stats.VenomMult`/`VenomReachBonus`); looks: `FxKind.VenomDart`/`VenomSpread`, the tint, drips, stuck dart and status icon (`Docs/Mockups/poison-dart.html`, look A)
- `ForcePact.cs`, `Game/ForcePactLook.cs`: Force Pact (Tank Summoner ability, `AbilityId.GuardianSpirit`): binds the enemy nearest the cursor, the thrall (`MinionKind.Thrall`: type, health, fights near you, respawns via `Player.PactCd`), the pact slots; the Soul Chains look and the `FxKind.Pact*` effects
- `Parry.cs`, `Stun.cs`, `Knock.cs`, `Repulsion.cs`: parry shield (a perfect parry's needle, flash and the world slowing: `WorldSlowT`, `Game/ParryLook.cs`, `Game/ParrySoundBank.cs`), stun, knockback slides, repulsion cannon
- `Upgrades.cs`, `StatSheet.cs`, `BuildPower.cs`: upgrade cards and ability registry (the milestone pool is added last, `WithMilestones`), stat panel numbers, boss health scaling
