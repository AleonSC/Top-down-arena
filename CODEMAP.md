# CODEMAP

Quick index of the source. Paths are under `Assets/Scripts/` (`Core/` = plain C# logic, namespace `Arena`, no Unity;
`Game/` = Unity front end). `Game` (Core) is one big `partial class` split by feature; `ArenaApp` (Game/) is the
front-end `partial class`. Detailed per-file notes: `HANDOFF.md` §2. Player-facing reference: `README.md`.

## Engine and match flow (Core)
- `Game.cs`: match state, lifecycle, frame loop (`FixedStep`, `HostTick`), picks, local movement/dash
- `Entities.cs`: data types (`Player`, `Enemy`, `Bullet`, `Orb`, `Zone`, `Minion`, `Effect`, `PlayerInput`)
- `Waves.cs`: wave ticking, wave cleared, game over
- `WaveReady.cs`: the between-waves Ready / Auto-ready system (`Msg.WaveReady`) and the cleared wave's numbers and awards (`Msg.WaveSummary`); box drawn by `Game/WaveClearedUi.cs`
- `WaveDesign.cs`: wave editor designs, `.waves` format, playing them in Practice
- `DesignedWaves.cs`, `DesignedWaves.Generated.cs`: the wave sets built into the game from `Data/Waves/*.waves` (generated by `Tools/WaveGen/build.sh`)
- `Practice.cs`: Practice Range plan and picker list
- `Hunts.cs`: Boss Hunt mode (ten waves ending in a boss)
- `Contraband.cs`: Contraband (the cycle chest on waves 6, 11, 16... when anyone has it; its holder's chest items are rare or better, in `Props.cs`)
- `RiotGearLook.cs` (Game): Riot Gear's Plate look: the plate that swings toward incoming shots, the recharge ghost, the block effect (`FxKind.RiotBlock`) and the HUD chip above the XP strip
- `RiotGear.cs`: Riot Gear's shield (`RiotBlocks`, called first in `Damage`; `TickRiot`; `PerksFor` picks who brings what at match start, `GivePerks` applies it on every screen); burns, gas, acid and lava ticks pass `overTime` and go through
- `Perks.cs`: perks (`PerkId`, `Perks.All`): unlock rules, slots, equip/unequip (one per boss), cleaning the profile's `perks` line; the effects come next (`Docs/RiotGear.md`)
- `Challenges.cs`: challenge rooms (portal, crystal, gatekeeper, kill rush, altar)
- `Paths.cs`: choose-your-path offers, path enemy mixes, hazards, arena rules
- `Props.cs`: crates, pots, barrels, treasure chests
- `Shop.cs`: the Bazaar (gold, shelves, shop actions)
- `Difficulty.cs`, `Modifiers.cs`, `Balance.cs`: difficulty presets, run modifiers, general balance numbers
- `Pause.cs`, `DevTools.cs`, `Showcase.cs`: pause sync, F1 dev tools, lobby loadout preview game (with stand-in teammates for ally abilities)
- `Tsunami.cs`: the Elementalist DPS's Tsunami: the wave, wet (slow) and lightning that arcs to every wet enemy (`WetArcs`, called from each lightning source)
- `Nimbus.cs`, `Chalk.cs`: the Nimbus (cloud minion, rain, strikes) and Spirit Chalk (ink dots, burn/heal/bullets, loops that pen enemies in)
- `DpsTools.cs`: DPS meter data (`DpsTrack`, `Game.Dps`, host only) and target dummies (`DevSpawnDummy`, `TickDummy`, `Foes()`)
- `MathTypes.cs`: `V2`, `Rgba`, `MathX`

## Combat (Core)
- `Combat.cs`: bullets, collisions, damage to players/enemies, on-hit/on-kill, burn, orbs
- `Weapons.cs`: fire rate, firing, volleys, range, Revolver, Mender's Bow
- `Abilities.cs`, `Ultimates.cs`, `Mastery.cs`: Soldier abilities, the 12 ultimates, ability XP/levels
- `Sanctum.cs`: the Healer Elementalist's Sanctum ultimate: seven healing pulses from the dome's centre, each ally healed as the ring reaches them (`SanctumPulse`, called from `TickZones`; `SanctumLook.cs` draws the bubble)
- `Aegis.cs`: the Elementalist tank's Aeroaegis vortex (goes to the player the cursor is nearest, enemies held out; `AeroaegisLook.cs` draws it)
- `Dome.cs`: the Tank Soldier's Fortress dome (enemies and their shots held out, `DomeLook.cs` draws it)
- `Parry.cs`, `Stun.cs`, `Knock.cs`, `Repulsion.cs`: parry shield, stun, knockback slides, repulsion cannon
- `Upgrades.cs`, `StatSheet.cs`, `BuildPower.cs`: upgrade cards and ability registry (the milestone pool is added last, `WithMilestones`), stat panel numbers, boss health scaling

## Classes (Core)
- `Classes.cs`, `Kits.cs`, `Defs.cs`: class registry and hooks, minion/zone defs, weapon/enemy/bullet defs
- `Knight.cs`: melee swings, sword waves, Knight abilities
- `Ninja.cs`: Ninja weapons, Substitution, Shadow Step, clones, Vanish
- `BloodMage.cs`: Hemomancer (health costs, Implosion, Blood Pact)
- `Minions.cs`, `KitWeapons.cs`: Elementalist/Summoner abilities, minions, zones, their weapons
- `LumenWard.cs`: the Healer Elementalist's Lumen Ward (`AbilityId.Sanctify`, renamed): a cone zone (`ZoneKind.LumenWard`, `Zone.Aim`) that heals inside, burns bullets in it and holds the ones outside (`Bullet.Stasis`, worked out on every peer)
- `RadiantOrb.cs`: Healer's Radiant Orb
- `Adrenaline.cs`: Healer Soldier's Adrenaline Shot (replaced Haven; target pick; speed, fire rate and damage cut live in `Entities.cs` `Player.Speed`, `Weapons.cs` `FireRate`, `Combat.cs`); look in `Game/AdrenalineLook.cs`.
- `SoulLantern.cs`, `Medkit.cs`: Healer Summoner's Soul Lantern (linked lantern minion), Healer Soldier's Deployable Medkit (thrown case, healing orbs); `SoulGhost.cs` is the retired Soul Link ghost, no longer cast

## Enemies and bosses (Core)
- `EnemyAI.cs`: enemy roster per wave, movement, firing, contact damage, bullet budget
- `Variety.cs`, `Readability.cs`: wave 15+ enemies and elite traits, shooters taking turns
- `Zombies.cs`: Zombie Infection modifier (seven zombie kinds)
- `Approaches.cs`, `WardenFaction.cs`: boss factions/approach mixes, Warden's faction enemies
- Bosses by wave: `Warden.cs` (5), `Queen.cs` (10), `Reapers.cs` (15), `Singularity.cs` (20), `Colossus.cs` (25),
  `Duelist.cs` (30), `Lich.cs` (35), `Herald.cs` (40), `Devourer.cs` (45), `Eclipse.cs` (50); `LateGame.cs` is the shared
  late-boss logic
- `BossLab.cs`, `BotBrain.cs`: Boss Lab sessions, the "smart" bot
- Boss loot: `QueenLoot.cs`, `ReaperLoot.cs` (legendary weapons/abilities)

## Networking (Core)
- `Pings.cs`: pings (`PingKind`, `Session.SendPing`, host pause and cap, Focus snapping to an enemy; `Msg.PingMark`); a partial of `Session`
- `LobbyAvatars.cs`: the Hangout room's characters, their positions and `Msg.LobbyMove`/`LobbyPos`; a partial of `Session`
- `LobbyCombat.cs`: Hangout PvP: shots, health, knock-out, respawn, the Dash/Barrier/Burst heal abilities (`Msg.LobbyFire`/`LobbyShot`/`LobbyAbility`); a partial of `Session`
- `Session.cs`: host/join, lobby, **`Msg` enum**, **`ProtocolVersion`** (bump when a message or snapshot changes)
- `Net.cs`: `NetPeer`, length-prefixed TCP
- `GameNet.cs`, `NetSync.cs`: all snapshot and input serialisation (one ref pass for read and write)
- `SessionRecorder.cs`: session JSON recorder
- `Replay.cs`: replays: `ReplayFile` (.replay: header, marks, frame lengths, every host message), `ReplayRecorder` (fed by `Session.Broadcast` and `Game.FixedStep`), `ReplayPlayer` (a private client-mode `Session` that reads the messages; seek, step, speeds)

## Saving, stats and text data (Core)
- `SaveData.cs`, `RunSaves.cs`, `Profile.cs`: saved runs, profile, records, unlocks
- `ProfileStore.cs`, `ProfilesUi.cs`: up to 3 profiles (a folder each under `profiles/`), the "Who's playing?" screen before the menu: always four boxes (`Docs/Profiles.md`)
- `ProfileTransition.cs`: the half-speed move between that screen and the main menu (plot points), with its sounds
- `Rank.cs`: the cosmetic account rank: XP curve, tiers, XP for a run (`Rank.Award`), the cosmetics table (frames, trails, a background); XP, equipped cosmetics and recent runs live on `Profile`, the lobby carries each player's `Rank`/`Frame`/`Trail` (`Msg.Cosmetics`, `Session.ShareCosmetics`)
- `RunStats.cs`: per-player run stats for the end screen
- `BestiaryData.cs`: Bestiary entry types (`BestiaryData.Generated.cs` is generated, don't edit)
- `TextOverrides.cs`: dev text-editor edits
- `WeaponDps.Generated.cs`: generated weapon DPS table, don't edit
- `BossSounds.cs`: audio cues for boss attacks

## World rendering (Game)
- `WorldRenderer.cs`: GL drawing of the arena (floor, layers, `DrawEnemy`/`DrawPlayer` entry, GL primitives)
- `WorldPlayers.cs`, `WorldEnemies.cs`, `WorldShots.cs`, `WorldEffects.cs`: players, enemies/zones/minions, shots, effects
- `CharacterModels.cs`: the 18 player models (role build + class costume) and one held-weapon model per `WeaponId`
- `ClassLooks.cs`, `AbilityLooks.cs`, `BloodMageLook.cs`: per-class ability/buff overlays on the model, ability feedback, Blood Mage visuals
- `RepulseLook.cs`: the Repulsion Cannon's blast (muzzle star, smoke, ring, sparks, a small kick; Visual effects on)
- `FrostLook.cs`: the Frost Lance's crystal-spear icicle and shard splinters (Visual effects on)
- `RootLook.cs`: the Treant Rod's root lash and the root tether to its Treant (`DrawRoot`, `DrawRootLashFx`)
- `LumenWardLook.cs`: Lumen Ward's cone of light (look A, Cathedral, of `Docs/Mockups/sanctify-cone.html`: bands, god rays, dust) and the halo on bullets it holds
- `LifelineLook.cs`: the Healer Soldier's Lifeline (heartbeat pulse, halo and column of light on each player it reached; `FxKind.Lifeline`)
- `ElementalistLooks.cs`: Meteor (falling rock, impact), Blizzard (whiteout) and Tsunami (crest, wet drips) looks
- `NimbusLook.cs`, `ChalkLook.cs`: the Nimbus's cloud, rain and lightning; Spirit Chalk's ink, sealed loops and the ring on penned enemies
- `ShotLooks.cs`, `Juice.cs`, `Ambience.cs`: weapon shot looks, screen shake/particles/glow, weather and trails
- `CameraLean.cs`: the camera lean (view eases toward your aim and movement, 50% of 5% of the arena; Settings → Gameplay), `ToUi` for labels placed from a world position
- `MeleeLooks.cs`: melee attacks with Visual effects on: the weapon model swinging through a glowing arc (style E)
- `BearLooks.cs`: the Bear Totem: a claw on each hand, claw trails, the Bear companion, claw scars on bleeding enemies
- `EnemyPresenceLook.cs`: regular enemies' soft shadow and quiet colour glow with Visual effects on (look D of `Docs/Mockups/enemy-prominence.html`)
- `RadiantOrbLooks.cs`: Radiant Orb's stardust trail with Visual effects on (style B): motes and sparkles in the glow pass
- `AirTrailLooks.cs`: the air trail with Visual effects on (look A, Ribbons): two fading streamlines behind any player, enemy or minion, scaled by drawn speed (none under 110 px/s, full at 330)
- `GunBowLooks.cs`: the Healer's Revolver (kick, flash, smoke, brass) and Mender's Bow (flexing limbs, snapping string, ring of light) with Visual effects on
- `TankGunLooks.cs`: the Tank Soldier's Scattergun (recoil, pump, shell), Flak Cannon (recoil, heat, lifted shell, airburst) and Riot Repeater (bolt kick, brass, glowing rounds) with Visual effects on
- `SoldierLooks.cs`: Soldier DPS ability looks with Visual effects on: Dash's comet streak, the Cluster Grenade's flight ring and Frag Burst blast, Mark for Death's reticle and skull
- `ChainLightLooks.cs`: Chain Light with Visual effects on: a soft fan of light, beams and halos (style A, Radiance)
- `EnergyWardLook.cs`: the Healer Elementalist's Energy Ward bubble with Visual effects on: a spring-driven radius that shrinks and squashes toward each hit, blue to green, and a green heal core growing inside (Heal core + Jelly hits)
- `MendPulseLook.cs`: the Healer Soldier's Mend Pulse with Visual effects on: a swelling fill, a double-beat ring and a heart on each ally reached (style B, Heartbeat)
- `TankLooks.cs`: the Tank Soldier's Roar (wave, enemy "!" pops, orbiting plates, draining rim, tethers) and Bulldoze (lane, afterimages, bow wave, hit sparks, landing stomp) with Visual effects on, timed from the cast's `FxKind.Callout`
- `SanctumLook.cs`: Sanctum's Bubble look (shimmering shell, pulse discs and sparkles, rim flare, flicker at the end); the old plain dome with Visual effects off
- `AeroaegisLook.cs`, `FrostNovaLook.cs`, `FlameWallLook.cs`: Aeroaegis's gust shell, Frost Nova's shatter and ice blocks, Flame Wall's magma ring, with Visual effects on (`Docs/Mockups/aeroaegis.html`, `frost-nova-rework.html`, `flame-wall-rework.html`)
- `DomeLook.cs`: the Fortress dome, a ring of gold plates that flash as enemy shots reach it (look B of `Docs/Mockups/fortress-dome.html`)
- `BulletRainLook.cs`: Bullet Rain (Elementalist DPS ultimate, `ZoneKind.BulletRain`): arena-wide rain, needles hardening in the zone
- `ArtilleryLook.cs`: Artillery Assault (DPS Soldier ultimate, once Bullet Storm): heat aura and ring, speed streaks, muzzle flash
- `BarrierLook.cs`: the Tank Soldier's Barrier with Visual effects on: eight armour plates that slam down, recoil on blocks and are knocked off as it soaks (style C, Bulwark Plates)
- `ArcLightningLooks.cs`: Arc Lightning with Visual effects on: charge halo, sparks and dashed strip, the spine bolt (style A, Thunderhead)
- `PropsLook.cs`, `ZombieLook.cs`, `VarietyLook.cs`, `ChallengeLook.cs`: props, zombies, wave 15+ enemies, challenge rooms
- Boss visuals: `WardenLook.cs`, `QueenLook.cs`, `ReaperLook.cs`, `SingularityLook.cs`, `ColossusLook.cs`,
  `DuelistLook.cs`, `LichLook.cs`, `HeraldLook.cs`, `DevourerLook.cs`, `EclipseLook.cs`, `StageShiftLook.cs`,
  `QueenLootLook.cs`, `ReaperLootLook.cs`
- `PixelArt.cs`: 16x16 icons as text grids

## HUD and in-run UI (Game)
- `WaveClearedUi.cs`: the "Wave n cleared" box between waves (squad tally, awards, next wave, Ready and Auto-ready bar)
- `ChatBox.cs`: the match text chat (Enter opens, bottom right, pills that fade; state in `Session.Chat`)
- `Hud.cs`: wave plate, player cards, ability bar, banners
- `CardArt.cs`: the picker cards' shaded gradient bodies (one texture per colour; `Ui.Glow`/`Ui.Dot` draw the glow and stack circles)
- `HudBossBars.cs`, `HudPicker.cs`, `HudSummary.cs`, `HudTab.cs`, `HudTiles.cs`: boss bars, upgrade picker, run summary, Tab menu, ability tile animations
- `HealthBars.cs`, `Tooltips.cs`, `ApproachUi.cs`: damage-ghost bars, hover tooltips, approach chip
- `PickIntro.cs`, `PickOutro.cs`, `RunEnding.cs`, `Recall.cs`, `LaunchIntro.cs`: picker open/close, run end, beam-out, run launch
- `PathsUi.cs`, `ShopUi.cs`, `RunStatsUi.cs`, `HuntUi.cs`: path cards, shop screen, run stats screen, hunt tab

## Menus and lobby (Game)
- `ArenaApp.cs`: boot, input, frame loop, layout/scaling, `OnGUI` dispatch
- `Ui.cs`: IMGUI style kit (panels, buttons, bars)
- `Menus.cs`, `MenuIntro.cs`, `MenuDrawer.cs`, `WindowAnim.cs`: main menu, pause/settings/records drawers, animations
- `MenuVhs.cs`: the main menu buttons' VHS hover (with the `Sfx.MenuHover` tape-head tick on enter, via `MenuVhs.OnHover`; `Ui.SilentHover` mutes the generic hover tick for these buttons) and snap-to-focus click (`MenuVhs.Draw` wraps a button's draw call in `Menus.cs` and `SavesUi.cs`)
- `Keybinds.cs`, `KeybindsUi.cs`: every rebindable key (`KeyAction`, defaults, saved in PlayerPrefs, rebind capture) and Settings → Keybinds. Weapon secondaries read `PlayerInput.WeaponAction` (Left Alt) in `Core/Ninja.cs` `LocalWeaponDash`
- `PerksUi.cs`: the lobby's Perks tab (look A: your slots left, perk cards right; click to equip, hover for the rules) and the perk icons on party cards (`DrawSeatPerks`); see `Docs/RiotGear.md`
- `CosmeticsUi.cs`: your cosmetics window (the drawer opened by the main menu's name chip: Frame, Trail, Background, Reward ladder)
- `RankLook.cs`, `RankUi.cs`, `RankTrails.cs`, `FrameLooks.cs` (the animated frames), `CardBackgrounds.cs` (the ten party-card fills, rank 6-55), `NameEffects.cs` (the twelve name looks, `DrawName`), `AimLineLooks.cs` (the nine rank-unlocked aim lines and Classic, `DrawAimLook`; local only), `CursorLooks.cs` (the ten rank-unlocked crosshairs and Classic, `DrawCursorLook`; local only), `EmoteLooks.cs` / `EmoteBar.cs` (the thirteen emotes and the hold-to-open bar; state in `Session.Emotes`), `LightCycleTrail.cs` (the rank-45 corner-snapped wall trail): the rank badge (a pixel shield per tier), the results strip, badge hover card and Records > Rank, and the trails drawn behind players
- `LobbyRoom.cs` (with `Core/LobbyAvatars.cs`): the lobby's Hangout tab, a big scrolling room where everyone's character walks with WASD wearing their cosmetics, with a minimap, a PvP switch and the preview-window grow transition (`Docs/Hangout.md`)
- `SavesUi.cs`: the Play picker in the main menu box (latest run, New run, Older runs), Records, unlock toasts. The box sweeps between the menu buttons and this picker with a scan line (`menuPlayK`, `Menus.cs`)
- `FieldGuide.cs`, `ReferencePages.cs`: in-game Bestiary/Armory, links to the web pages

## Dev tools and editors (Game)
- `DpsMeterUi.cs`: F2 DPS meter overlay and the F1 DPS tab
- `ReplayUi.cs`: F1 Replays tab (record, folder, list, rename, delete) and the replay timeline bar; `Core/Replay.cs` is the file, recorder and player
- `DevMenu.cs`, `DevTextEdit.cs`, `BossLabUi.cs`, `PracticeUi.cs`, `WaveEditorUi.cs`: F1 tools, F4 text edit, Boss Lab, Practice Range, F1 wave editor
- `../Editor/ArenaEditor.cs`: scene setup and Windows build menu item

## Audio (Game)
- `SoundBank.cs`: every synthesised sound effect
- `GameMusic.cs`, `MenuMusic.cs`: in-run and menu music
- `MusicPack.cs`: the Inspector asset for your own clips (replace menu / battle / boss music, extra tracks); `CustomMusic.cs`: the music folder scan and WAV decoder for your own tracks; `MusicLibrary.cs`: every track the music player lists (extra mood recipes) and the `Jukebox` selection logic; `MusicPlayerUi.cs`: Settings > Audio > Music player
- `PickSounds.cs`, `PauseGlitch.cs`: upgrade picker and pause-menu sounds
